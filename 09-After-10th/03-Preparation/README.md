# Entrance preparation alongside boards

1. Choose your intended stream and 2–3 eligible entrance options.
2. Download the **current official entrance syllabus** for each option; it can
   differ from CBSE board content. Check test duration and negative marking.
3. Use the [Class 10 subject hubs](https://clickalex.github.io/Class10CBSE/)
   to revise Maths, Science, English and Social Science as appropriate.
4. During the week, practise weak concepts and short timed question sets.
5. Once a week, attempt an official past/sample paper where available and
   record mistakes. Avoid calling unofficial practice papers “official”.
6. At 9 PM, inspect the admission watch report and verify any changed notice.

Prepare the requested photos, signatures, date-of-birth proof and applicable
category/residence documents. Check rules for applying before Class X results.
Save application/payment receipts privately. Keep a local-school backup and
confirm hostel, fees and travel arrangements before accepting a seat.
