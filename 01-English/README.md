## What to keep per chapter

For literature the marks come from reference-to-context extracts and short/long
answers, so a chapter folder is most useful with:

- `summary.md` — 150 words, in your own words
- `characters-themes.md` — who, what they represent, the central idea
- `extracts.md` — 4–5 likely passages with the answers you would write
- `word-meanings.md` — difficult words with meanings, for the unseen/grammar sections
