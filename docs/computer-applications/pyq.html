CBSE • CLASS 10 • COMPUTER APPLICATIONS

Computer Applications — PYQ trends

Where past papers keep returning, chapter by chapter. Use it to decide revision order — not to guess the paper.

Trend notes below are an original reading of publicly available past papers and sample papers. They are not official CBSE predictions.

ChapterTitleWhat past papers ask
Ch 1Internet BasicsOne or two definition questions every paper. The ARPANET and IPv6 facts are the most repeated.
Ch 2Internet Services & ProtocolsProtocol full forms and port numbers appear in almost every paper. The URL breakdown is a reliable 2-3 marker.
Ch 3Network Types, Topologies & DevicesOne topology question with a diagram, plus a device-comparison question, appears in most papers.
Ch 4Mobile & Wireless TechnologyA generations table question is very common, and it is fully memorisable.
Ch 5Web Browsers, Search & Web ConceptsBrowser versus search engine and cookies versus cache are the two most repeated comparisons in Unit I.
Ch 6HTML I — Introduction & Basic TagsWriting a complete page by hand appears in every paper. The <title>-in-<head> point is a perennial one-marker.
Ch 7HTML II — Text Formatting & ListsList questions with a specific type or start value are extremely common, as are output-prediction snippets.
Ch 8HTML III — Links & ImagesOne image-tag question and one link question appear in most papers. href versus src is the standard one-marker.
Ch 9HTML TablesA table-writing question with at least one merged cell appears in most papers. It is the highest-value single question in Unit II.
Ch 10HTML FormsA complete form-writing question appears in most papers, usually with radio buttons so the shared-name rule can be tested.
Ch 11CSS and MultimediaOne short CSS question (name the property / write one rule) plus audio-video tags — the closing marks of Unit II.
Ch 12Netiquette, E-Commerce & Digital DivideShort 2–3 mark definitions: one netiquette list, one e-commerce safety question, one digital-divide question.
Ch 13Cybercrime, IPR & Open SourceOne IPR question and one FOSS-versus-proprietary question appear in most papers.
