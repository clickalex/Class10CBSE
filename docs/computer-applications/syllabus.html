Removed from the syllabus

  • Scratch II, Python Revision and Python Conditionals & Loops appear in several publisher books but are not part of the current 165 theory units.
  • CSS is theory, not just practical. Colour, font, border, margin, float and the rest are listed in Unit II of the 2026–27 syllabus.
  • Frames, <marquee> and <pre> are not in Unit II. Frames appear only as a lab exercise; audio-video tags are the examinable “special” tags.
  • The IT Act, e-waste and digital footprint are not in Unit III. Unit III is netiquette, e-commerce (privacy, fraud, secure transmission), freedom of information, the digital divide, IPR and open source.
  • Viruses and malware are lab-blog topics, not theory questions — Unit III tests behaviour and rights, not threat names.
