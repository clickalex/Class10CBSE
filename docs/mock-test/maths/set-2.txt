SECTION B - Algebra (Q3-Q10, 8 marks)
-------------------------------------

Q3. A polynomial of degree 3 is called:
    (a) linear
    (b) quadratic
    (c) cubic
    (d) biquadratic

Q4. If one zero of x² − 4x + 1 is 2 + √3, the other zero is:
    (a) 2 − √3
    (b) −2 − √3
    (c) √3 − 2
    (d) −√3

Q5. If a boat’s downstream speed is 12 km/h and upstream speed is 8 km/h, its still-water speed is:
    (a) 10
    (b) 4
    (c) 2
    (d) 20 km/h

Q6. If x + 2y = 5 and 3x + ky = 15 are coincident lines, then k = :
    (a) 2
    (b) 3
    (c) 6
    (d) 15

Q7. For x² + 4x + k = 0 to have real roots, k must satisfy:
    (a) k ≤ 4
    (b) k ≥ 4
    (c) k ≤ −4
    (d) k = 4

Q8. If the roots of x² − 8x + k = 0 are equal, then k = :
    (a) 8
    (b) 16
    (c) −16
    (d) 64

Q9. The sum of the first 10 natural numbers is:
    (a) 45
    (b) 50
    (c) 55
    (d) 100

Q10. If the 3rd term of an AP is 4 and the 9th term is −8, its first term is:
    (a) 8
    (b) 6
    (c) 4
    (d) 2

-------------------------------------------

    (b) 2
    (c) 5/4
    (d) 9/4

Q18. sec²A − tan²A equals:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1
    (c) −1

Q19. If the angle of elevation of the sun is 45°, the length of the shadow of a pole equals:
