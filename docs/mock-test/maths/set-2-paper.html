Section A · Number Systems Q1–Q2 · 2 marks

1. Which has a terminating decimal expansion?

  • (a) 7/24
  • (b) 5/13
  • (c) 9/40
  • (d) 11/21

2. The HCF of the smallest prime number and the smallest composite number is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 4
  • (d) 6

Section B · Algebra Q3–Q10 · 8 marks

3. A polynomial of degree 3 is called:

  • (a) linear
  • (b) quadratic
  • (c) cubic
  • (d) biquadratic

4. If one zero of x² − 4x + 1 is 2 + √3, the other zero is:

  • (a) 2 − √3
  • (b) −2 − √3
  • (c) √3 − 2
  • (d) −√3

5. If a boat’s downstream speed is 12 km/h and upstream speed is 8 km/h, its still-water speed is:

  • (a) 10
  • (b) 4
  • (c) 2
  • (d) 20 km/h

6. If x + 2y = 5 and 3x + ky = 15 are coincident lines, then k = :

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 3
  • (c) 6
  • (d) 15

7. For x² + 4x + k = 0 to have real roots, k must satisfy:

  • (a) k ≤ 4
  • (b) k ≥ 4
  • (c) k ≤ −4
  • (d) k = 4

8. If the roots of x² − 8x + k = 0 are equal, then k = :

  • (a) 8
  • (b) 16
  • (c) −16
  • (d) 64

9. The sum of the first 10 natural numbers is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 50
  • (c) 55
  • (d) 100

10. If the 3rd term of an AP is 4 and the 9th term is −8, its first term is:

  • (a) 8
  • (b) 6
  • (c) 4
  • (d) 2

Section C · Coordinate Geometry Q11–Q12 · 2 marks

11. The distance of the point (3, 4) from the origin is:

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 7
  • (c) 25
  • (d) 12

12. The point (−3, 0) lies on:

  • (a) the x-axis
  • (b) the y-axis
  • (c) the line y = x
  • (d) the origin

Section D · Geometry Q13–Q16 · 4 marks

13. The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is 25:49. The ratio of their corresponding sides is:

  • (a) 25:49
  • (b) 5:7
  • (c) 7:5
  • (d) 49:25

14. If the corresponding sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 4 : 9, their areas are in the ratio:

  • (a) 4 : 9
  • (b) 2 : 3
  • (c) 16 : 81
  • (d) 8 : 27

15. The number of tangents from a point on the circle is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) 2
  • (d) infinite

16. A line that cuts a circle in two distinct points is called a:

  • (a) tangent
  • (b) secant
  • (c) chord
  • (d) diameter

Section E · Trigonometry Q17–Q20 · 4 marks

17. The value of sin²60° + 2 tan²45° − cos²30° is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 5/4
  • (d) 9/4

18. sec²A − tan²A equals:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) −1
  • (d) sin²A

19. If the angle of elevation of the sun is 45°, the length of the shadow of a pole equals:

  • (a) half its height
  • (b) its height
  • (c) twice its height
  • (d) √3 times its height

20. The angle made by the line of sight with the horizontal when the object is below the observer is the angle of:

  • (a) elevation
  • (b) depression
  • (c) reflection
  • (d) inclination

Section F · Mensuration Q21–Q24 · 4 marks

21. The area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm is:

  • (a) 77 cm²
  • (b) 154 cm²
  • (c) 308 cm²
  • (d) 616 cm²

22. The area of a semicircle of radius r is:

  • (a) πr²
  • (b) πr²/2
  • (c) 2πr
  • (d) πr²/4

23. When a solid is melted and recast into another shape, which quantity is conserved?

  • (a) surface area
  • (b) volume
  • (c) both
  • (d) neither

24. The volume of a sphere of radius r is:

  • (a) (4/3)πr³
  • (b) 4πr²
  • (c) (2/3)πr³
  • (d) πr³

Section G · Statistics & Probability Q25–Q28 · 4 marks

25. The mode of 3, 5, 7, 5, 9, 5, 8 is:

  • (a) 3
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 9

26. The class mark of the class 45–55 is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 50
  • (c) 55
  • (d) 100

27. In one throw of a die, the probability of getting a number less than 7 is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1/6
  • (c) 5/6
  • (d) 1

28. If P(E) = 0.37, then P(not E) = :

  • (a) 0.37
  • (b) 0.63
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 0.53
