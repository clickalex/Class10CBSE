SECTION B - Algebra (Q3-Q10, 8 marks)
-------------------------------------

Q3. The zeroes of x² + 7x + 10 are:
    (a) 2, 5
    (b) −2, −5
    (c) 2, −5
    (d) −2, 5

Q4. If α, β are zeroes of x² − 5x + 6, then α + β = ?
    (a) 6
    (b) 5
    (c) −5
    (d) 1

Q5. The pair x + y = 5 and 2x + 2y = 10 represents:
    (a) intersecting lines
    (b) parallel lines
    (c) coincident lines
    (d) none

Q6. The solution of x + y = 14 and x − y = 4 is:
    (a) x = 9, y = 5
    (b) x = 5, y = 9
    (c) x = 10, y = 4
    (d) x = 8, y = 6

Q7. The sum and product of the roots of 2x² − 7x + 3 = 0 are:
    (a) 7/2 and 3/2
    (b) 7/2 and 3
    (c) −7/2 and 3/2
    (d) 7/2 and −3/2

Q8. Which one is NOT a quadratic equation?
    (a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0
    (b) (x + 1)² = 2(x − 3)
    (c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0
    (d) 2x² = 8

Q9. S₅ − S₄ equals:
    (a) the 5th term
    (b) the 4th term
    (c) the 9th term
    (d) zero

Q10. The common difference of the AP whose nth term is 3n + 5 is:
    (a) 3
    (b) 5
    (c) 8
    (d) 2
