Section A · Number Systems Q1–Q2 · 2 marks

1. The HCF of 8 and 12 is:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 4
  • (c) 8
  • (d) 24

2. The decimal expansion of 23/(2³ × 5²) terminates after:

  • (a) 2 places
  • (b) 3 places
  • (c) 4 places
  • (d) 5 places

Section B · Algebra Q3–Q10 · 8 marks

3. The zeroes of x² + 7x + 10 are:

  • (a) 2, 5
  • (b) −2, −5
  • (c) 2, −5
  • (d) −2, 5

4. If α, β are zeroes of x² − 5x + 6, then α + β = ?

  • (a) 6
  • (b) 5
  • (c) −5
  • (d) 1

5. The pair x + y = 5 and 2x + 2y = 10 represents:

  • (a) intersecting lines
  • (b) parallel lines
  • (c) coincident lines
  • (d) none

6. The solution of x + y = 14 and x − y = 4 is:

  • (a) x = 9, y = 5
  • (b) x = 5, y = 9
  • (c) x = 10, y = 4
  • (d) x = 8, y = 6

7. The sum and product of the roots of 2x² − 7x + 3 = 0 are:

  • (a) 7/2 and 3/2
  • (b) 7/2 and 3
  • (c) −7/2 and 3/2
  • (d) 7/2 and −3/2

8. Which one is NOT a quadratic equation?

  • (a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0
  • (b) (x + 1)² = 2(x − 3)
  • (c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0
  • (d) 2x² = 8

9. S₅ − S₄ equals:

  • (a) the 5th term
  • (b) the 4th term
  • (c) the 9th term
  • (d) zero

10. The common difference of the AP whose nth term is 3n + 5 is:

  • (a) 3
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 8
  • (d) 2

Section C · Coordinate Geometry Q11–Q12 · 2 marks

11. The midpoint of (2, −3) and (−6, 5) is:

  • (a) (−2, 1)
  • (b) (2, −1)
  • (c) (−4, 2)
  • (d) (4, −8)

12. The centroid of the triangle with vertices (2, 3), (−4, 7) and (2, −4) is:

  • (a) (0, 2)
  • (b) (0, 0)
  • (c) (2, 2)
  • (d) (0, 3)

Section D · Geometry Q13–Q16 · 4 marks

13. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are:

  • (a) equal
  • (b) supplementary
  • (c) complementary
  • (d) proportional

14. In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC with AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 5 cm. Then EC = :

  • (a) 6.5 cm
  • (b) 7.5 cm
  • (c) 8 cm
  • (d) 9 cm

15. The angle between a tangent and the radius at the point of contact is:

  • (a) 45°
  • (b) 60°
  • (c) 90°
  • (d) 180°

16. Two tangents from an external point to a circle are:

  • (a) unequal
  • (b) equal
  • (c) always perpendicular
  • (d) parallel

Section E · Trigonometry Q17–Q20 · 4 marks

17. sin²30° + cos²30° equals:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1/2
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 3/4

18. If sin θ = cos θ, then θ = :

  • (a) 0°
  • (b) 30°
  • (c) 45°
  • (d) 90°

19. From the top of a tower the angle of depression of an object on the ground is 30°. The angle of elevation of the top from that object is:

  • (a) 30°
  • (b) 45°
  • (c) 60°
  • (d) 90°

20. A 10 m ladder reaches a window 8 m above the ground. Its foot is from the wall at a distance of:

  • (a) 4 m
  • (b) 6 m
  • (c) 7 m
  • (d) 9 m

Section F · Mensuration Q21–Q24 · 4 marks

21. The area of a quadrant of a circle of radius r is:

  • (a) πr²/2
  • (b) πr²/4
  • (c) 2πr
  • (d) πr²

22. If the radius of a circle is doubled, its area becomes:

  • (a) 2 times
  • (b) 4 times
  • (c) 8 times
  • (d) unchanged

23. The volume of a cone of radius r and height h is:

  • (a) πr²h
  • (b) πr²h/3
  • (c) 2πr²h
  • (d) πrh

24. The slant height of a frustum with radii R and r and height h is:

  • (a) √[h² + (R + r)²]
  • (b) √[h² + (R − r)²]
  • (c) h + R + r
  • (d) √(R² − r²)

Section G · Statistics & Probability Q25–Q28 · 4 marks

25. The class mark of the class 15–25 is:

  • (a) 15
  • (b) 20
  • (c) 25
  • (d) 10

26. The empirical relation connecting the three measures of central tendency is:

  • (a) Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean
  • (b) Median = 3 Mode − 2 Mean
  • (c) Mean = 3 Median − 2 Mode
  • (d) Mode = 2 Median − Mean

27. Two dice are thrown together. The total number of outcomes is:

  • (a) 6
  • (b) 12
  • (c) 36
  • (d) 24

28. One card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck. The probability of a black queen is:

  • (a) 1/52
  • (b) 1/26
  • (c) 1/13
  • (d) 2/13
