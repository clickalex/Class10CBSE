SECTION A - Number Systems (Q1-Q2, 2 marks)
-------------------------------------------

Q1. If the HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 60, one number being 12, the other is:
    (a) 20
    (b) 30
    (c) 40
    (d) 60

Q2. Which of these is irrational?
    (a) √(16/9)
    (b) √12
    (c) 0.444…
    (d) 22/7


SECTION B - Algebra (Q3-Q10, 8 marks)
-------------------------------------

Q3. A quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 3 and −3 is:
    (a) x² − 9
    (b) x² + 9
    (c) x² − 6x + 9
    (d) x² − 3

Q4. A quadratic polynomial can have at most:
    (a) 0 zeroes
    (b) 1 zero
    (c) 2 zeroes
    (d) 3 zeroes

Q5. For what value of k does kx + 3y = 7 and 2x + 6y = 14 have infinitely many solutions?
    (a) 1
    (b) −1
    (c) 2
    (d) 1/2

Q6. The pair 6x − 3y + 10 = 0 and 2x − y + 9 = 0 has:
    (a) one solution
    (b) two solutions
    (c) infinitely many solutions
    (d) no solution

Q7. The roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0 are:
    (a) 2, 3
    (b) −2, −3
    (c) 1, 6
    (d) −1, −6

Q8. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are:
    (a) real and distinct
    (b) real and equal
    (c) not real
    (d) always integers

Q9. The 10th term of the AP 2, 7, 12, … is:
    (a) 45
    (b) 47
    (c) 50
    (d) 52

Q10. The sum of the first 100 natural numbers is:
    (a) 5000
    (b) 5050
    (c) 4950
    (d) 5100

    (b) a²

    (d) 2a

    (a) equal

    (a) 10 cm

SECTION E - Trigonometry (Q17-Q20, 4 marks)
-------------------------------------------

Q17. The value of cos 60° is:
    (a) 1/2

    (d) 1

Q18. tan 45° × cot 45° is:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1

    (a) the vertical
    (b) the horizontal

SECTION F - Mensuration (Q21-Q24, 4 marks)
------------------------------------------

Q21. The perimeter of a sector includes:
    (a) only the arc
    (b) the arc and one radius
    (c) the arc and two radii
    (d) the chord and the arc

Q22. The area of a segment is found by:
    (a) sector − triangle
    (b) sector + triangle
    (c) circle − sector
    (d) triangle − sector

Q23. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius r and height h is:
    (a) 2πrh
    (b) πr²h
    (c) 2πr(r + h)

Q24. A cone, a hemisphere and a cylinder have equal bases and equal heights. The ratio of their volumes is:
