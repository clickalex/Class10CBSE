Section A · Number Systems Q1–Q2 · 2 marks

1. If the HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 60, one number being 12, the other is:

  • (a) 20
  • (b) 30
  • (c) 40
  • (d) 60

2. Which of these is irrational?

  • (a) √(16/9)
  • (b) √12
  • (c) 0.444…
  • (d) 22/7

Section B · Algebra Q3–Q10 · 8 marks

3. A quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 3 and −3 is:

  • (a) x² − 9
  • (b) x² + 9
  • (c) x² − 6x + 9
  • (d) x² − 3

4. A quadratic polynomial can have at most:

  • (a) 0 zeroes
  • (b) 1 zero
  • (c) 2 zeroes
  • (d) 3 zeroes

5. For what value of k does kx + 3y = 7 and 2x + 6y = 14 have infinitely many solutions?

  • (a) 1
  • (b) −1
  • (c) 2
  • (d) 1/2

6. The pair 6x − 3y + 10 = 0 and 2x − y + 9 = 0 has:

  • (a) one solution
  • (b) two solutions
  • (c) infinitely many solutions
  • (d) no solution

7. The roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0 are:

  • (a) 2, 3
  • (b) −2, −3
  • (c) 1, 6
  • (d) −1, −6

8. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are:

  • (a) real and distinct
  • (b) real and equal
  • (c) not real
  • (d) always integers

9. The 10th term of the AP 2, 7, 12, … is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 47
  • (c) 50
  • (d) 52

10. The sum of the first 100 natural numbers is:

  • (a) 5000
  • (b) 5050
  • (c) 4950
  • (d) 5100

Section C · Coordinate Geometry Q11–Q12 · 2 marks

11. Three points are collinear if the area of the triangle they form is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) maximum
  • (c) 0
  • (d) negative

12. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:

  • (a) a
  • (b) a²
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 2a

Section D · Geometry Q13–Q16 · 4 marks

13. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding sides are:

  • (a) equal
  • (b) proportional
  • (c) perpendicular
  • (d) parallel

14. In a right triangle with legs 6 cm and 8 cm, the hypotenuse is:

  • (a) 10 cm
  • (b) 12 cm
  • (c) 14 cm
  • (d) 9 cm

15. The number of tangents that can be drawn from a point outside a circle is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 3
  • (d) infinite

16. A tangent of length 24 cm is drawn from a point 25 cm from the centre. The radius is:

  • (a) 5 cm
  • (b) 7 cm
  • (c) 12 cm
  • (d) 24 cm

Section E · Trigonometry Q17–Q20 · 4 marks

17. The value of cos 60° is:

  • (a) 1/2
  • (b) √3/2
  • (c) 1/√2
  • (d) 1

18. tan 45° × cot 45° is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) 2
  • (d) undefined

19. The angle of depression is always measured from:

  • (a) the vertical
  • (b) the horizontal
  • (c) the object
  • (d) the ground below

20. When the shadow of a pole equals its height, the sun’s angle of elevation is:

  • (a) 30°
  • (b) 45°
  • (c) 60°
  • (d) 90°

Section F · Mensuration Q21–Q24 · 4 marks

21. The perimeter of a sector includes:

  • (a) only the arc
  • (b) the arc and one radius
  • (c) the arc and two radii
  • (d) the chord and the arc

22. The area of a segment is found by:

  • (a) sector − triangle
  • (b) sector + triangle
  • (c) circle − sector
  • (d) triangle − sector

23. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius r and height h is:

  • (a) 2πrh
  • (b) πr²h
  • (c) 2πr(r + h)
  • (d) πrl

24. A cone, a hemisphere and a cylinder have equal bases and equal heights. The ratio of their volumes is:

  • (a) 1 : 2 : 3
  • (b) 2 : 3 : 1
  • (c) 3 : 1 : 2
  • (d) 1 : 3 : 2

Section G · Statistics & Probability Q25–Q28 · 4 marks

25. The median class is the class whose cumulative frequency is:

  • (a) equal to N/2
  • (b) the first one greater than N/2
  • (c) the largest
  • (d) the smallest

26. If mean = 20 and mode = 26, then by the empirical relation the median is:

  • (a) 22
  • (b) 22.67
  • (c) 23
  • (d) 24

27. The probability of an impossible event is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 0
  • (c) 1/2
  • (d) undefined

28. The number of face cards in a deck of 52 cards is:

  • (a) 8
  • (b) 12
  • (c) 16
  • (d) 4
