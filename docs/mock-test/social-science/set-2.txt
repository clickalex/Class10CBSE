SECTION A - History (Q1-Q8, 8 marks)
------------------------------------

Q1. The Zollverein, started in 1834, was:
    (a) a French army
    (b) a customs union formed at Prussia's initiative
    (c) the parliament at Frankfurt
    (d) an Italian secret society

Q2. Who is supposed to have said, ‘when France sneezes, the rest of Europe catches cold'?
    (a) Bismarck
    (b) Metternich
    (c) Mazzini
    (d) Napoleon

Q3. The Swaraj flag designed by Gandhiji in 1921 had in its centre:
    (a) a lotus
    (b) a crescent moon
    (c) a spinning wheel
    (d) the Ashoka chakra

Q4. The Non-Cooperation Movement was called off after:
    (a) the Jallianwala Bagh massacre
    (b) the Chauri Chaura incident
    (c) the Rowlatt Act
    (d) the Simon Commission

Q5. The Bretton Woods institutions are:
    (a) the IMF and the World Bank
    (b) the WTO and UNICEF
    (c) the League of Nations and the UN
    (d) UNESCO and WHO

Q6. The Great Depression of the 1930s began with:
    (a) the Wall Street Crash of 1929
    (b) the First World War
    (c) the oil shock
    (d) the Russian Revolution

Q7. The first book printed by Johann Gutenberg was the:
    (a) Bible
    (b) Quran
    (c) Mahabharata
    (d) Ramcharitmanas

Q8. The Vernacular Press Act was enacted in:
    (a) 1857
    (b) 1878
    (c) 1905
    (d) 1919

    (a) the lifelines of India

    (b) Tamil Nadu

    (a) air pollution
    (b) water pollution

------------------------------------------------

    (a) unequal
    (b) equal
    (c) decided by the King
    (d) decided by the majority

    (a) Panchayati Raj
    (b) municipalities
    (c) GST
    (d) the right to education

Q19. The Equal Remuneration Act, which provides for equal wages for equal work, was passed in:
    (a) 1972
    (b) 1976
    (c) 1988
    (d) 1993

Q20. Compared with men, women in India have:
    (a) a higher literacy rate
    (b) the same literacy rate
    (c) a lower literacy rate
    (d) over 95% literacy

Q21. Which party was founded in 1984 and uses the elephant as its symbol?
    (a) BJP
    (b) BSP
    (c) CPI-M
    (d) NCP

    (a) one seat
    (b) two seats
    (c) four seats
    (d) six seats

Q23. Which of these supports the dignity of citizens in a democracy?

    (b) censorship of the press
