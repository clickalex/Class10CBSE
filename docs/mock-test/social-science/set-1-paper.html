Section A · History Q1–Q8 · 8 marks

1. The female allegory of the German nation, wearing a crown of oak leaves, was:

  • (a) Marianne
  • (b) Germania
  • (c) Britannia
  • (d) Liberty

2. The Treaty of Vienna was signed in:

  • (a) 1789
  • (b) 1815
  • (c) 1848
  • (d) 1871

3. The Lahore session of the Congress (December 1929), presided over by Jawaharlal Nehru, demanded:

  • (a) dominion status
  • (b) Purna Swaraj
  • (c) separate electorates
  • (d) abolition of zamindari

4. The Dandi March began on:

  • (a) 12 March 1930
  • (b) 6 April 1919
  • (c) 26 January 1930
  • (d) 8 August 1942

5. The disease that most helped the Spanish conquer America was:

  • (a) cholera
  • (b) smallpox
  • (c) influenza
  • (d) plague

6. The pre-modern trade routes linking Asia with Europe and northern Africa are called:

  • (a) the spice routes
  • (b) the Silk Routes
  • (c) the gold routes
  • (d) the pilgrim routes

7. The first English newspaper in India, the Bengal Gazette (1780), was started by:

  • (a) James Augustus Hicky
  • (b) Raja Rammohun Roy
  • (c) Bal Gangadhar Tilak
  • (d) William Carey

8. Kesari and Maratha were newspapers edited by:

  • (a) Mahatma Gandhi
  • (b) Bal Gangadhar Tilak
  • (c) Raja Rammohun Roy
  • (d) Bharatendu Harishchandra

Section B · Geography Q9–Q16 · 8 marks

9. Resources that are surveyed and their quality and quantity determined for utilisation are called:

  • (a) potential
  • (b) developed
  • (c) stock
  • (d) reserves

10. The Bishnoi community, famous for protecting blackbuck and khejarli trees, lives in:

  • (a) Rajasthan
  • (b) Kerala
  • (c) Assam
  • (d) West Bengal

11. The Bhakra Nangal project is built on the river:

  • (a) Mahanadi
  • (b) Sutlej
  • (c) Narmada
  • (d) Krishna

12. The Green Revolution in India was most successful in:

  • (a) Punjab, Haryana and western Uttar Pradesh
  • (b) Rajasthan and Gujarat
  • (c) Kerala and Tamil Nadu
  • (d) Assam and Odisha

13. Petroleum is popularly called:

  • (a) black gold
  • (b) brown diamond
  • (c) white coal
  • (d) green fuel

14. The first modern iron and steel works in India was set up at:

  • (a) Jamshedpur
  • (b) Kulti in West Bengal
  • (c) Bhilai
  • (d) Rourkela

15. The Golden Quadrilateral superhighway connects:

  • (a) Srinagar and Kanyakumari
  • (b) Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai and Mumbai
  • (c) Silchar and Porbandar
  • (d) Kandla and Haldia

16. Air transport in India was nationalised in:

  • (a) 1947
  • (b) 1953
  • (c) 1965
  • (d) 1972

Section C · Political Science Q17–Q24 · 8 marks

17. In Belgium, the community government is elected by:

  • (a) all citizens of the country
  • (b) people belonging to one language group, wherever they live
  • (c) the residents of Brussels only
  • (d) the Flemish region only

18. Horizontal distribution of power means sharing power among:

  • (a) different levels of government
  • (b) the legislature, executive and judiciary
  • (c) social groups
  • (d) political parties only

19. Which constitutional amendment made regular elections to local bodies mandatory and reserved one-third of the seats for women?

  • (a) 42nd
  • (b) 73rd and 74th of 1992
  • (c) 86th
  • (d) 101st

20. Which of these subjects is in the State List?

  • (a) defence
  • (b) police
  • (c) banking
  • (d) foreign affairs

21. The Women's Reservation Act, 2023 reserves what share of seats in the Lok Sabha for women?

  • (a) 10%
  • (b) 33%
  • (c) 50%
  • (d) 25%

22. The number of political parties registered with the Election Commission in India is:

  • (a) fewer than 10
  • (b) about 50
  • (c) more than 2,000, counting registered unrecognised parties
  • (d) exactly six

23. Democracy is better than dictatorship mainly because it:

  • (a) takes decisions faster
  • (b) is more efficient
  • (c) is accountable, responsive and legitimate
  • (d) has no opposition

24. Criticism of the functioning of democracy by citizens is:

  • (a) a sign of its weakness
  • (b) a sign of its strength and of people's rising expectations
  • (c) proof that democracy fails
  • (d) irrelevant

  • (a) private companies
  • (b) the government

  • (a) SBI

  • (b) issuing currency notes
  • (c) lending directly to farmers

  • (a) Maruti
  • (b) Tata Motors

32. Which Indian company bought the Anglo-Dutch steel maker Corus in 2007?

  • (a) Tata Steel
  • (b) Reliance Industries
  • (c) Infosys
  • (d) Mahindra and Mahindra
