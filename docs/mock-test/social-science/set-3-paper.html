Section A · History Q1–Q8 · 8 marks

1. Who proclaimed the German Empire in 1871?

  • (a) Napoleon Bonaparte
  • (b) Kaiser Wilhelm I
  • (c) Otto von Bismarck
  • (d) Friedrich Wilhelm IV

2. Italy was unified under:

  • (a) Garibaldi alone
  • (b) Cavour and Victor Emmanuel II, helped by Garibaldi's Red Shirts
  • (c) Mazzini's Young Italy alone
  • (d) Bismarck's armies

3. The Poona Pact of September 1932 was signed between Gandhiji and:

  • (a) Muhammad Ali Jinnah
  • (b) Dr B. R. Ambedkar
  • (c) Jawaharlal Nehru
  • (d) Sardar Patel

4. The Simon Commission was boycotted because:

  • (a) it had no Indian member
  • (b) it recommended partition
  • (c) it raised land revenue
  • (d) it banned the Congress

5. Which of the following crops reached Europe from the Americas after Columbus's voyages?

  • (a) rice and wheat
  • (b) potato and maize
  • (c) cotton and indigo
  • (d) tea and coffee

6. Indentured labourers were recruited mainly from:

  • (a) Africa and Japan
  • (b) India, China and Africa
  • (c) Australia and Europe
  • (d) North America

7. The oldest printed Japanese book, the Buddhist Diamond Sutra, was printed in:

  • (a) AD 768
  • (b) AD 868
  • (c) AD 1450
  • (d) AD 1579

8. Kesari was edited by:

  • (a) Bal Gangadhar Tilak
  • (b) Raja Rammohun Roy
  • (c) Mahatma Gandhi
  • (d) Jawaharlal Nehru

Section B · Geography Q9–Q16 · 8 marks

9. Black soil is mainly found in:

  • (a) the Gangetic plain
  • (b) the Deccan trap region
  • (c) the Thar desert
  • (d) the eastern coast

10. Which of these is an endangered species in India?

  • (a) cattle
  • (b) Asiatic elephant
  • (c) black buck
  • (d) blue sheep

11. Project Tiger was launched in:

  • (a) 1972
  • (b) 1973
  • (c) 1980
  • (d) 1988

12. In which state has rooftop rainwater harvesting been made compulsory for all houses?

  • (a) Rajasthan
  • (b) Meghalaya
  • (c) Tamil Nadu
  • (d) West Bengal

13. Which of these is a plantation crop?

  • (a) wheat
  • (b) tea
  • (c) maize
  • (d) bajra

14. The finest quality of iron ore with about 70% iron content is:

  • (a) magnetite
  • (b) hematite
  • (c) limonite
  • (d) siderite

15. Which industry uses bauxite as its raw material?

  • (a) iron and steel
  • (b) aluminium smelting
  • (c) cement
  • (d) fertiliser

16. The Konkan Railway was built mainly to:

  • (a) link Delhi and Mumbai
  • (b) provide a fast route along the western coast through difficult terrain
  • (c) carry coal
  • (d) connect Kolkata and Chennai

Section C · Political Science Q17–Q24 · 8 marks

17. The 1956 act in Sri Lanka recognised which language as the only official language?

  • (a) Tamil
  • (b) English
  • (c) Sinhala
  • (d) Sinhala and Tamil

18. Which of these was a majoritarian measure in Sri Lanka?

  • (a) making Sinhala the only official language in 1956
  • (b) power sharing
  • (c) federalism
  • (d) proportional representation

19. Which of the following subjects falls in the Concurrent List?

  • (a) police
  • (b) defence
  • (c) education
  • (d) banking

20. India is an example of a:

  • (a) coming together federation
  • (b) holding together federation
  • (c) unitary state
  • (d) confederation

21. Communalism means:

  • (a) being a religious person
  • (b) treating one's religion as the basis of political community and of rival interests
  • (c) keeping religion out of public life
  • (d) the state supporting one religion

22. Which of these is an expression of communalism?

  • (a) respect for all religions
  • (b) a religious procession accompanied by violence against another community
  • (c) celebrating festivals together
  • (d) inter-faith marriage

23. A party is recognised as a national party if it secures at least what percentage of the votes polled in four or more states?

  • (a) 4%
  • (b) 6%
  • (c) 8%
  • (d) 10%

24. Transparency in a democracy means:

  • (a) the government may withhold all information
  • (b) citizens have the right to know and to examine the process of decision-making
  • (c) only leaders know the rules
  • (d) elections are unnecessary

Section D · Economics Q25–Q32 · 8 marks

25. Which of these is included in the Human Development Index?

  • (a) life expectancy at birth
  • (b) the number of cars
  • (c) military spending
  • (d) the area of the country

26. A country with a high per capita income but poor health and education would be regarded as:

  • (a) fully developed
  • (b) not necessarily developed
  • (c) socialist
  • (d) underdeveloped by definition

27. Which sector contributes the largest share to India's GDP today?

  • (a) primary
  • (b) secondary
  • (c) tertiary
  • (d) all equally

28. MGNREGA 2005 guarantees how many days of wage employment in a year to a rural household?

  • (a) 50
  • (b) 100
  • (c) 150
  • (d) 200

29. The Grameen Bank, which demonstrated that poor women are reliable borrowers, was set up in:

  • (a) India
  • (b) Bangladesh
  • (c) Sri Lanka
  • (d) Nepal

30. Collateral is best defined as:

  • (a) the interest on a loan
  • (b) an asset used as a guarantee until the loan is repaid
  • (c) the principal amount
  • (d) the bank's profit margin

31. The World Trade Organization was started in:

  • (a) 1991
  • (b) 1995
  • (c) 2000
  • (d) 2005

32. Liberalisation means:

  • (a) increasing import duties
  • (b) removing barriers set by the government on trade and foreign investment
  • (c) nationalising industries
  • (d) banning foreign companies
