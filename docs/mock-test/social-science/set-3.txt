SECTION A - History (Q1-Q8, 8 marks)
------------------------------------

Q1. Who proclaimed the German Empire in 1871?
    (a) Napoleon Bonaparte
    (b) Kaiser Wilhelm I
    (c) Otto von Bismarck
    (d) Friedrich Wilhelm IV

Q2. Italy was unified under:
    (a) Garibaldi alone
    (b) Cavour and Victor Emmanuel II, helped by Garibaldi's Red Shirts
    (c) Mazzini's Young Italy alone
    (d) Bismarck's armies

Q3. The Poona Pact of September 1932 was signed between Gandhiji and:
    (a) Muhammad Ali Jinnah
    (b) Dr B. R. Ambedkar
    (c) Jawaharlal Nehru
    (d) Sardar Patel

Q4. The Simon Commission was boycotted because:
    (a) it had no Indian member
    (b) it recommended partition
    (c) it raised land revenue
    (d) it banned the Congress

Q5. Which of the following crops reached Europe from the Americas after Columbus's voyages?
    (a) rice and wheat
    (b) potato and maize
    (c) cotton and indigo
    (d) tea and coffee

Q6. Indentured labourers were recruited mainly from:
    (a) Africa and Japan
    (b) India, China and Africa
    (c) Australia and Europe
    (d) North America

Q7. The oldest printed Japanese book, the Buddhist Diamond Sutra, was printed in:
    (a) AD 768
    (b) AD 868
    (c) AD 1450
    (d) AD 1579

Q8. Kesari was edited by:
    (a) Bal Gangadhar Tilak
    (b) Raja Rammohun Roy
    (c) Mahatma Gandhi
    (d) Jawaharlal Nehru

SECTION C - Political Science (Q17-Q24, 8 marks)
------------------------------------------------

Q17. The 1956 act in Sri Lanka recognised which language as the only official language?
    (a) Tamil
    (b) English
    (c) Sinhala
    (d) Sinhala and Tamil

Q18. Which of these was a majoritarian measure in Sri Lanka?
    (a) making Sinhala the only official language in 1956
    (b) power sharing
    (c) federalism
    (d) proportional representation

Q19. Which of the following subjects falls in the Concurrent List?
    (a) police
    (b) defence
    (c) education
    (d) banking

Q20. India is an example of a:
    (a) coming together federation
    (b) holding together federation
    (c) unitary state
    (d) confederation

Q21. Communalism means:
    (a) being a religious person
    (b) treating one's religion as the basis of political community and of rival interests
    (c) keeping religion out of public life
    (d) the state supporting one religion

Q22. Which of these is an expression of communalism?
    (a) respect for all religions
    (b) a religious procession accompanied by violence against another community
    (c) celebrating festivals together
    (d) inter-faith marriage

Q23. A party is recognised as a national party if it secures at least what percentage of the votes polled in four or more states?
    (a) 4%
    (b) 6%
    (c) 8%
    (d) 10%

Q24. Transparency in a democracy means:
    (a) the government may withhold all information
    (b) citizens have the right to know and to examine the process of decision-making
    (c) only leaders know the rules
    (d) elections are unnecessary
