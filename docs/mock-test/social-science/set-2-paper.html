Section A · History Q1–Q8 · 8 marks

1. The Zollverein, started in 1834, was:

  • (a) a French army
  • (b) a customs union formed at Prussia's initiative
  • (c) the parliament at Frankfurt
  • (d) an Italian secret society

2. Who is supposed to have said, ‘when France sneezes, the rest of Europe catches cold'?

  • (a) Bismarck
  • (b) Metternich
  • (c) Mazzini
  • (d) Napoleon

3. The Swaraj flag designed by Gandhiji in 1921 had in its centre:

  • (a) a lotus
  • (b) a crescent moon
  • (c) a spinning wheel
  • (d) the Ashoka chakra

4. The Non-Cooperation Movement was called off after:

  • (a) the Jallianwala Bagh massacre
  • (b) the Chauri Chaura incident
  • (c) the Rowlatt Act
  • (d) the Simon Commission

5. The Bretton Woods institutions are:

  • (a) the IMF and the World Bank
  • (b) the WTO and UNICEF
  • (c) the League of Nations and the UN
  • (d) UNESCO and WHO

6. The Great Depression of the 1930s began with:

  • (a) the Wall Street Crash of 1929
  • (b) the First World War
  • (c) the oil shock
  • (d) the Russian Revolution

7. The first book printed by Johann Gutenberg was the:

  • (a) Bible
  • (b) Quran
  • (c) Mahabharata
  • (d) Ramcharitmanas

8. The Vernacular Press Act was enacted in:

  • (a) 1857
  • (b) 1878
  • (c) 1905
  • (d) 1919

Section B · Geography Q9–Q16 · 8 marks

9. Agenda 21 was adopted at the Earth Summit held at:

  • (a) Johannesburg in 2002
  • (b) Rio de Janeiro in 1992
  • (c) Stockholm in 1972
  • (d) Kyoto in 1997

10. Which of these is a non-renewable resource?

  • (a) solar energy
  • (b) wind
  • (c) coal
  • (d) forests

11. The ‘Red Data Book' is a record of:

  • (a) national parks
  • (b) endangered species
  • (c) forest area
  • (d) soil types

12. Jawaharlal Nehru proudly proclaimed dams as:

  • (a) the lifelines of India
  • (b) the temples of modern India
  • (c) the pride of the nation
  • (d) the wealth of India

13. Which crop requires about 210 frost-free days and 50–100 cm of rainfall?

  • (a) rice
  • (b) wheat
  • (c) cotton
  • (d) jute

14. Which state has the largest installed wind-power capacity in India?

  • (a) Kerala
  • (b) Tamil Nadu
  • (c) Bihar
  • (d) Punjab

15. Hot water from factories drained into rivers before cooling causes:

  • (a) air pollution
  • (b) water pollution
  • (c) thermal pollution
  • (d) noise pollution

16. The first port developed soon after Independence to ease the volume of trade on Mumbai port was:

  • (a) Kandla
  • (b) Marmagao
  • (c) Tuticorin
  • (d) Kochi

Section C · Political Science Q17–Q24 · 8 marks

17. In Belgium's central government, the number of Dutch-speaking and French-speaking ministers must be:

  • (a) unequal
  • (b) equal
  • (c) decided by the King
  • (d) decided by the majority

18. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act is concerned with:

  • (a) Panchayati Raj
  • (b) municipalities
  • (c) GST
  • (d) the right to education

19. The Equal Remuneration Act, which provides for equal wages for equal work, was passed in:

  • (a) 1972
  • (b) 1976
  • (c) 1988
  • (d) 1993

20. Compared with men, women in India have:

  • (a) a higher literacy rate
  • (b) the same literacy rate
  • (c) a lower literacy rate
  • (d) over 95% literacy

21. Which party was founded in 1984 and uses the elephant as its symbol?

  • (a) BJP
  • (b) BSP
  • (c) CPI-M
  • (d) NCP

22. A party is recognised as a state party if it secures at least 6% of votes in the state Assembly elections and wins at least:

  • (a) one seat
  • (b) two seats
  • (c) four seats
  • (d) six seats

23. Which of these supports the dignity of citizens in a democracy?

  • (a) universal adult franchise
  • (b) censorship of the press
  • (c) hereditary rule
  • (d) one-party rule

24. Democracy is described as ‘rule by discussion and persuasion' because:

  • (a) it relies on consultation
  • (b) it relies on force
  • (c) it bans debate
  • (d) the army decides

Section D · Economics Q25–Q32 · 8 marks

  • (a) the World Bank
  • (b) the UNDP

  • (d) the RBI

  • (a) 7 years and above
  • (b) 18 years and above

  • (d) 5 years and above

27. About what percentage of workers in India are in the unorganised sector?

  • (a) 50%
  • (b) 70%
  • (c) 80%
  • (d) 90%

28. Activities that produce goods using natural resources belong to the:

  • (a) primary sector
  • (b) secondary sector
  • (c) tertiary sector
  • (d) quaternary sector

29. A self-help group generally consists of about:

  • (a) 5 members
  • (b) 15 to 20 members
  • (c) 100 members
  • (d) 500 members

30. Cheap and affordable credit matters because it:

  • (a) increases dependence on moneylenders
  • (b) lets borrowers invest and raise their income
  • (c) raises taxes
  • (d) reduces savings

31. The World Trade Organization was established in:

  • (a) 1947
  • (b) 1995
  • (c) 2000
  • (d) 1991

32. Which of these is a barrier to foreign trade?

  • (a) free exchange of goods
  • (b) import duties and quotas
  • (c) lower transport costs
  • (d) transfer of technology
