========================================================================

Set     : 1 of 2
Time    : 2 h 30 min
Marks   : 100 (+1 correct · no negative marking)
Pattern : Full-length - Public descriptions of the Class XI LEST give 100 one-mark MCQs in five sections of 20 (Mental Ability, English, Science, Social Science, Mathematics) in 2½ hours with no negative marking. Confirm in the current NVS prospectus.

Instructions
1. Each question has exactly one correct option.
