CLASS 10 CBSE STUDY HUB - MOCK TEST
========================================================================
Exam    : JNV Class XI — Lateral Entry Selection Test (LEST)
Set     : 2 of 2
Time    : 2 h 30 min
Marks   : 100 (+1 correct · no negative marking)
Pattern : Full-length - Public descriptions of the Class XI LEST give 100 one-mark MCQs in five sections of 20 (Mental Ability, English, Science, Social Science, Mathematics) in 2½ hours with no negative marking. Confirm in the current NVS prospectus.
========================================================================

Instructions
1. Each question has exactly one correct option.
2. Write your answers in the answer grid at the end, then check the key file.
3. +1 correct · no negative marking; unattempted questions score 0.


SECTION A - Mental Ability (Q1-Q20, 20 marks)
---------------------------------------------

    (b) 26
    (c) 38
    (d) 50

    (b) 20

    (d) 26

    (a) 84
    (b) 102
    (c) 112
    (d) 128

    (b) P
    (c) O
    (d) N

    (d) E

    (a) BDF
    (b) HJL
    (c) NPR
    (d) TVY

Q7. Hand : Fingers :: Foot : ?

    (a) Energy

    (d) Pressure

    (a) Copper
    (b) Iron
    (c) Mercury
    (d) Aluminium

Q11. Which word is the odd one out?
    (a) Cricket
    (b) Football
    (c) Chess
    (d) Hockey

Q12. In a code, 'sky is blue' is 'la pa ta', 'sea is deep' is 'pa na ro' and 'blue sea' is 'ta na'. What is the code for 'deep'?
    (a) pa
    (b) na
    (c) ro
    (d) la

Q13. If A = 1, B = 2, C = 3 and so on, what is the sum of the letters of the word BAG?
    (a) 9
    (b) 10
    (c) 11
    (d) 12

Q14. A boy walks 3 km north, turns right and walks 4 km. How far is he from his starting point?
    (a) 7 km
    (b) 5 km
    (c) 1 km
    (d) 12 km

Q15. A person facing north turns 90° clockwise and then 180° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
    (a) East
    (b) West
    (c) South

    (a) Sister
    (b) Aunt
    (c) Mother
    (d) Grandmother

Q17. A clock shows 4:20. What time does its mirror image show?
    (a) 7:40
    (b) 8:40
    (c) 7:20
    (d) 8:20

Q18. Five people A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing north. E sits at the left end. A sits immediately to the left of B. C sits between B and D. Who sits at the right end?
    (a) B
    (b) C
    (c) D
    (d) A

Q19. A father is 4 times as old as his son. After 5 years he will be 3 times as old. What is the son's present age?
    (a) 5 years
    (b) 10 years
    (c) 15 years
    (d) 20 years

Q20. Among five friends, P is taller than Q but shorter than R. S is taller than R. T is shorter than Q. Who is the shortest?
    (a) Q
    (b) T
    (c) P
    (d) R
