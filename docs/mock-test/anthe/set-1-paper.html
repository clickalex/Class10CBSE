Section A · Mental Ability Q1–Q10 · 40 marks

1. Find the next number: 4, 7, 12, 19, 28, ?

  • (a) 37
  • (b) 38
  • (c) 39
  • (d) 41

2. Find the next letter: B, D, H, P, ?

  • (a) X
  • (b) F
  • (c) Z
  • (d) E

3. 5 : 30 :: 7 : ?

  • (a) 42
  • (b) 49
  • (c) 56
  • (d) 63

4. Which number is the odd one out?

  • (a) 13
  • (b) 17
  • (c) 21
  • (d) 23

5. If RED is coded as 27 by adding letter positions, what is the code for BLUE?

  • (a) 38
  • (b) 40
  • (c) 42
  • (d) 44

6. If 1 = 5, 2 = 25, 3 = 125 and 4 = 625, then 5 = ?

  • (a) 3125
  • (b) 1
  • (c) 25
  • (d) 5

7. If South-East becomes North and North-East becomes West, what does West become?

  • (a) South-East
  • (b) North-West
  • (c) South-West
  • (d) North-East

8. A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. D is the father of A. How is C related to D?

  • (a) Husband
  • (b) Wife
  • (c) Sister
  • (d) Mother

9. If today is Monday, what day of the week will it be after 100 days?

  • (a) Tuesday
  • (b) Wednesday
  • (c) Thursday
  • (d) Sunday

10. If 5 machines take 5 minutes to make 5 toys, how long would 100 machines take to make 100 toys?

  • (a) 100 minutes
  • (b) 20 minutes
  • (c) 5 minutes
  • (d) 1 minute

Section B · Physics Q11–Q18 · 32 marks

11. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?

  • (a) at F
  • (b) at 2F
  • (c) at infinity
  • (d) between F and 2F

12. The image formed by a concave lens of a real object is always:

  • (a) real and inverted
  • (b) virtual, erect and diminished
  • (c) virtual, erect and magnified
  • (d) real and magnified

13. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:

  • (a) pupil
  • (b) retina
  • (c) ciliary muscles
  • (d) iris

14. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:

  • (a) myopia
  • (b) hypermetropia
  • (c) astigmatism
  • (d) night blindness

15. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:

  • (a) 3 kWh
  • (b) 30 kWh
  • (c) 300 kWh
  • (d) 3000 kWh

16. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:

  • (a) 24 Ω
  • (b) 6 Ω
  • (c) 0.17 Ω
  • (d) 14 Ω

17. An electric motor converts:

  • (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
  • (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
  • (c) heat into light
  • (d) chemical energy into sound

18. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:

  • (a) induced current in a coil
  • (b) magnetic field due to a current
  • (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
  • (d) current produced by a generator

Section C · Chemistry Q19–Q26 · 32 marks

19. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:

  • (a) combination
  • (b) decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) double displacement

20. Which of these is a combination reaction?

  • (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
  • (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
  • (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
  • (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

21. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:

  • (a) sodium chloride
  • (b) sodium carbonate
  • (c) calcium sulphate
  • (d) ammonium chloride

22. Baking soda is:

  • (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
  • (b) NaHCO₃
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Na₂CO₃

23. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:

  • (a) MgCl
  • (b) MgCl₂
  • (c) Mg₂Cl
  • (d) MgCl₃

24. Which metal reacts vigorously with cold water?

  • (a) iron
  • (b) zinc
  • (c) sodium
  • (d) copper

25. The functional group present in an alcohol is:

  • (a) –CHO
  • (b) –COOH
  • (c) –OH
  • (d) –CO–

26. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?

  • (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
  • (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
  • (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
  • (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH

Section D · Mathematics Q27–Q33 · 28 marks

27. The HCF of 8 and 12 is:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 4
  • (c) 8
  • (d) 24

28. A quadratic polynomial can have at most:

  • (a) 0 zeroes
  • (b) 1 zero
  • (c) 2 zeroes
  • (d) 3 zeroes

29. If x + 2y = 5 and 3x + ky = 15 are coincident lines, then k = :

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 3
  • (c) 6
  • (d) 15

30. Which one is NOT a quadratic equation?

  • (a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0
  • (b) (x + 1)² = 2(x − 3)
  • (c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0
  • (d) 2x² = 8

31. S₅ − S₄ equals:

  • (a) the 5th term
  • (b) the 4th term
  • (c) the 9th term
  • (d) zero

32. The empirical relation connecting the three measures of central tendency is:

  • (a) Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean
  • (b) Median = 3 Mode − 2 Mean
  • (c) Mean = 3 Median − 2 Mode
  • (d) Mode = 2 Median − Mean

33. The number of face cards in a deck of 52 cards is:

  • (a) 8
  • (b) 12
  • (c) 16
  • (d) 4

  • (d) natural gas

40. The ozone layer is being depleted mainly by:

  • (a) CFCs
  • (b) methane
  • (c) nitrogen fertilisers
  • (d) carbon monoxide
