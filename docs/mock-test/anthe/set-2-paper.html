Section A · Mental Ability Q1–Q10 · 40 marks

1. Which number is wrong in the series 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 38, 50?

  • (a) 17
  • (b) 26
  • (c) 38
  • (d) 50

2. Find the next pair: AZ, BY, CX, DW, ?

  • (a) EV
  • (b) EU
  • (c) FV
  • (d) EW

3. Hand : Fingers :: Foot : ?

  • (a) Legs
  • (b) Toes
  • (c) Shoes
  • (d) Ankle

4. Which is the odd one out?

  • (a) Square
  • (b) Rectangle
  • (c) Rhombus
  • (d) Circle

5. If A = 1, B = 2, … and CAT is coded as 24, what is the code for DOG?

  • (a) 25
  • (b) 26
  • (c) 27
  • (d) 28

6. A boy walks 3 km north, turns right and walks 4 km. How far is he from his starting point?

  • (a) 7 km
  • (b) 5 km
  • (c) 1 km
  • (d) 12 km

7. A person facing north turns 90° clockwise and then 180° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?

  • (a) East
  • (b) West
  • (c) South
  • (d) North

8. Introducing a man, a woman said, “His mother is the only daughter of my mother.” How is the woman related to the man?

  • (a) Sister
  • (b) Aunt
  • (c) Mother
  • (d) Grandmother

9. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 3:00?

  • (a) 60°
  • (b) 90°
  • (c) 120°
  • (d) 180°

10. How many numbers from 1 to 100 are divisible by both 3 and 5?

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 6
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 15

Section B · Physics Q11–Q18 · 32 marks

11. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:

  • (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

12. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?

  • (a) plane
  • (b) concave
  • (c) convex
  • (d) parabolic

13. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:

  • (a) scattering
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) total internal reflection
  • (d) polarisation

14. Stars twinkle mainly because of:

  • (a) atmospheric refraction
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) reflection from clouds
  • (d) their own flickering

15. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:

  • (a) more resistance
  • (b) less resistance
  • (c) the same resistance
  • (d) no resistance

16. The resistance of a wire doubles when:

  • (a) its length is doubled
  • (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
  • (c) its length is halved
  • (d) its diameter is doubled

17. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is:

  • (a) AC generator has an electromagnet
  • (b) DC generator has a commutator
  • (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator
  • (d) AC generator produces more power

18. The insulation on the earth wire in a domestic circuit is:

  • (a) red
  • (b) black
  • (c) green
  • (d) blue

Section C · Chemistry Q19–Q26 · 32 marks

19. Which of the following is an endothermic process?

  • (a) respiration
  • (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
  • (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
  • (d) quicklime + water

20. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:

  • (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
  • (b) CuO and Ag2O
  • (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
  • (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

21. Dilute acid added to an active metal produces:

  • (a) oxygen
  • (b) hydrogen
  • (c) carbon dioxide
  • (d) nitrogen

22. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:

  • (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
  • (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
  • (c) CaSO₄
  • (d) CaCO₃

23. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:

  • (a) less reactive than silver
  • (b) more reactive than silver
  • (c) equally reactive
  • (d) an inert metal

24. Which pair is amphoteric?

  • (a) Na2O and CaO
  • (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
  • (c) CO2 and SO2
  • (d) CuO and FeO

25. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?

  • (a) C₃H₈
  • (b) C₂H₆
  • (c) C₄H₁₀
  • (d) C₂H₄

26. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:

  • (a) H₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) CO₂
  • (d) CO

Section D · Mathematics Q27–Q33 · 28 marks

27. In a right triangle with legs 6 cm and 8 cm, the hypotenuse is:

  • (a) 10 cm
  • (b) 12 cm
  • (c) 14 cm
  • (d) 9 cm

28. The centroid of the triangle with vertices (2, 3), (−4, 7) and (2, −4) is:

  • (a) (0, 2)
  • (b) (0, 0)
  • (c) (2, 2)
  • (d) (0, 3)

29. sin²30° + cos²30° equals:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1/2
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 3/4

30. A 10 m ladder reaches a window 8 m above the ground. Its foot is from the wall at a distance of:

  • (a) 4 m
  • (b) 6 m
  • (c) 7 m
  • (d) 9 m

31. The angle between a tangent and the radius at the point of contact is:

  • (a) 45°
  • (b) 60°
  • (c) 90°
  • (d) 180°

32. The area of a semicircle of radius r is:

  • (a) πr²
  • (b) πr²/2
  • (c) 2πr
  • (d) πr²/4

33. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius r and height h is:

  • (a) 2πrh
  • (b) πr²h
  • (c) 2πr(r + h)
  • (d) πrl

Section E · Biology Q34–Q40 · 28 marks

34. The breakdown of pyruvate to give CO2, water and energy takes place in the:

  • (a) cytoplasm
  • (b) mitochondria
  • (c) chloroplast
  • (d) nucleus

35. Which of these is an autotroph?

  • (a) mushroom
  • (b) Cuscuta
  • (c) green plant
  • (d) human being

36. The gap between two neurons is called:

  • (a) dendrite
  • (b) synapse
  • (c) axon
  • (d) impulse

37. Which plant hormone is a growth inhibitor?

  • (a) auxin
  • (b) gibberellin
  • (c) cytokinin
  • (d) abscisic acid

38. Which of these is an asexual mode of reproduction?

  • (a) budding in yeast
  • (b) pollination
  • (c) fertilisation
  • (d) seed germination

39. The phenotypic ratio obtained in a dihybrid cross between two F₁ hybrids is:

  • (a) 3 : 1
  • (b) 9 : 3 : 3 : 1
  • (c) 1 : 1
  • (d) 1 : 2 : 1

40. Which of the following groups contains only biodegradable substances?

  • (a) paper, plastic, wood
  • (b) vegetable peels, paper, lime juice
  • (c) glass, wood, plastic
  • (d) lime juice, grass, aluminium foil
