SECTION A - Mental Ability (Q1-Q10, 40 marks)
---------------------------------------------

Q1. Find the next number: 4, 7, 12, 19, 28, ?
    (a) 37
    (b) 38
    (c) 39
    (d) 41

Q2. Find the next letter: B, D, H, P, ?
    (a) X
    (b) F
    (c) Z
    (d) E

Q3. 5 : 30 :: 7 : ?
    (a) 42
    (b) 49
    (c) 56
    (d) 63

Q4. Which number is the odd one out?
    (a) 13
    (b) 17
    (c) 21
    (d) 23

Q5. If RED is coded as 27 by adding letter positions, what is the code for BLUE?
    (a) 38
    (b) 40
    (c) 42
    (d) 44

Q6. If 1 = 5, 2 = 25, 3 = 125 and 4 = 625, then 5 = ?
    (a) 3125
    (b) 1
    (c) 25
    (d) 5

Q7. If South-East becomes North and North-East becomes West, what does West become?
    (a) South-East
    (b) North-West
    (c) South-West
    (d) North-East

Q8. A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. D is the father of A. How is C related to D?
    (a) Husband
    (b) Wife
    (c) Sister
    (d) Mother

Q9. If today is Monday, what day of the week will it be after 100 days?
    (a) Tuesday
    (b) Wednesday
    (c) Thursday
    (d) Sunday

Q10. If 5 machines take 5 minutes to make 5 toys, how long would 100 machines take to make 100 toys?
    (a) 100 minutes
    (b) 20 minutes
    (c) 5 minutes
    (d) 1 minute


SECTION B - Physics (Q11-Q18, 32 marks)
---------------------------------------

Q11. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?
    (a) at F
    (b) at 2F
    (c) at infinity
    (d) between F and 2F

Q12. The image formed by a concave lens of a real object is always:
    (a) real and inverted
    (b) virtual, erect and diminished
    (c) virtual, erect and magnified
    (d) real and magnified

Q13. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:
    (a) pupil
    (b) retina
    (c) ciliary muscles
    (d) iris

Q14. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:
    (a) myopia
    (b) hypermetropia
    (c) astigmatism
    (d) night blindness

Q15. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:
    (a) 3 kWh
    (b) 30 kWh
    (c) 300 kWh
    (d) 3000 kWh

Q16. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:
    (a) 24 Ω
    (b) 6 Ω
    (c) 0.17 Ω
    (d) 14 Ω

Q17. An electric motor converts:
    (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
    (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
    (c) heat into light
    (d) chemical energy into sound

Q18. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:
    (a) induced current in a coil
    (b) magnetic field due to a current
    (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
    (d) current produced by a generator


SECTION C - Chemistry (Q19-Q26, 32 marks)
-----------------------------------------

    (a) combination
    (b) decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) double displacement

Q20. Which of these is a combination reaction?
    (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
    (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
    (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
    (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

Q21. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:
    (a) sodium chloride
    (b) sodium carbonate
    (c) calcium sulphate
    (d) ammonium chloride

Q22. Baking soda is:
    (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
    (b) NaHCO₃
    (c) NaOH
    (d) Na₂CO₃

Q23. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:
    (a) MgCl
    (b) MgCl₂
    (c) Mg₂Cl
    (d) MgCl₃

Q24. Which metal reacts vigorously with cold water?
    (a) iron
    (b) zinc
    (c) sodium
    (d) copper

Q25. The functional group present in an alcohol is:
