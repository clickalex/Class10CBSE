SECTION A - Mental Ability (Q1-Q10, 40 marks)
---------------------------------------------

Q1. Which number is wrong in the series 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 38, 50?
    (a) 17
    (b) 26
    (c) 38
    (d) 50

Q2. Find the next pair: AZ, BY, CX, DW, ?
    (a) EV
    (b) EU
    (c) FV
    (d) EW

Q3. Hand : Fingers :: Foot : ?
    (a) Legs
    (b) Toes
    (c) Shoes
    (d) Ankle

Q4. Which is the odd one out?
    (a) Square
    (b) Rectangle
    (c) Rhombus
    (d) Circle

Q5. If A = 1, B = 2, … and CAT is coded as 24, what is the code for DOG?
    (a) 25
    (b) 26
    (c) 27
    (d) 28

Q6. A boy walks 3 km north, turns right and walks 4 km. How far is he from his starting point?
    (a) 7 km
    (b) 5 km
    (c) 1 km
    (d) 12 km

Q7. A person facing north turns 90° clockwise and then 180° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
    (a) East
    (b) West
    (c) South
    (d) North

Q8. Introducing a man, a woman said, “His mother is the only daughter of my mother.” How is the woman related to the man?
    (a) Sister
    (b) Aunt
    (c) Mother
    (d) Grandmother

Q9. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 3:00?
    (a) 60°
    (b) 90°
    (c) 120°
    (d) 180°

Q10. How many numbers from 1 to 100 are divisible by both 3 and 5?
    (a) 5
    (b) 6
    (c) 7
    (d) 15


SECTION B - Physics (Q11-Q18, 32 marks)
---------------------------------------

Q11. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:
    (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
    (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
    (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
    (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

Q12. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?
    (a) plane
    (b) concave
    (c) convex
    (d) parabolic

Q13. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:
    (a) scattering
    (b) dispersion
    (c) total internal reflection
    (d) polarisation

Q14. Stars twinkle mainly because of:
    (a) atmospheric refraction
    (b) dispersion
    (c) reflection from clouds
    (d) their own flickering

Q15. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:
    (a) more resistance
    (b) less resistance
    (c) the same resistance
    (d) no resistance

Q16. The resistance of a wire doubles when:
    (a) its length is doubled
    (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
    (c) its length is halved
    (d) its diameter is doubled

Q17. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is:
    (a) AC generator has an electromagnet
    (b) DC generator has a commutator
    (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator
    (d) AC generator produces more power

Q18. The insulation on the earth wire in a domestic circuit is:
    (a) red
    (b) black
    (c) green
    (d) blue


SECTION C - Chemistry (Q19-Q26, 32 marks)
-----------------------------------------

Q19. Which of the following is an endothermic process?
    (a) respiration
    (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
    (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
    (d) quicklime + water

Q20. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:
    (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
    (b) CuO and Ag2O
    (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
    (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

Q21. Dilute acid added to an active metal produces:
    (a) oxygen
    (b) hydrogen
    (c) carbon dioxide
    (d) nitrogen

Q22. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:
    (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
    (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
    (c) CaSO₄
    (d) CaCO₃

Q23. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:
    (a) less reactive than silver
    (b) more reactive than silver
    (c) equally reactive
    (d) an inert metal

Q24. Which pair is amphoteric?
    (a) Na2O and CaO
    (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
    (c) CO2 and SO2
    (d) CuO and FeO

Q25. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?
    (a) C₃H₈
    (b) C₂H₆
    (c) C₄H₁₀
    (d) C₂H₄

Q26. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:
    (a) H₂
    (b) O₂
    (c) CO₂
    (d) CO

    (a) 4 m
