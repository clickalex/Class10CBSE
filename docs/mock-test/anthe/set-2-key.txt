Q1. (c) 38. The pattern is n² + 1: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 37, 50. The sixth term should be 37, not 38.
      Topic: Number series (Mental Ability)

Q2. (a) EV. The first letter moves forward (A, B, C, D, E) while the second moves backward from Z (Z, Y, X, W, V).
      Topic: Letter series (Mental Ability)

Q3. (b) Toes. Fingers are the digits of a hand; toes are the digits of a foot.
      Topic: Analogy (Mental Ability)

Q4. (d) Circle. The other three are quadrilaterals with four straight sides; a circle has no sides.
      Topic: Odd one out (Mental Ability)

Q5. (b) 26. The code is the sum of letter positions: D (4) + O (15) + G (7) = 26. Check: C (3) + A (1) + T (20) = 24.
      Topic: Coding–decoding (Mental Ability)

Q6. (b) 5 km. North then east form a right angle; the distance is √(3² + 4²) = 5 km.
      Topic: Direction sense (Mental Ability)

Q7. (b) West. North → (90° clockwise) east → (180° anticlockwise) west.
      Topic: Direction sense (Mental Ability)

Q8. (c) Mother. The only daughter of the woman's mother is the woman herself, so she is the man's mother.
      Topic: Blood relations (Mental Ability)

Q9. (b) 90°. Each hour mark is 30° apart; at 3:00 the hands are 3 marks apart: 3 × 30° = 90°.
      Topic: Clock and calendar (Mental Ability)

Q10. (b) 6. A number divisible by both 3 and 5 is a multiple of 15: 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90.
      Topic: Ranking, arrangement and puzzles (Mental Ability)

Q11. (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s. v = c/n = (3 × 10⁸)/1.33.

Q12. (c) convex. Its image is always virtual, erect and smaller, which is why it is used as a rear-view mirror.

Q13. (b) dispersion. Different wavelengths bend by different amounts inside a prism, so the colours separate.

Q14. (a) atmospheric refraction. Moving layers of air of changing density bend the starlight by varying amounts, so its apparent position shifts rapidly.

Q15. (b) less resistance, since R = ρl/A and a larger area of cross-section reduces the resistance.
