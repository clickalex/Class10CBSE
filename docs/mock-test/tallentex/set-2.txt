SECTION A - Physics (Q1-Q15, 60 marks)
--------------------------------------

Q1. The image formed by a concave lens of a real object is always:
    (a) real and inverted
    (b) virtual, erect and diminished
    (c) virtual, erect and magnified
    (d) real and magnified

Q2. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:
    (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
    (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
    (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
    (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

Q3. An object is placed 20 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. The image is formed:
    (a) 10 cm in front of the mirror
    (b) 20 cm in front of the mirror
    (c) 20 cm behind the mirror
    (d) at infinity

Q4. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:
    (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
    (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
    (c) concave, focal length 2 m
    (d) convex, focal length 2 m

Q5. Stars twinkle mainly because of:
    (a) atmospheric refraction
    (b) dispersion
    (c) reflection from clouds
    (d) their own flickering

Q6. The least distance of distinct vision for a young adult with normal vision is about:
    (a) 2.5 cm
    (b) 25 cm
    (c) 25 m
    (d) infinity

Q7. The clear sky appears blue because:
    (a) blue light is absorbed the most
    (b) shorter wavelengths are scattered more by air molecules
    (c) the sea reflects blue light upwards
    (d) the atmosphere refracts blue light towards the eye

Q8. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:
    (a) more resistance
    (b) less resistance
    (c) the same resistance
    (d) no resistance

Q9. One kilowatt hour equals:
    (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
    (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
    (c) 360 J
    (d) 6 × 10µ J

Q10. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:
    (a) in series
    (b) in parallel
    (c) across the supply
    (d) either way

Q11. Three 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is:
    (a) 18 Ω
    (b) 6 Ω
    (c) 2 Ω
    (d) 3 Ω

Q12. The production of a current in a closed coil by a changing magnetic field is called:
    (a) electromagnetic induction
    (b) magnetic effect of current
    (c) electrolysis
    (d) electrostatic induction

Q13. An electric motor converts:
    (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
    (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
    (c) heat into light
    (d) chemical energy into sound

Q14. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:
    (a) increases as you move away from the wire
    (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
    (c) does not depend on the current
    (d) is zero everywhere around the wire

Q15. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:
    (a) induced current in a coil
    (b) magnetic field due to a current
    (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
    (d) current produced by a generator

-----------------------------------------

    (a) copper and tin

Q26. Galvanisation protects iron from rusting by coating it with a thin layer of:
    (a) tin
    (b) chromium

    (d) copper

Q27. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:
    (a) 5
    (b) 6
    (c) 7
    (d) 8

Q28. The functional group present in an alcohol is:
    (a) –CHO
    (b) –COOH
    (c) –OH
    (d) –CO–

Q29. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:
    (a) H₂
    (b) O₂
    (c) CO₂

Q30. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?
    (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄

    (a) dendrite
    (b) synapse
    (c) axon

    (a) cerebrum
    (b) cerebellum

    (a) cerebrum
    (b) spinal cord

    (d) medulla only

Q37. Which of these is an asexual mode of reproduction?
    (a) budding in yeast
    (b) pollination
    (c) fertilisation
    (d) seed germination

    (a) penis
    (b) testes
    (c) vas deferens
    (d) seminal vesicles

    (a) preventing ovulation with pills
    (b) surgically blocking the gamete ducts

    (a) 1:3

Q42. The number of chromosomes in a human zygote is:
    (a) 23
    (b) 46
    (c) 92
    (d) 22

Q43. Which of the following groups contains only biodegradable substances?
    (a) paper, plastic, wood
    (b) vegetable peels, paper, lime juice
    (c) glass, wood, plastic
    (d) lime juice, grass, aluminium foil

    (a) energy

    (a) coal
    (b) petroleum
    (c) sunlight
