SECTION A - Physics (Q1-Q15, 60 marks)
--------------------------------------

Q1. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:
    (a) less than one
    (b) more than one
    (c) equal to one
    (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

Q2. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?
    (a) at F
    (b) at 2F
    (c) at infinity
    (d) between F and 2F

Q3. The power of a convex lens of focal length 50 cm is:
    (a) +2 D
    (b) −2 D
    (c) +0.5 D
    (d) −0.5 D

Q4. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) does not bend
    (d) is totally absorbed

Q5. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:
    (a) pupil
    (b) retina
    (c) ciliary muscles
    (d) iris

Q6. The danger signals installed at the top of tall buildings are red because red light:
    (a) is scattered the most by smoke or fog
    (b) is scattered the least by smoke or fog
    (c) is absorbed the most
    (d) moves fastest in air

Q7. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:
    (a) scattering
    (b) dispersion
    (c) total internal reflection
    (d) polarisation

Q8. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:
    (a) 3 kWh
    (b) 30 kWh
    (c) 300 kWh
    (d) 3000 kWh

Q9. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:
    (a) joule
    (b) watt
    (c) kilowatt-hour
    (d) volt

Q10. The resistance of a wire doubles when:
    (a) its length is doubled
    (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
    (c) its length is halved
    (d) its diameter is doubled

Q11. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:
    (a) 24 Ω
    (b) 6 Ω
    (c) 0.17 Ω
    (d) 14 Ω

Q12. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:
    (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
    (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
    (c) the right-hand thumb rule
    (d) Lenz's law

Q13. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is:
    (a) AC generator has an electromagnet
    (b) DC generator has a commutator
    (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator
    (d) AC generator produces more power

Q14. The frequency of AC supply in India is 50 Hz. The direction of the current changes:
    (a) 50 times per second
    (b) 100 times per second
    (c) 25 times per second
    (d) 200 times per second

Q15. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:
    (a) concentric circles
    (b) parallel straight lines
    (c) diverging curves
    (d) absent
