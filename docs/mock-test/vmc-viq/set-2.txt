SECTION A - Physics (Q1-Q14, 56 marks)
--------------------------------------

Q1. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?
    (a) at F
    (b) at 2F
    (c) at infinity
    (d) between F and 2F

Q2. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:
    (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
    (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
    (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
    (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

Q3. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) does not bend
    (d) is totally absorbed

Q4. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:
    (a) myopia
    (b) hypermetropia
    (c) astigmatism
    (d) night blindness

Q5. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:
    (a) scattering
    (b) dispersion
    (c) total internal reflection
    (d) polarisation

Q6. The least distance of distinct vision for a young adult with normal vision is about:
    (a) 2.5 cm
    (b) 25 cm
    (c) 25 m
    (d) infinity

Q7. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:
    (a) more than 100 Ω
    (b) less than 1 Ω
    (c) 107 Ω
    (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

Q8. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:
    (a) joule
    (b) watt
    (c) kilowatt-hour
    (d) volt

Q9. The resistance of a wire doubles when:
    (a) its length is doubled
    (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
    (c) its length is halved
    (d) its diameter is doubled

Q10. Three 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is:
    (a) 18 Ω
    (b) 6 Ω
    (c) 2 Ω
    (d) 3 Ω

Q11. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:
    (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
    (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
    (c) the right-hand thumb rule
    (d) Lenz's law

Q12. The production of a current in a closed coil by a changing magnetic field is called:
    (a) electromagnetic induction
    (b) magnetic effect of current
    (c) electrolysis
    (d) electrostatic induction

Q13. An electric motor converts:
    (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
    (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
    (c) heat into light
    (d) chemical energy into sound

Q14. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:
    (a) increases as you move away from the wire
    (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
    (c) does not depend on the current
    (d) is zero everywhere around the wire


SECTION B - Chemistry (Q15-Q28, 56 marks)
-----------------------------------------

Q15. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:
    (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
    (b) CuO and Ag2O
    (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
    (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

Q16. Which of these is a combination reaction?
    (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
    (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
    (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
    (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

Q17. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by:
    (a) storing it in the open
    (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
    (c) adding oxidising agents
    (d) heating it repeatedly

Q18. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:
    (a) 2
    (b) 3
    (c) 4
    (d) 6

Q19. Which one is a strong base?
    (a) NH4OH
    (b) Mg(OH)2
    (c) NaOH
    (d) Cu(OH)2

Q20. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:
    (a) 2
    (b) 5
    (c) 7
    (d) 10

Q21. Dilute acid added to an active metal produces:
    (a) oxygen
    (b) hydrogen
    (c) carbon dioxide
    (d) nitrogen

Q22. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:
    (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
    (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
    (c) CaSO₄
    (d) CaCO₃

Q23. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:
    (a) less reactive than silver
    (b) more reactive than silver
    (c) equally reactive
    (d) an inert metal

Q24. The best conductor of electricity among these is:
    (a) copper
    (b) silver
    (c) aluminium
    (d) iron

Q25. Which metal reacts vigorously with cold water?
    (a) iron
    (b) zinc
    (c) sodium
    (d) copper

Q26. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:
    (a) 5
    (b) 6
    (c) 7
    (d) 8

Q27. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?
    (a) C₃H₈
    (b) C₂H₆
    (c) C₄H₁₀
    (d) C₂H₄

Q28. The functional group present in an alcohol is:
    (a) –CHO
    (b) –COOH
    (c) –OH
    (d) –CO–

-------------------------------------------

Q29. Which has a terminating decimal expansion?

    (a) 1
    (b) 2

    (a) 6
    (b) 5
    (c) −5
    (d) 1

    (a) 2 − √3
    (b) −2 − √3
    (c) √3 − 2
    (d) −√3

Q33. The solution of x + y = 14 and x − y = 4 is:
    (a) x = 9, y = 5
    (b) x = 5, y = 9
    (c) x = 10, y = 4
    (d) x = 8, y = 6

Q34. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are:
    (a) real and distinct
    (b) real and equal
    (c) not real
    (d) always integers

Q35. The sum of the first 100 natural numbers is:
    (a) 5000
    (b) 5050
    (c) 4950
    (d) 5100

Q36. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding sides are:
    (a) equal
    (b) proportional
    (c) perpendicular
    (d) parallel

Q37. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:
    (a) a
    (b) a²

    (d) 2a

Q38. tan 45° × cot 45° is:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1
    (c) 2
    (d) undefined

Q39. From the top of a tower the angle of depression of an object on the ground is 30°. The angle of elevation of the top from that object is:
    (a) 30°
    (b) 45°
    (c) 60°
    (d) 90°

Q40. A line that cuts a circle in two distinct points is called a:
    (a) tangent
    (b) secant
    (c) chord
    (d) diameter

Q41. The perimeter of a sector includes:
    (a) only the arc
    (b) the arc and one radius
    (c) the arc and two radii
    (d) the chord and the arc

Q42. A cone, a hemisphere and a cylinder have equal bases and equal heights. The ratio of their volumes is:
    (a) 1 : 2 : 3
    (b) 2 : 3 : 1
    (c) 3 : 1 : 2
    (d) 1 : 3 : 2

Q43. The class mark of the class 45–55 is:
    (a) 45
    (b) 50
    (c) 55
    (d) 100

    (a) 8
