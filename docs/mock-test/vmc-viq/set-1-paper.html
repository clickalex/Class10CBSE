Section A · Physics Q1–Q14 · 56 marks

1. The image formed by a concave lens of a real object is always:

  • (a) real and inverted
  • (b) virtual, erect and diminished
  • (c) virtual, erect and magnified
  • (d) real and magnified

2. The power of a convex lens of focal length 50 cm is:

  • (a) +2 D
  • (b) −2 D
  • (c) +0.5 D
  • (d) −0.5 D

3. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?

  • (a) plane
  • (b) concave
  • (c) convex
  • (d) parabolic

4. An object is placed 20 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. The image is formed:

  • (a) 10 cm in front of the mirror
  • (b) 20 cm in front of the mirror
  • (c) 20 cm behind the mirror
  • (d) at infinity

5. The danger signals installed at the top of tall buildings are red because red light:

  • (a) is scattered the most by smoke or fog
  • (b) is scattered the least by smoke or fog
  • (c) is absorbed the most
  • (d) moves fastest in air

6. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:

  • (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
  • (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
  • (c) concave, focal length 2 m
  • (d) convex, focal length 2 m

7. Stars twinkle mainly because of:

  • (a) atmospheric refraction
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) reflection from clouds
  • (d) their own flickering

8. The clear sky appears blue because:

  • (a) blue light is absorbed the most
  • (b) shorter wavelengths are scattered more by air molecules
  • (c) the sea reflects blue light upwards
  • (d) the atmosphere refracts blue light towards the eye

9. One kilowatt hour equals:

  • (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
  • (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
  • (c) 360 J
  • (d) 6 × 10µ J

10. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:

  • (a) in series
  • (b) in parallel
  • (c) across the supply
  • (d) either way

11. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:

  • (a) 24 Ω
  • (b) 6 Ω
  • (c) 0.17 Ω
  • (d) 14 Ω

12. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is:

  • (a) AC generator has an electromagnet
  • (b) DC generator has a commutator
  • (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator
  • (d) AC generator produces more power

13. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:

  • (a) concentric circles
  • (b) parallel straight lines
  • (c) diverging curves
  • (d) absent

14. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:

  • (a) induced current in a coil
  • (b) magnetic field due to a current
  • (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
  • (d) current produced by a generator

Section B · Chemistry Q15–Q28 · 56 marks

15. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:

  • (a) combination
  • (b) decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) double displacement

16. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of:

  • (a) NO₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) NO
  • (d) PbO

17. A shiny brown element X becomes black when heated in air. X and the black compound are:

  • (a) Cu and CuO
  • (b) Fe and Fe₂O₃
  • (c) Ag and Ag₂S
  • (d) Al and Al₂O₃

18. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains:

  • (a) NaCl
  • (b) HCl
  • (c) LiCl
  • (d) KCl

19. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:

  • (a) sodium chloride
  • (b) sodium carbonate
  • (c) calcium sulphate
  • (d) ammonium chloride

20. Baking soda is:

  • (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
  • (b) NaHCO₃
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Na₂CO₃

21. Which pair is amphoteric?

  • (a) Na2O and CaO
  • (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
  • (c) CO2 and SO2
  • (d) CuO and FeO

22. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:

  • (a) reduction with carbon
  • (b) self reduction
  • (c) electrolytic reduction
  • (d) roasting

23. Brass is an alloy of:

  • (a) copper and tin
  • (b) copper and zinc
  • (c) lead and tin
  • (d) mercury and zinc

24. The correct decreasing order of reactivity is:

  • (a) Zn > Mg > Cu
  • (b) Mg > Zn > Cu
  • (c) Cu > Zn > Mg
  • (d) Zn > Cu > Mg

25. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)?

  • (a) C₂H₆
  • (b) C₂H₄
  • (c) CH₄
  • (d) C₃H₈

26. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:

  • (a) H₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) CO₂
  • (d) CO

27. The IUPAC name of CH₃–CH₂–CHO is:

  • (a) propanol
  • (b) propanal
  • (c) propanone
  • (d) propanoic acid

28. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?

  • (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
  • (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
  • (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
  • (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH

Section C · Mathematics Q29–Q44 · 64 marks

29. Which of these is irrational?

  • (a) √(16/9)
  • (b) √12
  • (c) 0.444…
  • (d) 22/7

30. A quadratic polynomial can have at most:

  • (a) 0 zeroes
  • (b) 1 zero
  • (c) 2 zeroes
  • (d) 3 zeroes

31. If a boat’s downstream speed is 12 km/h and upstream speed is 8 km/h, its still-water speed is:

  • (a) 10
  • (b) 4
  • (c) 2
  • (d) 20 km/h

32. For x² + 4x + k = 0 to have real roots, k must satisfy:

  • (a) k ≤ 4
  • (b) k ≥ 4
  • (c) k ≤ −4
  • (d) k = 4

33. The sum of the first 10 natural numbers is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 50
  • (c) 55
  • (d) 100

34. If the corresponding sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 4 : 9, their areas are in the ratio:

  • (a) 4 : 9
  • (b) 2 : 3
  • (c) 16 : 81
  • (d) 8 : 27

35. The distance of the point (3, 4) from the origin is:

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 7
  • (c) 25
  • (d) 12

36. The value of sin²60° + 2 tan²45° − cos²30° is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 5/4
  • (d) 9/4

37. The angle of depression is always measured from:

  • (a) the vertical
  • (b) the horizontal
  • (c) the object
  • (d) the ground below

38. The number of tangents that can be drawn from a point outside a circle is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 3
  • (d) infinite

39. The area of a semicircle of radius r is:

  • (a) πr²
  • (b) πr²/2
  • (c) 2πr
  • (d) πr²/4

40. The volume of a cone of radius r and height h is:

  • (a) πr²h
  • (b) πr²h/3
  • (c) 2πr²h
  • (d) πrh

41. The class mark of the class 15–25 is:

  • (a) 15
  • (b) 20
  • (c) 25
  • (d) 10

42. The empirical relation connecting the three measures of central tendency is:

  • (a) Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean
  • (b) Median = 3 Mode − 2 Mean
  • (c) Mean = 3 Median − 2 Mode
  • (d) Mode = 2 Median − Mean

43. If P(E) = 0.37, then P(not E) = :

  • (a) 0.37
  • (b) 0.63
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 0.53

44. One card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck. The probability of a black queen is:

  • (a) 1/52
  • (b) 1/26
  • (c) 1/13
  • (d) 2/13
