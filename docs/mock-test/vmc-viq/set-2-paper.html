Section A · Physics Q1–Q14 · 56 marks

1. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?

  • (a) at F
  • (b) at 2F
  • (c) at infinity
  • (d) between F and 2F

2. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:

  • (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

3. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:

  • (a) bends towards the normal
  • (b) bends away from the normal
  • (c) does not bend
  • (d) is totally absorbed

4. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:

  • (a) myopia
  • (b) hypermetropia
  • (c) astigmatism
  • (d) night blindness

5. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:

  • (a) scattering
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) total internal reflection
  • (d) polarisation

6. The least distance of distinct vision for a young adult with normal vision is about:

  • (a) 2.5 cm
  • (b) 25 cm
  • (c) 25 m
  • (d) infinity

7. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:

  • (a) more than 100 Ω
  • (b) less than 1 Ω
  • (c) 107 Ω
  • (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

8. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:

  • (a) joule
  • (b) watt
  • (c) kilowatt-hour
  • (d) volt

9. The resistance of a wire doubles when:

  • (a) its length is doubled
  • (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
  • (c) its length is halved
  • (d) its diameter is doubled

10. Three 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is:

  • (a) 18 Ω
  • (b) 6 Ω
  • (c) 2 Ω
  • (d) 3 Ω

11. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:

  • (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
  • (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
  • (c) the right-hand thumb rule
  • (d) Lenz's law

12. The production of a current in a closed coil by a changing magnetic field is called:

  • (a) electromagnetic induction
  • (b) magnetic effect of current
  • (c) electrolysis
  • (d) electrostatic induction

13. An electric motor converts:

  • (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
  • (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
  • (c) heat into light
  • (d) chemical energy into sound

14. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:

  • (a) increases as you move away from the wire
  • (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
  • (c) does not depend on the current
  • (d) is zero everywhere around the wire

Section B · Chemistry Q15–Q28 · 56 marks

15. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:

  • (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
  • (b) CuO and Ag2O
  • (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
  • (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

16. Which of these is a combination reaction?

  • (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
  • (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
  • (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
  • (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

17. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by:

  • (a) storing it in the open
  • (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
  • (c) adding oxidising agents
  • (d) heating it repeatedly

18. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 3
  • (c) 4
  • (d) 6

19. Which one is a strong base?

  • (a) NH4OH
  • (b) Mg(OH)2
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Cu(OH)2

20. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 10

21. Dilute acid added to an active metal produces:

  • (a) oxygen
  • (b) hydrogen
  • (c) carbon dioxide
  • (d) nitrogen

22. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:

  • (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
  • (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
  • (c) CaSO₄
  • (d) CaCO₃

23. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:

  • (a) less reactive than silver
  • (b) more reactive than silver
  • (c) equally reactive
  • (d) an inert metal

24. The best conductor of electricity among these is:

  • (a) copper
  • (b) silver
  • (c) aluminium
  • (d) iron

25. Which metal reacts vigorously with cold water?

  • (a) iron
  • (b) zinc
  • (c) sodium
  • (d) copper

26. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 6
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 8

27. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?

  • (a) C₃H₈
  • (b) C₂H₆
  • (c) C₄H₁₀
  • (d) C₂H₄

28. The functional group present in an alcohol is:

  • (a) –CHO
  • (b) –COOH
  • (c) –OH
  • (d) –CO–

Section C · Mathematics Q29–Q44 · 64 marks

29. Which has a terminating decimal expansion?

  • (a) 7/24
  • (b) 5/13
  • (c) 9/40
  • (d) 11/21

30. The HCF of the smallest prime number and the smallest composite number is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 4
  • (d) 6

31. If α, β are zeroes of x² − 5x + 6, then α + β = ?

  • (a) 6
  • (b) 5
  • (c) −5
  • (d) 1

32. If one zero of x² − 4x + 1 is 2 + √3, the other zero is:

  • (a) 2 − √3
  • (b) −2 − √3
  • (c) √3 − 2
  • (d) −√3

33. The solution of x + y = 14 and x − y = 4 is:

  • (a) x = 9, y = 5
  • (b) x = 5, y = 9
  • (c) x = 10, y = 4
  • (d) x = 8, y = 6

34. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are:

  • (a) real and distinct
  • (b) real and equal
  • (c) not real
  • (d) always integers

35. The sum of the first 100 natural numbers is:

  • (a) 5000
  • (b) 5050
  • (c) 4950
  • (d) 5100

36. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding sides are:

  • (a) equal
  • (b) proportional
  • (c) perpendicular
  • (d) parallel

37. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:

  • (a) a
  • (b) a²
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 2a

38. tan 45° × cot 45° is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) 2
  • (d) undefined

39. From the top of a tower the angle of depression of an object on the ground is 30°. The angle of elevation of the top from that object is:

  • (a) 30°
  • (b) 45°
  • (c) 60°
  • (d) 90°

40. A line that cuts a circle in two distinct points is called a:

  • (a) tangent
  • (b) secant
  • (c) chord
  • (d) diameter

41. The perimeter of a sector includes:

  • (a) only the arc
  • (b) the arc and one radius
  • (c) the arc and two radii
  • (d) the chord and the arc

42. A cone, a hemisphere and a cylinder have equal bases and equal heights. The ratio of their volumes is:

  • (a) 1 : 2 : 3
  • (b) 2 : 3 : 1
  • (c) 3 : 1 : 2
  • (d) 1 : 3 : 2

43. The class mark of the class 45–55 is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 50
  • (c) 55
  • (d) 100

44. The number of face cards in a deck of 52 cards is:

  • (a) 8
  • (b) 12
  • (c) 16
  • (d) 4

  • (a) killing bacteria
  • (b) digestion of protein
  • (c) absorption of digested food
  • (d) protection of the stomach wall by mucus

  • (a) axon
  • (b) dendrite
  • (c) cell body
  • (d) myelin sheath

47. Binary fission in amoeba begins with the division of the:

  • (a) cytoplasm
  • (b) nucleus
  • (c) cell membrane
  • (d) pseudopodia

48. The sex of a human child is determined by:

  • (a) the mother's egg
  • (b) the father's sperm
  • (c) both parents equally
  • (d) the diet of the mother

49. In the food chain Grass → Deer → Lion, if 10,000 J of energy is available to the grass, the energy available to the lion is:

  • (a) 1000 J
  • (b) 100 J

  • (d) 1 J

  • (a) energy
  • (b) water
  • (c) minerals
  • (d) oxygen
