Section A · First Flight · Prose Q1–Q11 · 11 marks

1. [A Letter to God] The letter Lencho received was signed:

  • (a) the postmaster
  • (b) God
  • (c) the employees of the post office
  • (d) the President

2. [Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom] Mandela's autobiography is titled:

  • (a) Long Walk to Freedom
  • (b) The Struggle Is My Life
  • (c) The Prison Diary
  • (d) Invictus

3. [Two Stories about Flying] The young seagull was afraid to fly because:

  • (a) he hated his family
  • (b) he felt his wings would not support him over the great depth below
  • (c) he was ill
  • (d) the weather was bad

4. [From the Diary of Anne Frank] The third essay Mr Keesing assigned was:

  • (a) A Chatterbox
  • (b) An Incorrigible Chatterbox
  • (c) Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterbox
  • (d) A Talkative Girl

5. [From the Diary of Anne Frank] Anne Frank named her diary:

  • (a) Kitty
  • (b) Margot
  • (c) Lies
  • (d) Sanne

6. [Glimpses of India] According to the chapter, how many cups of tea are drunk every day throughout the world?

  • (a) eight crore
  • (b) eighty crore
  • (c) eight hundred crore
  • (d) eight lakh

7. [Glimpses of India] In Goa, the sweet bread given as a marriage gift is called:

  • (a) pao
  • (b) bol
  • (c) bolinhas
  • (d) kabai

8. [Mijbil the Otter] The author of ‘Mijbil the Otter' is:

  • (a) Gavin Maxwell
  • (b) Gerald Durrell
  • (c) Ruskin Bond
  • (d) James Herriot

9. [Madam Rides the Bus] The conductor called Valli:

  • (a) child
  • (b) madam
  • (c) sister
  • (d) little girl

10. [The Sermon at Benares] The Buddha asked Kisa Gotami to bring mustard seed from a house where:

  • (a) there was no poverty
  • (b) no one had died
  • (c) everyone was happy
  • (d) there were no children

11. [The Proposal] Lomov wants to marry Natalya mainly because:

  • (a) she is an excellent housekeeper and he is thirty-five and needs a settled life
  • (b) he loves her
  • (c) his father insists
  • (d) she is very rich

Section B · First Flight · Poems Q12–Q22 · 11 marks

12. [Dust of Snow] The word 'rued' in the poem means:

  • (a) enjoyed
  • (b) regretted
  • (c) remembered
  • (d) forgot

13. [Fire and Ice] ‘Ice' in the poem stands for:

  • (a) hatred and coldness
  • (b) winter
  • (c) the sea
  • (d) innocence

14. [A Tiger in the Zoo] The tiger in the zoo is described as stalking the length of his:

  • (a) cage
  • (b) jungle
  • (c) river bank
  • (d) cave

15. [How to Tell Wild Animals] The animal that 'hugs you very, very hard' is the:

  • (a) bear
  • (b) hyena
  • (c) leopard
  • (d) chameleon

16. [The Ball Poem] The boy's ball finally goes:

  • (a) into the water
  • (b) into a shop
  • (c) to another boy
  • (d) into a drain

17. [The Ball Poem] According to the poet, money:

  • (a) is external and cannot replace what is lost
  • (b) can buy anything
  • (c) should be saved
  • (d) is the cause of grief

18. [Amanda!] Amanda's daydreams are about being:

  • (a) a mermaid, Rapunzel and an orphan
  • (b) a queen and a teacher
  • (c) a bird
  • (d) a soldier

19. [The Trees] The long-cramped boughs are compared to:

  • (a) newly discharged patients
  • (b) prisoners escaping
  • (c) birds returning
  • (d) children running

20. [Fog] The fog sits looking over:

  • (a) harbour and city
  • (b) the sea only
  • (c) the hills
  • (d) the garden

21. [The Tale of Custard the Dragon] Custard is teased by the others because he:

  • (a) is cowardly and cries for a nice safe cage
  • (b) cannot fly
  • (c) has no teeth
  • (d) is too small

22. [For Anne Gregory] 'For Anne Gregory' is written by:

  • (a) W. B. Yeats
  • (b) Ogden Nash
  • (c) Carl Sandburg
  • (d) Robert Frost

Section C · Footprints Without Feet Q23–Q32 · 10 marks

23. [A Triumph of Surgery] Mrs Pumphrey sent Tricki to the surgery with:

  • (a) eggs, wine and brandy
  • (b) only water
  • (c) medicines
  • (d) toys

24. [The Thief's Story] The thief gave his name as:

  • (a) Anil
  • (b) Hari Singh
  • (c) Ruskin
  • (d) Arun

25. [The Midnight Visitor] Max entered Ausable's room to steal:

  • (a) a report about new missiles
  • (b) money
  • (c) a passport
  • (d) a gun

26. [A Question of Trust] Horace was arrested because:

  • (a) the woman informed the police
  • (b) he left his fingerprints
  • (c) he dropped his tools
  • (d) he was seen by a servant

27. [Footprints without Feet] Griffin was a scientist who discovered:

  • (a) how to make the human body transparent
  • (b) how to fly
  • (c) a new gas
  • (d) a cure for blindness

28. [The Making of a Scientist] Ebright's teacher Richard A. Weiherer taught him to:

  • (a) win at any cost
  • (b) compete in order to do the best job he could
  • (c) avoid competition
  • (d) study only butterflies

29. [The Necklace] 'The Necklace' is written by:

  • (a) Guy de Maupassant
  • (b) Anton Chekhov
  • (c) Ruskin Bond
  • (d) H. G. Wells

30. [Bholi] Bholi refuses to marry Bishamber Nath because he:

  • (a) demands dowry from her father after the garlands are exchanged
  • (b) is poor
  • (c) is uneducated
  • (d) is a widower

31. [The Book That Saved the Earth] Think-Tank first mistakes the book for:

  • (a) a mirror
  • (b) an earth sandwich
  • (c) a weapon
  • (d) a map

32. [The Book That Saved the Earth] The commander of the Martian invasion force was:

  • (a) Think-Tank
  • (b) Noodle
  • (c) Omega
  • (d) Iota

Section D · Reading, Grammar & Writing Q33–Q40 · 8 marks

33. [Reading: Unseen and Case-based Passages] The length of the discursive passage in the reading section is:

  • (a) 200–250 words
  • (b) 300–350 words
  • (c) 400–450 words
  • (d) 500–550 words

34. [Reading: Unseen and Case-based Passages] Answers to an unseen passage should be:

  • (a) in your own words and within the word limit
  • (b) as long as possible
  • (c) copied from the passage
  • (d) written as bullet points only

35. [Grammar in Context] Fill in the blank: She has been waiting ____ two hours.

  • (a) for
  • (b) since
  • (c) from
  • (d) during

36. [Grammar in Context] The correct reported form of ‘He said, “I am busy”' is:

  • (a) He said that he was busy
  • (b) He said that he is busy
  • (c) He said that I was busy
  • (d) He says he was busy

37. [Formal Letter Writing] In a letter of complaint or order, the subject line should be:

  • (a) short and specific
  • (b) long and detailed
  • (c) left out
  • (d) written as a question

38. [Formal Letter Writing] A letter of enquiry should always ask for:

  • (a) specific details such as price, terms and delivery
  • (b) personal favours
  • (c) a job
  • (d) an autograph

39. [Analytical Paragraph] An analytical paragraph is based on:

  • (a) a story
  • (b) a chart, graph, table or set of clues
  • (c) a poem
  • (d) a personal experience

40. [Analytical Paragraph] The word limit for an analytical paragraph is:

  • (a) 50–60 words
  • (b) 100–120 words
  • (c) 150–200 words
  • (d) no limit
