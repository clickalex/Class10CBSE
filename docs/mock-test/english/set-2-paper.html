Section A · First Flight · Prose Q1–Q11 · 11 marks

1. [A Letter to God] Lencho asked God for:

  • (a) 30 pesos
  • (b) 70 pesos
  • (c) 100 pesos
  • (d) 1000 pesos

2. [Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom] According to Mandela, courage is:

  • (a) the absence of fear
  • (b) the triumph over fear
  • (c) the denial of pain
  • (d) the love of danger

3. [Two Stories about Flying] How long was the young seagull left alone on his ledge before he flew?

  • (a) twelve hours
  • (b) twenty-four hours
  • (c) two days
  • (d) three days

4. [From the Diary of Anne Frank] Anne named her diary:

  • (a) Margot
  • (b) Kitty
  • (c) Sanne
  • (d) Anne

5. [Glimpses of India] The people of Coorg wear a long black coat with an embroidered waist-belt called:

  • (a) kabai
  • (b) Kuppia
  • (c) kuffia
  • (d) bol

6. [Mijbil the Otter] Where did the author collect Mijbil from?

  • (a) Kerala
  • (b) the Tigris marshes near Basra
  • (c) the Nile delta
  • (d) Assam

7. [Mijbil the Otter] Mijbil loved to play with:

  • (a) marbles and ping-pong balls
  • (b) sticks and stones
  • (c) paper boats
  • (d) balloons

8. [Madam Rides the Bus] Valli's age at the time of the story was:

  • (a) six years
  • (b) eight years and some months
  • (c) ten years
  • (d) twelve years

9. [Madam Rides the Bus] Valli travelled to the town on the:

  • (a) morning bus
  • (b) one o'clock bus
  • (c) evening bus
  • (d) night bus

10. [The Sermon at Benares] The Buddha compared mortal life to:

  • (a) ripe fruits that may fall and earthen vessels fashioned by the potter that must all end broken
  • (b) a river
  • (c) a mountain
  • (d) a lamp

11. [The Proposal] ‘The Proposal' was written by:

  • (a) Anton Chekhov
  • (b) Guy de Maupassant
  • (c) Oscar Wilde
  • (d) George Bernard Shaw

Section B · First Flight · Poems Q12–Q22 · 11 marks

12. [Dust of Snow] The rhyme scheme of ‘Dust of Snow’ is:

  • (a) abab cdcd
  • (b) aabb ccdd
  • (c) abcb abcb
  • (d) aba abc

13. [Fire and Ice] The rhyme scheme of 'Fire and Ice' is:

  • (a) abab cdcd
  • (b) aba abc bcb
  • (c) aabb ccdd
  • (d) abcb abcb

  • (a) Leslie Norris
  • (b) Robert Frost
  • (c) Vicky Tree
  • (d) Carolyn Wells

15. [How to Tell Wild Animals] 'How to Tell Wild Animals' is written by:

  • (a) Carolyn Wells
  • (b) Leslie Norris
  • (c) Vicky Tree
  • (d) Walt Whitman

16. [The Ball Poem] The ball cost:

  • (a) a penny
  • (b) a dime
  • (c) a dollar
  • (d) five cents

17. [Amanda!] As Rapunzel, Amanda would:

  • (a) let down her bright hair
  • (b) never let down her bright hair
  • (c) climb down the tower
  • (d) call for help

18. [Amanda!] In the poem, Amanda's own thoughts and replies are shown:

  • (a) in brackets
  • (b) in bold letters
  • (c) in capital letters
  • (d) in footnotes

19. [The Trees] The poet of ‘The Trees' is:

  • (a) Adrienne Rich
  • (b) Robin Klein
  • (c) Leslie Norris
  • (d) Carolyn Wells

20. [Fog] The fog comes:

  • (a) with thunder
  • (b) on little cat feet
  • (c) over the mountains
  • (d) after the rain

21. [The Tale of Custard the Dragon] 'The Tale of Custard the Dragon' is written by:

  • (a) Ogden Nash
  • (b) Carl Sandburg
  • (c) Robin Klein
  • (d) Adrienne Rich

22. [For Anne Gregory] 'Honey-coloured ramparts' refers to:

  • (a) the walls of a castle
  • (b) Anne's yellow hair
  • (c) the setting sun
  • (d) a religious text

Section C · Footprints Without Feet Q23–Q32 · 10 marks

23. [A Triumph of Surgery] Tricki was a:

  • (a) large Alsatian
  • (b) small Pomeranian
  • (c) greyhound
  • (d) spaniel

24. [A Triumph of Surgery] The vet treated Tricki with:

  • (a) an operation
  • (b) antibiotics
  • (c) no medicine, only water and exercise
  • (d) cod-liver oil

25. [The Thief's Story] The thief returned the money because:

  • (a) he was caught
  • (b) he feared the police
  • (c) he realised he had lost Anil's trust
  • (d) the notes were ruined

26. [The Midnight Visitor] Ausable is described as:

  • (a) tall and handsome
  • (b) very fat and sloppy
  • (c) thin and sinister
  • (d) old and blind

27. [A Question of Trust] Horace Danby robbed a safe:

  • (a) every month
  • (b) once a year
  • (c) whenever he needed money
  • (d) only from the rich and the poor alike

28. [Footprints without Feet] The inn where Griffin stayed was at:

  • (a) Drury Lane
  • (b) Iping
  • (c) London Bridge
  • (d) Shotover

29. [The Making of a Scientist] The twelve tiny gold spots on a monarch pupa:

  • (a) are ornamental
  • (b) produce a hormone needed for the butterfly's development
  • (c) attract mates
  • (d) protect the pupa from heat

30. [The Necklace] The dress Matilda's husband gave her money for cost:

  • (a) 400 francs
  • (b) 4,000 francs
  • (c) 40 francs
  • (d) 40,000 francs

31. [Bholi] Bishamber Nath, the bridegroom Ramlal finds for Bholi, was:

  • (a) a middle-aged grocer and widower from another village
  • (b) a young schoolteacher
  • (c) a rich landlord
  • (d) a doctor

32. [The Book That Saved the Earth] The book that frightened Think-Tank was:

  • (a) the Bible
  • (b) Mother Goose
  • (c) an atlas
  • (d) a Martian history

Section D · Reading, Grammar & Writing Q33–Q40 · 8 marks

33. [Reading: Unseen and Case-based Passages] Objective type questions in the reading section assess:

  • (a) memorisation
  • (b) inference, analysis, interpretation, evaluation and vocabulary
  • (c) handwriting
  • (d) grammar rules

34. [Reading: Unseen and Case-based Passages] The best first step in attempting an unseen passage is to:

  • (a) read the passage three times
  • (b) read the questions first
  • (c) answer from general knowledge
  • (d) begin with the hardest question

35. [Grammar in Context] Choose the correct sentence:

  • (a) The committee have taken their seats.
  • (b) The committee has taken its seat.
  • (c) The committee have taken its seat.
  • (d) The committee has taken their seats.

36. [Grammar in Context] Choose the correct form: Neither of the boys ___ present.

  • (a) were
  • (b) was
  • (c) are
  • (d) have been

37. [Formal Letter Writing] In a formal letter, the sender's name is written:

  • (a) at the top with the address
  • (b) at the bottom, under the signature
  • (c) in the subject line
  • (d) after the salutation

38. [Formal Letter Writing] The word limit for a formal letter in the board examination is:

  • (a) 50–60 words
  • (b) 100–120 words
  • (c) 150–200 words
  • (d) no limit

39. [Analytical Paragraph] The concluding sentence of an analytical paragraph should:

  • (a) give a personal opinion
  • (b) state a trend or inference from the data
  • (c) list more figures
  • (d) repeat the introduction

40. [Analytical Paragraph] The usual length of an analytical paragraph in the board paper is:

  • (a) 100 to 120 words
  • (b) 300 words
  • (c) 20 words
  • (d) 500 words
