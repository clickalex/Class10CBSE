SECTION A - First Flight · Prose (Q1-Q11, 11 marks)
---------------------------------------------------

Q1. [A Letter to God] The letter Lencho received was signed:
    (a) the postmaster
    (b) God
    (c) the employees of the post office
    (d) the President

Q2. [Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom] Mandela's autobiography is titled:
    (a) Long Walk to Freedom
    (b) The Struggle Is My Life
    (c) The Prison Diary
    (d) Invictus

Q3. [Two Stories about Flying] The young seagull was afraid to fly because:
    (a) he hated his family
    (b) he felt his wings would not support him over the great depth below
    (c) he was ill
    (d) the weather was bad

Q4. [From the Diary of Anne Frank] The third essay Mr Keesing assigned was:
    (a) A Chatterbox
    (b) An Incorrigible Chatterbox
    (c) Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterbox
    (d) A Talkative Girl

Q5. [From the Diary of Anne Frank] Anne Frank named her diary:
    (a) Kitty
    (b) Margot
    (c) Lies
    (d) Sanne

Q6. [Glimpses of India] According to the chapter, how many cups of tea are drunk every day throughout the world?
    (a) eight crore
    (b) eighty crore
    (c) eight hundred crore
    (d) eight lakh

Q7. [Glimpses of India] In Goa, the sweet bread given as a marriage gift is called:
    (a) pao
    (b) bol
    (c) bolinhas
    (d) kabai

Q8. [Mijbil the Otter] The author of ‘Mijbil the Otter' is:
    (a) Gavin Maxwell
    (b) Gerald Durrell
    (c) Ruskin Bond
    (d) James Herriot

Q9. [Madam Rides the Bus] The conductor called Valli:
    (a) child
    (b) madam
    (c) sister
    (d) little girl

Q10. [The Sermon at Benares] The Buddha asked Kisa Gotami to bring mustard seed from a house where:
    (a) there was no poverty
    (b) no one had died
    (c) everyone was happy
    (d) there were no children

Q11. [The Proposal] Lomov wants to marry Natalya mainly because:
    (a) she is an excellent housekeeper and he is thirty-five and needs a settled life
    (b) he loves her
    (c) his father insists
    (d) she is very rich

    (a) bear
    (b) hyena

    (a) into the water
    (b) into a shop
    (c) to another boy

    (a) is external and cannot replace what is lost

    (a) a mermaid, Rapunzel and an orphan
    (b) a queen and a teacher

    (a) harbour and city

    (c) the hills

Q21. [The Tale of Custard the Dragon] Custard is teased by the others because he:
    (a) is cowardly and cries for a nice safe cage

Q33. [Reading: Unseen and Case-based Passages] The length of the discursive passage in the reading section is:
    (a) 200–250 words
    (b) 300–350 words
    (c) 400–450 words
    (d) 500–550 words
