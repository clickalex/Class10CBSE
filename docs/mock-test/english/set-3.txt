SECTION A - First Flight · Prose (Q1-Q11, 11 marks)
---------------------------------------------------

Q1. [A Letter to God] The postmaster and his staff managed to collect:
    (a) 100 pesos
    (b) 70 pesos
    (c) 30 pesos
    (d) 700 pesos

Q2. [Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom] The policy of racial discrimination followed in South Africa was called:
    (a) apartheid
    (b) satyagraha
    (c) segregation by choice
    (d) democracy

Q3. [Two Stories about Flying] In ‘His First Flight', the young seagull's mother tempted him with:
    (a) a piece of fish
    (b) a worm
    (c) a leaf
    (d) her call alone

Q4. [From the Diary of Anne Frank] The line 'paper has more patience than people' means that:
    (a) paper lasts longer than people
    (b) a diary will listen without judging or tiring
    (c) people are impatient with writing
    (d) diaries are more valuable than friends

Q5. [Glimpses of India] The traditional bakers of Goa are called:
    (a) paders
    (b) pao wallahs
    (c) bakers
    (d) lohars

Q6. [Mijbil the Otter] Mijbil was scientifically classified as:
    (a) Lutrogale perspicillata maxwelli
    (b) Lutra lutra
    (c) Aonyx cinereus
    (d) Enhydra lutris

Q7. [Madam Rides the Bus] Valli saved what amount for her bus journey?
    (a) 30 paise
    (b) 60 paise
    (c) one rupee
    (d) two rupees

Q8. [The Sermon at Benares] Kisa Gotami was asked to bring mustard seed from a house:
    (a) where no one had died
    (b) where a child had been born
    (c) where a wedding was held
    (d) that belonged to a king

Q9. [The Sermon at Benares] The Buddha delivered his first sermon at:
    (a) Sarnath near Benares
    (b) Kushinagar
    (c) Lumbini
    (d) Bodh Gaya

Q10. [The Proposal] The second quarrel in the play is about:
    (a) the Oxen Meadows
    (b) two hunting dogs
    (c) money
    (d) Natalya's age

Q11. [The Proposal] Lomov suffers from:
    (a) asthma
    (b) palpitations and a twitching eyebrow
    (c) a limp
    (d) poor eyesight
