Section A · First Flight · Prose Q1–Q11 · 11 marks

1. [A Letter to God] The postmaster and his staff managed to collect:

  • (a) 100 pesos
  • (b) 70 pesos
  • (c) 30 pesos
  • (d) 700 pesos

2. [Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom] The policy of racial discrimination followed in South Africa was called:

  • (a) apartheid
  • (b) satyagraha
  • (c) segregation by choice
  • (d) democracy

3. [Two Stories about Flying] In ‘His First Flight', the young seagull's mother tempted him with:

  • (a) a piece of fish
  • (b) a worm
  • (c) a leaf
  • (d) her call alone

4. [From the Diary of Anne Frank] The line 'paper has more patience than people' means that:

  • (a) paper lasts longer than people
  • (b) a diary will listen without judging or tiring
  • (c) people are impatient with writing
  • (d) diaries are more valuable than friends

5. [Glimpses of India] The traditional bakers of Goa are called:

  • (a) paders
  • (b) pao wallahs
  • (c) bakers
  • (d) lohars

6. [Mijbil the Otter] Mijbil was scientifically classified as:

  • (a) Lutrogale perspicillata maxwelli
  • (b) Lutra lutra
  • (c) Aonyx cinereus
  • (d) Enhydra lutris

7. [Madam Rides the Bus] Valli saved what amount for her bus journey?

  • (a) 30 paise
  • (b) 60 paise
  • (c) one rupee
  • (d) two rupees

8. [The Sermon at Benares] Kisa Gotami was asked to bring mustard seed from a house:

  • (a) where no one had died
  • (b) where a child had been born
  • (c) where a wedding was held
  • (d) that belonged to a king

9. [The Sermon at Benares] The Buddha delivered his first sermon at:

  • (a) Sarnath near Benares
  • (b) Kushinagar
  • (c) Lumbini
  • (d) Bodh Gaya

10. [The Proposal] The second quarrel in the play is about:

  • (a) the Oxen Meadows
  • (b) two hunting dogs
  • (c) money
  • (d) Natalya's age

11. [The Proposal] Lomov suffers from:

  • (a) asthma
  • (b) palpitations and a twitching eyebrow
  • (c) a limp
  • (d) poor eyesight

Section B · First Flight · Poems Q12–Q22 · 11 marks

12. [Dust of Snow] The bird in the poem is a:

  • (a) sparrow
  • (b) crow
  • (c) robin
  • (d) cuckoo

13. [Fire and Ice] In the poem, 'ice' symbolises:

  • (a) death
  • (b) hatred
  • (c) sorrow
  • (d) winter

14. [A Tiger in the Zoo] 'Quiet rage' is an example of:

  • (a) simile
  • (b) oxymoron
  • (c) metaphor
  • (d) alliteration

15. [How to Tell Wild Animals] According to the poem, the Asian lion is best known because:

  • (a) it roars while killing its victim
  • (b) it is striped
  • (c) it is spotted
  • (d) it lives in the hills

16. [The Ball Poem] ‘The Ball Poem’ is written in:

  • (a) free verse
  • (b) rhymed couplets
  • (c) sonnet form
  • (d) ballad form

17. [Amanda!] Amanda is told not to:

  • (a) bite her nails and hunch her shoulders
  • (b) read books
  • (c) go to school
  • (d) play outside

18. [The Trees] 'The Trees' is written by:

  • (a) Adrienne Rich
  • (b) Robin Klein
  • (c) Leslie Norris
  • (d) Carolyn Wells

19. [The Trees] The roots of the trees try to disengage themselves from:

  • (a) cracks in the veranda floor
  • (b) the forest soil
  • (c) flower pots
  • (d) the garden path

20. [Fog] The comparison of fog to a cat is an example of:

  • (a) simile
  • (b) metaphor
  • (c) hyperbole
  • (d) onomatopoeia

21. [The Tale of Custard the Dragon] Blink in the poem is:

  • (a) a little black kitten
  • (b) a little grey mouse
  • (c) a little yellow dog
  • (d) a little pet dragon

22. [For Anne Gregory] According to the poet, young men are thrown into despair by:

  • (a) Anne's honey-coloured hair
  • (b) her wealth
  • (c) her silence
  • (d) her voice

Section C · Footprints Without Feet Q23–Q32 · 10 marks

23. [A Triumph of Surgery] Tricki recovered fully in:

  • (a) a fortnight
  • (b) two days
  • (c) a month
  • (d) six months

24. [The Thief's Story] Anil was a:

  • (a) wrestler
  • (b) struggling writer
  • (c) moneylender
  • (d) shopkeeper

25. [The Thief's Story] Hari Singh says that he changes his name:

  • (a) every month to hide from the police and old employers
  • (b) every year
  • (c) never
  • (d) every week

26. [The Midnight Visitor] Ausable told Max that the balcony he saw belonged to:

  • (a) the next apartment
  • (b) Ausable's own room
  • (c) the hotel manager
  • (d) nobody

27. [A Question of Trust] The safe that Horace planned to rob was at:

  • (a) Shotover Grange
  • (b) Shotover House
  • (c) a London bank
  • (d) a jeweller's shop

28. [Footprints without Feet] The village constable who tried to arrest Griffin was:

  • (a) Mr Hall
  • (b) Mr Jaffers
  • (c) Mr Griffin
  • (d) Mr Kemp

29. [The Making of a Scientist] Ebright's first serious scientific work was on:

  • (a) monarch butterflies and a viral disease
  • (b) ants and their nests
  • (c) frog anatomy
  • (d) the stars

30. [The Necklace] The Loisels spent how many years repaying the debt?

  • (a) five
  • (b) ten
  • (c) twenty
  • (d) two

31. [Bholi] Bholi's real name is:

  • (a) Sulekha
  • (b) Bholi
  • (c) Mangla
  • (d) Radha

32. [The Book That Saved the Earth] The play is set in:

  • (a) the twenty-fifth century, the Era of the Book
  • (b) the twentieth century
  • (c) the twenty-first century
  • (d) ancient times

Section D · Reading, Grammar & Writing Q33–Q40 · 8 marks

33. [Reading: Unseen and Case-based Passages] Before writing the answers to an unseen passage you should:

  • (a) read the questions, then read the passage
  • (b) start writing immediately
  • (c) copy lines from the passage
  • (d) skip the passage

34. [Reading: Unseen and Case-based Passages] In a case-based factual passage, the visual input is usually:

  • (a) a chart, table or graph
  • (b) a photograph
  • (c) a map only
  • (d) a cartoon

35. [Grammar in Context] Report the sentence: He said, 'I am leaving tomorrow.'

  • (a) He said that he was leaving the next day.
  • (b) He said that he is leaving tomorrow.
  • (c) He said that he left tomorrow.
  • (d) He said that he will leave the next day.

36. [Grammar in Context] Fill in the blank: He asked me ____ I could help him.

  • (a) if
  • (b) that
  • (c) what
  • (d) to

37. [Formal Letter Writing] 'Yours faithfully' is used when the letter begins with:

  • (a) Dear Mr Sharma
  • (b) Sir or Madam
  • (c) Respected Principal
  • (d) My dear friend

38. [Formal Letter Writing] A letter to the editor begins with:

  • (a) the sender's address, date, and then ‘The Editor'
  • (b) the subject line
  • (c) ‘Dear Sir' alone
  • (d) a question

39. [Analytical Paragraph] An analytical paragraph must be based on:

  • (a) the data given in the chart or table
  • (b) personal opinion only
  • (c) imaginary facts
  • (d) a news report

40. [Analytical Paragraph] Figures in an analytical paragraph should be:

  • (a) compared with each other to show a trend
  • (b) listed one by one without comment
  • (c) invented to fill space
  • (d) rounded off to zero
