Section A · Mathematics Q1–Q24 · 96 marks

1. Which has a terminating decimal expansion?

  • (a) 7/24
  • (b) 5/13
  • (c) 9/40
  • (d) 11/21

2. Which of these is irrational?

  • (a) √(16/9)
  • (b) √12
  • (c) 0.444…
  • (d) 22/7

3. If α, β are zeroes of x² − 5x + 6, then α + β = ?

  • (a) 6
  • (b) 5
  • (c) −5
  • (d) 1

4. A quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 3 and −3 is:

  • (a) x² − 9
  • (b) x² + 9
  • (c) x² − 6x + 9
  • (d) x² − 3

5. For what value of k does kx + 3y = 7 and 2x + 6y = 14 have infinitely many solutions?

  • (a) 1
  • (b) −1
  • (c) 2
  • (d) 1/2

6. If x + 2y = 5 and 3x + ky = 15 are coincident lines, then k = :

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 3
  • (c) 6
  • (d) 15

7. For x² + 4x + k = 0 to have real roots, k must satisfy:

  • (a) k ≤ 4
  • (b) k ≥ 4
  • (c) k ≤ −4
  • (d) k = 4

8. If the roots of x² − 8x + k = 0 are equal, then k = :

  • (a) 8
  • (b) 16
  • (c) −16
  • (d) 64

9. The 10th term of the AP 2, 7, 12, … is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 47
  • (c) 50
  • (d) 52

10. S₅ − S₄ equals:

  • (a) the 5th term
  • (b) the 4th term
  • (c) the 9th term
  • (d) zero

11. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding sides are:

  • (a) equal
  • (b) proportional
  • (c) perpendicular
  • (d) parallel

12. In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC with AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 5 cm. Then EC = :

  • (a) 6.5 cm
  • (b) 7.5 cm
  • (c) 8 cm
  • (d) 9 cm

13. The distance of the point (3, 4) from the origin is:

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 7
  • (c) 25
  • (d) 12

14. The point (−3, 0) lies on:

  • (a) the x-axis
  • (b) the y-axis
  • (c) the line y = x
  • (d) the origin

15. If sin θ = cos θ, then θ = :

  • (a) 0°
  • (b) 30°
  • (c) 45°
  • (d) 90°

16. sec²A − tan²A equals:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) −1
  • (d) sin²A

17. From the top of a tower the angle of depression of an object on the ground is 30°. The angle of elevation of the top from that object is:

  • (a) 30°
  • (b) 45°
  • (c) 60°
  • (d) 90°

18. When the shadow of a pole equals its height, the sun’s angle of elevation is:

  • (a) 30°
  • (b) 45°
  • (c) 60°
  • (d) 90°

19. Two tangents from an external point to a circle are:

  • (a) unequal
  • (b) equal
  • (c) always perpendicular
  • (d) parallel

20. The area of a quadrant of a circle of radius r is:

  • (a) πr²/2
  • (b) πr²/4
  • (c) 2πr
  • (d) πr²

21. The volume of a sphere of radius r is:

  • (a) (4/3)πr³
  • (b) 4πr²
  • (c) (2/3)πr³
  • (d) πr³

22. The mode of 3, 5, 7, 5, 9, 5, 8 is:

  • (a) 3
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 9

23. Two dice are thrown together. The total number of outcomes is:

  • (a) 6
  • (b) 12
  • (c) 36
  • (d) 24

24. If P(E) = 0.37, then P(not E) = :

  • (a) 0.37
  • (b) 0.63
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 0.53

Section B · Physics Q25–Q37 · 52 marks

25. The power of a convex lens of focal length 50 cm is:

  • (a) +2 D
  • (b) −2 D
  • (c) +0.5 D
  • (d) −0.5 D

26. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?

  • (a) plane
  • (b) concave
  • (c) convex
  • (d) parabolic

27. An object is placed 20 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. The image is formed:

  • (a) 10 cm in front of the mirror
  • (b) 20 cm in front of the mirror
  • (c) 20 cm behind the mirror
  • (d) at infinity

28. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:

  • (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
  • (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
  • (c) concave, focal length 2 m
  • (d) convex, focal length 2 m

29. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:

  • (a) myopia
  • (b) hypermetropia
  • (c) astigmatism
  • (d) night blindness

30. Stars twinkle mainly because of:

  • (a) atmospheric refraction
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) reflection from clouds
  • (d) their own flickering

31. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:

  • (a) more resistance
  • (b) less resistance
  • (c) the same resistance
  • (d) no resistance

32. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:

  • (a) 3 kWh
  • (b) 30 kWh
  • (c) 300 kWh
  • (d) 3000 kWh

33. The resistance of a wire doubles when:

  • (a) its length is doubled
  • (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
  • (c) its length is halved
  • (d) its diameter is doubled

34. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:

  • (a) in series
  • (b) in parallel
  • (c) across the supply
  • (d) either way

35. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:

  • (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
  • (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
  • (c) the right-hand thumb rule
  • (d) Lenz's law

36. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:

  • (a) concentric circles
  • (b) parallel straight lines
  • (c) diverging curves
  • (d) absent

37. An electric motor converts:

  • (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
  • (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
  • (c) heat into light
  • (d) chemical energy into sound

Section C · Chemistry Q38–Q50 · 52 marks

38. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:

  • (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
  • (b) CuO and Ag2O
  • (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
  • (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

39. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of:

  • (a) NO₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) NO
  • (d) PbO

40. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 3
  • (c) 4
  • (d) 6

41. A shiny brown element X becomes black when heated in air. X and the black compound are:

  • (a) Cu and CuO
  • (b) Fe and Fe₂O₃
  • (c) Ag and Ag₂S
  • (d) Al and Al₂O₃

42. Bleaching powder is manufactured by passing chlorine gas over:

  • (a) dry slaked lime
  • (b) quicklime
  • (c) limestone
  • (d) gypsum

43. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:

  • (a) sodium chloride
  • (b) sodium carbonate
  • (c) calcium sulphate
  • (d) ammonium chloride

44. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:

  • (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
  • (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
  • (c) CaSO₄
  • (d) CaCO₃

45. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:

  • (a) MgCl
  • (b) MgCl₂
  • (c) Mg₂Cl
  • (d) MgCl₃

46. Which metal reacts vigorously with cold water?

  • (a) iron
  • (b) zinc
  • (c) sodium
  • (d) copper

47. The correct decreasing order of reactivity is:

  • (a) Zn > Mg > Cu
  • (b) Mg > Zn > Cu
  • (c) Cu > Zn > Mg
  • (d) Zn > Cu > Mg

48. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 6
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 8

49. Which compound decolorises bromine water?

  • (a) CH4
  • (b) C2H6
  • (c) C2H4
  • (d) C3H8

50. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)?

  • (a) C₂H₆
  • (b) C₂H₄
  • (c) CH₄
  • (d) C₃H₈
