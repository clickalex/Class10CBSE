SECTION A - Mathematics (Q1-Q24, 96 marks)
------------------------------------------

Q1. If the HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 60, one number being 12, the other is:
    (a) 20
    (b) 30
    (c) 40
    (d) 60

Q2. The decimal expansion of 23/(2³ × 5²) terminates after:
    (a) 2 places
    (b) 3 places
    (c) 4 places
    (d) 5 places

Q3. A polynomial of degree 3 is called:
    (a) linear
    (b) quadratic
    (c) cubic
    (d) biquadratic

Q4. If one zero of x² − 4x + 1 is 2 + √3, the other zero is:
    (a) 2 − √3
    (b) −2 − √3
    (c) √3 − 2
    (d) −√3

Q5. The pair x + y = 5 and 2x + 2y = 10 represents:
    (a) intersecting lines
    (b) parallel lines
    (c) coincident lines
    (d) none

Q6. The pair 6x − 3y + 10 = 0 and 2x − y + 9 = 0 has:
    (a) one solution
    (b) two solutions
    (c) infinitely many solutions
    (d) no solution

Q7. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are:
    (a) real and distinct
    (b) real and equal
    (c) not real
    (d) always integers

Q8. Which one is NOT a quadratic equation?
    (a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0
    (b) (x + 1)² = 2(x − 3)
    (c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0
    (d) 2x² = 8

Q9. The common difference of the AP whose nth term is 3n + 5 is:
    (a) 3
    (b) 5
    (c) 8
    (d) 2

Q10. The sum of the first 100 natural numbers is:
    (a) 5000
    (b) 5050
    (c) 4950
    (d) 5100

Q11. If the corresponding sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 4 : 9, their areas are in the ratio:
    (a) 4 : 9
    (b) 2 : 3
    (c) 16 : 81
    (d) 8 : 27

Q12. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:
    (a) a
    (b) a²
    (c) 1
    (d) 2a

Q13. The value of cos 60° is:
    (a) 1/2
    (b) √3/2
    (c) 1/√2
    (d) 1

Q14. A 10 m ladder reaches a window 8 m above the ground. Its foot is from the wall at a distance of:
    (a) 4 m
    (b) 6 m
    (c) 7 m
    (d) 9 m

Q15. The angle between a tangent and the radius at the point of contact is:
    (a) 45°
    (b) 60°
    (c) 90°
    (d) 180°

Q16. The number of tangents that can be drawn from a point outside a circle is:
    (a) 1
    (b) 2
    (c) 3
    (d) infinite

Q17. The area of a segment is found by:
    (a) sector − triangle
    (b) sector + triangle
    (c) circle − sector
    (d) triangle − sector

Q18. The area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm is:
    (a) 77 cm²
    (b) 154 cm²
    (c) 308 cm²
    (d) 616 cm²

Q19. The volume of a cone of radius r and height h is:
    (a) πr²h
    (b) πr²h/3
    (c) 2πr²h
    (d) πrh

Q20. When a solid is melted and recast into another shape, which quantity is conserved?
    (a) surface area
    (b) volume
    (c) both
    (d) neither

Q21. The class mark of the class 15–25 is:
    (a) 15
    (b) 20
    (c) 25
    (d) 10

Q22. The median class is the class whose cumulative frequency is:
    (a) equal to N/2
    (b) the first one greater than N/2
    (c) the largest
    (d) the smallest

Q23. The probability of an impossible event is:
    (a) 1
    (b) 0
    (c) 1/2
    (d) undefined

Q24. One card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck. The probability of a black queen is:
    (a) 1/52
    (b) 1/26
    (c) 1/13
    (d) 2/13


SECTION B - Physics (Q25-Q37, 52 marks)
---------------------------------------

Q25. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:
    (a) less than one
    (b) more than one
    (c) equal to one
    (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

Q26. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?
    (a) at F
    (b) at 2F
    (c) at infinity
    (d) between F and 2F

Q27. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) does not bend
    (d) is totally absorbed

Q28. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:
    (a) pupil
    (b) retina
    (c) ciliary muscles
    (d) iris

Q29. The danger signals installed at the top of tall buildings are red because red light:
    (a) is scattered the most by smoke or fog
    (b) is scattered the least by smoke or fog
    (c) is absorbed the most
    (d) moves fastest in air

Q30. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:
    (a) hypermetropia
    (b) myopia
    (c) presbyopia
    (d) cataract

Q31. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:
    (a) joule
    (b) watt
    (c) kilowatt-hour
    (d) volt

Q32. One kilowatt hour equals:
    (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
    (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
    (c) 360 J
    (d) 6 × 10µ J

Q33. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:
    (a) 24 Ω
    (b) 6 Ω
    (c) 0.17 Ω
    (d) 14 Ω

Q34. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is:
    (a) AC generator has an electromagnet
    (b) DC generator has a commutator
    (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator
    (d) AC generator produces more power

Q35. The frequency of AC supply in India is 50 Hz. The direction of the current changes:
    (a) 50 times per second
    (b) 100 times per second
    (c) 25 times per second
    (d) 200 times per second

Q36. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:
    (a) increases as you move away from the wire
    (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
    (c) does not depend on the current
    (d) is zero everywhere around the wire

Q37. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:
    (a) induced current in a coil
    (b) magnetic field due to a current
    (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
    (d) current produced by a generator


SECTION C - Chemistry (Q38-Q50, 52 marks)
-----------------------------------------

Q38. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:
    (a) combination
    (b) decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) double displacement

Q39. The reaction 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ used in black-and-white photography is a:
    (a) thermal decomposition
    (b) photochemical decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) combination reaction

Q40. Which of these is a combination reaction?
    (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
    (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
    (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
    (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

Q41. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains:
    (a) NaCl
    (b) HCl
    (c) LiCl
    (d) KCl

Q42. The pH of gastric juice released during digestion is:
    (a) less than 7
    (b) more than 7
    (c) equal to 7
    (d) equal to 0

Q43. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:
    (a) 2
    (b) 5
    (c) 7
    (d) 10

Q44. Baking soda is:
    (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
    (b) NaHCO₃
    (c) NaOH
    (d) Na₂CO₃

Q45. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:
    (a) less reactive than silver
    (b) more reactive than silver
    (c) equally reactive
    (d) an inert metal

Q46. Which pair is amphoteric?
    (a) Na2O and CaO
    (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
    (c) CO2 and SO2
    (d) CuO and FeO

Q47. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:
    (a) reduction with carbon
    (b) self reduction
    (c) electrolytic reduction
    (d) roasting

Q48. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?
    (a) C₃H₈
    (b) C₂H₆
    (c) C₄H₁₀
    (d) C₂H₄

Q49. Vinegar is a solution of:
    (a) 5–8% ethanoic acid in water
    (b) 50–80% ethanoic acid
    (c) pure ethanoic acid
    (d) ethanoic acid in alcohol

Q50. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?
    (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
    (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
    (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
    (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH
