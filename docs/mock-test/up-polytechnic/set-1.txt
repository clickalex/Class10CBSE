SECTION A - Mathematics (Q1-Q24, 96 marks)
------------------------------------------

Q1. Which has a terminating decimal expansion?
    (a) 7/24
    (b) 5/13
    (c) 9/40
    (d) 11/21

Q2. Which of these is irrational?
    (a) √(16/9)
    (b) √12
    (c) 0.444…
    (d) 22/7

Q3. If α, β are zeroes of x² − 5x + 6, then α + β = ?
    (a) 6
    (b) 5
    (c) −5
    (d) 1

Q4. A quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 3 and −3 is:
    (a) x² − 9
    (b) x² + 9
    (c) x² − 6x + 9
    (d) x² − 3

Q5. For what value of k does kx + 3y = 7 and 2x + 6y = 14 have infinitely many solutions?
    (a) 1
    (b) −1
    (c) 2
    (d) 1/2

Q6. If x + 2y = 5 and 3x + ky = 15 are coincident lines, then k = :
    (a) 2
    (b) 3
    (c) 6
    (d) 15

Q7. For x² + 4x + k = 0 to have real roots, k must satisfy:
    (a) k ≤ 4
    (b) k ≥ 4
    (c) k ≤ −4
    (d) k = 4

Q8. If the roots of x² − 8x + k = 0 are equal, then k = :
    (a) 8
    (b) 16
    (c) −16
    (d) 64

Q9. The 10th term of the AP 2, 7, 12, … is:
    (a) 45
    (b) 47
    (c) 50
    (d) 52

Q10. S₅ − S₄ equals:
    (a) the 5th term
    (b) the 4th term
    (c) the 9th term
    (d) zero

Q11. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding sides are:
    (a) equal
    (b) proportional
    (c) perpendicular
    (d) parallel

Q12. In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC with AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 5 cm. Then EC = :
    (a) 6.5 cm
    (b) 7.5 cm
    (c) 8 cm
    (d) 9 cm

Q13. The distance of the point (3, 4) from the origin is:
    (a) 5
    (b) 7
    (c) 25
    (d) 12

Q14. The point (−3, 0) lies on:
    (a) the x-axis
    (b) the y-axis
    (c) the line y = x
    (d) the origin

Q15. If sin θ = cos θ, then θ = :
    (a) 0°
    (b) 30°
    (c) 45°
    (d) 90°

Q16. sec²A − tan²A equals:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1
    (c) −1
    (d) sin²A

Q17. From the top of a tower the angle of depression of an object on the ground is 30°. The angle of elevation of the top from that object is:
    (a) 30°
    (b) 45°
    (c) 60°
    (d) 90°

Q18. When the shadow of a pole equals its height, the sun’s angle of elevation is:
    (a) 30°
    (b) 45°
    (c) 60°
    (d) 90°

Q19. Two tangents from an external point to a circle are:
    (a) unequal
    (b) equal
    (c) always perpendicular
    (d) parallel

Q20. The area of a quadrant of a circle of radius r is:
    (a) πr²/2
    (b) πr²/4
    (c) 2πr
    (d) πr²

Q21. The volume of a sphere of radius r is:
    (a) (4/3)πr³
    (b) 4πr²
    (c) (2/3)πr³
    (d) πr³

Q22. The mode of 3, 5, 7, 5, 9, 5, 8 is:
    (a) 3
    (b) 5
    (c) 7
    (d) 9

Q23. Two dice are thrown together. The total number of outcomes is:
    (a) 6
    (b) 12
    (c) 36
    (d) 24

Q24. If P(E) = 0.37, then P(not E) = :
    (a) 0.37
    (b) 0.63
    (c) 1
    (d) 0.53


SECTION B - Physics (Q25-Q37, 52 marks)
---------------------------------------

Q25. The power of a convex lens of focal length 50 cm is:
    (a) +2 D
    (b) −2 D
    (c) +0.5 D
    (d) −0.5 D

Q26. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?
    (a) plane
    (b) concave
    (c) convex
    (d) parabolic

Q27. An object is placed 20 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. The image is formed:
    (a) 10 cm in front of the mirror
    (b) 20 cm in front of the mirror
    (c) 20 cm behind the mirror
    (d) at infinity

Q28. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:
    (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
    (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
    (c) concave, focal length 2 m
    (d) convex, focal length 2 m

Q29. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:
    (a) myopia
    (b) hypermetropia
    (c) astigmatism
    (d) night blindness

Q30. Stars twinkle mainly because of:
    (a) atmospheric refraction
    (b) dispersion
    (c) reflection from clouds
    (d) their own flickering

Q31. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:
    (a) more resistance
    (b) less resistance
    (c) the same resistance
    (d) no resistance

Q32. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:
    (a) 3 kWh
    (b) 30 kWh
    (c) 300 kWh
    (d) 3000 kWh

Q33. The resistance of a wire doubles when:
    (a) its length is doubled
    (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
    (c) its length is halved
    (d) its diameter is doubled

Q34. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:
    (a) in series
    (b) in parallel
    (c) across the supply
    (d) either way

Q35. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:
    (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
    (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
    (c) the right-hand thumb rule
    (d) Lenz's law

Q36. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:
    (a) concentric circles
    (b) parallel straight lines
    (c) diverging curves
    (d) absent

Q37. An electric motor converts:
    (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
    (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
    (c) heat into light
    (d) chemical energy into sound


SECTION C - Chemistry (Q38-Q50, 52 marks)
-----------------------------------------

Q38. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:
    (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
    (b) CuO and Ag2O
    (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
    (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

Q39. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of:
    (a) NO₂
    (b) O₂
    (c) NO
    (d) PbO

Q40. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:
    (a) 2
    (b) 3
    (c) 4
    (d) 6

Q41. A shiny brown element X becomes black when heated in air. X and the black compound are:
    (a) Cu and CuO
    (b) Fe and Fe₂O₃
    (c) Ag and Ag₂S
    (d) Al and Al₂O₃

Q42. Bleaching powder is manufactured by passing chlorine gas over:
    (a) dry slaked lime
    (b) quicklime
    (c) limestone
    (d) gypsum

Q43. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:
    (a) sodium chloride
    (b) sodium carbonate
    (c) calcium sulphate
    (d) ammonium chloride

Q44. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:
    (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
    (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
    (c) CaSO₄
    (d) CaCO₃

Q45. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:
    (a) MgCl
    (b) MgCl₂
    (c) Mg₂Cl
    (d) MgCl₃

Q46. Which metal reacts vigorously with cold water?
    (a) iron
    (b) zinc
    (c) sodium
    (d) copper

Q47. The correct decreasing order of reactivity is:
    (a) Zn > Mg > Cu
    (b) Mg > Zn > Cu
    (c) Cu > Zn > Mg
    (d) Zn > Cu > Mg

Q48. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:
    (a) 5
    (b) 6
    (c) 7
    (d) 8

Q49. Which compound decolorises bromine water?
    (a) CH4
    (b) C2H6
    (c) C2H4
    (d) C3H8

Q50. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)?
    (a) C₂H₆
    (b) C₂H₄
    (c) CH₄
    (d) C₃H₈
