Section A · Mathematics Q1–Q24 · 96 marks

1. If the HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 60, one number being 12, the other is:

  • (a) 20
  • (b) 30
  • (c) 40
  • (d) 60

2. The decimal expansion of 23/(2³ × 5²) terminates after:

  • (a) 2 places
  • (b) 3 places
  • (c) 4 places
  • (d) 5 places

3. A polynomial of degree 3 is called:

  • (a) linear
  • (b) quadratic
  • (c) cubic
  • (d) biquadratic

4. If one zero of x² − 4x + 1 is 2 + √3, the other zero is:

  • (a) 2 − √3
  • (b) −2 − √3
  • (c) √3 − 2
  • (d) −√3

5. The pair x + y = 5 and 2x + 2y = 10 represents:

  • (a) intersecting lines
  • (b) parallel lines
  • (c) coincident lines
  • (d) none

6. The pair 6x − 3y + 10 = 0 and 2x − y + 9 = 0 has:

  • (a) one solution
  • (b) two solutions
  • (c) infinitely many solutions
  • (d) no solution

7. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are:

  • (a) real and distinct
  • (b) real and equal
  • (c) not real
  • (d) always integers

8. Which one is NOT a quadratic equation?

  • (a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0
  • (b) (x + 1)² = 2(x − 3)
  • (c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0
  • (d) 2x² = 8

9. The common difference of the AP whose nth term is 3n + 5 is:

  • (a) 3
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 8
  • (d) 2

10. The sum of the first 100 natural numbers is:

  • (a) 5000
  • (b) 5050
  • (c) 4950
  • (d) 5100

11. If the corresponding sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 4 : 9, their areas are in the ratio:

  • (a) 4 : 9
  • (b) 2 : 3
  • (c) 16 : 81
  • (d) 8 : 27

12. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:

  • (a) a
  • (b) a²
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 2a

13. The value of cos 60° is:

  • (a) 1/2
  • (b) √3/2
  • (c) 1/√2
  • (d) 1

14. A 10 m ladder reaches a window 8 m above the ground. Its foot is from the wall at a distance of:

  • (a) 4 m
  • (b) 6 m
  • (c) 7 m
  • (d) 9 m

15. The angle between a tangent and the radius at the point of contact is:

  • (a) 45°
  • (b) 60°
  • (c) 90°
  • (d) 180°

16. The number of tangents that can be drawn from a point outside a circle is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 3
  • (d) infinite

17. The area of a segment is found by:

  • (a) sector − triangle
  • (b) sector + triangle
  • (c) circle − sector
  • (d) triangle − sector

18. The area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm is:

  • (a) 77 cm²
  • (b) 154 cm²
  • (c) 308 cm²
  • (d) 616 cm²

19. The volume of a cone of radius r and height h is:

  • (a) πr²h
  • (b) πr²h/3
  • (c) 2πr²h
  • (d) πrh

20. When a solid is melted and recast into another shape, which quantity is conserved?

  • (a) surface area
  • (b) volume
  • (c) both
  • (d) neither

21. The class mark of the class 15–25 is:

  • (a) 15
  • (b) 20
  • (c) 25
  • (d) 10

22. The median class is the class whose cumulative frequency is:

  • (a) equal to N/2
  • (b) the first one greater than N/2
  • (c) the largest
  • (d) the smallest

23. The probability of an impossible event is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 0
  • (c) 1/2
  • (d) undefined

24. One card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck. The probability of a black queen is:

  • (a) 1/52
  • (b) 1/26
  • (c) 1/13
  • (d) 2/13

Section B · Physics Q25–Q37 · 52 marks

25. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:

  • (a) less than one
  • (b) more than one
  • (c) equal to one
  • (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

26. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?

  • (a) at F
  • (b) at 2F
  • (c) at infinity
  • (d) between F and 2F

27. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:

  • (a) bends towards the normal
  • (b) bends away from the normal
  • (c) does not bend
  • (d) is totally absorbed

28. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:

  • (a) pupil
  • (b) retina
  • (c) ciliary muscles
  • (d) iris

29. The danger signals installed at the top of tall buildings are red because red light:

  • (a) is scattered the most by smoke or fog
  • (b) is scattered the least by smoke or fog
  • (c) is absorbed the most
  • (d) moves fastest in air

30. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:

  • (a) hypermetropia
  • (b) myopia
  • (c) presbyopia
  • (d) cataract

31. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:

  • (a) joule
  • (b) watt
  • (c) kilowatt-hour
  • (d) volt

32. One kilowatt hour equals:

  • (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
  • (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
  • (c) 360 J
  • (d) 6 × 10µ J

33. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:

  • (a) 24 Ω
  • (b) 6 Ω
  • (c) 0.17 Ω
  • (d) 14 Ω

34. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is:

  • (a) AC generator has an electromagnet
  • (b) DC generator has a commutator
  • (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator
  • (d) AC generator produces more power

35. The frequency of AC supply in India is 50 Hz. The direction of the current changes:

  • (a) 50 times per second
  • (b) 100 times per second
  • (c) 25 times per second
  • (d) 200 times per second

36. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:

  • (a) increases as you move away from the wire
  • (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
  • (c) does not depend on the current
  • (d) is zero everywhere around the wire

37. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:

  • (a) induced current in a coil
  • (b) magnetic field due to a current
  • (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
  • (d) current produced by a generator

Section C · Chemistry Q38–Q50 · 52 marks

38. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:

  • (a) combination
  • (b) decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) double displacement

39. The reaction 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ used in black-and-white photography is a:

  • (a) thermal decomposition
  • (b) photochemical decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) combination reaction

40. Which of these is a combination reaction?

  • (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
  • (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
  • (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
  • (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

41. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains:

  • (a) NaCl
  • (b) HCl
  • (c) LiCl
  • (d) KCl

42. The pH of gastric juice released during digestion is:

  • (a) less than 7
  • (b) more than 7
  • (c) equal to 7
  • (d) equal to 0

43. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 10

44. Baking soda is:

  • (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
  • (b) NaHCO₃
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Na₂CO₃

45. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:

  • (a) less reactive than silver
  • (b) more reactive than silver
  • (c) equally reactive
  • (d) an inert metal

46. Which pair is amphoteric?

  • (a) Na2O and CaO
  • (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
  • (c) CO2 and SO2
  • (d) CuO and FeO

47. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:

  • (a) reduction with carbon
  • (b) self reduction
  • (c) electrolytic reduction
  • (d) roasting

48. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?

  • (a) C₃H₈
  • (b) C₂H₆
  • (c) C₄H₁₀
  • (d) C₂H₄

49. Vinegar is a solution of:

  • (a) 5–8% ethanoic acid in water
  • (b) 50–80% ethanoic acid
  • (c) pure ethanoic acid
  • (d) ethanoic acid in alcohol

50. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?

  • (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
  • (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
  • (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
  • (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH
