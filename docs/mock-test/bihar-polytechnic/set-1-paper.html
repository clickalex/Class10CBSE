Section A · Physics Q1–Q15 · 75 marks

1. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?

  • (a) at F
  • (b) at 2F
  • (c) at infinity
  • (d) between F and 2F

2. The image formed by a concave lens of a real object is always:

  • (a) real and inverted
  • (b) virtual, erect and diminished
  • (c) virtual, erect and magnified
  • (d) real and magnified

3. The power of a convex lens of focal length 50 cm is:

  • (a) +2 D
  • (b) −2 D
  • (c) +0.5 D
  • (d) −0.5 D

4. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?

  • (a) plane
  • (b) concave
  • (c) convex
  • (d) parabolic

5. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:

  • (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
  • (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
  • (c) concave, focal length 2 m
  • (d) convex, focal length 2 m

6. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:

  • (a) myopia
  • (b) hypermetropia
  • (c) astigmatism
  • (d) night blindness

7. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:

  • (a) hypermetropia
  • (b) myopia
  • (c) presbyopia
  • (d) cataract

8. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:

  • (a) scattering
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) total internal reflection
  • (d) polarisation

9. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:

  • (a) more resistance
  • (b) less resistance
  • (c) the same resistance
  • (d) no resistance

10. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:

  • (a) joule
  • (b) watt
  • (c) kilowatt-hour
  • (d) volt

11. The resistance of a wire doubles when:

  • (a) its length is doubled
  • (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
  • (c) its length is halved
  • (d) its diameter is doubled

12. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:

  • (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
  • (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
  • (c) the right-hand thumb rule
  • (d) Lenz's law

13. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is:

  • (a) AC generator has an electromagnet
  • (b) DC generator has a commutator
  • (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator
  • (d) AC generator produces more power

14. The production of a current in a closed coil by a changing magnetic field is called:

  • (a) electromagnetic induction
  • (b) magnetic effect of current
  • (c) electrolysis
  • (d) electrostatic induction

15. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:

  • (a) concentric circles
  • (b) parallel straight lines
  • (c) diverging curves
  • (d) absent

Section B · Chemistry Q16–Q30 · 75 marks

16. Which of the following is an endothermic process?

  • (a) respiration
  • (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
  • (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
  • (d) quicklime + water

17. Which of these is a combination reaction?

  • (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
  • (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
  • (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
  • (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

18. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by:

  • (a) storing it in the open
  • (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
  • (c) adding oxidising agents
  • (d) heating it repeatedly

19. A shiny brown element X becomes black when heated in air. X and the black compound are:

  • (a) Cu and CuO
  • (b) Fe and Fe₂O₃
  • (c) Ag and Ag₂S
  • (d) Al and Al₂O₃

20. Bleaching powder is manufactured by passing chlorine gas over:

  • (a) dry slaked lime
  • (b) quicklime
  • (c) limestone
  • (d) gypsum

21. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:

  • (a) sodium chloride
  • (b) sodium carbonate
  • (c) calcium sulphate
  • (d) ammonium chloride

22. Baking soda is:

  • (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
  • (b) NaHCO₃
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Na₂CO₃

23. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:

  • (a) less reactive than silver
  • (b) more reactive than silver
  • (c) equally reactive
  • (d) an inert metal

24. Which pair is amphoteric?

  • (a) Na2O and CaO
  • (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
  • (c) CO2 and SO2
  • (d) CuO and FeO

25. The best conductor of electricity among these is:

  • (a) copper
  • (b) silver
  • (c) aluminium
  • (d) iron

26. Which metal reacts vigorously with cold water?

  • (a) iron
  • (b) zinc
  • (c) sodium
  • (d) copper

27. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?

  • (a) C₃H₈
  • (b) C₂H₆
  • (c) C₄H₁₀
  • (d) C₂H₄

28. Vinegar is a solution of:

  • (a) 5–8% ethanoic acid in water
  • (b) 50–80% ethanoic acid
  • (c) pure ethanoic acid
  • (d) ethanoic acid in alcohol

29. The functional group present in an alcohol is:

  • (a) –CHO
  • (b) –COOH
  • (c) –OH
  • (d) –CO–

30. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:

  • (a) H₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) CO₂
  • (d) CO

Section C · Mathematics Q31–Q45 · 75 marks

31. The decimal expansion of 23/(2³ × 5²) terminates after:

  • (a) 2 places
  • (b) 3 places
  • (c) 4 places
  • (d) 5 places

32. If α, β are zeroes of x² − 5x + 6, then α + β = ?

  • (a) 6
  • (b) 5
  • (c) −5
  • (d) 1

33. If a boat’s downstream speed is 12 km/h and upstream speed is 8 km/h, its still-water speed is:

  • (a) 10
  • (b) 4
  • (c) 2
  • (d) 20 km/h

34. For x² + 4x + k = 0 to have real roots, k must satisfy:

  • (a) k ≤ 4
  • (b) k ≥ 4
  • (c) k ≤ −4
  • (d) k = 4

35. S₅ − S₄ equals:

  • (a) the 5th term
  • (b) the 4th term
  • (c) the 9th term
  • (d) zero

36. In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC with AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 5 cm. Then EC = :

  • (a) 6.5 cm
  • (b) 7.5 cm
  • (c) 8 cm
  • (d) 9 cm

37. Three points are collinear if the area of the triangle they form is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) maximum
  • (c) 0
  • (d) negative

38. sec²A − tan²A equals:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) −1
  • (d) sin²A

39. From the top of a tower the angle of depression of an object on the ground is 30°. The angle of elevation of the top from that object is:

  • (a) 30°
  • (b) 45°
  • (c) 60°
  • (d) 90°

40. The angle between a tangent and the radius at the point of contact is:

  • (a) 45°
  • (b) 60°
  • (c) 90°
  • (d) 180°

41. A tangent of length 24 cm is drawn from a point 25 cm from the centre. The radius is:

  • (a) 5 cm
  • (b) 7 cm
  • (c) 12 cm
  • (d) 24 cm

42. The area of a segment is found by:

  • (a) sector − triangle
  • (b) sector + triangle
  • (c) circle − sector
  • (d) triangle − sector

43. The volume of a sphere of radius r is:

  • (a) (4/3)πr³
  • (b) 4πr²
  • (c) (2/3)πr³
  • (d) πr³

44. The empirical relation connecting the three measures of central tendency is:

  • (a) Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean
  • (b) Median = 3 Mode − 2 Mean
  • (c) Mean = 3 Median − 2 Mode
  • (d) Mode = 2 Median − Mean

45. The probability of an impossible event is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 0
  • (c) 1/2
  • (d) undefined
