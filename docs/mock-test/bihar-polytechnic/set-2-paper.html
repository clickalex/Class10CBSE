Section A · Physics Q1–Q15 · 75 marks

1. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:

  • (a) less than one
  • (b) more than one
  • (c) equal to one
  • (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

2. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:

  • (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

3. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:

  • (a) bends towards the normal
  • (b) bends away from the normal
  • (c) does not bend
  • (d) is totally absorbed

4. A ray of light enters a denser medium along the normal (angle of incidence 0°). It:

  • (a) bends towards the normal
  • (b) bends away from the normal
  • (c) passes undeviated
  • (d) is totally reflected

5. The danger signals installed at the top of tall buildings are red because red light:

  • (a) is scattered the most by smoke or fog
  • (b) is scattered the least by smoke or fog
  • (c) is absorbed the most
  • (d) moves fastest in air

6. Stars twinkle mainly because of:

  • (a) atmospheric refraction
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) reflection from clouds
  • (d) their own flickering

7. The clear sky appears blue because:

  • (a) blue light is absorbed the most
  • (b) shorter wavelengths are scattered more by air molecules
  • (c) the sea reflects blue light upwards
  • (d) the atmosphere refracts blue light towards the eye

8. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:

  • (a) more than 100 Ω
  • (b) less than 1 Ω
  • (c) 107 Ω
  • (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

9. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:

  • (a) 3 kWh
  • (b) 30 kWh
  • (c) 300 kWh
  • (d) 3000 kWh

10. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:

  • (a) in series
  • (b) in parallel
  • (c) across the supply
  • (d) either way

11. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:

  • (a) 24 Ω
  • (b) 6 Ω
  • (c) 0.17 Ω
  • (d) 14 Ω

12. The frequency of AC supply in India is 50 Hz. The direction of the current changes:

  • (a) 50 times per second
  • (b) 100 times per second
  • (c) 25 times per second
  • (d) 200 times per second

13. An electric motor converts:

  • (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
  • (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
  • (c) heat into light
  • (d) chemical energy into sound

14. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:

  • (a) increases as you move away from the wire
  • (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
  • (c) does not depend on the current
  • (d) is zero everywhere around the wire

15. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:

  • (a) induced current in a coil
  • (b) magnetic field due to a current
  • (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
  • (d) current produced by a generator

Section B · Chemistry Q16–Q30 · 75 marks

16. The reaction 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ used in black-and-white photography is a:

  • (a) thermal decomposition
  • (b) photochemical decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) combination reaction

17. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:

  • (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
  • (b) CuO and Ag2O
  • (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
  • (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

18. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of:

  • (a) NO₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) NO
  • (d) PbO

19. Which one is a strong base?

  • (a) NH4OH
  • (b) Mg(OH)2
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Cu(OH)2

20. The pH of gastric juice released during digestion is:

  • (a) less than 7
  • (b) more than 7
  • (c) equal to 7
  • (d) equal to 0

21. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 10

22. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:

  • (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
  • (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
  • (c) CaSO₄
  • (d) CaCO₃

23. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:

  • (a) MgCl
  • (b) MgCl₂
  • (c) Mg₂Cl
  • (d) MgCl₃

24. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:

  • (a) reduction with carbon
  • (b) self reduction
  • (c) electrolytic reduction
  • (d) roasting

25. The correct decreasing order of reactivity is:

  • (a) Zn > Mg > Cu
  • (b) Mg > Zn > Cu
  • (c) Cu > Zn > Mg
  • (d) Zn > Cu > Mg

26. Galvanisation protects iron from rusting by coating it with a thin layer of:

  • (a) tin
  • (b) chromium
  • (c) zinc
  • (d) copper

27. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 6
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 8

28. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)?

  • (a) C₂H₆
  • (b) C₂H₄
  • (c) CH₄
  • (d) C₃H₈

29. The IUPAC name of CH₃–CH₂–CHO is:

  • (a) propanol
  • (b) propanal
  • (c) propanone
  • (d) propanoic acid

30. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?

  • (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
  • (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
  • (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
  • (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH

Section C · Mathematics Q31–Q45 · 75 marks

31. Which has a terminating decimal expansion?

  • (a) 7/24
  • (b) 5/13
  • (c) 9/40
  • (d) 11/21

32. A polynomial of degree 3 is called:

  • (a) linear
  • (b) quadratic
  • (c) cubic
  • (d) biquadratic

33. The pair 6x − 3y + 10 = 0 and 2x − y + 9 = 0 has:

  • (a) one solution
  • (b) two solutions
  • (c) infinitely many solutions
  • (d) no solution

34. If the roots of x² − 8x + k = 0 are equal, then k = :

  • (a) 8
  • (b) 16
  • (c) −16
  • (d) 64

35. The sum of the first 10 natural numbers is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 50
  • (c) 55
  • (d) 100

36. The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is 25:49. The ratio of their corresponding sides is:

  • (a) 25:49
  • (b) 5:7
  • (c) 7:5
  • (d) 49:25

37. The midpoint of (2, −3) and (−6, 5) is:

  • (a) (−2, 1)
  • (b) (2, −1)
  • (c) (−4, 2)
  • (d) (4, −8)

38. tan 45° × cot 45° is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) 2
  • (d) undefined

39. The angle of depression is always measured from:

  • (a) the vertical
  • (b) the horizontal
  • (c) the object
  • (d) the ground below

40. A line that cuts a circle in two distinct points is called a:

  • (a) tangent
  • (b) secant
  • (c) chord
  • (d) diameter

41. If the radius of a circle is doubled, its area becomes:

  • (a) 2 times
  • (b) 4 times
  • (c) 8 times
  • (d) unchanged

42. The area of a semicircle of radius r is:

  • (a) πr²
  • (b) πr²/2
  • (c) 2πr
  • (d) πr²/4

43. A cone, a hemisphere and a cylinder have equal bases and equal heights. The ratio of their volumes is:

  • (a) 1 : 2 : 3
  • (b) 2 : 3 : 1
  • (c) 3 : 1 : 2
  • (d) 1 : 3 : 2

44. The mode of 3, 5, 7, 5, 9, 5, 8 is:

  • (a) 3
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 9

45. One card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck. The probability of a black queen is:

  • (a) 1/52
  • (b) 1/26
  • (c) 1/13
  • (d) 2/13
