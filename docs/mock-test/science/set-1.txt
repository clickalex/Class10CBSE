SECTION A - Chemical Substances (Q1-Q10, 10 marks)
--------------------------------------------------

Q1. Which of these is a combination reaction?
    (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
    (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
    (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
    (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

Q2. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by:
    (a) storing it in the open
    (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
    (c) adding oxidising agents
    (d) heating it repeatedly

Q3. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:
    (a) 2
    (b) 3
    (c) 4
    (d) 6

Q4. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains:
    (a) NaCl
    (b) HCl
    (c) LiCl
    (d) KCl

Q5. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:
    (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
    (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
    (c) CaSO₄
    (d) CaCO₃

Q6. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:
    (a) reduction with carbon
    (b) self reduction
    (c) electrolytic reduction
    (d) roasting

Q7. Brass is an alloy of:
    (a) copper and tin
    (b) copper and zinc
    (c) lead and tin
    (d) mercury and zinc

Q8. Which compound decolorises bromine water?
    (a) CH4
    (b) C2H6
    (c) C2H4
    (d) C3H8

Q9. Vinegar is a solution of:
    (a) 5–8% ethanoic acid in water
    (b) 50–80% ethanoic acid
    (c) pure ethanoic acid
    (d) ethanoic acid in alcohol

Q10. The functional group present in an alcohol is:
    (a) –CHO
    (b) –COOH
    (c) –OH
    (d) –CO–


SECTION B - World of Living (Q11-Q18, 8 marks)
----------------------------------------------

Q11. Photosynthesis in a leaf takes place inside the:
    (a) mitochondria
    (b) chloroplast
    (c) ribosome
    (d) vacuole

Q12. The main nitrogenous waste filtered out by the kidneys is:
    (a) carbon dioxide
    (b) urea
    (c) glucose
    (d) glycogen

Q13. Which plant hormone is a growth inhibitor?
    (a) auxin
    (b) gibberellin
    (c) cytokinin
    (d) abscisic acid

Q14. The part of a neuron that receives the impulse is the:
    (a) axon
    (b) dendrite
    (c) cell body
    (d) myelin sheath

Q15. Binary fission in amoeba begins with the division of the:
    (a) cytoplasm
    (b) nucleus
    (c) cell membrane
    (d) pseudopodia

Q16. Which of the following is a barrier method of contraception?
    (a) oral pill
    (b) copper-T
    (c) condom
    (d) vasectomy

Q17. In a monohybrid cross between a tall (TT) and a dwarf (tt) pea plant, the F2 phenotypic ratio is:
    (a) 1:1
    (b) 3:1
    (c) 9:3:3:1
    (d) 1:2:1

Q18. The sex of a human child is determined by:
    (a) the mother's egg
    (b) the father's sperm
    (c) both parents equally
    (d) the diet of the mother


SECTION C - Natural Phenomena (Q19-Q23, 5 marks)
------------------------------------------------

Q19. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:
    (a) less than one
    (b) more than one
    (c) equal to one
    (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

Q20. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?
    (a) plane
    (b) concave
    (c) convex
    (d) parabolic

Q21. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:
    (a) pupil
    (b) retina
    (c) ciliary muscles
    (d) iris

Q22. The danger signals installed at the top of tall buildings are red because red light:
    (a) is scattered the most by smoke or fog
    (b) is scattered the least by smoke or fog
    (c) is absorbed the most
    (d) moves fastest in air

Q23. The clear sky appears blue because:
    (a) blue light is absorbed the most
    (b) shorter wavelengths are scattered more by air molecules
    (c) the sea reflects blue light upwards
    (d) the atmosphere refracts blue light towards the eye


SECTION D - Effects of Current (Q24-Q28, 5 marks)
-------------------------------------------------

Q24. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:
    (a) 3 kWh
    (b) 30 kWh
    (c) 300 kWh
    (d) 3000 kWh

Q25. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:
    (a) joule
    (b) watt
    (c) kilowatt-hour
    (d) volt

Q26. One kilowatt hour equals:
    (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
    (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
    (c) 360 J
    (d) 6 × 10µ J

Q27. The frequency of AC supply in India is 50 Hz. The direction of the current changes:
    (a) 50 times per second
    (b) 100 times per second
    (c) 25 times per second
    (d) 200 times per second

Q28. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:
    (a) increases as you move away from the wire
    (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
    (c) does not depend on the current
    (d) is zero everywhere around the wire
