SECTION A - Chemical Substances (Q1-Q10, 10 marks)
--------------------------------------------------

Q1. Which of the following is an endothermic process?
    (a) respiration
    (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
    (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
    (d) quicklime + water

Q2. The reaction 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ used in black-and-white photography is a:
    (a) thermal decomposition
    (b) photochemical decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) combination reaction

Q3. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:
    (a) 2
    (b) 5
    (c) 7
    (d) 10

Q4. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:
    (a) sodium chloride
    (b) sodium carbonate
    (c) calcium sulphate
    (d) ammonium chloride

Q5. Baking soda is:
    (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
    (b) NaHCO₃
    (c) NaOH
    (d) Na₂CO₃

Q6. Which pair is amphoteric?
    (a) Na2O and CaO
    (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
    (c) CO2 and SO2
    (d) CuO and FeO

Q7. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:
    (a) MgCl
    (b) MgCl₂
    (c) Mg₂Cl
    (d) MgCl₃

Q8. The best conductor of electricity among these is:
    (a) copper
    (b) silver
    (c) aluminium
    (d) iron

Q9. The IUPAC name of CH₃–CH₂–CHO is:
    (a) propanol
    (b) propanal
    (c) propanone
    (d) propanoic acid

Q10. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?
    (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
    (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
    (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
    (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH


SECTION B - World of Living (Q11-Q18, 8 marks)
----------------------------------------------

Q11. The breakdown of pyruvate to give CO2, water and energy takes place in the:
    (a) cytoplasm
    (b) mitochondria
    (c) chloroplast
    (d) nucleus

Q12. Which of these is an autotroph?
    (a) mushroom
    (b) Cuscuta
    (c) green plant
    (d) human being

Q13. The gap between two neurons is called:
    (a) dendrite
    (b) synapse
    (c) axon
    (d) impulse

Q14. The hormone that regulates the body's metabolic rate is:
    (a) insulin
    (b) thyroxine
    (c) growth hormone
    (d) adrenaline

Q15. The anther contains:
    (a) sepals
    (b) ovules
    (c) carpel
    (d) pollen grains

Q16. Sterilisation in human beings means:
    (a) preventing ovulation with pills
    (b) surgically blocking the gamete ducts
    (c) killing germs
    (d) using a condom

Q17. A cross between F1 hybrid tall plants (Tt × Tt) gives dwarf plants in F2. The expected ratio of tall to dwarf is:
    (a) 1:3
    (b) 3:1
    (c) 1:1
    (d) 2:2

Q18. The phenotypic ratio obtained in a dihybrid cross between two F₁ hybrids is:
    (a) 3 : 1
    (b) 9 : 3 : 3 : 1
    (c) 1 : 1
    (d) 1 : 2 : 1


SECTION C - Natural Phenomena (Q19-Q23, 5 marks)
------------------------------------------------

Q19. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?
    (a) at F
    (b) at 2F
    (c) at infinity
    (d) between F and 2F

Q20. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:
    (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
    (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
    (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
    (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

Q21. An object is placed 20 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. The image is formed:
    (a) 10 cm in front of the mirror
    (b) 20 cm in front of the mirror
    (c) 20 cm behind the mirror
    (d) at infinity

Q22. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:
    (a) myopia
    (b) hypermetropia
    (c) astigmatism
    (d) night blindness

Q23. Stars twinkle mainly because of:
    (a) atmospheric refraction
    (b) dispersion
    (c) reflection from clouds
    (d) their own flickering


SECTION D - Effects of Current (Q24-Q28, 5 marks)
-------------------------------------------------

    (a) 24 Ω
    (b) 6 Ω
    (c) 0.17 Ω
    (d) 14 Ω

Q25. Three 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is:
    (a) 18 Ω
    (b) 6 Ω
    (c) 2 Ω
    (d) 3 Ω

Q26. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:
    (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
    (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
    (c) the right-hand thumb rule
    (d) Lenz's law

Q27. An electric motor converts:
    (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
    (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
    (c) heat into light
    (d) chemical energy into sound

Q28. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:
    (a) induced current in a coil
    (b) magnetic field due to a current
    (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
