SECTION A - Chemical Substances (Q1-Q10, 10 marks)
--------------------------------------------------

Q1. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:
    (a) combination
    (b) decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) double displacement

Q2. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of:
    (a) NO₂
    (b) O₂
    (c) NO
    (d) PbO

Q3. A shiny brown element X becomes black when heated in air. X and the black compound are:
    (a) Cu and CuO
    (b) Fe and Fe₂O₃
    (c) Ag and Ag₂S
    (d) Al and Al₂O₃

Q4. Which one is a strong base?
    (a) NH4OH
    (b) Mg(OH)2
    (c) NaOH
    (d) Cu(OH)2

Q5. The pH of gastric juice released during digestion is:
    (a) less than 7
    (b) more than 7
    (c) equal to 7
    (d) equal to 0

Q6. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:
    (a) less reactive than silver
    (b) more reactive than silver
    (c) equally reactive
    (d) an inert metal

Q7. Galvanisation protects iron from rusting by coating it with a thin layer of:
    (a) tin
    (b) chromium
    (c) zinc
    (d) copper

Q8. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:
    (a) 5
    (b) 6
    (c) 7
    (d) 8

Q9. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?
    (a) C₃H₈
    (b) C₂H₆
    (c) C₄H₁₀
    (d) C₂H₄

Q10. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)?
    (a) C₂H₆
    (b) C₂H₄
    (c) CH₄
    (d) C₃H₈


SECTION B - World of Living (Q11-Q18, 8 marks)
----------------------------------------------

Q11. The kidneys in human beings are a part of the system for:
    (a) nutrition
    (b) respiration
    (c) excretion
    (d) transportation

Q12. Which of the following is not a function of the stomach?
    (a) killing bacteria
    (b) digestion of protein
    (c) absorption of digested food
    (d) protection of the stomach wall by mucus

Q13. The part of the brain that maintains the posture and balance of the body is:
    (a) cerebrum
    (b) cerebellum
    (c) pons
    (d) medulla

Q14. In a reflex action the impulse is processed in the:
    (a) cerebrum
    (b) spinal cord
    (c) cerebellum
    (d) medulla only

Q15. Which of these is an asexual mode of reproduction?
    (a) budding in yeast
    (b) pollination
    (c) fertilisation
    (d) seed germination

Q16. Sperms are produced in the:
    (a) penis
    (b) testes
    (c) vas deferens
    (d) seminal vesicles

Q17. Which of these traits can be inherited?
    (a) the cut tail of a mouse
    (b) large muscles built by exercise
    (c) eye colour
    (d) a scar

Q18. The number of chromosomes in a human zygote is:
    (a) 23
    (b) 46
    (c) 92
    (d) 22


SECTION C - Natural Phenomena (Q19-Q23, 5 marks)
------------------------------------------------

Q19. The image formed by a concave lens of a real object is always:
    (a) real and inverted
    (b) virtual, erect and diminished
    (c) virtual, erect and magnified
    (d) real and magnified

Q20. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) does not bend
    (d) is totally absorbed

Q21. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:
    (a) hypermetropia
    (b) myopia
    (c) presbyopia
    (d) cataract

Q22. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:
    (a) scattering
    (b) dispersion
    (c) total internal reflection
    (d) polarisation

Q23. The least distance of distinct vision for a young adult with normal vision is about:
    (a) 2.5 cm
    (b) 25 cm
    (c) 25 m
    (d) infinity


SECTION D - Effects of Current (Q24-Q28, 5 marks)
-------------------------------------------------

    (a) more than 100 Ω
    (b) less than 1 Ω
    (c) 107 Ω
    (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

Q25. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:
    (a) more resistance
    (b) less resistance
    (c) the same resistance
    (d) no resistance

Q26. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:
    (a) in series
    (b) in parallel
    (c) across the supply
    (d) either way

Q27. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:
    (a) concentric circles
    (b) parallel straight lines
    (c) diverging curves
    (d) absent

Q28. The insulation on the earth wire in a domestic circuit is:
    (a) red
    (b) black
    (c) green
    (d) blue
