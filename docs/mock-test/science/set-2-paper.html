Section A · Chemical Substances Q1–Q10 · 10 marks

1. Which of the following is an endothermic process?

  • (a) respiration
  • (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
  • (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
  • (d) quicklime + water

2. The reaction 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ used in black-and-white photography is a:

  • (a) thermal decomposition
  • (b) photochemical decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) combination reaction

3. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 10

4. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:

  • (a) sodium chloride
  • (b) sodium carbonate
  • (c) calcium sulphate
  • (d) ammonium chloride

5. Baking soda is:

  • (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
  • (b) NaHCO₃
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Na₂CO₃

6. Which pair is amphoteric?

  • (a) Na2O and CaO
  • (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
  • (c) CO2 and SO2
  • (d) CuO and FeO

7. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:

  • (a) MgCl
  • (b) MgCl₂
  • (c) Mg₂Cl
  • (d) MgCl₃

8. The best conductor of electricity among these is:

  • (a) copper
  • (b) silver
  • (c) aluminium
  • (d) iron

9. The IUPAC name of CH₃–CH₂–CHO is:

  • (a) propanol
  • (b) propanal
  • (c) propanone
  • (d) propanoic acid

10. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?

  • (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
  • (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
  • (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
  • (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH

Section B · World of Living Q11–Q18 · 8 marks

11. The breakdown of pyruvate to give CO2, water and energy takes place in the:

  • (a) cytoplasm
  • (b) mitochondria
  • (c) chloroplast
  • (d) nucleus

12. Which of these is an autotroph?

  • (a) mushroom
  • (b) Cuscuta
  • (c) green plant
  • (d) human being

13. The gap between two neurons is called:

  • (a) dendrite
  • (b) synapse
  • (c) axon
  • (d) impulse

14. The hormone that regulates the body's metabolic rate is:

  • (a) insulin
  • (b) thyroxine
  • (c) growth hormone
  • (d) adrenaline

15. The anther contains:

  • (a) sepals
  • (b) ovules
  • (c) carpel
  • (d) pollen grains

16. Sterilisation in human beings means:

  • (a) preventing ovulation with pills
  • (b) surgically blocking the gamete ducts
  • (c) killing germs
  • (d) using a condom

17. A cross between F1 hybrid tall plants (Tt × Tt) gives dwarf plants in F2. The expected ratio of tall to dwarf is:

  • (a) 1:3
  • (b) 3:1
  • (c) 1:1
  • (d) 2:2

18. The phenotypic ratio obtained in a dihybrid cross between two F₁ hybrids is:

  • (a) 3 : 1
  • (b) 9 : 3 : 3 : 1
  • (c) 1 : 1
  • (d) 1 : 2 : 1

Section C · Natural Phenomena Q19–Q23 · 5 marks

19. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?

  • (a) at F
  • (b) at 2F
  • (c) at infinity
  • (d) between F and 2F

20. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:

  • (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

21. An object is placed 20 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. The image is formed:

  • (a) 10 cm in front of the mirror
  • (b) 20 cm in front of the mirror
  • (c) 20 cm behind the mirror
  • (d) at infinity

22. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:

  • (a) myopia
  • (b) hypermetropia
  • (c) astigmatism
  • (d) night blindness

23. Stars twinkle mainly because of:

  • (a) atmospheric refraction
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) reflection from clouds
  • (d) their own flickering

Section D · Effects of Current Q24–Q28 · 5 marks

24. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:

  • (a) 24 Ω
  • (b) 6 Ω
  • (c) 0.17 Ω
  • (d) 14 Ω

25. Three 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is:

  • (a) 18 Ω
  • (b) 6 Ω
  • (c) 2 Ω
  • (d) 3 Ω

26. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:

  • (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
  • (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
  • (c) the right-hand thumb rule
  • (d) Lenz's law

27. An electric motor converts:

  • (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
  • (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
  • (c) heat into light
  • (d) chemical energy into sound

28. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:

  • (a) induced current in a coil
  • (b) magnetic field due to a current
  • (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
  • (d) current produced by a generator

  • (d) natural gas

30. The ozone layer is being depleted mainly by:

  • (a) CFCs
  • (b) methane
  • (c) nitrogen fertilisers
  • (d) carbon monoxide
