Section A · Chemical Substances Q1–Q10 · 10 marks

1. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:

  • (a) combination
  • (b) decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) double displacement

2. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of:

  • (a) NO₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) NO
  • (d) PbO

3. A shiny brown element X becomes black when heated in air. X and the black compound are:

  • (a) Cu and CuO
  • (b) Fe and Fe₂O₃
  • (c) Ag and Ag₂S
  • (d) Al and Al₂O₃

4. Which one is a strong base?

  • (a) NH4OH
  • (b) Mg(OH)2
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Cu(OH)2

5. The pH of gastric juice released during digestion is:

  • (a) less than 7
  • (b) more than 7
  • (c) equal to 7
  • (d) equal to 0

6. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:

  • (a) less reactive than silver
  • (b) more reactive than silver
  • (c) equally reactive
  • (d) an inert metal

7. Galvanisation protects iron from rusting by coating it with a thin layer of:

  • (a) tin
  • (b) chromium
  • (c) zinc
  • (d) copper

8. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 6
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 8

9. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?

  • (a) C₃H₈
  • (b) C₂H₆
  • (c) C₄H₁₀
  • (d) C₂H₄

10. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)?

  • (a) C₂H₆
  • (b) C₂H₄
  • (c) CH₄
  • (d) C₃H₈

Section B · World of Living Q11–Q18 · 8 marks

11. The kidneys in human beings are a part of the system for:

  • (a) nutrition
  • (b) respiration
  • (c) excretion
  • (d) transportation

12. Which of the following is not a function of the stomach?

  • (a) killing bacteria
  • (b) digestion of protein
  • (c) absorption of digested food
  • (d) protection of the stomach wall by mucus

13. The part of the brain that maintains the posture and balance of the body is:

  • (a) cerebrum
  • (b) cerebellum
  • (c) pons
  • (d) medulla

14. In a reflex action the impulse is processed in the:

  • (a) cerebrum
  • (b) spinal cord
  • (c) cerebellum
  • (d) medulla only

15. Which of these is an asexual mode of reproduction?

  • (a) budding in yeast
  • (b) pollination
  • (c) fertilisation
  • (d) seed germination

16. Sperms are produced in the:

  • (a) penis
  • (b) testes
  • (c) vas deferens
  • (d) seminal vesicles

17. Which of these traits can be inherited?

  • (a) the cut tail of a mouse
  • (b) large muscles built by exercise
  • (c) eye colour
  • (d) a scar

18. The number of chromosomes in a human zygote is:

  • (a) 23
  • (b) 46
  • (c) 92
  • (d) 22

Section C · Natural Phenomena Q19–Q23 · 5 marks

19. The image formed by a concave lens of a real object is always:

  • (a) real and inverted
  • (b) virtual, erect and diminished
  • (c) virtual, erect and magnified
  • (d) real and magnified

20. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:

  • (a) bends towards the normal
  • (b) bends away from the normal
  • (c) does not bend
  • (d) is totally absorbed

21. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:

  • (a) hypermetropia
  • (b) myopia
  • (c) presbyopia
  • (d) cataract

22. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:

  • (a) scattering
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) total internal reflection
  • (d) polarisation

23. The least distance of distinct vision for a young adult with normal vision is about:

  • (a) 2.5 cm
  • (b) 25 cm
  • (c) 25 m
  • (d) infinity

Section D · Effects of Current Q24–Q28 · 5 marks

24. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:

  • (a) more than 100 Ω
  • (b) less than 1 Ω
  • (c) 107 Ω
  • (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

25. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:

  • (a) more resistance
  • (b) less resistance
  • (c) the same resistance
  • (d) no resistance

26. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:

  • (a) in series
  • (b) in parallel
  • (c) across the supply
  • (d) either way

27. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:

  • (a) concentric circles
  • (b) parallel straight lines
  • (c) diverging curves
  • (d) absent

28. The insulation on the earth wire in a domestic circuit is:

  • (a) red
  • (b) black
  • (c) green
  • (d) blue

Section E · Natural Resources Q29–Q30 · 2 marks

29. In the food chain Grass → Deer → Lion, if 10,000 J of energy is available to the grass, the energy available to the lion is:

  • (a) 1000 J
  • (b) 100 J
  • (c) 10 J
  • (d) 1 J

30. The ‘10% law’ in an ecosystem describes the transfer of:

  • (a) energy
  • (b) water
  • (c) minerals
  • (d) oxygen
