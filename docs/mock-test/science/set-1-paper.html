Section A · Chemical Substances Q1–Q10 · 10 marks

1. Which of these is a combination reaction?

  • (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
  • (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
  • (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
  • (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

2. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by:

  • (a) storing it in the open
  • (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
  • (c) adding oxidising agents
  • (d) heating it repeatedly

3. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 3
  • (c) 4
  • (d) 6

4. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains:

  • (a) NaCl
  • (b) HCl
  • (c) LiCl
  • (d) KCl

5. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:

  • (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
  • (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
  • (c) CaSO₄
  • (d) CaCO₃

6. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:

  • (a) reduction with carbon
  • (b) self reduction
  • (c) electrolytic reduction
  • (d) roasting

7. Brass is an alloy of:

  • (a) copper and tin
  • (b) copper and zinc
  • (c) lead and tin
  • (d) mercury and zinc

8. Which compound decolorises bromine water?

  • (a) CH4
  • (b) C2H6
  • (c) C2H4
  • (d) C3H8

9. Vinegar is a solution of:

  • (a) 5–8% ethanoic acid in water
  • (b) 50–80% ethanoic acid
  • (c) pure ethanoic acid
  • (d) ethanoic acid in alcohol

10. The functional group present in an alcohol is:

  • (a) –CHO
  • (b) –COOH
  • (c) –OH
  • (d) –CO–

Section B · World of Living Q11–Q18 · 8 marks

11. Photosynthesis in a leaf takes place inside the:

  • (a) mitochondria
  • (b) chloroplast
  • (c) ribosome
  • (d) vacuole

12. The main nitrogenous waste filtered out by the kidneys is:

  • (a) carbon dioxide
  • (b) urea
  • (c) glucose
  • (d) glycogen

13. Which plant hormone is a growth inhibitor?

  • (a) auxin
  • (b) gibberellin
  • (c) cytokinin
  • (d) abscisic acid

14. The part of a neuron that receives the impulse is the:

  • (a) axon
  • (b) dendrite
  • (c) cell body
  • (d) myelin sheath

15. Binary fission in amoeba begins with the division of the:

  • (a) cytoplasm
  • (b) nucleus
  • (c) cell membrane
  • (d) pseudopodia

16. Which of the following is a barrier method of contraception?

  • (a) oral pill
  • (b) copper-T
  • (c) condom
  • (d) vasectomy

17. In a monohybrid cross between a tall (TT) and a dwarf (tt) pea plant, the F2 phenotypic ratio is:

  • (a) 1:1
  • (b) 3:1
  • (c) 9:3:3:1
  • (d) 1:2:1

18. The sex of a human child is determined by:

  • (a) the mother's egg
  • (b) the father's sperm
  • (c) both parents equally
  • (d) the diet of the mother

Section C · Natural Phenomena Q19–Q23 · 5 marks

19. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:

  • (a) less than one
  • (b) more than one
  • (c) equal to one
  • (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

20. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?

  • (a) plane
  • (b) concave
  • (c) convex
  • (d) parabolic

21. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:

  • (a) pupil
  • (b) retina
  • (c) ciliary muscles
  • (d) iris

22. The danger signals installed at the top of tall buildings are red because red light:

  • (a) is scattered the most by smoke or fog
  • (b) is scattered the least by smoke or fog
  • (c) is absorbed the most
  • (d) moves fastest in air

23. The clear sky appears blue because:

  • (a) blue light is absorbed the most
  • (b) shorter wavelengths are scattered more by air molecules
  • (c) the sea reflects blue light upwards
  • (d) the atmosphere refracts blue light towards the eye

Section D · Effects of Current Q24–Q28 · 5 marks

24. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:

  • (a) 3 kWh
  • (b) 30 kWh
  • (c) 300 kWh
  • (d) 3000 kWh

25. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:

  • (a) joule
  • (b) watt
  • (c) kilowatt-hour
  • (d) volt

26. One kilowatt hour equals:

  • (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
  • (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
  • (c) 360 J
  • (d) 6 × 10µ J

27. The frequency of AC supply in India is 50 Hz. The direction of the current changes:

  • (a) 50 times per second
  • (b) 100 times per second
  • (c) 25 times per second
  • (d) 200 times per second

28. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:

  • (a) increases as you move away from the wire
  • (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
  • (c) does not depend on the current
  • (d) is zero everywhere around the wire

Section E · Natural Resources Q29–Q30 · 2 marks

29. The depletion of the ozone layer is mainly caused by:

  • (a) CO2
  • (b) CFCs
  • (c) methane
  • (d) SO2

30. Which of the following groups contains only biodegradable substances?

  • (a) paper, plastic, wood
  • (b) vegetable peels, paper, lime juice
  • (c) glass, wood, plastic
  • (d) lime juice, grass, aluminium foil
