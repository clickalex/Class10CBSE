SECTION A - Physics (Q1-Q9, 9 marks)
------------------------------------

Q1. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?
    (a) at F
    (b) at 2F
    (c) at infinity
    (d) between F and 2F

Q2. A ray of light enters a denser medium along the normal (angle of incidence 0°). It:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) passes undeviated
    (d) is totally reflected

Q3. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:
    (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
    (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
    (c) concave, focal length 2 m
    (d) convex, focal length 2 m

Q4. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:
    (a) scattering
    (b) dispersion
    (c) total internal reflection
    (d) polarisation

Q5. Stars twinkle mainly because of:
    (a) atmospheric refraction
    (b) dispersion
    (c) reflection from clouds
    (d) their own flickering

Q6. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:
    (a) more than 100 Ω
    (b) less than 1 Ω
    (c) 107 Ω
    (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

Q7. One kilowatt hour equals:
    (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
    (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
    (c) 360 J
    (d) 6 × 10µ J

Q8. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:
    (a) concentric circles
    (b) parallel straight lines
    (c) diverging curves
    (d) absent

Q9. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:
    (a) induced current in a coil
    (b) magnetic field due to a current
    (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
    (d) current produced by a generator


SECTION B - Chemistry (Q10-Q18, 9 marks)
----------------------------------------

Q10. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:
    (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
    (b) CuO and Ag2O
    (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
    (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

Q11. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:
    (a) 2
    (b) 3
    (c) 4
    (d) 6

Q12. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:
    (a) sodium chloride
    (b) sodium carbonate
    (c) calcium sulphate
    (d) ammonium chloride

Q13. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:
    (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
    (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
    (c) CaSO₄
    (d) CaCO₃

Q14. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:
    (a) MgCl
    (b) MgCl₂
    (c) Mg₂Cl
    (d) MgCl₃

Q15. The best conductor of electricity among these is:
    (a) copper
    (b) silver
    (c) aluminium
    (d) iron

Q16. Brass is an alloy of:
    (a) copper and tin
    (b) copper and zinc
    (c) lead and tin
    (d) mercury and zinc

Q17. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?
    (a) C₃H₈
    (b) C₂H₆
    (c) C₄H₁₀
    (d) C₂H₄

Q18. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)?
    (a) C₂H₆
    (b) C₂H₄
    (c) CH₄
    (d) C₃H₈


SECTION C - Biology (Q19-Q26, 8 marks)
--------------------------------------

Q19. Photosynthesis in a leaf takes place inside the:
    (a) mitochondria
    (b) chloroplast
    (c) ribosome
    (d) vacuole

Q20. Which of these is an autotroph?
    (a) mushroom
    (b) Cuscuta
    (c) green plant
    (d) human being

Q21. Which plant hormone is a growth inhibitor?
    (a) auxin
    (b) gibberellin
    (c) cytokinin
    (d) abscisic acid

Q22. In a reflex action the impulse is processed in the:
    (a) cerebrum
    (b) spinal cord
    (c) cerebellum
    (d) medulla only

Q23. Sperms are produced in the:
    (a) penis
    (b) testes
    (c) vas deferens
    (d) seminal vesicles

Q24. Sterilisation in human beings means:
    (a) preventing ovulation with pills
    (b) surgically blocking the gamete ducts
    (c) killing germs
    (d) using a condom

Q25. The phenotypic ratio obtained in a dihybrid cross between two F₁ hybrids is:
    (a) 3 : 1
    (b) 9 : 3 : 3 : 1
    (c) 1 : 1
    (d) 1 : 2 : 1

Q26. The ozone layer is being depleted mainly by:
    (a) CFCs
    (b) methane
    (c) nitrogen fertilisers
    (d) carbon monoxide


SECTION D - Mathematics (Q27-Q44, 18 marks)
-------------------------------------------

Q27. The decimal expansion of 23/(2³ × 5²) terminates after:
    (a) 2 places
    (b) 3 places
    (c) 4 places
    (d) 5 places

Q28. A quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 3 and −3 is:
    (a) x² − 9
    (b) x² + 9
    (c) x² − 6x + 9
    (d) x² − 3

Q29. If one zero of x² − 4x + 1 is 2 + √3, the other zero is:
    (a) 2 − √3
    (b) −2 − √3
    (c) √3 − 2
    (d) −√3

Q30. The pair x + y = 5 and 2x + 2y = 10 represents:
    (a) intersecting lines
    (b) parallel lines
    (c) coincident lines
    (d) none

Q31. The pair 6x − 3y + 10 = 0 and 2x − y + 9 = 0 has:
    (a) one solution
    (b) two solutions
    (c) infinitely many solutions
    (d) no solution

Q32. The roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0 are:
    (a) 2, 3
    (b) −2, −3
    (c) 1, 6
    (d) −1, −6

Q33. The sum and product of the roots of 2x² − 7x + 3 = 0 are:
    (a) 7/2 and 3/2
    (b) 7/2 and 3
    (c) −7/2 and 3/2
    (d) 7/2 and −3/2

Q34. The 10th term of the AP 2, 7, 12, … is:
    (a) 45
    (b) 47
    (c) 50
    (d) 52

Q35. The sum of the first 10 natural numbers is:
    (a) 45
    (b) 50
    (c) 55
    (d) 100

Q36. The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is 25:49. The ratio of their corresponding sides is:
    (a) 25:49
    (b) 5:7
    (c) 7:5
    (d) 49:25

Q37. Three points are collinear if the area of the triangle they form is:
    (a) 1
    (b) maximum
    (c) 0
    (d) negative

Q38. The value of sin²60° + 2 tan²45° − cos²30° is:
    (a) 1
    (b) 2
    (c) 5/4
    (d) 9/4

Q39. When the shadow of a pole equals its height, the sun’s angle of elevation is:
    (a) 30°
    (b) 45°
    (c) 60°
    (d) 90°

Q40. A line that cuts a circle in two distinct points is called a:
    (a) tangent
    (b) secant
    (c) chord
    (d) diameter

Q41. The area of a segment is found by:
    (a) sector − triangle
    (b) sector + triangle
    (c) circle − sector
    (d) triangle − sector

Q42. A cone, a hemisphere and a cylinder have equal bases and equal heights. The ratio of their volumes is:
    (a) 1 : 2 : 3
    (b) 2 : 3 : 1
    (c) 3 : 1 : 2
    (d) 1 : 3 : 2

Q43. The median class is the class whose cumulative frequency is:
    (a) equal to N/2
    (b) the first one greater than N/2
    (c) the largest
    (d) the smallest

Q44. If P(E) = 0.37, then P(not E) = :
    (a) 0.37
    (b) 0.63
    (c) 1
    (d) 0.53
