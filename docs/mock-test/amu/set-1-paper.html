Section A · Physics Q1–Q9 · 9 marks

1. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?

  • (a) at F
  • (b) at 2F
  • (c) at infinity
  • (d) between F and 2F

2. A ray of light enters a denser medium along the normal (angle of incidence 0°). It:

  • (a) bends towards the normal
  • (b) bends away from the normal
  • (c) passes undeviated
  • (d) is totally reflected

3. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:

  • (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
  • (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
  • (c) concave, focal length 2 m
  • (d) convex, focal length 2 m

4. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:

  • (a) scattering
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) total internal reflection
  • (d) polarisation

5. Stars twinkle mainly because of:

  • (a) atmospheric refraction
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) reflection from clouds
  • (d) their own flickering

6. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:

  • (a) more than 100 Ω
  • (b) less than 1 Ω
  • (c) 107 Ω
  • (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

7. One kilowatt hour equals:

  • (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
  • (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
  • (c) 360 J
  • (d) 6 × 10µ J

8. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:

  • (a) concentric circles
  • (b) parallel straight lines
  • (c) diverging curves
  • (d) absent

9. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:

  • (a) induced current in a coil
  • (b) magnetic field due to a current
  • (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
  • (d) current produced by a generator

Section B · Chemistry Q10–Q18 · 9 marks

10. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:

  • (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
  • (b) CuO and Ag2O
  • (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
  • (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

11. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 3
  • (c) 4
  • (d) 6

12. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:

  • (a) sodium chloride
  • (b) sodium carbonate
  • (c) calcium sulphate
  • (d) ammonium chloride

13. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:

  • (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
  • (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
  • (c) CaSO₄
  • (d) CaCO₃

14. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:

  • (a) MgCl
  • (b) MgCl₂
  • (c) Mg₂Cl
  • (d) MgCl₃

15. The best conductor of electricity among these is:

  • (a) copper
  • (b) silver
  • (c) aluminium
  • (d) iron

16. Brass is an alloy of:

  • (a) copper and tin
  • (b) copper and zinc
  • (c) lead and tin
  • (d) mercury and zinc

17. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?

  • (a) C₃H₈
  • (b) C₂H₆
  • (c) C₄H₁₀
  • (d) C₂H₄

18. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)?

  • (a) C₂H₆
  • (b) C₂H₄
  • (c) CH₄
  • (d) C₃H₈

Section C · Biology Q19–Q26 · 8 marks

19. Photosynthesis in a leaf takes place inside the:

  • (a) mitochondria
  • (b) chloroplast
  • (c) ribosome
  • (d) vacuole

20. Which of these is an autotroph?

  • (a) mushroom
  • (b) Cuscuta
  • (c) green plant
  • (d) human being

21. Which plant hormone is a growth inhibitor?

  • (a) auxin
  • (b) gibberellin
  • (c) cytokinin
  • (d) abscisic acid

22. In a reflex action the impulse is processed in the:

  • (a) cerebrum
  • (b) spinal cord
  • (c) cerebellum
  • (d) medulla only

23. Sperms are produced in the:

  • (a) penis
  • (b) testes
  • (c) vas deferens
  • (d) seminal vesicles

24. Sterilisation in human beings means:

  • (a) preventing ovulation with pills
  • (b) surgically blocking the gamete ducts
  • (c) killing germs
  • (d) using a condom

25. The phenotypic ratio obtained in a dihybrid cross between two F₁ hybrids is:

  • (a) 3 : 1
  • (b) 9 : 3 : 3 : 1
  • (c) 1 : 1
  • (d) 1 : 2 : 1

26. The ozone layer is being depleted mainly by:

  • (a) CFCs
  • (b) methane
  • (c) nitrogen fertilisers
  • (d) carbon monoxide

Section D · Mathematics Q27–Q44 · 18 marks

27. The decimal expansion of 23/(2³ × 5²) terminates after:

  • (a) 2 places
  • (b) 3 places
  • (c) 4 places
  • (d) 5 places

28. A quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 3 and −3 is:

  • (a) x² − 9
  • (b) x² + 9
  • (c) x² − 6x + 9
  • (d) x² − 3

29. If one zero of x² − 4x + 1 is 2 + √3, the other zero is:

  • (a) 2 − √3
  • (b) −2 − √3
  • (c) √3 − 2
  • (d) −√3

30. The pair x + y = 5 and 2x + 2y = 10 represents:

  • (a) intersecting lines
  • (b) parallel lines
  • (c) coincident lines
  • (d) none

31. The pair 6x − 3y + 10 = 0 and 2x − y + 9 = 0 has:

  • (a) one solution
  • (b) two solutions
  • (c) infinitely many solutions
  • (d) no solution

32. The roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0 are:

  • (a) 2, 3
  • (b) −2, −3
  • (c) 1, 6
  • (d) −1, −6

33. The sum and product of the roots of 2x² − 7x + 3 = 0 are:

  • (a) 7/2 and 3/2
  • (b) 7/2 and 3
  • (c) −7/2 and 3/2
  • (d) 7/2 and −3/2

34. The 10th term of the AP 2, 7, 12, … is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 47
  • (c) 50
  • (d) 52

35. The sum of the first 10 natural numbers is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 50
  • (c) 55
  • (d) 100

36. The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is 25:49. The ratio of their corresponding sides is:

  • (a) 25:49
  • (b) 5:7
  • (c) 7:5
  • (d) 49:25

37. Three points are collinear if the area of the triangle they form is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) maximum
  • (c) 0
  • (d) negative

38. The value of sin²60° + 2 tan²45° − cos²30° is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 5/4
  • (d) 9/4

39. When the shadow of a pole equals its height, the sun’s angle of elevation is:

  • (a) 30°
  • (b) 45°
  • (c) 60°
  • (d) 90°

40. A line that cuts a circle in two distinct points is called a:

  • (a) tangent
  • (b) secant
  • (c) chord
  • (d) diameter

41. The area of a segment is found by:

  • (a) sector − triangle
  • (b) sector + triangle
  • (c) circle − sector
  • (d) triangle − sector

42. A cone, a hemisphere and a cylinder have equal bases and equal heights. The ratio of their volumes is:

  • (a) 1 : 2 : 3
  • (b) 2 : 3 : 1
  • (c) 3 : 1 : 2
  • (d) 1 : 3 : 2

43. The median class is the class whose cumulative frequency is:

  • (a) equal to N/2
  • (b) the first one greater than N/2
  • (c) the largest
  • (d) the smallest

44. If P(E) = 0.37, then P(not E) = :

  • (a) 0.37
  • (b) 0.63
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 0.53

Section E · English Q45–Q50 · 6 marks

45. [Reading: Unseen and Case-based Passages] The best first step in attempting an unseen passage is to:

  • (a) read the passage three times
  • (b) read the questions first
  • (c) answer from general knowledge
  • (d) begin with the hardest question

46. [Grammar in Context] Fill in the blank: She has been waiting ____ two hours.

  • (a) for
  • (b) since
  • (c) from
  • (d) during

47. [Formal Letter Writing] 'Yours faithfully' is used when the letter begins with:

  • (a) Dear Mr Sharma
  • (b) Sir or Madam
  • (c) Respected Principal
  • (d) My dear friend

48. [Formal Letter Writing] In a letter of complaint or order, the subject line should be:

  • (a) short and specific
  • (b) long and detailed
  • (c) left out
  • (d) written as a question

49. [Analytical Paragraph] An analytical paragraph must be based on:

  • (a) the data given in the chart or table
  • (b) personal opinion only
  • (c) imaginary facts
  • (d) a news report

50. [Analytical Paragraph] Figures in an analytical paragraph should be:

  • (a) compared with each other to show a trend
  • (b) listed one by one without comment
  • (c) invented to fill space
  • (d) rounded off to zero
