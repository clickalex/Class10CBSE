SECTION A - Physics (Q1-Q9, 9 marks)
------------------------------------

Q1. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?
    (a) plane
    (b) concave
    (c) convex
    (d) parabolic

Q2. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) does not bend
    (d) is totally absorbed

Q3. The danger signals installed at the top of tall buildings are red because red light:
    (a) is scattered the most by smoke or fog
    (b) is scattered the least by smoke or fog
    (c) is absorbed the most
    (d) moves fastest in air

Q4. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:
    (a) hypermetropia
    (b) myopia
    (c) presbyopia
    (d) cataract

Q5. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:
    (a) joule
    (b) watt
    (c) kilowatt-hour
    (d) volt

Q6. The resistance of a wire doubles when:
    (a) its length is doubled
    (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
    (c) its length is halved
    (d) its diameter is doubled

Q7. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:
    (a) 24 Ω
    (b) 6 Ω
    (c) 0.17 Ω
    (d) 14 Ω

Q8. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:
    (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
    (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
    (c) the right-hand thumb rule
    (d) Lenz's law

Q9. The production of a current in a closed coil by a changing magnetic field is called:
    (a) electromagnetic induction
    (b) magnetic effect of current
    (c) electrolysis
    (d) electrostatic induction


SECTION B - Chemistry (Q10-Q18, 9 marks)
----------------------------------------

Q10. Which of the following is an endothermic process?
    (a) respiration
    (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
    (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
    (d) quicklime + water

Q11. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by:
    (a) storing it in the open
    (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
    (c) adding oxidising agents
    (d) heating it repeatedly

Q12. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains:
    (a) NaCl
    (b) HCl
    (c) LiCl
    (d) KCl

Q13. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:
    (a) 2
    (b) 5
    (c) 7
    (d) 10

Q14. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:
    (a) reduction with carbon
    (b) self reduction
    (c) electrolytic reduction
    (d) roasting

Q15. The correct decreasing order of reactivity is:
    (a) Zn > Mg > Cu
    (b) Mg > Zn > Cu
    (c) Cu > Zn > Mg
    (d) Zn > Cu > Mg

Q16. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:
    (a) 5
    (b) 6
    (c) 7
    (d) 8

Q17. The functional group present in an alcohol is:
    (a) –CHO
    (b) –COOH
    (c) –OH
    (d) –CO–

Q18. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:
    (a) H₂
    (b) O₂
    (c) CO₂
    (d) CO


SECTION C - Biology (Q19-Q26, 8 marks)
--------------------------------------

Q19. The kidneys in human beings are a part of the system for:
    (a) nutrition
    (b) respiration
    (c) excretion
    (d) transportation

Q20. Which of the following is not a function of the stomach?
    (a) killing bacteria
    (b) digestion of protein
    (c) absorption of digested food
    (d) protection of the stomach wall by mucus

Q21. The part of the brain that maintains the posture and balance of the body is:
    (a) cerebrum
    (b) cerebellum
    (c) pons
    (d) medulla

Q22. Which of these is an asexual mode of reproduction?
    (a) budding in yeast
    (b) pollination
    (c) fertilisation
    (d) seed germination

Q23. The sex of a human child is determined by:
    (a) the mother's egg
    (b) the father's sperm
    (c) both parents equally
    (d) the diet of the mother

Q24. Which of these traits can be inherited?
    (a) the cut tail of a mouse
    (b) large muscles built by exercise
    (c) eye colour
    (d) a scar

Q25. In the food chain Grass → Deer → Lion, if 10,000 J of energy is available to the grass, the energy available to the lion is:
    (a) 1000 J
    (b) 100 J
    (c) 10 J
    (d) 1 J

Q26. The ‘10% law’ in an ecosystem describes the transfer of:
    (a) energy
    (b) water
    (c) minerals
    (d) oxygen


SECTION D - Mathematics (Q27-Q44, 18 marks)
-------------------------------------------

Q27. The HCF of the smallest prime number and the smallest composite number is:
    (a) 1
    (b) 2
    (c) 4
    (d) 6

Q28. If α, β are zeroes of x² − 5x + 6, then α + β = ?
    (a) 6
    (b) 5
    (c) −5
    (d) 1

Q29. If x + 2y = 5 and 3x + ky = 15 are coincident lines, then k = :
    (a) 2
    (b) 3
    (c) 6
    (d) 15

Q30. Which one is NOT a quadratic equation?
    (a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0
    (b) (x + 1)² = 2(x − 3)
    (c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0
    (d) 2x² = 8

Q31. S₅ − S₄ equals:
    (a) the 5th term
    (b) the 4th term
    (c) the 9th term
    (d) zero

Q32. In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC with AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 5 cm. Then EC = :
    (a) 6.5 cm
    (b) 7.5 cm
    (c) 8 cm
    (d) 9 cm

Q33. If the corresponding sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 4 : 9, their areas are in the ratio:
    (a) 4 : 9
    (b) 2 : 3
    (c) 16 : 81
    (d) 8 : 27

Q34. The distance of the point (3, 4) from the origin is:
    (a) 5
    (b) 7
    (c) 25
    (d) 12

Q35. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:
    (a) a
    (b) a²
    (c) 1
    (d) 2a

Q36. sin²30° + cos²30° equals:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1/2
    (c) 1
    (d) 3/4

Q37. sec²A − tan²A equals:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1
    (c) −1
    (d) sin²A

Q38. The angle of depression is always measured from:
    (a) the vertical
    (b) the horizontal
    (c) the object
    (d) the ground below

Q39. If the angle of elevation of the sun is 45°, the length of the shadow of a pole equals:
    (a) half its height
    (b) its height
    (c) twice its height
    (d) √3 times its height

Q40. The number of tangents from a point on the circle is:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1
    (c) 2
    (d) infinite

Q41. If the radius of a circle is doubled, its area becomes:
    (a) 2 times
    (b) 4 times
    (c) 8 times
    (d) unchanged

Q42. The volume of a cone of radius r and height h is:
    (a) πr²h
    (b) πr²h/3
    (c) 2πr²h
    (d) πrh

Q43. The mode of 3, 5, 7, 5, 9, 5, 8 is:
    (a) 3
    (b) 5
    (c) 7
    (d) 9

Q44. In one throw of a die, the probability of getting a number less than 7 is:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1/6
    (c) 5/6
    (d) 1
