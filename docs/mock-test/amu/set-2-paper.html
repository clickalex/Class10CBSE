Section A · Physics Q1–Q9 · 9 marks

1. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?

  • (a) plane
  • (b) concave
  • (c) convex
  • (d) parabolic

2. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:

  • (a) bends towards the normal
  • (b) bends away from the normal
  • (c) does not bend
  • (d) is totally absorbed

3. The danger signals installed at the top of tall buildings are red because red light:

  • (a) is scattered the most by smoke or fog
  • (b) is scattered the least by smoke or fog
  • (c) is absorbed the most
  • (d) moves fastest in air

4. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:

  • (a) hypermetropia
  • (b) myopia
  • (c) presbyopia
  • (d) cataract

5. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:

  • (a) joule
  • (b) watt
  • (c) kilowatt-hour
  • (d) volt

6. The resistance of a wire doubles when:

  • (a) its length is doubled
  • (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
  • (c) its length is halved
  • (d) its diameter is doubled

7. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:

  • (a) 24 Ω
  • (b) 6 Ω
  • (c) 0.17 Ω
  • (d) 14 Ω

8. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:

  • (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
  • (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
  • (c) the right-hand thumb rule
  • (d) Lenz's law

9. The production of a current in a closed coil by a changing magnetic field is called:

  • (a) electromagnetic induction
  • (b) magnetic effect of current
  • (c) electrolysis
  • (d) electrostatic induction

Section B · Chemistry Q10–Q18 · 9 marks

10. Which of the following is an endothermic process?

  • (a) respiration
  • (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
  • (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
  • (d) quicklime + water

11. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by:

  • (a) storing it in the open
  • (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
  • (c) adding oxidising agents
  • (d) heating it repeatedly

12. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains:

  • (a) NaCl
  • (b) HCl
  • (c) LiCl
  • (d) KCl

13. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 10

14. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:

  • (a) reduction with carbon
  • (b) self reduction
  • (c) electrolytic reduction
  • (d) roasting

15. The correct decreasing order of reactivity is:

  • (a) Zn > Mg > Cu
  • (b) Mg > Zn > Cu
  • (c) Cu > Zn > Mg
  • (d) Zn > Cu > Mg

16. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 6
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 8

17. The functional group present in an alcohol is:

  • (a) –CHO
  • (b) –COOH
  • (c) –OH
  • (d) –CO–

18. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:

  • (a) H₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) CO₂
  • (d) CO

Section C · Biology Q19–Q26 · 8 marks

19. The kidneys in human beings are a part of the system for:

  • (a) nutrition
  • (b) respiration
  • (c) excretion
  • (d) transportation

20. Which of the following is not a function of the stomach?

  • (a) killing bacteria
  • (b) digestion of protein
  • (c) absorption of digested food
  • (d) protection of the stomach wall by mucus

21. The part of the brain that maintains the posture and balance of the body is:

  • (a) cerebrum
  • (b) cerebellum
  • (c) pons
  • (d) medulla

22. Which of these is an asexual mode of reproduction?

  • (a) budding in yeast
  • (b) pollination
  • (c) fertilisation
  • (d) seed germination

23. The sex of a human child is determined by:

  • (a) the mother's egg
  • (b) the father's sperm
  • (c) both parents equally
  • (d) the diet of the mother

24. Which of these traits can be inherited?

  • (a) the cut tail of a mouse
  • (b) large muscles built by exercise
  • (c) eye colour
  • (d) a scar

25. In the food chain Grass → Deer → Lion, if 10,000 J of energy is available to the grass, the energy available to the lion is:

  • (a) 1000 J
  • (b) 100 J
  • (c) 10 J
  • (d) 1 J

26. The ‘10% law’ in an ecosystem describes the transfer of:

  • (a) energy
  • (b) water
  • (c) minerals
  • (d) oxygen

Section D · Mathematics Q27–Q44 · 18 marks

27. The HCF of the smallest prime number and the smallest composite number is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 4
  • (d) 6

28. If α, β are zeroes of x² − 5x + 6, then α + β = ?

  • (a) 6
  • (b) 5
  • (c) −5
  • (d) 1

29. If x + 2y = 5 and 3x + ky = 15 are coincident lines, then k = :

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 3
  • (c) 6
  • (d) 15

30. Which one is NOT a quadratic equation?

  • (a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0
  • (b) (x + 1)² = 2(x − 3)
  • (c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0
  • (d) 2x² = 8

31. S₅ − S₄ equals:

  • (a) the 5th term
  • (b) the 4th term
  • (c) the 9th term
  • (d) zero

32. In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC with AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 5 cm. Then EC = :

  • (a) 6.5 cm
  • (b) 7.5 cm
  • (c) 8 cm
  • (d) 9 cm

33. If the corresponding sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 4 : 9, their areas are in the ratio:

  • (a) 4 : 9
  • (b) 2 : 3
  • (c) 16 : 81
  • (d) 8 : 27

34. The distance of the point (3, 4) from the origin is:

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 7
  • (c) 25
  • (d) 12

35. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:

  • (a) a
  • (b) a²
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 2a

36. sin²30° + cos²30° equals:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1/2
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 3/4

37. sec²A − tan²A equals:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) −1
  • (d) sin²A

38. The angle of depression is always measured from:

  • (a) the vertical
  • (b) the horizontal
  • (c) the object
  • (d) the ground below

39. If the angle of elevation of the sun is 45°, the length of the shadow of a pole equals:

  • (a) half its height
  • (b) its height
  • (c) twice its height
  • (d) √3 times its height

40. The number of tangents from a point on the circle is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) 2
  • (d) infinite

41. If the radius of a circle is doubled, its area becomes:

  • (a) 2 times
  • (b) 4 times
  • (c) 8 times
  • (d) unchanged

42. The volume of a cone of radius r and height h is:

  • (a) πr²h
  • (b) πr²h/3
  • (c) 2πr²h
  • (d) πrh

43. The mode of 3, 5, 7, 5, 9, 5, 8 is:

  • (a) 3
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 9

44. In one throw of a die, the probability of getting a number less than 7 is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1/6
  • (c) 5/6
  • (d) 1

Section E · English Q45–Q50 · 6 marks

45. [Reading: Unseen and Case-based Passages] The length of the discursive passage in the reading section is:

  • (a) 200–250 words
  • (b) 300–350 words
  • (c) 400–450 words
  • (d) 500–550 words

46. [Reading: Unseen and Case-based Passages] Before writing the answers to an unseen passage you should:

  • (a) read the questions, then read the passage
  • (b) start writing immediately
  • (c) copy lines from the passage
  • (d) skip the passage

47. [Grammar in Context] The correct reported form of ‘He said, “I am busy”' is:

  • (a) He said that he was busy
  • (b) He said that he is busy
  • (c) He said that I was busy
  • (d) He says he was busy

48. [Grammar in Context] Fill in the blank: He asked me ____ I could help him.

  • (a) if
  • (b) that
  • (c) what
  • (d) to

49. [Formal Letter Writing] The word limit for a formal letter in the board examination is:

  • (a) 50–60 words
  • (b) 100–120 words
  • (c) 150–200 words
  • (d) no limit

50. [Analytical Paragraph] The concluding sentence of an analytical paragraph should:

  • (a) give a personal opinion
  • (b) state a trend or inference from the data
  • (c) list more figures
  • (d) repeat the introduction
