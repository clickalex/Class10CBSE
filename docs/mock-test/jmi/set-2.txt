SECTION B - Physics (Q26-Q40, 15 marks)
---------------------------------------

Q26. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:
    (a) less than one
    (b) more than one
    (c) equal to one
    (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

Q27. The image formed by a concave lens of a real object is always:
    (a) real and inverted
    (b) virtual, erect and diminished
    (c) virtual, erect and magnified
    (d) real and magnified

Q28. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:
    (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
    (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
    (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
    (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

Q29. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) does not bend
    (d) is totally absorbed

Q30. The danger signals installed at the top of tall buildings are red because red light:
    (a) is scattered the most by smoke or fog
    (b) is scattered the least by smoke or fog
    (c) is absorbed the most
    (d) moves fastest in air

Q31. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:
    (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
    (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
    (c) concave, focal length 2 m
    (d) convex, focal length 2 m

Q32. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:
    (a) scattering
    (b) dispersion
    (c) total internal reflection
    (d) polarisation

Q33. The clear sky appears blue because:
    (a) blue light is absorbed the most
    (b) shorter wavelengths are scattered more by air molecules
    (c) the sea reflects blue light upwards
    (d) the atmosphere refracts blue light towards the eye

Q34. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:
    (a) more than 100 Ω
    (b) less than 1 Ω
    (c) 107 Ω
    (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

Q35. The resistance of a wire doubles when:
    (a) its length is doubled
    (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
    (c) its length is halved
    (d) its diameter is doubled

Q36. One kilowatt hour equals:
    (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
    (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
    (c) 360 J
    (d) 6 × 10µ J

Q37. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:
    (a) in series
    (b) in parallel
    (c) across the supply
    (d) either way

Q38. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is:
    (a) AC generator has an electromagnet
    (b) DC generator has a commutator
    (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator
    (d) AC generator produces more power

Q39. The production of a current in a closed coil by a changing magnetic field is called:
    (a) electromagnetic induction
    (b) magnetic effect of current
    (c) electrolysis
    (d) electrostatic induction

Q40. An electric motor converts:
    (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
    (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
    (c) heat into light
    (d) chemical energy into sound
