Section A · English Q1–Q25 · 25 marks

1. [A Letter to God] The letter Lencho received was signed:

  • (a) the postmaster
  • (b) God
  • (c) the employees of the post office
  • (d) the President

2. [Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom] Mandela was sworn in as South Africa's first black President on:

  • (a) 10 May 1994
  • (b) 10 May 1995
  • (c) 26 April 1994
  • (d) 27 April 1993

3. [Two Stories about Flying] How long was the young seagull left alone on his ledge before he flew?

  • (a) twelve hours
  • (b) twenty-four hours
  • (c) two days
  • (d) three days

4. [From the Diary of Anne Frank] Anne Frank named her diary:

  • (a) Kitty
  • (b) Margot
  • (c) Lies
  • (d) Sanne

  • (a) the jungle's edge
  • (b) the city centre
  • (c) the zoo gates
  • (d) the river dam

  • (a) Carolyn Wells
  • (b) Ogden Nash
  • (c) W. B. Yeats
  • (d) Robin Klein

7. [The Ball Poem] 'The epistemology of loss' means:

  • (a) the fear of loss
  • (b) the knowledge of what it is to lose something

  • (a) Robin Klein
  • (b) Robert Frost

  • (d) John Berryman

  • (a) full and bright

  • (c) hidden behind clouds
  • (d) setting

  • (a) with thunder
  • (b) on little cat feet
  • (c) over the mountains
  • (d) after the rain

  • (a) is cowardly and cries for a nice safe cage
  • (b) cannot fly
  • (c) has no teeth
  • (d) is too small

  • (a) for himself alone and not for his yellow hair
  • (b) for his wealth
  • (c) for his beauty
  • (d) for his fame

13. [A Triumph of Surgery] The phrase 'a triumph of surgery' is spoken by:

  • (a) the vet
  • (b) Mrs Pumphrey
  • (c) Mrs Pumphrey's friends
  • (d) Tricki

14. [The Thief's Story] Anil was a:

  • (a) wrestler
  • (b) struggling writer
  • (c) moneylender
  • (d) shopkeeper

15. [The Midnight Visitor] Ausable told Max that the balcony he saw belonged to:

  • (a) the next apartment
  • (b) Ausable's own room
  • (c) the hotel manager
  • (d) nobody

16. [A Question of Trust] Horace was arrested because:

  • (a) the woman informed the police
  • (b) he left his fingerprints
  • (c) he dropped his tools
  • (d) he was seen by a servant

17. [Footprints without Feet] The village constable who tried to arrest Griffin was:

  • (a) Mr Hall
  • (b) Mr Jaffers
  • (c) Mr Griffin
  • (d) Mr Kemp

18. [The Making of a Scientist] Ebright's teacher Richard A. Weiherer taught him to:

  • (a) win at any cost
  • (b) compete in order to do the best job he could
  • (c) avoid competition
  • (d) study only butterflies

19. [The Necklace] The necklace the Loisels bought to replace the lost one cost:

  • (a) 18,000 francs
  • (b) 36,000 francs
  • (c) 40,000 francs
  • (d) 500 francs

20. [Bholi] Bholi refuses to marry Bishamber Nath because he:

  • (a) demands dowry from her father after the garlands are exchanged

  • (a) a mirror
  • (b) an earth sandwich

  • (d) a map

  • (a) a chart, table or graph
  • (b) a photograph
  • (c) a map only
  • (d) a cartoon

  • (a) He said that he was busy

  • (b) listed one by one without comment

  • (a) myopia
  • (b) hypermetropia

  • (a) hypermetropia

  • (a) 2.5 cm

  • (a) more resistance
  • (b) less resistance

  • (a) 3 kWh
  • (b) 30 kWh

  • (a) joule
  • (b) watt

  • (a) 24 Ω
  • (b) 6 Ω

  • (a) 50 times per second
  • (b) 100 times per second
  • (c) 25 times per second
  • (d) 200 times per second

38. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:

  • (a) concentric circles
  • (b) parallel straight lines
  • (c) diverging curves
  • (d) absent

  • (a) red
  • (b) black
  • (c) green
  • (d) blue

  • (a) induced current in a coil
  • (b) magnetic field due to a current
  • (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
