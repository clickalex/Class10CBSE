SECTION A - Physics (Q1-Q10, 40 marks)
--------------------------------------

Q1. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:
    (a) less than one
    (b) more than one
    (c) equal to one
    (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

Q2. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) does not bend
    (d) is totally absorbed

Q3. An object is placed 20 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. The image is formed:
    (a) 10 cm in front of the mirror
    (b) 20 cm in front of the mirror
    (c) 20 cm behind the mirror
    (d) at infinity

Q4. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:
    (a) hypermetropia
    (b) myopia
    (c) presbyopia
    (d) cataract

Q5. Stars twinkle mainly because of:
    (a) atmospheric refraction
    (b) dispersion
    (c) reflection from clouds
    (d) their own flickering

Q6. The least distance of distinct vision for a young adult with normal vision is about:
    (a) 2.5 cm
    (b) 25 cm
    (c) 25 m
    (d) infinity

Q7. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:
    (a) more than 100 Ω
    (b) less than 1 Ω
    (c) 107 Ω
    (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

Q8. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:
    (a) 3 kWh
    (b) 30 kWh
    (c) 300 kWh
    (d) 3000 kWh

Q9. The production of a current in a closed coil by a changing magnetic field is called:
    (a) electromagnetic induction
    (b) magnetic effect of current
    (c) electrolysis
    (d) electrostatic induction

Q10. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:
    (a) increases as you move away from the wire
    (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
    (c) does not depend on the current
    (d) is zero everywhere around the wire


SECTION B - Chemistry (Q11-Q20, 40 marks)
-----------------------------------------

Q11. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:
    (a) combination
    (b) decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) double displacement

Q12. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:
    (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
    (b) CuO and Ag2O
    (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
    (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

Q13. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:
    (a) 2
    (b) 3
    (c) 4
    (d) 6

Q14. Bleaching powder is manufactured by passing chlorine gas over:
    (a) dry slaked lime
    (b) quicklime
    (c) limestone
    (d) gypsum

Q15. Baking soda is:
    (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
    (b) NaHCO₃
    (c) NaOH
    (d) Na₂CO₃

Q16. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:
    (a) less reactive than silver
    (b) more reactive than silver
    (c) equally reactive
    (d) an inert metal

Q17. Which pair is amphoteric?
    (a) Na2O and CaO
    (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
    (c) CO2 and SO2
    (d) CuO and FeO

Q18. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:
    (a) 5
    (b) 6
    (c) 7
    (d) 8

Q19. Which compound decolorises bromine water?
    (a) CH4
    (b) C2H6
    (c) C2H4
    (d) C3H8

Q20. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?
    (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
    (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
    (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
    (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH


SECTION C - Mathematics (Q21-Q30, 40 marks)
-------------------------------------------

Q21. Which has a terminating decimal expansion?
    (a) 7/24
    (b) 5/13
    (c) 9/40
    (d) 11/21

Q22. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding sides are:
    (a) equal
    (b) proportional
    (c) perpendicular
    (d) parallel

Q23. Three points are collinear if the area of the triangle they form is:
    (a) 1
    (b) maximum
    (c) 0
    (d) negative

Q24. tan 45° × cot 45° is:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1
    (c) 2
    (d) undefined

Q25. The angle of depression is always measured from:
    (a) the vertical
    (b) the horizontal
    (c) the object
    (d) the ground below

Q26. Two tangents from an external point to a circle are:
    (a) unequal
    (b) equal
    (c) always perpendicular
    (d) parallel

Q27. The area of a semicircle of radius r is:
    (a) πr²
    (b) πr²/2
    (c) 2πr
    (d) πr²/4

Q28. The volume of a sphere of radius r is:
    (a) (4/3)πr³
    (b) 4πr²
    (c) (2/3)πr³
    (d) πr³

Q29. The class mark of the class 45–55 is:
    (a) 45
    (b) 50
    (c) 55
    (d) 100

Q30. The probability of an impossible event is:
    (a) 1
    (b) 0
    (c) 1/2
    (d) undefined
