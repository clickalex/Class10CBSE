Section A · Physics Q1–Q10 · 40 marks

1. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:

  • (a) less than one
  • (b) more than one
  • (c) equal to one
  • (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

2. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:

  • (a) bends towards the normal
  • (b) bends away from the normal
  • (c) does not bend
  • (d) is totally absorbed

3. An object is placed 20 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. The image is formed:

  • (a) 10 cm in front of the mirror
  • (b) 20 cm in front of the mirror
  • (c) 20 cm behind the mirror
  • (d) at infinity

4. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:

  • (a) hypermetropia
  • (b) myopia
  • (c) presbyopia
  • (d) cataract

5. Stars twinkle mainly because of:

  • (a) atmospheric refraction
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) reflection from clouds
  • (d) their own flickering

6. The least distance of distinct vision for a young adult with normal vision is about:

  • (a) 2.5 cm
  • (b) 25 cm
  • (c) 25 m
  • (d) infinity

7. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:

  • (a) more than 100 Ω
  • (b) less than 1 Ω
  • (c) 107 Ω
  • (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

8. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:

  • (a) 3 kWh
  • (b) 30 kWh
  • (c) 300 kWh
  • (d) 3000 kWh

9. The production of a current in a closed coil by a changing magnetic field is called:

  • (a) electromagnetic induction
  • (b) magnetic effect of current
  • (c) electrolysis
  • (d) electrostatic induction

10. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:

  • (a) increases as you move away from the wire
  • (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
  • (c) does not depend on the current
  • (d) is zero everywhere around the wire

Section B · Chemistry Q11–Q20 · 40 marks

11. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:

  • (a) combination
  • (b) decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) double displacement

12. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:

  • (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
  • (b) CuO and Ag2O
  • (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
  • (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

13. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 3
  • (c) 4
  • (d) 6

14. Bleaching powder is manufactured by passing chlorine gas over:

  • (a) dry slaked lime
  • (b) quicklime
  • (c) limestone
  • (d) gypsum

15. Baking soda is:

  • (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
  • (b) NaHCO₃
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Na₂CO₃

16. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:

  • (a) less reactive than silver
  • (b) more reactive than silver
  • (c) equally reactive
  • (d) an inert metal

17. Which pair is amphoteric?

  • (a) Na2O and CaO
  • (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
  • (c) CO2 and SO2
  • (d) CuO and FeO

18. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 6
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 8

19. Which compound decolorises bromine water?

  • (a) CH4
  • (b) C2H6
  • (c) C2H4
  • (d) C3H8

20. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?

  • (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
  • (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
  • (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
  • (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH

Section C · Mathematics Q21–Q30 · 40 marks

21. Which has a terminating decimal expansion?

  • (a) 7/24
  • (b) 5/13
  • (c) 9/40
  • (d) 11/21

22. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding sides are:

  • (a) equal
  • (b) proportional
  • (c) perpendicular
  • (d) parallel

23. Three points are collinear if the area of the triangle they form is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) maximum
  • (c) 0
  • (d) negative

24. tan 45° × cot 45° is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) 2
  • (d) undefined

25. The angle of depression is always measured from:

  • (a) the vertical
  • (b) the horizontal
  • (c) the object
  • (d) the ground below

26. Two tangents from an external point to a circle are:

  • (a) unequal
  • (b) equal
  • (c) always perpendicular
  • (d) parallel

27. The area of a semicircle of radius r is:

  • (a) πr²
  • (b) πr²/2
  • (c) 2πr
  • (d) πr²/4

28. The volume of a sphere of radius r is:

  • (a) (4/3)πr³
  • (b) 4πr²
  • (c) (2/3)πr³
  • (d) πr³

29. The class mark of the class 45–55 is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 50
  • (c) 55
  • (d) 100

30. The probability of an impossible event is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 0
  • (c) 1/2
  • (d) undefined

Section D · Biology Q31–Q35 · 20 marks

31. Which of these is an autotroph?

  • (a) mushroom
  • (b) Cuscuta
  • (c) green plant
  • (d) human being

32. The part of a neuron that receives the impulse is the:

  • (a) axon
  • (b) dendrite
  • (c) cell body
  • (d) myelin sheath

33. Sperms are produced in the:

  • (a) penis
  • (b) testes
  • (c) vas deferens
  • (d) seminal vesicles

34. The sex of a human child is determined by:

  • (a) the mother's egg
  • (b) the father's sperm
  • (c) both parents equally
  • (d) the diet of the mother

35. Which of these is a renewable resource?

  • (a) coal
  • (b) petroleum
  • (c) sunlight
  • (d) natural gas

Section E · Mental Ability Q36–Q40 · 20 marks

36. 5 : 30 :: 7 : ?

  • (a) 42
  • (b) 49
  • (c) 56
  • (d) 63

37. Which word is the odd one out?

  • (a) Cricket
  • (b) Football
  • (c) Chess
  • (d) Hockey

38. In a code language TABLE is written as ELBAT. How is CHAIR written?

  • (a) RIAHC
  • (b) RAIHC
  • (c) CHIAR
  • (d) RIHAC

39. A person facing north turns 90° clockwise and then 180° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?

  • (a) East
  • (b) West
  • (c) South
  • (d) North

40. Introducing a man, a woman said, “His mother is the only daughter of my mother.” How is the woman related to the man?

  • (a) Sister
  • (b) Aunt
  • (c) Mother
  • (d) Grandmother
