SECTION A - Physics (Q1-Q10, 40 marks)
--------------------------------------

Q1. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:
    (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
    (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
    (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
    (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

Q2. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?
    (a) plane
    (b) concave
    (c) convex
    (d) parabolic

Q3. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:
    (a) pupil
    (b) retina
    (c) ciliary muscles
    (d) iris

Q4. The clear sky appears blue because:
    (a) blue light is absorbed the most
    (b) shorter wavelengths are scattered more by air molecules
    (c) the sea reflects blue light upwards
    (d) the atmosphere refracts blue light towards the eye

Q5. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:
    (a) more resistance
    (b) less resistance
    (c) the same resistance
    (d) no resistance

Q6. The resistance of a wire doubles when:
    (a) its length is doubled
    (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
    (c) its length is halved
    (d) its diameter is doubled

Q7. One kilowatt hour equals:
    (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
    (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
    (c) 360 J
    (d) 6 × 10µ J

Q8. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:
    (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
    (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
    (c) the right-hand thumb rule
    (d) Lenz's law

Q9. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:
    (a) concentric circles
    (b) parallel straight lines
    (c) diverging curves
    (d) absent

Q10. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:
    (a) induced current in a coil
    (b) magnetic field due to a current
    (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
    (d) current produced by a generator


SECTION B - Chemistry (Q11-Q20, 40 marks)
-----------------------------------------

Q11. The reaction 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ used in black-and-white photography is a:
    (a) thermal decomposition
    (b) photochemical decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) combination reaction

Q12. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of:
    (a) NO₂
    (b) O₂
    (c) NO
    (d) PbO

Q13. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains:
    (a) NaCl
    (b) HCl
    (c) LiCl
    (d) KCl

Q14. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:
    (a) sodium chloride
    (b) sodium carbonate
    (c) calcium sulphate
    (d) ammonium chloride

Q15. Dilute acid added to an active metal produces:
    (a) oxygen
    (b) hydrogen
    (c) carbon dioxide
    (d) nitrogen

Q16. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:
    (a) reduction with carbon
    (b) self reduction
    (c) electrolytic reduction
    (d) roasting

Q17. The best conductor of electricity among these is:
    (a) copper
    (b) silver
    (c) aluminium
    (d) iron

Q18. Brass is an alloy of:
    (a) copper and tin
    (b) copper and zinc
    (c) lead and tin
    (d) mercury and zinc

Q19. Vinegar is a solution of:
    (a) 5–8% ethanoic acid in water
    (b) 50–80% ethanoic acid
    (c) pure ethanoic acid
    (d) ethanoic acid in alcohol

Q20. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)?
    (a) C₂H₆
    (b) C₂H₄
    (c) CH₄
    (d) C₃H₈


SECTION C - Mathematics (Q21-Q30, 40 marks)
-------------------------------------------

Q21. A polynomial of degree 3 is called:
    (a) linear
    (b) quadratic
    (c) cubic
    (d) biquadratic

Q22. If x + 2y = 5 and 3x + ky = 15 are coincident lines, then k = :
    (a) 2
    (b) 3
    (c) 6
    (d) 15

Q23. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are:
    (a) real and distinct
    (b) real and equal
    (c) not real
    (d) always integers

Q24. The sum of the first 10 natural numbers is:
    (a) 45
    (b) 50
    (c) 55
    (d) 100

Q25. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are:
    (a) equal
    (b) supplementary
    (c) complementary
    (d) proportional

Q26. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:
    (a) a
    (b) a²
    (c) 1
    (d) 2a

Q27. If sin θ = cos θ, then θ = :
    (a) 0°
    (b) 30°
    (c) 45°
    (d) 90°

Q28. A 10 m ladder reaches a window 8 m above the ground. Its foot is from the wall at a distance of:
    (a) 4 m
    (b) 6 m
    (c) 7 m
    (d) 9 m

Q29. The number of tangents from a point on the circle is:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1
    (c) 2
    (d) infinite

Q30. The area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm is:
    (a) 77 cm²
    (b) 154 cm²
    (c) 308 cm²
    (d) 616 cm²

---------------------------------------

    (a) mitochondria
    (b) chloroplast
    (c) ribosome
    (d) vacuole

Q32. The part of the brain that maintains the posture and balance of the body is:
    (a) cerebrum
    (b) cerebellum
    (c) pons
    (d) medulla

Q33. Which of these is an asexual mode of reproduction?
    (a) budding in yeast
    (b) pollination
    (c) fertilisation
    (d) seed germination

Q34. In a monohybrid cross between a tall (TT) and a dwarf (tt) pea plant, the F2 phenotypic ratio is:
    (a) 1:1
    (b) 3:1
    (c) 9:3:3:1
    (d) 1:2:1

Q35. In the food chain Grass → Deer → Lion, if 10,000 J of energy is available to the grass, the energy available to the lion is:
    (a) 1000 J
    (b) 100 J
    (c) 10 J
    (d) 1 J
