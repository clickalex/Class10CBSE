Section A · Physics Q1–Q10 · 40 marks

1. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:

  • (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

2. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?

  • (a) plane
  • (b) concave
  • (c) convex
  • (d) parabolic

3. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:

  • (a) pupil
  • (b) retina
  • (c) ciliary muscles
  • (d) iris

4. The clear sky appears blue because:

  • (a) blue light is absorbed the most
  • (b) shorter wavelengths are scattered more by air molecules
  • (c) the sea reflects blue light upwards
  • (d) the atmosphere refracts blue light towards the eye

5. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:

  • (a) more resistance
  • (b) less resistance
  • (c) the same resistance
  • (d) no resistance

6. The resistance of a wire doubles when:

  • (a) its length is doubled
  • (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
  • (c) its length is halved
  • (d) its diameter is doubled

7. One kilowatt hour equals:

  • (a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J
  • (b) 3.6 × 10³ J
  • (c) 360 J
  • (d) 6 × 10µ J

8. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:

  • (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
  • (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
  • (c) the right-hand thumb rule
  • (d) Lenz's law

9. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:

  • (a) concentric circles
  • (b) parallel straight lines
  • (c) diverging curves
  • (d) absent

10. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:

  • (a) induced current in a coil
  • (b) magnetic field due to a current
  • (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
  • (d) current produced by a generator

Section B · Chemistry Q11–Q20 · 40 marks

11. The reaction 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ used in black-and-white photography is a:

  • (a) thermal decomposition
  • (b) photochemical decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) combination reaction

12. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of:

  • (a) NO₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) NO
  • (d) PbO

13. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains:

  • (a) NaCl
  • (b) HCl
  • (c) LiCl
  • (d) KCl

14. The salt used to remove permanent hardness of water is:

  • (a) sodium chloride
  • (b) sodium carbonate
  • (c) calcium sulphate
  • (d) ammonium chloride

15. Dilute acid added to an active metal produces:

  • (a) oxygen
  • (b) hydrogen
  • (c) carbon dioxide
  • (d) nitrogen

16. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:

  • (a) reduction with carbon
  • (b) self reduction
  • (c) electrolytic reduction
  • (d) roasting

17. The best conductor of electricity among these is:

  • (a) copper
  • (b) silver
  • (c) aluminium
  • (d) iron

18. Brass is an alloy of:

  • (a) copper and tin
  • (b) copper and zinc
  • (c) lead and tin
  • (d) mercury and zinc

19. Vinegar is a solution of:

  • (a) 5–8% ethanoic acid in water
  • (b) 50–80% ethanoic acid
  • (c) pure ethanoic acid
  • (d) ethanoic acid in alcohol

20. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)?

  • (a) C₂H₆
  • (b) C₂H₄
  • (c) CH₄
  • (d) C₃H₈

Section C · Mathematics Q21–Q30 · 40 marks

21. A polynomial of degree 3 is called:

  • (a) linear
  • (b) quadratic
  • (c) cubic
  • (d) biquadratic

22. If x + 2y = 5 and 3x + ky = 15 are coincident lines, then k = :

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 3
  • (c) 6
  • (d) 15

23. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are:

  • (a) real and distinct
  • (b) real and equal
  • (c) not real
  • (d) always integers

24. The sum of the first 10 natural numbers is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 50
  • (c) 55
  • (d) 100

25. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are:

  • (a) equal
  • (b) supplementary
  • (c) complementary
  • (d) proportional

26. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:

  • (a) a
  • (b) a²
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 2a

27. If sin θ = cos θ, then θ = :

  • (a) 0°
  • (b) 30°
  • (c) 45°
  • (d) 90°

28. A 10 m ladder reaches a window 8 m above the ground. Its foot is from the wall at a distance of:

  • (a) 4 m
  • (b) 6 m
  • (c) 7 m
  • (d) 9 m

29. The number of tangents from a point on the circle is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) 2
  • (d) infinite

30. The area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm is:

  • (a) 77 cm²
  • (b) 154 cm²
  • (c) 308 cm²
  • (d) 616 cm²

Section D · Biology Q31–Q35 · 20 marks

31. Photosynthesis in a leaf takes place inside the:

  • (a) mitochondria
  • (b) chloroplast
  • (c) ribosome
  • (d) vacuole

32. The part of the brain that maintains the posture and balance of the body is:

  • (a) cerebrum
  • (b) cerebellum
  • (c) pons
  • (d) medulla

33. Which of these is an asexual mode of reproduction?

  • (a) budding in yeast
  • (b) pollination
  • (c) fertilisation
  • (d) seed germination

34. In a monohybrid cross between a tall (TT) and a dwarf (tt) pea plant, the F2 phenotypic ratio is:

  • (a) 1:1
  • (b) 3:1
  • (c) 9:3:3:1
  • (d) 1:2:1

35. In the food chain Grass → Deer → Lion, if 10,000 J of energy is available to the grass, the energy available to the lion is:

  • (a) 1000 J
  • (b) 100 J
  • (c) 10 J
  • (d) 1 J

37. Find the next letter: A, C, F, J, O, ?

38. ACE : BDF :: MOQ : ?

  • (b) NQS

39. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:30?

  • (a) 60°
  • (b) 75°
  • (c) 90°
  • (d) 105°

40. How many times does the digit 7 appear when the numbers from 1 to 100 are written out?

  • (a) 10
  • (b) 11
  • (c) 19
  • (d) 20
