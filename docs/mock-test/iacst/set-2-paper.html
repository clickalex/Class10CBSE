Section A · Physics Q1–Q10 · 40 marks

1. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:

  • (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

2. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?

  • (a) plane
  • (b) concave
  • (c) convex
  • (d) parabolic

3. A ray of light enters a denser medium along the normal (angle of incidence 0°). It:

  • (a) bends towards the normal
  • (b) bends away from the normal
  • (c) passes undeviated
  • (d) is totally reflected

4. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:

  • (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
  • (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
  • (c) concave, focal length 2 m
  • (d) convex, focal length 2 m

5. The least distance of distinct vision for a young adult with normal vision is about:

  • (a) 2.5 cm
  • (b) 25 cm
  • (c) 25 m
  • (d) infinity

6. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:

  • (a) more than 100 Ω
  • (b) less than 1 Ω
  • (c) 107 Ω
  • (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

7. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:

  • (a) joule
  • (b) watt
  • (c) kilowatt-hour
  • (d) volt

8. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:

  • (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
  • (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
  • (c) the right-hand thumb rule
  • (d) Lenz's law

9. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:

  • (a) concentric circles
  • (b) parallel straight lines
  • (c) diverging curves
  • (d) absent

10. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:

  • (a) increases as you move away from the wire
  • (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
  • (c) does not depend on the current
  • (d) is zero everywhere around the wire

Section B · Chemistry Q11–Q20 · 40 marks

11. The reaction 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ used in black-and-white photography is a:

  • (a) thermal decomposition
  • (b) photochemical decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) combination reaction

12. Which of these is a combination reaction?

  • (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
  • (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
  • (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
  • (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

13. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by:

  • (a) storing it in the open
  • (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
  • (c) adding oxidising agents
  • (d) heating it repeatedly

14. Which one is a strong base?

  • (a) NH4OH
  • (b) Mg(OH)2
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Cu(OH)2

15. Bleaching powder is manufactured by passing chlorine gas over:

  • (a) dry slaked lime
  • (b) quicklime
  • (c) limestone
  • (d) gypsum

16. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 10

17. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:

  • (a) MgCl
  • (b) MgCl₂
  • (c) Mg₂Cl
  • (d) MgCl₃

18. The best conductor of electricity among these is:

  • (a) copper
  • (b) silver
  • (c) aluminium
  • (d) iron

19. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?

  • (a) C₃H₈
  • (b) C₂H₆
  • (c) C₄H₁₀
  • (d) C₂H₄

20. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:

  • (a) H₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) CO₂
  • (d) CO

Section C · Mathematics Q21–Q30 · 40 marks

21. Which of these is irrational?

  • (a) √(16/9)
  • (b) √12
  • (c) 0.444…
  • (d) 22/7

22. In a right triangle with legs 6 cm and 8 cm, the hypotenuse is:

  • (a) 10 cm
  • (b) 12 cm
  • (c) 14 cm
  • (d) 9 cm

23. The centroid of the triangle with vertices (2, 3), (−4, 7) and (2, −4) is:

  • (a) (0, 2)
  • (b) (0, 0)
  • (c) (2, 2)
  • (d) (0, 3)

24. The value of sin²60° + 2 tan²45° − cos²30° is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 5/4
  • (d) 9/4

25. If the angle of elevation of the sun is 45°, the length of the shadow of a pole equals:

  • (a) half its height
  • (b) its height
  • (c) twice its height
  • (d) √3 times its height

26. Two tangents from an external point to a circle are:

  • (a) unequal
  • (b) equal
  • (c) always perpendicular
  • (d) parallel

27. The area of a semicircle of radius r is:

  • (a) πr²
  • (b) πr²/2
  • (c) 2πr
  • (d) πr²/4

28. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius r and height h is:

  • (a) 2πrh
  • (b) πr²h
  • (c) 2πr(r + h)
  • (d) πrl

29. The empirical relation connecting the three measures of central tendency is:

  • (a) Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean
  • (b) Median = 3 Mode − 2 Mean
  • (c) Mean = 3 Median − 2 Mode
  • (d) Mode = 2 Median − Mean

30. One card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck. The probability of a black queen is:

  • (a) 1/52
  • (b) 1/26
  • (c) 1/13
  • (d) 2/13

Section D · Biology Q31–Q35 · 20 marks

31. The main nitrogenous waste filtered out by the kidneys is:

  • (a) carbon dioxide
  • (b) urea
  • (c) glucose
  • (d) glycogen

32. The part of the brain that maintains the posture and balance of the body is:

  • (a) cerebrum
  • (b) cerebellum
  • (c) pons
  • (d) medulla

33. Which of the following is a barrier method of contraception?

  • (a) oral pill
  • (b) copper-T
  • (c) condom
  • (d) vasectomy

34. The sex of a human child is determined by:

  • (a) the mother's egg
  • (b) the father's sperm
  • (c) both parents equally
  • (d) the diet of the mother

35. Which of these is a renewable resource?

  • (a) coal
  • (b) petroleum
  • (c) sunlight
  • (d) natural gas

Section E · Mental Ability Q36–Q40 · 20 marks

36. If PEN is written as QFO, how is INK written?

  • (a) JOL
  • (b) JNL
  • (c) HMJ
  • (d) JOK

37. Ravi walks 10 m south, turns left and walks 10 m, then turns left again and walks 10 m. In which direction is he from his starting point?

  • (a) North
  • (b) South
  • (c) East
  • (d) West

38. Introducing a man, a woman said, “His mother is the only daughter of my mother.” How is the woman related to the man?

  • (a) Sister
  • (b) Aunt
  • (c) Mother
  • (d) Grandmother

39. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:30?

  • (a) 60°
  • (b) 75°
  • (c) 90°
  • (d) 105°

40. One tap fills a tank in 6 hours and another fills it in 3 hours. How long do both taps together take to fill the tank?

  • (a) 2 hours
  • (b) 3 hours
  • (c) 4.5 hours
  • (d) 9 hours
