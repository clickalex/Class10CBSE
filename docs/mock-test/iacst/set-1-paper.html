Section A · Physics Q1–Q10 · 40 marks

1. The power of a convex lens of focal length 50 cm is:

  • (a) +2 D
  • (b) −2 D
  • (c) +0.5 D
  • (d) −0.5 D

2. An object is placed 20 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. The image is formed:

  • (a) 10 cm in front of the mirror
  • (b) 20 cm in front of the mirror
  • (c) 20 cm behind the mirror
  • (d) at infinity

3. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:

  • (a) myopia
  • (b) hypermetropia
  • (c) astigmatism
  • (d) night blindness

4. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:

  • (a) hypermetropia
  • (b) myopia
  • (c) presbyopia
  • (d) cataract

5. Stars twinkle mainly because of:

  • (a) atmospheric refraction
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) reflection from clouds
  • (d) their own flickering

6. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:

  • (a) 3 kWh
  • (b) 30 kWh
  • (c) 300 kWh
  • (d) 3000 kWh

7. The resistance of a wire doubles when:

  • (a) its length is doubled
  • (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
  • (c) its length is halved
  • (d) its diameter is doubled

8. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:

  • (a) in series
  • (b) in parallel
  • (c) across the supply
  • (d) either way

9. The frequency of AC supply in India is 50 Hz. The direction of the current changes:

  • (a) 50 times per second
  • (b) 100 times per second
  • (c) 25 times per second
  • (d) 200 times per second

10. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:

  • (a) induced current in a coil
  • (b) magnetic field due to a current
  • (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
  • (d) current produced by a generator

Section B · Chemistry Q11–Q20 · 40 marks

11. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:

  • (a) combination
  • (b) decomposition
  • (c) displacement
  • (d) double displacement

12. Which of the following is an endothermic process?

  • (a) respiration
  • (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
  • (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
  • (d) quicklime + water

13. The pH of gastric juice released during digestion is:

  • (a) less than 7
  • (b) more than 7
  • (c) equal to 7
  • (d) equal to 0

14. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:

  • (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
  • (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
  • (c) CaSO₄
  • (d) CaCO₃

15. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:

  • (a) less reactive than silver
  • (b) more reactive than silver
  • (c) equally reactive
  • (d) an inert metal

16. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:

  • (a) reduction with carbon
  • (b) self reduction
  • (c) electrolytic reduction
  • (d) roasting

17. Galvanisation protects iron from rusting by coating it with a thin layer of:

  • (a) tin
  • (b) chromium
  • (c) zinc
  • (d) copper

18. The functional group present in an alcohol is:

  • (a) –CHO
  • (b) –COOH
  • (c) –OH
  • (d) –CO–

19. The IUPAC name of CH₃–CH₂–CHO is:

  • (a) propanol
  • (b) propanal
  • (c) propanone
  • (d) propanoic acid

20. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?

  • (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
  • (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
  • (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
  • (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH

Section C · Mathematics Q21–Q30 · 40 marks

21. Which has a terminating decimal expansion?

  • (a) 7/24
  • (b) 5/13
  • (c) 9/40
  • (d) 11/21

22. A quadratic polynomial can have at most:

  • (a) 0 zeroes
  • (b) 1 zero
  • (c) 2 zeroes
  • (d) 3 zeroes

23. The solution of x + y = 14 and x − y = 4 is:

  • (a) x = 9, y = 5
  • (b) x = 5, y = 9
  • (c) x = 10, y = 4
  • (d) x = 8, y = 6

24. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are:

  • (a) real and distinct
  • (b) real and equal
  • (c) not real
  • (d) always integers

25. The common difference of the AP whose nth term is 3n + 5 is:

  • (a) 3
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 8
  • (d) 2

26. The number of tangents from a point on the circle is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1
  • (c) 2
  • (d) infinite

27. The area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm is:

  • (a) 77 cm²
  • (b) 154 cm²
  • (c) 308 cm²
  • (d) 616 cm²

28. The slant height of a frustum with radii R and r and height h is:

  • (a) √[h² + (R + r)²]
  • (b) √[h² + (R − r)²]
  • (c) h + R + r
  • (d) √(R² − r²)

29. The class mark of the class 45–55 is:

  • (a) 45
  • (b) 50
  • (c) 55
  • (d) 100

30. Two dice are thrown together. The total number of outcomes is:

  • (a) 6
  • (b) 12
  • (c) 36
  • (d) 24

Section D · Biology Q31–Q35 · 20 marks

31. Photosynthesis in a leaf takes place inside the:

  • (a) mitochondria
  • (b) chloroplast
  • (c) ribosome
  • (d) vacuole

32. The gap between two neurons is called:

  • (a) dendrite
  • (b) synapse
  • (c) axon
  • (d) impulse

33. The anther contains:

  • (a) sepals
  • (b) ovules
  • (c) carpel
  • (d) pollen grains

34. In a monohybrid cross between a tall (TT) and a dwarf (tt) pea plant, the F2 phenotypic ratio is:

  • (a) 1:1
  • (b) 3:1
  • (c) 9:3:3:1
  • (d) 1:2:1

35. In the food chain Grass → Deer → Lion, if 10,000 J of energy is available to the grass, the energy available to the lion is:

  • (a) 1000 J
  • (b) 100 J
  • (c) 10 J
  • (d) 1 J

Section E · Mental Ability Q36–Q40 · 20 marks

36. Find the next number: 0, 3, 8, 15, 24, ?

  • (a) 33
  • (b) 34
  • (c) 35
  • (d) 36

37. Find the next letter: Z, X, V, T, R, ?

  • (a) Q
  • (b) P
  • (c) O
  • (d) N

38. 5 : 30 :: 7 : ?

  • (a) 42
  • (b) 49
  • (c) 56
  • (d) 63

39. Which word is the odd one out?

  • (a) Cricket
  • (b) Football
  • (c) Chess
  • (d) Hockey

40. The sum of the ages of a mother and her daughter is 50 years. Five years ago the mother was 7 times as old as the daughter. What is the daughter's present age?

  • (a) 8 years
  • (b) 10 years
  • (c) 12 years
  • (d) 15 years
