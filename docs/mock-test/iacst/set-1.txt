SECTION A - Physics (Q1-Q10, 40 marks)
--------------------------------------

Q1. The power of a convex lens of focal length 50 cm is:
    (a) +2 D
    (b) −2 D
    (c) +0.5 D
    (d) −0.5 D

Q2. An object is placed 20 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. The image is formed:
    (a) 10 cm in front of the mirror
    (b) 20 cm in front of the mirror
    (c) 20 cm behind the mirror
    (d) at infinity

Q3. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:
    (a) myopia
    (b) hypermetropia
    (c) astigmatism
    (d) night blindness

Q4. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:
    (a) hypermetropia
    (b) myopia
    (c) presbyopia
    (d) cataract

Q5. Stars twinkle mainly because of:
    (a) atmospheric refraction
    (b) dispersion
    (c) reflection from clouds
    (d) their own flickering

Q6. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:
    (a) 3 kWh
    (b) 30 kWh
    (c) 300 kWh
    (d) 3000 kWh

Q7. The resistance of a wire doubles when:
    (a) its length is doubled
    (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
    (c) its length is halved
    (d) its diameter is doubled

Q8. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:
    (a) in series
    (b) in parallel
    (c) across the supply
    (d) either way

Q9. The frequency of AC supply in India is 50 Hz. The direction of the current changes:
    (a) 50 times per second
    (b) 100 times per second
    (c) 25 times per second
    (d) 200 times per second

Q10. Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of the:
    (a) induced current in a coil
    (b) magnetic field due to a current
    (c) force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
    (d) current produced by a generator


SECTION B - Chemistry (Q11-Q20, 40 marks)
-----------------------------------------

Q11. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:
    (a) combination
    (b) decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) double displacement

Q12. Which of the following is an endothermic process?
    (a) respiration
    (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
    (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
    (d) quicklime + water

Q13. The pH of gastric juice released during digestion is:
    (a) less than 7
    (b) more than 7
    (c) equal to 7
    (d) equal to 0

Q14. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:
    (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
    (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
    (c) CaSO₄
    (d) CaCO₃

Q15. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:
    (a) less reactive than silver
    (b) more reactive than silver
    (c) equally reactive
    (d) an inert metal

Q16. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:
    (a) reduction with carbon
    (b) self reduction
    (c) electrolytic reduction
    (d) roasting

Q17. Galvanisation protects iron from rusting by coating it with a thin layer of:
    (a) tin
    (b) chromium
    (c) zinc
    (d) copper

Q18. The functional group present in an alcohol is:
    (a) –CHO
    (b) –COOH
    (c) –OH
    (d) –CO–

Q19. The IUPAC name of CH₃–CH₂–CHO is:
    (a) propanol
    (b) propanal
    (c) propanone
    (d) propanoic acid

Q20. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?
    (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
    (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
    (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
    (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH


SECTION C - Mathematics (Q21-Q30, 40 marks)
-------------------------------------------

Q21. Which has a terminating decimal expansion?
    (a) 7/24
    (b) 5/13
    (c) 9/40
    (d) 11/21

Q22. A quadratic polynomial can have at most:
    (a) 0 zeroes
    (b) 1 zero
    (c) 2 zeroes
    (d) 3 zeroes

Q23. The solution of x + y = 14 and x − y = 4 is:
    (a) x = 9, y = 5
    (b) x = 5, y = 9
    (c) x = 10, y = 4
    (d) x = 8, y = 6

Q24. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are:
    (a) real and distinct
    (b) real and equal
    (c) not real
    (d) always integers

Q25. The common difference of the AP whose nth term is 3n + 5 is:
    (a) 3
    (b) 5
    (c) 8
    (d) 2

Q26. The number of tangents from a point on the circle is:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1
    (c) 2
    (d) infinite

Q27. The area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm is:
    (a) 77 cm²
    (b) 154 cm²
    (c) 308 cm²
    (d) 616 cm²

Q28. The slant height of a frustum with radii R and r and height h is:
    (a) √[h² + (R + r)²]
    (b) √[h² + (R − r)²]
    (c) h + R + r
    (d) √(R² − r²)

Q29. The class mark of the class 45–55 is:
    (a) 45
    (b) 50
    (c) 55
    (d) 100

Q30. Two dice are thrown together. The total number of outcomes is:
    (a) 6
    (b) 12
    (c) 36
    (d) 24
