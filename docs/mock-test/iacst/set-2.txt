SECTION A - Physics (Q1-Q10, 40 marks)
--------------------------------------

Q1. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:
    (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
    (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
    (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
    (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

Q2. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?
    (a) plane
    (b) concave
    (c) convex
    (d) parabolic

Q3. A ray of light enters a denser medium along the normal (angle of incidence 0°). It:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) passes undeviated
    (d) is totally reflected

Q4. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:
    (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
    (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
    (c) concave, focal length 2 m
    (d) convex, focal length 2 m

Q5. The least distance of distinct vision for a young adult with normal vision is about:
    (a) 2.5 cm
    (b) 25 cm
    (c) 25 m
    (d) infinity

Q6. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:
    (a) more than 100 Ω
    (b) less than 1 Ω
    (c) 107 Ω
    (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

Q7. The commercial unit of electrical energy is:
    (a) joule
    (b) watt
    (c) kilowatt-hour
    (d) volt

Q8. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:
    (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
    (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
    (c) the right-hand thumb rule
    (d) Lenz's law

Q9. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:
    (a) concentric circles
    (b) parallel straight lines
    (c) diverging curves
    (d) absent

Q10. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:
    (a) increases as you move away from the wire
    (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
    (c) does not depend on the current
    (d) is zero everywhere around the wire


SECTION B - Chemistry (Q11-Q20, 40 marks)
-----------------------------------------

Q11. The reaction 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ used in black-and-white photography is a:
    (a) thermal decomposition
    (b) photochemical decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) combination reaction

Q12. Which of these is a combination reaction?
    (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
    (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
    (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
    (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

Q13. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by:
    (a) storing it in the open
    (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
    (c) adding oxidising agents
    (d) heating it repeatedly

Q14. Which one is a strong base?
    (a) NH4OH
    (b) Mg(OH)2
    (c) NaOH
    (d) Cu(OH)2

Q15. Bleaching powder is manufactured by passing chlorine gas over:
    (a) dry slaked lime
    (b) quicklime
    (c) limestone
    (d) gypsum

Q16. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:
    (a) 2
    (b) 5
    (c) 7
    (d) 10

Q17. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:
    (a) MgCl
    (b) MgCl₂
    (c) Mg₂Cl
    (d) MgCl₃

Q18. The best conductor of electricity among these is:
    (a) copper
    (b) silver
    (c) aluminium
    (d) iron

Q19. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?
    (a) C₃H₈
    (b) C₂H₆
    (c) C₄H₁₀
    (d) C₂H₄

Q20. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:
    (a) H₂
    (b) O₂
    (c) CO₂
    (d) CO


SECTION C - Mathematics (Q21-Q30, 40 marks)
-------------------------------------------

Q21. Which of these is irrational?
    (a) √(16/9)
    (b) √12
    (c) 0.444…
    (d) 22/7

Q22. In a right triangle with legs 6 cm and 8 cm, the hypotenuse is:
    (a) 10 cm
    (b) 12 cm
    (c) 14 cm
    (d) 9 cm

Q23. The centroid of the triangle with vertices (2, 3), (−4, 7) and (2, −4) is:
    (a) (0, 2)
    (b) (0, 0)
    (c) (2, 2)
    (d) (0, 3)

Q24. The value of sin²60° + 2 tan²45° − cos²30° is:
    (a) 1
    (b) 2
    (c) 5/4
    (d) 9/4

Q25. If the angle of elevation of the sun is 45°, the length of the shadow of a pole equals:
    (a) half its height
    (b) its height
    (c) twice its height
    (d) √3 times its height

Q26. Two tangents from an external point to a circle are:
    (a) unequal
    (b) equal
    (c) always perpendicular
    (d) parallel

Q27. The area of a semicircle of radius r is:
    (a) πr²
    (b) πr²/2
    (c) 2πr
    (d) πr²/4

Q28. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius r and height h is:
    (a) 2πrh
    (b) πr²h
    (c) 2πr(r + h)
    (d) πrl

Q29. The empirical relation connecting the three measures of central tendency is:
    (a) Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean
    (b) Median = 3 Mode − 2 Mean
    (c) Mean = 3 Median − 2 Mode
    (d) Mode = 2 Median − Mean

Q30. One card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck. The probability of a black queen is:
    (a) 1/52
    (b) 1/26
    (c) 1/13
    (d) 2/13
