SECTION A - Mathematics (Q1-Q22, 22 marks)
------------------------------------------

Q1. Which of these is irrational?
    (a) √(16/9)
    (b) √12
    (c) 0.444…
    (d) 22/7

Q2. A polynomial of degree 3 is called:
    (a) linear
    (b) quadratic
    (c) cubic
    (d) biquadratic

Q3. If a boat’s downstream speed is 12 km/h and upstream speed is 8 km/h, its still-water speed is:
    (a) 10
    (b) 4
    (c) 2
    (d) 20 km/h

Q4. Which one is NOT a quadratic equation?
    (a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0
    (b) (x + 1)² = 2(x − 3)
    (c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0
    (d) 2x² = 8

Q5. The common difference of the AP whose nth term is 3n + 5 is:
    (a) 3
    (b) 5
    (c) 8
    (d) 2

Q6. The sum of the first 100 natural numbers is:
    (a) 5000
    (b) 5050
    (c) 4950
    (d) 5100

Q7. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are:
    (a) equal
    (b) supplementary
    (c) complementary
    (d) proportional

Q8. If the corresponding sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 4 : 9, their areas are in the ratio:
    (a) 4 : 9
    (b) 2 : 3
    (c) 16 : 81
    (d) 8 : 27

Q9. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:
    (a) a
    (b) a²
    (c) 1
    (d) 2a

Q10. The centroid of the triangle with vertices (2, 3), (−4, 7) and (2, −4) is:
    (a) (0, 2)
    (b) (0, 0)
    (c) (2, 2)
    (d) (0, 3)

Q11. sin²30° + cos²30° equals:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1/2
    (c) 1
    (d) 3/4

Q12. The value of sin²60° + 2 tan²45° − cos²30° is:
    (a) 1
    (b) 2
    (c) 5/4
    (d) 9/4

Q13. The angle of depression is always measured from:
    (a) the vertical
    (b) the horizontal
    (c) the object
    (d) the ground below

Q14. The angle made by the line of sight with the horizontal when the object is below the observer is the angle of:
    (a) elevation
    (b) depression
    (c) reflection
    (d) inclination

Q15. A line that cuts a circle in two distinct points is called a:
    (a) tangent
    (b) secant
    (c) chord
    (d) diameter

Q16. A tangent of length 24 cm is drawn from a point 25 cm from the centre. The radius is:
    (a) 5 cm
    (b) 7 cm
    (c) 12 cm
    (d) 24 cm

Q17. The area of a quadrant of a circle of radius r is:
    (a) πr²/2
    (b) πr²/4
    (c) 2πr
    (d) πr²

Q18. The perimeter of a sector includes:
    (a) only the arc
    (b) the arc and one radius
    (c) the arc and two radii
    (d) the chord and the arc

Q19. The slant height of a frustum with radii R and r and height h is:
    (a) √[h² + (R + r)²]
    (b) √[h² + (R − r)²]
    (c) h + R + r
    (d) √(R² − r²)

Q20. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius r and height h is:
    (a) 2πrh
    (b) πr²h
    (c) 2πr(r + h)
    (d) πrl

Q21. If mean = 20 and mode = 26, then by the empirical relation the median is:
    (a) 22
    (b) 22.67
    (c) 23
    (d) 24

Q22. In one throw of a die, the probability of getting a number less than 7 is:
    (a) 0
    (b) 1/6
    (c) 5/6
    (d) 1


SECTION B - Physics (Q23-Q36, 14 marks)
---------------------------------------

Q23. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:
    (a) less than one
    (b) more than one
    (c) equal to one
    (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

Q24. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?
    (a) at F
    (b) at 2F
    (c) at infinity
    (d) between F and 2F

Q25. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:
    (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
    (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
    (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
    (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

Q26. The power of a convex lens of focal length 50 cm is:
    (a) +2 D
    (b) −2 D
    (c) +0.5 D
    (d) −0.5 D

Q27. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:
    (a) pupil
    (b) retina
    (c) ciliary muscles
    (d) iris

Q28. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:
    (a) myopia
    (b) hypermetropia
    (c) astigmatism
    (d) night blindness

Q29. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:
    (a) hypermetropia
    (b) myopia
    (c) presbyopia
    (d) cataract

Q30. Stars twinkle mainly because of:
    (a) atmospheric refraction
    (b) dispersion
    (c) reflection from clouds
    (d) their own flickering

Q31. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:
    (a) more than 100 Ω
    (b) less than 1 Ω
    (c) 107 Ω
    (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

Q32. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:
    (a) in series
    (b) in parallel
    (c) across the supply
    (d) either way

Q33. Three 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is:
    (a) 18 Ω
    (b) 6 Ω
    (c) 2 Ω
    (d) 3 Ω

Q34. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is:
    (a) AC generator has an electromagnet
    (b) DC generator has a commutator
    (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator
    (d) AC generator produces more power

Q35. The frequency of AC supply in India is 50 Hz. The direction of the current changes:
    (a) 50 times per second
    (b) 100 times per second
    (c) 25 times per second
    (d) 200 times per second

Q36. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:
    (a) concentric circles
    (b) parallel straight lines
    (c) diverging curves
    (d) absent


SECTION C - Chemistry (Q37-Q50, 14 marks)
-----------------------------------------

Q37. Which of the following is an endothermic process?
    (a) respiration
    (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
    (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
    (d) quicklime + water

Q38. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:
    (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
    (b) CuO and Ag2O
    (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
    (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

Q39. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of:
    (a) NO₂
    (b) O₂
    (c) NO
    (d) PbO

Q40. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:
    (a) 2
    (b) 5
    (c) 7
    (d) 10

Q41. Dilute acid added to an active metal produces:
    (a) oxygen
    (b) hydrogen
    (c) carbon dioxide
    (d) nitrogen

Q42. Baking soda is:
    (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
    (b) NaHCO₃
    (c) NaOH
    (d) Na₂CO₃

Q43. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:
    (a) MgCl
    (b) MgCl₂
    (c) Mg₂Cl
    (d) MgCl₃

Q44. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:
    (a) reduction with carbon
    (b) self reduction
    (c) electrolytic reduction
    (d) roasting

Q45. The best conductor of electricity among these is:
    (a) copper
    (b) silver
    (c) aluminium
    (d) iron

Q46. Galvanisation protects iron from rusting by coating it with a thin layer of:
    (a) tin
    (b) chromium
    (c) zinc
    (d) copper

Q47. The functional group present in an alcohol is:
    (a) –CHO
    (b) –COOH
    (c) –OH
    (d) –CO–

Q48. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:
    (a) H₂
    (b) O₂
    (c) CO₂
    (d) CO

Q49. The IUPAC name of CH₃–CH₂–CHO is:
    (a) propanol
    (b) propanal
    (c) propanone
    (d) propanoic acid

Q50. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?
    (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
    (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
    (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
    (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH
