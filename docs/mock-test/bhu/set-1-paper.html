Section A · Mathematics Q1–Q22 · 22 marks

1. Which of these is irrational?

  • (a) √(16/9)
  • (b) √12
  • (c) 0.444…
  • (d) 22/7

2. A polynomial of degree 3 is called:

  • (a) linear
  • (b) quadratic
  • (c) cubic
  • (d) biquadratic

3. If a boat’s downstream speed is 12 km/h and upstream speed is 8 km/h, its still-water speed is:

  • (a) 10
  • (b) 4
  • (c) 2
  • (d) 20 km/h

4. Which one is NOT a quadratic equation?

  • (a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0
  • (b) (x + 1)² = 2(x − 3)
  • (c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0
  • (d) 2x² = 8

5. The common difference of the AP whose nth term is 3n + 5 is:

  • (a) 3
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 8
  • (d) 2

6. The sum of the first 100 natural numbers is:

  • (a) 5000
  • (b) 5050
  • (c) 4950
  • (d) 5100

7. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are:

  • (a) equal
  • (b) supplementary
  • (c) complementary
  • (d) proportional

8. If the corresponding sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 4 : 9, their areas are in the ratio:

  • (a) 4 : 9
  • (b) 2 : 3
  • (c) 16 : 81
  • (d) 8 : 27

9. The distance of (a cos θ, a sin θ) from the origin is:

  • (a) a
  • (b) a²
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 2a

10. The centroid of the triangle with vertices (2, 3), (−4, 7) and (2, −4) is:

  • (a) (0, 2)
  • (b) (0, 0)
  • (c) (2, 2)
  • (d) (0, 3)

11. sin²30° + cos²30° equals:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1/2
  • (c) 1
  • (d) 3/4

12. The value of sin²60° + 2 tan²45° − cos²30° is:

  • (a) 1
  • (b) 2
  • (c) 5/4
  • (d) 9/4

13. The angle of depression is always measured from:

  • (a) the vertical
  • (b) the horizontal
  • (c) the object
  • (d) the ground below

14. The angle made by the line of sight with the horizontal when the object is below the observer is the angle of:

  • (a) elevation
  • (b) depression
  • (c) reflection
  • (d) inclination

15. A line that cuts a circle in two distinct points is called a:

  • (a) tangent
  • (b) secant
  • (c) chord
  • (d) diameter

16. A tangent of length 24 cm is drawn from a point 25 cm from the centre. The radius is:

  • (a) 5 cm
  • (b) 7 cm
  • (c) 12 cm
  • (d) 24 cm

17. The area of a quadrant of a circle of radius r is:

  • (a) πr²/2
  • (b) πr²/4
  • (c) 2πr
  • (d) πr²

18. The perimeter of a sector includes:

  • (a) only the arc
  • (b) the arc and one radius
  • (c) the arc and two radii
  • (d) the chord and the arc

19. The slant height of a frustum with radii R and r and height h is:

  • (a) √[h² + (R + r)²]
  • (b) √[h² + (R − r)²]
  • (c) h + R + r
  • (d) √(R² − r²)

20. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius r and height h is:

  • (a) 2πrh
  • (b) πr²h
  • (c) 2πr(r + h)
  • (d) πrl

21. If mean = 20 and mode = 26, then by the empirical relation the median is:

  • (a) 22
  • (b) 22.67
  • (c) 23
  • (d) 24

22. In one throw of a die, the probability of getting a number less than 7 is:

  • (a) 0
  • (b) 1/6
  • (c) 5/6
  • (d) 1

Section B · Physics Q23–Q36 · 14 marks

23. The magnification produced by a rear-view mirror fitted in vehicles is:

  • (a) less than one
  • (b) more than one
  • (c) equal to one
  • (d) more than one or less than one depending on the position of the object

24. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the same size?

  • (a) at F
  • (b) at 2F
  • (c) at infinity
  • (d) between F and 2F

25. The refractive index of water is 1.33. The speed of light in water is:

  • (a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (b) 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (c) 1.33 × 10⁸ m/s
  • (d) 4 × 10⁸ m/s

26. The power of a convex lens of focal length 50 cm is:

  • (a) +2 D
  • (b) −2 D
  • (c) +0.5 D
  • (d) −0.5 D

27. The change in focal length of the eye lens is caused by the action of the:

  • (a) pupil
  • (b) retina
  • (c) ciliary muscles
  • (d) iris

28. A person cannot read a newspaper without glasses. He is probably suffering from:

  • (a) myopia
  • (b) hypermetropia
  • (c) astigmatism
  • (d) night blindness

29. The eye defect in which the far point is nearer than infinity is:

  • (a) hypermetropia
  • (b) myopia
  • (c) presbyopia
  • (d) cataract

30. Stars twinkle mainly because of:

  • (a) atmospheric refraction
  • (b) dispersion
  • (c) reflection from clouds
  • (d) their own flickering

31. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is:

  • (a) more than 100 Ω
  • (b) less than 1 Ω
  • (c) 107 Ω
  • (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

32. An ammeter is connected in a circuit:

  • (a) in series
  • (b) in parallel
  • (c) across the supply
  • (d) either way

33. Three 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is:

  • (a) 18 Ω
  • (b) 6 Ω
  • (c) 2 Ω
  • (d) 3 Ω

34. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is:

  • (a) AC generator has an electromagnet
  • (b) DC generator has a commutator
  • (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator
  • (d) AC generator produces more power

35. The frequency of AC supply in India is 50 Hz. The direction of the current changes:

  • (a) 50 times per second
  • (b) 100 times per second
  • (c) 25 times per second
  • (d) 200 times per second

36. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:

  • (a) concentric circles
  • (b) parallel straight lines
  • (c) diverging curves
  • (d) absent

Section C · Chemistry Q37–Q50 · 14 marks

37. Which of the following is an endothermic process?

  • (a) respiration
  • (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
  • (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
  • (d) quicklime + water

38. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively:

  • (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
  • (b) CuO and Ag2O
  • (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
  • (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

39. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of:

  • (a) NO₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) NO
  • (d) PbO

40. A solution turns colourless phenolphthalein pink. Its pH is likely to be:

  • (a) 2
  • (b) 5
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 10

41. Dilute acid added to an active metal produces:

  • (a) oxygen
  • (b) hydrogen
  • (c) carbon dioxide
  • (d) nitrogen

42. Baking soda is:

  • (a) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
  • (b) NaHCO₃
  • (c) NaOH
  • (d) Na₂CO₃

43. Magnesium (2, 8, 2) combines with chlorine (2, 8, 7) to form an ionic compound with the formula:

  • (a) MgCl
  • (b) MgCl₂
  • (c) Mg₂Cl
  • (d) MgCl₃

44. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is:

  • (a) reduction with carbon
  • (b) self reduction
  • (c) electrolytic reduction
  • (d) roasting

45. The best conductor of electricity among these is:

  • (a) copper
  • (b) silver
  • (c) aluminium
  • (d) iron

46. Galvanisation protects iron from rusting by coating it with a thin layer of:

  • (a) tin
  • (b) chromium
  • (c) zinc
  • (d) copper

47. The functional group present in an alcohol is:

  • (a) –CHO
  • (b) –COOH
  • (c) –OH
  • (d) –CO–

48. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is:

  • (a) H₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) CO₂
  • (d) CO

49. The IUPAC name of CH₃–CH₂–CHO is:

  • (a) propanol
  • (b) propanal
  • (c) propanone
  • (d) propanoic acid

50. Which pair belongs to the same homologous series?

  • (a) CH₄ and C₂H₄
  • (b) C₂H₆ and C₃H₈
  • (c) C₂H₄ and C₂H₂
  • (d) CH₃OH and CH₃COOH
