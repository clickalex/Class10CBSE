SECTION A - Mathematics (Q1-Q22, 22 marks)
------------------------------------------

Q1. The HCF of 8 and 12 is:
    (a) 2
    (b) 4
    (c) 8
    (d) 24

Q2. If the HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 60, one number being 12, the other is:
    (a) 20
    (b) 30
    (c) 40
    (d) 60

Q3. If one zero of x² − 4x + 1 is 2 + √3, the other zero is:
    (a) 2 − √3
    (b) −2 − √3
    (c) √3 − 2
    (d) −√3

Q4. A quadratic polynomial can have at most:
    (a) 0 zeroes
    (b) 1 zero
    (c) 2 zeroes
    (d) 3 zeroes

Q5. For what value of k does kx + 3y = 7 and 2x + 6y = 14 have infinitely many solutions?
    (a) 1
    (b) −1
    (c) 2
    (d) 1/2

Q6. The solution of x + y = 14 and x − y = 4 is:
    (a) x = 9, y = 5
    (b) x = 5, y = 9
    (c) x = 10, y = 4
    (d) x = 8, y = 6

Q7. The roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0 are:
    (a) 2, 3
    (b) −2, −3
    (c) 1, 6
    (d) −1, −6

Q8. If the roots of x² − 8x + k = 0 are equal, then k = :
    (a) 8
    (b) 16
    (c) −16
    (d) 64

Q9. The sum of the first 10 natural numbers is:
    (a) 45
    (b) 50
    (c) 55
    (d) 100

Q10. S₅ − S₄ equals:
    (a) the 5th term
    (b) the 4th term
    (c) the 9th term
    (d) zero

Q11. The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is 25:49. The ratio of their corresponding sides is:
    (a) 25:49
    (b) 5:7
    (c) 7:5
    (d) 49:25

Q12. In a right triangle with legs 6 cm and 8 cm, the hypotenuse is:
    (a) 10 cm
    (b) 12 cm
    (c) 14 cm
    (d) 9 cm

Q13. The distance of the point (3, 4) from the origin is:
    (a) 5
    (b) 7
    (c) 25
    (d) 12

Q14. The value of cos 60° is:
    (a) 1/2
    (b) √3/2
    (c) 1/√2
    (d) 1

Q15. If the angle of elevation of the sun is 45°, the length of the shadow of a pole equals:
    (a) half its height
    (b) its height
    (c) twice its height
    (d) √3 times its height

Q16. Two tangents from an external point to a circle are:
    (a) unequal
    (b) equal
    (c) always perpendicular
    (d) parallel

Q17. The area of a semicircle of radius r is:
    (a) πr²
    (b) πr²/2
    (c) 2πr
    (d) πr²/4

Q18. A cone, a hemisphere and a cylinder have equal bases and equal heights. The ratio of their volumes is:
    (a) 1 : 2 : 3
    (b) 2 : 3 : 1
    (c) 3 : 1 : 2
    (d) 1 : 3 : 2

Q19. The class mark of the class 15–25 is:
    (a) 15
    (b) 20
    (c) 25
    (d) 10

Q20. The median class is the class whose cumulative frequency is:
    (a) equal to N/2
    (b) the first one greater than N/2
    (c) the largest
    (d) the smallest

Q21. The probability of an impossible event is:
    (a) 1
    (b) 0
    (c) 1/2
    (d) undefined

Q22. Two dice are thrown together. The total number of outcomes is:
    (a) 6
    (b) 12
    (c) 36
    (d) 24


SECTION B - Physics (Q23-Q36, 14 marks)
---------------------------------------

Q23. Which mirror always forms an erect and diminished image?
    (a) plane
    (b) concave
    (c) convex
    (d) parabolic

Q24. When a ray of light goes from glass into air, it:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) does not bend
    (d) is totally absorbed

Q25. A ray of light enters a denser medium along the normal (angle of incidence 0°). It:
    (a) bends towards the normal
    (b) bends away from the normal
    (c) passes undeviated
    (d) is totally reflected

Q26. A student with myopia is prescribed a lens of power −2.0 D. The lens is:
    (a) convex, focal length 50 cm
    (b) concave, focal length 50 cm
    (c) concave, focal length 2 m
    (d) convex, focal length 2 m

Q27. The splitting of white light into its component colours is called:
    (a) scattering
    (b) dispersion
    (c) total internal reflection
    (d) polarisation

Q28. The least distance of distinct vision for a young adult with normal vision is about:
    (a) 2.5 cm
    (b) 25 cm
    (c) 25 m
    (d) infinity

Q29. Two wires of the same material and length but different cross-sectional areas are compared. The thicker wire has:
    (a) more resistance
    (b) less resistance
    (c) the same resistance
    (d) no resistance

Q30. A 100 W bulb is used for 10 hours every day. The electrical energy it consumes in 30 days is:
    (a) 3 kWh
    (b) 30 kWh
    (c) 300 kWh
    (d) 3000 kWh

Q31. The resistance of a wire doubles when:
    (a) its length is doubled
    (b) its area of cross-section is doubled
    (c) its length is halved
    (d) its diameter is doubled

Q32. A 12 V battery drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. The resistance of the resistor is:
    (a) 24 Ω
    (b) 6 Ω
    (c) 0.17 Ω
    (d) 14 Ω

Q33. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by:
    (a) Fleming's left-hand rule
    (b) Fleming's right-hand rule
    (c) the right-hand thumb rule
    (d) Lenz's law

Q34. An electric motor converts:
    (a) electrical energy into mechanical energy
    (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy
    (c) heat into light
    (d) chemical energy into sound

Q35. The insulation on the earth wire in a domestic circuit is:
    (a) red
    (b) black
    (c) green
    (d) blue

Q36. The magnetic field at a point near a long straight current-carrying wire:
    (a) increases as you move away from the wire
    (b) decreases as you move away from the wire
    (c) does not depend on the current
    (d) is zero everywhere around the wire


SECTION C - Chemistry (Q37-Q50, 14 marks)
-----------------------------------------

Q37. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of:
    (a) combination
    (b) decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) double displacement

Q38. The reaction 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ used in black-and-white photography is a:
    (a) thermal decomposition
    (b) photochemical decomposition
    (c) displacement
    (d) combination reaction

Q39. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by:
    (a) storing it in the open
    (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
    (c) adding oxidising agents
    (d) heating it repeatedly

Q40. When the equation Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ is balanced, the coefficient of steam (H₂O) is:
    (a) 2
    (b) 3
    (c) 4
    (d) 6

Q41. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains:
    (a) NaCl
    (b) HCl
    (c) LiCl
    (d) KCl

Q42. Which one is a strong base?
    (a) NH4OH
    (b) Mg(OH)2
    (c) NaOH
    (d) Cu(OH)2

Q43. The pH of gastric juice released during digestion is:
    (a) less than 7
    (b) more than 7
    (c) equal to 7
    (d) equal to 0

Q44. The chemical formula of Plaster of Paris is:
    (a) CaSO₄·2H₂O
    (b) CaSO₄·½H₂O
    (c) CaSO₄
    (d) CaCO₃

Q45. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is:
    (a) less reactive than silver
    (b) more reactive than silver
    (c) equally reactive
    (d) an inert metal

Q46. Which pair is amphoteric?
    (a) Na2O and CaO
    (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
    (c) CO2 and SO2
    (d) CuO and FeO

Q47. Which metal reacts vigorously with cold water?
    (a) iron
    (b) zinc
    (c) sodium
    (d) copper

Q48. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is:
    (a) 5
    (b) 6
    (c) 7
    (d) 8

Q49. Which hydrocarbon undergoes an addition reaction with hydrogen in the presence of a nickel catalyst?
    (a) C₃H₈
    (b) C₂H₆
    (c) C₄H₁₀
    (d) C₂H₄

Q50. Vinegar is a solution of:
    (a) 5–8% ethanoic acid in water
    (b) 50–80% ethanoic acid
    (c) pure ethanoic acid
    (d) ethanoic acid in alcohol
