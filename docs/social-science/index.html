Assessment at a glance

ComponentMarksPreparation
Theory — History 20, Geography 20, Political Science 20, Economics 20**80**Points, not paragraphs. Five distinct points for a 5-mark answer.
Map work — printed inside the History and Geography sectionsincludedPure recall from the official list of map items. Do not skip it.
Internal assessment — periodic tests, the Economics project, portfolio**20**The Consumer Rights project is the usual internal project.
Total100Three hours for the whole paper.

Topper’s study order

  1. Political Science first — five short chapters, 20 marks, fastest to finish.
  2. Economics — five chapters, definition-plus-example answers.
  3. Geography, and do the map items as you go rather than leaving them to the end.
  4. History last — the longest reading, so start it early in the year and revise late.
  5. Then one full three-hour paper: the real difficulty here is finishing in time, not the content.
