Written questions · Write

SHORTS13 marks

1. What is the difference between prudential and moral reasons for power sharing?

Show Answer

Ans:

Prudential reasons stress the good consequences of power sharing — it reduces conflict between social groups, ensures the stability of political order and prevents violence and political instability. Moral reasons stress the intrinsic worth of power sharing — that people have a right to be consulted on how they are to be governed, and that a legitimate government is one in which citizens acquire a stake in the system through participation. Prudential reasons are about outcomes; moral reasons are about principles.

LONGL25 marks

2. Describe the major steps taken towards power sharing in Belgium.

Show Answer

Ans:

Equal representation in the central government: the constitution prescribes an equal number of Dutch-speaking and French-speaking ministers, so no single community can take decisions unilaterally.
Devolution to the regions: many powers of the central government were given to the state governments of the Flemish and Walloon regions, and these regional governments are not subordinate to the central government.
Brussels: the capital was given a separate government with equal representation for both the French- and Dutch-speaking groups.
Community government: a third kind of government was set up, elected by people belonging to one language group — Dutch, French or German-speaking — irrespective of where they live, with power over cultural, educational and language-related issues.
This arrangement, worked out between 1932 and 1968 through four constitutional amendments, prevented civic strife and the division of the country on linguistic lines.

LONGL35 marks

3. What were the majoritarian measures adopted by the Sri Lankan government? What were their consequences?

Show Answer

Ans:

In 1956 an act was passed to recognise Sinhala as the only official language, disregarding Tamil.
Successive governments followed preferential policies that favoured Sinhala applicants for university positions and government employment.
The new constitution stipulated that the state shall protect and foster Buddhism.
Consequences: the Sri Lankan Tamils felt that none of the major political parties, led by the Buddhist Sinhala leaders, was sensitive to their language and culture. Their demand for recognition of Tamil as an official language, regional autonomy and equal opportunity was repeatedly denied, and the demand for a separate Tamil Eelam grew. This led to a civil war that lasted until 2009, in which thousands of people were killed and the social, cultural and economic life of the country was badly affected.

SHORTS43 marks

4. What are the different forms of power sharing in modern democracies? Give one example of each.

Show Answer

Ans:

Horizontal distribution among organs of government — legislature, executive and judiciary, as in India, where the judiciary can review the acts of the legislature. Vertical distribution among governments at different levels — Union, state and local governments in India. Distribution among social groups — the community government in Belgium and reserved constituencies for weaker sections and women in India. Distribution among political parties — coalition governments formed by two or more parties when no single party gains a clear majority.

SHORTS53 marks

5. What is a system of checks and balances?

Show Answer

Ans:

It is the arrangement in which the organs of government — legislature, executive and judiciary — are placed at the same level but each exercises a check on the others. The legislature makes laws, the executive implements them and the judiciary can declare a law invalid if it violates the constitution. Because of these checks no organ can exercise unlimited power, and this horizontal distribution of power is a basic feature of democracy.
