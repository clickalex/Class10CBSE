MCQs

Which industry is called a basic and key industry? (a) cotton textile (b) iron and steel (c) sugar (d) cement

(b) iron and steel — all other industries depend on it for their machinery.

Which city has emerged as the electronic capital of India? (a) Mumbai (b) Hyderabad (c) Bengaluru (d) Chennai

(c) Bengaluru — it is also called the Silicon Valley of India.

Hot water from factories drained into rivers before cooling causes: (a) air pollution (b) water pollution (c) thermal pollution (d) noise pollution

(c) thermal pollution, which affects aquatic life.

Written questions

3 marks Why is the iron and steel industry called a basic industry? Give three reasons.

It is called a basic industry because all other industries — heavy, medium and light — depend on it for their machinery, and because production and consumption of steel are regarded as the index of a country's level of development. It is also a heavy industry, since both its raw materials and its finished products are heavy and bulky, so transport costs are a major part of its total cost.

5 marks Describe the factors responsible for the location of the jute textile industry along the banks of the Hooghly.

Proximity of the jute-producing areas — the jute belt of West Bengal, Bihar, Odisha and Assam lies close by.
Inexpensive water transport, supported by a good network of railways, roadways and waterways for the movement of jute to the mills.
Abundant water for processing raw jute, which the Hooghly supplies.
Cheap labour from West Bengal and the adjoining states of Bihar, Odisha and Uttar Pradesh.
Kolkata as a large urban centre provides banking, insurance, port facilities for export and other commercial services.
As a result India is the largest producer of raw jute and jute goods in the world, though it faces competition from synthetic substitutes and from other producers such as Bangladesh.

5 marks What are the different types of industrial pollution? How can it be controlled?

Types: (1) Air pollution — caused by chemical and paper factories, brick kilns, refineries and smelting plants burning fossil fuels, and by toxic gas leaks such as the Bhopal tragedy; (2) Water pollution — caused by organic and inorganic industrial wastes and effluents discharged into rivers by paper, pulp, chemical, textile and dyeing, petroleum refineries and tanneries; (3) Thermal pollution — hot water from factories and power plants drained into rivers and ponds before cooling; (4) Noise pollution — from industrial and construction activity, machinery and generators, causing hearing impairment and stress.
Control: particulate matter can be controlled by fitting electrostatic precipitators, fabric filters, scrubbers and inertial separators to smokestacks; smoke can be reduced by using oil instead of coal; water pollution can be minimised by reusing and recycling water in two or more successive stages, harvesting rainwater, and treating hot water and effluents before releasing them, in three stages — primary (mechanical), secondary (biological) and tertiary (chemical and physical); noise can be reduced by fitting silencers on generators and machinery, redesigning machines and providing workers with earplugs.

3 marks Why has the sugar industry shifted to southern and western states, especially Maharashtra?

Because the sugarcane grown there has a higher sucrose content, the cooler climate ensures a longer crushing season, and the cooperatives in these states are more successful, so the mills get a better and more regular supply of cane than in the older north Indian centres.

3 marks How has the information technology industry contributed to India's economy?

It has generated large employment, especially for women, and has been a major earner of foreign exchange through the rapidly growing business process outsourcing (BPO) sector. Software technology parks provide single-window services and high data communication facilities, and Bengaluru has become the electronic capital of India. Continued growth in hardware and software depends on a skilled workforce and good connectivity.
