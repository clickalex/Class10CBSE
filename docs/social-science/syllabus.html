Removed from the syllabus

  • Democracy and Diversity — full chapter removed.
  • Popular Struggles and Movements — full chapter removed.
  • Challenges to Democracy — full chapter removed.
  • The Age of Industrialisation is still printed in the History book but is not assessed in the board paper.
  • The Making of a Global World: only subtopics 1 to 1.3 are examined.
  • Manufacturing Industries: much of the old industry-by-industry detail was trimmed.
  • Lifelines of National Economy: map pointing only.
