Ch 1 · The Rise of Nationalism in Europe (8)

MCQM11 mark

1. The Zollverein, started in 1834, was: (a) a French army (b) a customs union formed at Prussia's initiative (c) the parliament at Frankfurt (d) an Italian secret society

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) — it abolished tariff barriers, reduced the number of currencies from over thirty to two, and helped bind the Germans economically.

MCQM21 mark

2. Who proclaimed the German Empire in 1871? (a) Napoleon Bonaparte (b) Kaiser Wilhelm I (c) Otto von Bismarck (d) Friedrich Wilhelm IV

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) Kaiser Wilhelm I of Prussia, at Versailles in January 1871, with Bismarck as the architect of the process.

MCQM31 mark

(b) Germania — oak leaves stand for heroism.

MCQM61 mark

6. The Treaty of Vienna was signed in: (a) 1789 (b) 1815 (c) 1848 (d) 1871

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 1815. Its aim was to undo the changes Napoleon had made and to restore the old monarchies of Europe.

MCQM71 mark

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) Metternich. Austria's chief minister, who represented the conservative order established at Vienna.

MCQM81 mark

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) Cavour and Victor Emmanuel II, helped by Garibaldi's Red Shirts. Diplomacy and popular revolt together completed unification by 1870.

MCQM71 mark

Show Answer

MCQM81 mark

(b) the Chauri Chaura incident. In February 1922 an angry crowd burnt a police station, killing 22 policemen, and Gandhiji withdrew the movement.

Ch 5 · Resources and Development (8)

MCQM61 mark

6. Laterite soil develops mainly because of: (a) heavy leaching (b) glacial deposition (c) volcanic eruption (d) wind deposition

Show Answer

Ans:

Show Answer
