CBSE • CLASS 10 • SOCIAL SCIENCE

Social Science — Practical & internal assessment

Social Science has no lab exam, but it has map work inside the paper and a project in the internal assessment.

  • Map items (History): centres of the 1857 revolt, Congress sessions, sites of the Non-Cooperation and Civil Disobedience movements, major plantations and industrial regions.
  • Map items (Geography): dams, soil types, major crops, mineral belts, oil fields, steel plants, cotton and woollen textile centres, software parks, sea ports and airports.
  • How to practise: take a blank political outline map of India and point every item from the official list without looking. Repeat until you can do the whole list in ten minutes.
  • Project: the Consumer Rights chapter is assessed as project work — a survey of a local shop, an ISI/BIS label study, or a consumer-court case write-up all work well.
