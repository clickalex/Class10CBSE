MCQs · Drill

MCQM11 mark

1. The 10th term of the AP 2, 7, 12, … is: (a) 52 (b) 50 (c) 47 (d) 45

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) 47. a₁₀ = 2 + 9(5).

MCQM21 mark

2. The sum of the first 10 natural numbers is: (a) 100 (b) 55 (c) 50 (d) 45

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 55. n(n+1)/2 = 10 × 11/2.

MCQM31 mark

3. S₅ − S₄ equals: (a) the 5th term (b) the 4th term (c) the 9th term (d) zero

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) The 5th term. That is what aₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁ means.

MCQM41 mark

4. Which of the following is NOT an AP? (a) 2, 5, 8, 11, … (b) 1, 4, 9, 16, … (c) −3, −1, 1, 3, … (d) 7, 7, 7, 7, …

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 1, 4, 9, 16, …. The differences 3, 5, 7 are not equal, so 1, 4, 9, 16 has no common difference. The others have d = 3, 2 and 0.

MCQM51 mark

5. The sum of the first n odd natural numbers 1, 3, 5, … is: (a) n(n + 1)/2 (b) 2n (c) n² (d) n(n + 1)

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) n². Here a = 1, d = 2, so Sₙ = (n/2)[2 + (n − 1)2] = n².

MCQM61 mark

6. The common difference of the AP whose nth term is 3n + 5 is: (a) 5 (b) 8 (c) 2 (d) 3

Show Answer

Ans:

(d) 3. a₁ = 8 and a₂ = 11, so d = 3 — the coefficient of n.

MCQM71 mark

7. If the 3rd term of an AP is 4 and the 9th term is −8, its first term is: (a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 8

Show Answer

Ans:

(d) 8. a + 2d = 4 and a + 8d = −8 give 6d = −12, so d = −2 and a = 8.

MCQM81 mark

8. The sum of the first 100 natural numbers is: (a) 5050 (b) 5000 (c) 4950 (d) 5100

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) 5050. S = 100 × 101/2 = 5050.

Written questions · Write

SHORTS12 marks

1. Find the 20th term from the last term of the AP 3, 8, 13, …, 253.

Show Answer

Ans:

Counting from the end, the AP is 253, 248, 243, … with a = 253 and d = −5.
20th term = 253 + 19(−5) = 158.

SHORTS22 marks

2. Find the sum of the first 20 terms of the AP 7, 10, 13, …

Show Answer

Ans:

Here a = 7, d = 3 and n = 20. S₂₀ = (20/2)[2(7) + 19(3)] = 10(14 + 57) = 10 × 71 = 710.

SHORTS33 marks

3. Which term of the AP 3, 15, 27, 39, … will be 132 more than its 54th term?

Show Answer

Ans:

54th term = 3 + 53(12) = 639.
Required term = 639 + 132 = 771.
3 + (n − 1)12 = 771 gives (n − 1)12 = 768, so n = 65.

SHORTS43 marks

4. If the sum of the first n terms of an AP is 3n² + 5n, find its 10th term.

Show Answer

Ans:

aₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁.
S₁₀ = 300 + 50 = 350 and S₉ = 243 + 45 = 288.
a₁₀ = 350 − 288 = 62.

APPLICATIONA53 marks

5. A student finds the 12th term of the AP 5, 9, 13, … as 5 + 12 × 4 = 53. Find the error and give the correct term, with a quick check.

Show Answer

Ans:

Error: the formula is aₙ = a + (n − 1)d, not a + nd. The student counted 12 differences instead of 11.
Correct: a₁₂ = 5 + 11 × 4 = 49.
Check by listing: 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25, 29, 33, 37, 41, 45, 49 — the 12th term is 49 ✓.

APPLICATIONA63 marks

6. A taxi charges Rs 15 for the first kilometre and Rs 8 for every additional kilometre. Show that the fares for 1 km, 2 km, 3 km, … form an AP and find the fare for 12 km.

Show Answer

Ans:

Fares: 15, 23, 31, … — each is Rs 8 more than the previous, so they form an AP with a = 15 and d = 8.
The fare for n km is aₙ = 15 + (n − 1) × 8.
For 12 km: a₁₂ = 15 + 11 × 8 = Rs 103.

SHORTS73 marks

7. How many terms of the AP 24, 21, 18, … must be taken to give a sum of 78? Explain the two answers.

Show Answer

Ans:

Sₙ = (n/2)[2(24) + (n − 1)(−3)] = n(51 − 3n)/2 = 78.
⇒ 3n² − 51n + 156 = 0 ⇒ n² − 17n + 52 = 0 ⇒ (n − 4)(n − 13) = 0.
So n = 4 or n = 13. Both are valid: the terms from the 5th to the 13th are negative and cancel out the extra positive terms.

COMPETENCYC84 marks

8. Read and answer: Rohan saves Rs 500 in the first month and increases his saving by Rs 50 every month. (a) Write the first three terms of the AP formed by his monthly savings. (b) How much does he save in the 12th month? (c) How much has he saved in 12 months altogether?

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) 500, 550, 600, … with a = 500 and d = 50.
(b) a₁₂ = 500 + 11 × 50 = Rs 1050.
(c) S₁₂ = (12/2)(500 + 1050) = 6 × 1550 = Rs 9300.

COMPETENCYC94 marks

9. Read and answer: A hall has 20 rows of seats. The first row has 18 seats and every next row has 2 more seats than the one before. (a) How many seats are in the last row? (b) How many seats are there in all? (c) How much is collected from a full house at Rs 100 per seat?

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) a₂₀ = 18 + 19 × 2 = 56 seats.
(b) S₂₀ = (20/2)(18 + 56) = 10 × 74 = 740 seats.
(c) 740 × 100 = Rs 74,000.

LONGL104 marks

10. The first term of an AP is 5, the last term is 45 and the sum of all terms is 400. Find the number of terms and the common difference.

Show Answer

Ans:

Sₙ = (n/2)(a + l) ⇒ 400 = (n/2)(5 + 45) = 25n ⇒ n = 16.
l = a + (n − 1)d ⇒ 45 = 5 + 15d ⇒ d = 40/15 = 8/3.
So there are 16 terms with common difference 8/3.

LONGL115 marks

11. A sum of Rs 700 is to be used to give seven cash prizes, each prize being Rs 20 less than its preceding prize. Find the value of each prize.

Show Answer

Ans:

Seven prizes in AP with d = −20 and S₇ = 700.
700 = (7/2)[2a + 6(−20)] = (7/2)(2a − 120).
200 = 2a − 120, so a = 160.
The prizes are Rs 160, 140, 120, 100, 80, 60 and 40.

LONGL125 marks

12. The sum of the 4th and 8th terms of an AP is 24 and the sum of its 6th and 10th terms is 44. Find the first three terms of the AP.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a + 3d) + (a + 7d) = 24, so a + 5d = 12 … (1)
(a + 5d) + (a + 9d) = 44, so a + 7d = 22 … (2)
Subtracting (1) from (2): 2d = 10, so d = 5. Then a = 12 − 25 = −13.
The first three terms are −13, −8, −3. Check: a₄ + a₈ = 2 + 22 = 24 ✓ and a₆ + a₁₀ = 12 + 32 = 44 ✓.
