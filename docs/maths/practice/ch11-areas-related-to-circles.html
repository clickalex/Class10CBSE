MCQs · Drill

MCQM11 mark

1. The area of a quadrant of a circle of radius r is: (a) πr²/2 (b) πr²/4 (c) 2πr (d) πr²

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) πr²/4.

MCQM21 mark

2. The perimeter of a sector includes: (a) only the arc (b) the arc and one radius (c) the arc and two radii (d) the chord and the arc

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) The arc and two radii.

MCQM31 mark

3. The area of a segment is found by: (a) sector − triangle (b) sector + triangle (c) circle − sector (d) triangle − sector

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Sector − triangle.

MCQM41 mark

4. The length of the arc of a sector of angle θ in a circle of radius r is: (a) (θ/360°) × πr² (b) (θ/180°) × πr² (c) (θ/360°) × 2πr (d) (θ/360°) × 2r

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) (θ/360°) × 2πr. The arc is the fraction θ/360° of the whole circumference 2πr.

MCQM51 mark

5. The area of a sector of a circle of radius 7 cm with central angle 90° is (π = 22/7): (a) 77 cm² (b) 154 cm² (c) 19.25 cm² (d) 38.5 cm²

Show Answer

Ans:

(d) 38.5 cm². Area = (90/360) × (22/7) × 7² = ¼ × 154 = 38.5 cm².

MCQM61 mark

6. The area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm is: (a) 77 cm² (b) 154 cm² (c) 308 cm² (d) 616 cm²

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 154 cm². 2πr = 44 gives r = 7 cm, so the area is πr² = 154 cm².

MCQM71 mark

7. If the radius of a circle is doubled, its area becomes: (a) 2 times (b) 4 times (c) 8 times (d) unchanged

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 4 times. Area ∝ r², so doubling r multiplies the area by 2² = 4.

MCQM81 mark

8. The area of a semicircle of radius r is: (a) πr² (b) πr²/2 (c) 2πr (d) πr²/4

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) πr²/2. A semicircle is half of a full circle.

Written questions · Write

SHORTS12 marks

1. Find the length of an arc of a sector with radius 21 cm and central angle 120°.

Show Answer

Ans:

Arc length = (θ/360°) × 2πr = (120/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 21 = (1/3) × 132 = 44 cm. Use 22/7 for π whenever the radius is a multiple of 7, as it is here — the sevens cancel and the arithmetic stays exact.

SHORTS22 marks

2. The circumference of a circle exceeds its diameter by 30 cm. Find the radius (take π = 22/7).

Show Answer

Ans:

2πr − 2r = 30 ⇒ 2r(22/7 − 1) = 30 ⇒ 2r(15/7) = 30 ⇒ r = 7 cm. Check: circumference 44 cm, diameter 14 cm, difference 30 cm.

SHORTS33 marks

3. Find the area of a sector of radius 6 cm with a central angle of 60° (take π = 3.14).

Show Answer

Ans:

Area of sector = (θ/360°) × πr² = (60/360) × 3.14 × 36 = (1/6) × 113.04 = 18.84 cm². Here the radius is not a multiple of 7, so π = 3.14 as the question directs, and the unit is cm² because this is an area, not a length.

SHORTS43 marks

4. Find the perimeter of a sector with radius 14 cm and central angle 90°.

Show Answer

Ans:

Arc = (90/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 14 = (1/4) × 88 = 22 cm.
Perimeter = 2(14) + 22 = 50 cm.

APPLICATIONA53 marks

5. A student finds the area of a circular pond of diameter 28 m as (22/7) × 28² = 2464 m². Identify the error and give the correct area.

Show Answer

Ans:

Error: the formula πr² needs the radius, but the student substituted the diameter 28 m.
Radius = 28 ÷ 2 = 14 m.
Area = (22/7) × 14² = (22/7) × 196 = 616 m² (one-fourth of the wrong value).

APPLICATIONA63 marks

6. A horse is tied to a peg at one corner of a square field of side 15 m by a rope 5 m long. Find the area of the field over which the horse can graze. (Take π = 3.14.)

Show Answer

Ans:

The horse can reach points within 5 m of the corner, but only inside the field, which spans a 90° angle at that corner.
So the grazing region is a quadrant (sector of 90°) of radius 5 m.
Area = (90/360) × 3.14 × 5² = ¼ × 78.5 = 19.625 m².

SHORTS73 marks

7. Find the area of the minor segment of a circle of radius 14 cm whose central angle is 90° (π = 22/7).

Show Answer

Ans:

Area of sector = (90/360) × (22/7) × 14² = ¼ × 616 = 154 cm².
Area of the right triangle formed by the two radii = ½ × 14 × 14 = 98 cm².
Area of the minor segment = 154 − 98 = 56 cm².

LONGL84 marks

8. A square of side 14 cm has a circle inscribed in it. Find the area between the square and the circle.

Show Answer

Ans:

The inscribed circle has diameter 14 cm, so radius 7 cm.
Area of square = 196 cm². Area of circle = (22/7) × 49 = 154 cm².
Required area = 196 − 154 = 42 cm².

COMPETENCYC94 marks

9. Read and answer: A pizza of radius 14 cm is cut into 8 equal slices. (Take π = 22/7.) (a) What is the central angle of each slice? (b) What is the area of one slice? (c) What is the perimeter of one slice, counting both straight edges?

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) 360° ÷ 8 = 45°.
(b) Area = (45/360) × (22/7) × 14² = ⅛ × 616 = 77 cm².
(c) Arc length = (45/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 14 = ⅛ × 88 = 11 cm. Perimeter = 11 + 14 + 14 = 39 cm.

COMPETENCYC104 marks

10. Read and answer: A circular park of radius 14 m is surrounded by a path 7 m wide. (Take π = 22/7.) (a) What is the outer radius of the path? (b) What is the area of the path? (c) What is the cost of paving it at Rs 40 per m²?

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Outer radius = 14 + 7 = 21 m.
(b) Area of path = π(21² − 14²) = (22/7)(441 − 196) = (22/7) × 245 = 770 m².
(c) Cost = 770 × 40 = Rs 30,800.

APPLICATIONA114 marks

11. Two circles touch externally. The sum of their areas is 130π cm² and the distance between their centres is 14 cm. Find their radii.

Show Answer

Ans:

Let the radii be r₁ and r₂. Since they touch externally, r₁ + r₂ = 14.
πr₁² + πr₂² = 130π ⇒ r₁² + r₂² = 130.
(r₁ + r₂)² = r₁² + r₂² + 2r₁r₂ ⇒ 196 = 130 + 2r₁r₂ ⇒ r₁r₂ = 33.
So r₁ and r₂ are roots of t² − 14t + 33 = 0, giving 11 cm and 3 cm.

LONGL125 marks

12. The minute hand of a clock is 14 cm long. Find (i) the angle it turns in 10 minutes, (ii) the distance moved by its tip in that time and (iii) the area swept by the hand. (Take π = 22/7.)

Show Answer

Ans:

(i) The minute hand turns 360° in 60 minutes, so in 10 minutes it turns 60°.
(ii) The tip moves along an arc of a circle of radius 14 cm: arc = (60/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 14 = ⅙ × 88 = 44/3 cm ≈ 14.67 cm.
(iii) The hand sweeps a sector of angle 60°: area = (60/360) × (22/7) × 14² = ⅙ × 616 = 308/3 cm² ≈ 102.67 cm².
