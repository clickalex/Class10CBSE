Written questions · Write

SHORTS12 marks

1. Find the length of an arc of a sector with radius 21 cm and central angle 120°.

Show Answer

Ans:

Arc = (120/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 21 = (1/3) × 132 = 44 cm.

SHORTS23 marks

2. Find the area of a sector of radius 6 cm with a central angle of 60° (take π = 3.14).

Show Answer

Ans:

Area = (60/360) × 3.14 × 36 = (1/6) × 113.04 = 18.84 cm².

SHORTS33 marks

3. Find the perimeter of a sector with radius 14 cm and central angle 90°.

Show Answer

Ans:

Arc = (90/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 14 = (1/4) × 88 = 22 cm.
Perimeter = 2(14) + 22 = 50 cm.

LONGL44 marks

4. A square of side 14 cm has a circle inscribed in it. Find the area between the square and the circle.

Show Answer

Ans:

The inscribed circle has diameter 14 cm, so radius 7 cm.
Area of square = 196 cm². Area of circle = (22/7) × 49 = 154 cm².
Required area = 196 − 154 = 42 cm².

LONGL55 marks

5. Discuss “Areas Related to Circles” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

Sector. The region between two radii and the arc joining their endpoints. The angle at the centre is θ.
Marks lens: Part of the 10-mark Mensuration unit, roughly 4–5 marks.
Method / memory trick: Perimeter of a sector is 2r + arc, not just the arc. Missing the two radii is the most common error here.
Do not: Forgetting to add the two radii to the perimeter of a sector.
