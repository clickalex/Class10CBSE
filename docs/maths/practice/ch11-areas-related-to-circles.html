MCQs · Drill

MCQM11 mark

1. The area of a quadrant of a circle of radius r is: (a) πr²/2 (b) πr²/4 (c) 2πr (d) πr²

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) πr²/4.

MCQM21 mark

2. The perimeter of a sector includes: (a) only the arc (b) the arc and one radius (c) the arc and two radii (d) the chord and the arc

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) The arc and two radii.

MCQM31 mark

3. The area of a segment is found by: (a) sector − triangle (b) sector + triangle (c) circle − sector (d) triangle − sector

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Sector − triangle.

MCQM41 mark

4. The most exam-ready way to revise “Areas Related to Circles” is: (a) read the heading only (b) learn the key idea plus one example (c) skip written practice (d) memorise a random extra topic

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) learn the key idea plus one example. The chapter is Sectors, segments and combined figures..

MCQM51 mark

5. A 3-mark answer on “Areas Related to Circles” should: (a) be one sentence (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method (c) copy the whole chapter (d) leave the example out

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) start with the idea, then a reason or method. Step marks follow that frame.

MCQM61 mark

6. The area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm is: (a) 77 cm² (b) 154 cm² (c) 308 cm² (d) 616 cm²

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 154 cm². 2πr = 44 gives r = 7 cm, so the area is πr² = 154 cm².

MCQM71 mark

7. If the radius of a circle is doubled, its area becomes: (a) 2 times (b) 4 times (c) 8 times (d) unchanged

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 4 times. Area ∝ r², so doubling r multiplies the area by 2² = 4.

MCQM81 mark

8. The area of a semicircle of radius r is: (a) πr² (b) πr²/2 (c) 2πr (d) πr²/4

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) πr²/2. A semicircle is half of a full circle.

Written questions · Write

SHORTS12 marks

1. Find the length of an arc of a sector with radius 21 cm and central angle 120°.

Show Answer

Ans:

Arc = (120/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 21 = (1/3) × 132 = 44 cm.

SHORTS22 marks

2. The circumference of a circle exceeds its diameter by 30 cm. Find the radius (take π = 22/7).

Show Answer

Ans:

2πr − 2r = 30 ⇒ 2r(22/7 − 1) = 30 ⇒ 2r(15/7) = 30 ⇒ r = 7 cm. Check: circumference 44 cm, diameter 14 cm, difference 30 cm.

SHORTS33 marks

3. Find the area of a sector of radius 6 cm with a central angle of 60° (take π = 3.14).

Show Answer

Ans:

Area = (60/360) × 3.14 × 36 = (1/6) × 113.04 = 18.84 cm².

SHORTS43 marks

4. Find the perimeter of a sector with radius 14 cm and central angle 90°.

Show Answer

Ans:

Arc = (90/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 14 = (1/4) × 88 = 22 cm.
Perimeter = 2(14) + 22 = 50 cm.

APPLICATIONA53 marks

5. Riya is revising “Areas Related to Circles” (Sectors, segments and combined figures.). A classmate lost marks by doing this: Forgetting to add the two radii to the perimeter of a sector. Apply the correct method and rewrite the scoring answer.

Show Answer

Ans:

Correct method: Perimeter of a sector is 2r + arc, not just the arc. Missing the two radii is the most common error here.
Why the mistake costs marks: Forgetting to add the two radii to the perimeter of a sector.
Write the definition or formula first, then the working or example.

APPLICATIONA63 marks

6. Amit is given this exam-style task from “Areas Related to Circles”: “Find the length of an arc of a sector with radius 21 cm and central angle 120°.” Treat it as an application question. Identify the idea, then write the working in points.

Show Answer

Ans:

Idea: Sectors, segments and combined figures..
Arc = (120/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 21 = (1/3) × 132 = 44 cm.

SHORTS73 marks

7. Find the area of the minor segment of a circle of radius 14 cm whose central angle is 90° (π = 22/7).

Show Answer

Ans:

Area of sector = (90/360) × (22/7) × 14² = ¼ × 616 = 154 cm².
Area of the right triangle formed by the two radii = ½ × 14 × 14 = 98 cm².
Area of the minor segment = 154 − 98 = 56 cm².

LONGL84 marks

8. A square of side 14 cm has a circle inscribed in it. Find the area between the square and the circle.

Show Answer

Ans:

The inscribed circle has diameter 14 cm, so radius 7 cm.
Area of square = 196 cm². Area of circle = (22/7) × 49 = 154 cm².
Required area = 196 − 154 = 42 cm².

COMPETENCYC104 marks

Student B writes points, an example and a check. (a) Which answer frame should you use for full marks? (b) Write the model points. (c) Add one memory trick you would use on exam morning.

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) Arc = (120/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 21 = (1/3) × 132 = 44 cm.

APPLICATIONA114 marks

11. Two circles touch externally. The sum of their areas is 130π cm² and the distance between their centres is 14 cm. Find their radii.

Show Answer

Ans:

Let the radii be r₁ and r₂. Since they touch externally, r₁ + r₂ = 14.
πr₁² + πr₂² = 130π ⇒ r₁² + r₂² = 130.
(r₁ + r₂)² = r₁² + r₂² + 2r₁r₂ ⇒ 196 = 130 + 2r₁r₂ ⇒ r₁r₂ = 33.
So r₁ and r₂ are roots of t² − 14t + 33 = 0, giving 11 cm and 3 cm.

LONGL125 marks

12. Discuss “Areas Related to Circles” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

Sector. The region between two radii and the arc joining their endpoints. The angle at the centre is θ.
Marks lens: Part of the 10-mark Mensuration unit, roughly 4–5 marks.
Method / memory trick: Perimeter of a sector is 2r + arc, not just the arc. Missing the two radii is the most common error here.
Do not: Forgetting to add the two radii to the perimeter of a sector.
