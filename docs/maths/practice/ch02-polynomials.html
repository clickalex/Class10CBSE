MCQs · Drill

MCQM11 mark

1. The zeroes of x² + 7x + 10 are: (a) 2, 5 (b) −2, −5 (c) 2, −5 (d) −2, 5

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) −2, −5. Sum = −7, product = 10, so both zeroes are negative.

MCQM21 mark

2. If α, β are zeroes of x² − 5x + 6, then α + β = ? (a) 6 (b) 5 (c) −5 (d) 1

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 5. Sum = −b/a = 5.

MCQM31 mark

3. A quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 3 and −3 is: (a) x² − 9 (b) x² + 9 (c) x² − 6x + 9 (d) x² − 3

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) x² − 9. Sum = 0 and product = −9.

MCQM41 mark

MCQM51 mark

5. A 3-mark answer on “Polynomials” should: (a) be one sentence (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method (c) copy the whole chapter (d) leave the example out

Show Answer

MCQM61 mark

6. A polynomial of degree 3 is called: (a) linear (b) quadratic (c) cubic (d) biquadratic

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) cubic. Degree 1 is linear, 2 is quadratic, 3 is cubic and 4 is biquadratic.

MCQM71 mark

7. If one zero of x² − 4x + 1 is 2 + √3, the other zero is: (a) 2 − √3 (b) −2 − √3 (c) √3 − 2 (d) −√3

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) 2 − √3. Sum of zeroes = 4, so the other zero = 4 − (2 + √3) = 2 − √3.

MCQM81 mark

8. A quadratic polynomial can have at most: (a) 0 zeroes (b) 1 zero (c) 2 zeroes (d) 3 zeroes

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) 2 zeroes. A polynomial of degree n has at most n zeroes, so degree 2 gives at most two.

Written questions · Write

SHORTS12 marks

1. Find the zeroes of x² − 2x − 8 and verify the relationship with the coefficients.

Show Answer

Ans:

x² − 2x − 8 = (x − 4)(x + 2), so the zeroes are 4 and −2.
Sum = 4 + (−2) = 2 = −(−2)/1 = −b/a ✓
Product = 4 × (−2) = −8 = c/a ✓

SHORTS22 marks

2. If α and β are the zeroes of 6x² − 3x − 7, find 1/α + 1/β.

Show Answer

Ans:

α + β = 3/6 = 1/2 and αβ = −7/6.
1/α + 1/β = (α + β)/(αβ) = (1/2)/(−7/6) = −3/7.

SHORTS33 marks

3. Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes have sum 1/4 and product −1.

Show Answer

Ans:

A quadratic with zeroes whose sum is S and product is P is k[x² − Sx + P]. Here S = 1/4 and P = −1, so p(x) = k[x² − (1/4)x − 1]. Taking k = 4 to clear the fraction gives 4x² − x − 4. Any non-zero value of k is accepted, since multiplying a polynomial by a constant does not change its zeroes.

SHORTS43 marks

4. If one zero of (k² − 1)x² − 3x + 2 is the reciprocal of the other, find k.

Show Answer

Ans:

Product of zeroes = c/a = 2/(k² − 1).
Reciprocal zeroes ⇒ product = 1, so 2/(k² − 1) = 1.
k² − 1 = 2, k² = 3, so k = ±√3.

APPLICATIONA63 marks

6. Amit is given this exam-style task from “Polynomials”: “Find the zeroes of x² − 2x − 8 and verify the relationship with the coefficients.” Treat it as an application question. Identify the idea, then write the working in points.

Show Answer

Ans:

Idea: Zeroes of a quadratic and their relation to coefficients..
x² − 2x − 8 = (x − 4)(x + 2), so the zeroes are 4 and −2. Sum = 4 + (−2) = 2 = −(−2)/1 = −b/a ✓ Product = 4 × (−2) = −8 = c/a ✓

SHORTS73 marks

7. If α and β are the zeroes of x² − 6x + k and 3α + 2β = 20, find k.

Show Answer

Ans:

α + β = 6 (sum of zeroes) and 3α + 2β = 20.
Subtract twice the first equation from the second: α = 20 − 12 = 8, so β = −2.
Product αβ = 8(−2) = −16, and product = c/a = k, so k = −16.

SHORTS83 marks

8. Divide 3x³ + x² + 2x + 5 by x² + 2x + 1 and verify the division algorithm.

Show Answer

Ans:

Quotient = 3x − 5, remainder = 9x + 10.
Check: (x² + 2x + 1)(3x − 5) + 9x + 10
= 3x³ + 6x² + 3x − 5x² − 10x − 5 + 9x + 10
= 3x³ + x² + 2x + 5, which is the dividend — so dividend = divisor × quotient + remainder.

APPLICATIONA114 marks

11. A quadratic polynomial cuts the x-axis at −2 and 3 and its graph passes through (0, 6). Find the polynomial.

Show Answer

Ans:

With zeroes −2 and 3, p(x) = a(x + 2)(x − 3).
It passes through (0, 6), so 6 = a(2)(−3) = −6a, giving a = −1.
Hence p(x) = −(x + 2)(x − 3) = −x² + x + 6.
