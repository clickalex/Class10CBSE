MCQs · Drill

MCQM11 mark

1. The pair x + y = 5 and 2x + 2y = 10 represents: (a) intersecting lines (b) parallel lines (c) coincident lines (d) none

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) Coincident lines. All three ratios equal 1/2.

MCQM21 mark

2. For what value of k does kx + 3y = 7 and 2x + 6y = 14 have infinitely many solutions? (a) 1 (b) −1 (c) 2 (d) 1/2

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) 1. We need k/2 = 3/6 = 7/14, and 3/6 = 1/2, so k = 1.

MCQM31 mark

3. If a boat’s downstream speed is 12 km/h and upstream speed is 8 km/h, its still-water speed is: (a) 10 (b) 4 (c) 2 (d) 20 km/h

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) 10 km/h. Still-water speed = (downstream + upstream)/2.

MCQM41 mark

4. The most exam-ready way to revise “Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables” is: (a) read the heading only (b) learn the key idea plus one example (c) skip written practice (d) memorise a random extra topic

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) learn the key idea plus one example. The chapter is Consistency conditions, three solving methods, word problems..

MCQM51 mark

5. A 3-mark answer on “Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables” should: (a) be one sentence (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method (c) copy the whole chapter (d) leave the example out

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) start with the idea, then a reason or method. Step marks follow that frame.

MCQM61 mark

6. The pair 6x − 3y + 10 = 0 and 2x − y + 9 = 0 has: (a) one solution (b) two solutions (c) infinitely many solutions (d) no solution

Show Answer

Ans:

(d) no solution. 6/2 = 3 and (−3)/(−1) = 3 but 10/9 ≠ 3, so the lines are parallel and distinct.

MCQM71 mark

7. If x + 2y = 5 and 3x + ky = 15 are coincident lines, then k = : (a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 6 (d) 15

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) 6. Coincident lines need 1/3 = 2/k = 5/15, and 2/k = 1/3 gives k = 6.

MCQM81 mark

8. The solution of x + y = 14 and x − y = 4 is: (a) x = 9, y = 5 (b) x = 5, y = 9 (c) x = 10, y = 4 (d) x = 8, y = 6

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) x = 9, y = 5. Adding the equations gives 2x = 18, so x = 9 and then y = 5.

Written questions · Write

SHORTS12 marks

1. Without solving, tell whether 2x − 3y = 7 and 4x − 6y = 14 have a unique solution.

Show Answer

Ans:

a₁/a₂ = 2/4 = 1/2, b₁/b₂ = −3/−6 = 1/2, c₁/c₂ = (−7)/(−14) = 1/2.
All three ratios are equal, so the lines are coincident — infinitely many solutions, not a unique one.

SHORTS23 marks

2. Solve by elimination: 3x + 4y = 10 and 2x − 3y = 1.

Show Answer

Ans:

Multiply the first by 3 and the second by 4: 9x + 12y = 30 and 8x − 12y = 4.
Adding: 17x = 34, so x = 2.
Substituting in the first: 6 + 4y = 10, so y = 1.

APPLICATIONA33 marks

3. Riya is revising “Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables” (Consistency conditions, three solving methods, word problems.). A classmate lost marks by doing this: Declaring a pair inconsistent when only two of the three ratios are equal. You must check c₁/c₂ as well. Apply the correct method and rewrite the scoring answer.

Show Answer

Ans:

Correct method: Ratio order matters. Compare a with a, b with b, c with c — never a with b.
Why the mistake costs marks: Declaring a pair inconsistent when only two of the three ratios are equal. You must check c₁/c₂ as well.
Write the definition or formula first, then the working or example.

APPLICATIONA43 marks

4. Amit is given this exam-style task from “Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables”: “Without solving, tell whether 2x − 3y = 7 and 4x − 6y = 14 have a unique solution.” Treat it as an application question. Identify the idea, then write the working in points.

Show Answer

Ans:

Idea: Consistency conditions, three solving methods, word problems..
a₁/a₂ = 2/4 = 1/2, b₁/b₂ = −3/−6 = 1/2, c₁/c₂ = (−7)/(−14) = 1/2. All three ratios are equal, so the lines are coincident — infinitely many solutions, not a unique one.

SHORTS53 marks

5. For what value of k does the pair 3x + y = 1 and (2k − 1)x + (k − 1)y = 2k + 1 have no solution?

Show Answer

Ans:

For no solution the lines must be parallel: a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂.
3/(2k − 1) = 1/(k − 1) ⇒ 3k − 3 = 2k − 1 ⇒ k = 2.
Check: at k = 2 the equations become 3x + y = 1 and 3x + y = 5, which are parallel and distinct — so no solution.

SHORTS63 marks

6. Solve by cross-multiplication: 2x + 3y = 46 and 3x + 5y = 74.

Show Answer

Ans:

Write as 2x + 3y − 46 = 0 and 3x + 5y − 74 = 0.
x/(3(−74) − 5(−46)) = y/(−46(3) − (−74)(2)) = 1/(2(5) − 3(3))
x/(−222 + 230) = y/(−138 + 148) = 1/1, so x = 8 and y = 10.
Check: 2(8) + 3(10) = 46 and 3(8) + 5(10) = 74.

SHORTS73 marks

7. Solve by substitution: x + 3y = 16 and 2x − y = 4.

Show Answer

Ans:

From the second equation, y = 2x − 4.
Substituting in the first: x + 3(2x − 4) = 16, so x + 6x − 12 = 16.
Hence 7x = 28, giving x = 4 and y = 2(4) − 4 = 4.
Check: 4 + 12 = 16 and 8 − 4 = 4. The solution is x = 4, y = 4.

LONGL84 marks

8. Five years ago, Nuri was thrice as old as Sonu. Ten years later, Nuri will be twice as old as Sonu. How old are they?

Show Answer

Ans:

Let Nuri be x and Sonu be y now.
x − 5 = 3(y − 5) ⇒ x − 3y = −10.
x + 10 = 2(y + 10) ⇒ x − 2y = 10.
Subtracting: −y = −20, so y = 20 and x = 50.
Nuri is 50 and Sonu is 20.

COMPETENCYC94 marks

9. Read and answer: A Class 10 student is preparing “Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables”. Their note says: Nature of the pair. Compare a₁/a₂, b₁/b₂ and c₁/c₂ for the pair a₁x + b₁y + c₁ = 0 and a₂x + b₂y + c₂ = 0. The chapter is described as: Consistency conditions, three solving methods, word problems.. (a) Name the key idea. (b) How is this usually asked in the paper? (c) State one mistake that would cost a mark.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Consistency conditions, three solving methods, word problems.
(b) –6 marks. One consistency/nature question (2 marks) and one word problem (4–5 marks).
(c) Declaring a pair inconsistent when only two of the three ratios are equal. You must check c₁/c₂ as well.

COMPETENCYC104 marks

10. Read and answer: Two students attempt: “Without solving, tell whether 2x − 3y = 7 and 4x − 6y = 14 have a unique solution.” Student A writes one line. Student B writes points, an example and a check. (a) Which answer frame should you use for full marks? (b) Write the model points. (c) Add one memory trick you would use on exam morning.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Statement → working or reasons in points → example or check. One-line answers lose step marks.
(b) a₁/a₂ = 2/4 = 1/2, b₁/b₂ = −3/−6 = 1/2, c₁/c₂ = (−7)/(−14) = 1/2. All three ratios are equal, so the lines are coincident — infinitely many solutions, not a unique one.
(c) Ratio order matters. Compare a with a, b with b, c with c — never a with b.

APPLICATIONA114 marks

11. A fraction becomes 1/3 when 1 is subtracted from its numerator, and becomes 1/4 when 8 is added to its denominator. Find the fraction.

Show Answer

Ans:

Let the fraction be x/y.
(x − 1)/y = 1/3 ⇒ y = 3x − 3. And x/(y + 8) = 1/4 ⇒ 4x = y + 8.
Substituting: 4x = 3x − 3 + 8 ⇒ x = 5, so y = 12.
The fraction is 5/12. Check: (5 − 1)/12 = 1/3 and 5/20 = 1/4.

LONGL125 marks

12. A boat goes 30 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 10 hours. In 13 hours it can go 40 km upstream and 55 km downstream. Find the speed of the stream and of the boat in still water.

Show Answer

Ans:

Let the still-water speed be x km/h and the stream’s speed be y km/h.
Put u = 1/(x − y) and v = 1/(x + y).
30u + 44v = 10 and 40u + 55v = 13.
Solving gives u = 1/8 and v = 1/11, so x − y = 8 and x + y = 11.
Therefore x = 9.5 km/h and y = 1.5 km/h.
