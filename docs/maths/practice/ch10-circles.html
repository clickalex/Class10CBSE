Written questions · Write

SHORTS11 mark

1. How many tangents can be drawn from a point inside a circle?

Show Answer

Ans:

None. A line through an interior point must cut the circle at two points.

SHORTS22 marks

2. From a point 13 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm, a tangent is drawn. Find its length.

Show Answer

Ans:

The tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact, so the three lengths form a right triangle.
Length = √(13² − 5²) = √144 = 12 cm.

SHORTS33 marks

3. Prove that the lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal.

Show Answer

Ans:

Let P be the external point and PA, PB the tangents with points of contact A and B.
Join OA, OB and OP.
In ΔOAP and ΔOBP: OA = OB (radii), OP is common, and ∠OAP = ∠OBP = 90° (tangent ⊥ radius).
By RHS congruence, ΔOAP ≅ ΔOBP, so PA = PB.

LONGL44 marks

4. A quadrilateral ABCD circumscribes a circle. Prove that AB + CD = AD + BC.

Show Answer

Ans:

Let the tangent segments from A, B, C, D be p, q, r, s respectively.
Then AB = p + q, BC = q + r, CD = r + s and AD = p + s.
AB + CD = p + q + r + s and AD + BC = p + s + q + r.
The two sums are identical, so AB + CD = AD + BC.

LONGL55 marks

5. Discuss “Circles” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

Tangent. A line that touches the circle at exactly one point. That point is the point of contact.
Marks lens: The rest of the 15-mark Geometry unit, roughly 5–6 marks.
Method / memory trick: Always join the centre to the point of contact. That creates the right angle you need, and it is the first line of almost every proof here.
Do not: Forgetting to state that the radius is perpendicular to the tangent — that is a marked step.
