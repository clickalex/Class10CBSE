MCQs · Drill

MCQM11 mark

1. The roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0 are: (a) 2, 3 (b) −2, −3 (c) 1, 6 (d) −1, −6

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) 2, 3. Product 6 and sum 5, both positive.

MCQM21 mark

2. If the discriminant of a quadratic is zero, the roots are: (a) real and distinct (b) real and equal (c) not real (d) always integers

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) Real and equal.

MCQM31 mark

3. For x² + 4x + k = 0 to have real roots, k must satisfy: (a) k ≤ 4 (b) k ≥ 4 (c) k ≤ −4 (d) k = 4

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) k ≤ 4. D = 16 − 4k ≥ 0 gives k ≤ 4.

MCQM51 mark

Show Answer

MCQM61 mark

6. The sum and product of the roots of 2x² − 7x + 3 = 0 are: (a) 7/2 and 3/2 (b) 7/2 and 3 (c) −7/2 and 3/2 (d) 7/2 and −3/2

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) 7/2 and 3/2. Sum = −b/a = 7/2 and product = c/a = 3/2.

MCQM71 mark

7. Which one is NOT a quadratic equation? (a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0 (b) (x + 1)² = 2(x − 3) (c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0 (d) 2x² = 8

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) x³ − x² + 1 = 0. Its degree is 3, so it is a cubic; the others reduce to degree 2.

MCQM81 mark

8. If the roots of x² − 8x + k = 0 are equal, then k = : (a) 8 (b) 16 (c) −16 (d) 64

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 16. D = 0 ⇒ 64 − 4k = 0 ⇒ k = 16.

Written questions · Write

SHORTS12 marks

1. Find the nature of the roots of 2x² − 6x + 3 = 0.

Show Answer

Ans:

D = (−6)² − 4(2)(3) = 36 − 24 = 12.
D > 0 but 12 is not a perfect square, so the roots are real, distinct and irrational.

SHORTS22 marks

2. For what values of k does x² + kx + 16 = 0 have equal roots?

Show Answer

Ans:

Equal roots means the discriminant is zero: k² − 4(1)(16) = 0, so k² = 64 and k = ±8. For k = 8 the root is −4; for k = −8 the root is 4.

SHORTS33 marks

3. Find the value of k for which 2x² + kx + 3 = 0 has two equal roots.

Show Answer

Ans:

For equal roots, D = 0, so k² − 4(2)(3) = 0.
k² = 24, so k = ±2√6.

SHORTS43 marks

4. Solve 6x² − x − 2 = 0 by factorisation.

Show Answer

Ans:

Split −x using numbers with product 6 × (−2) = −12 and sum −1: these are −4 and 3.
6x² − 4x + 3x − 2 = 2x(3x − 2) + 1(3x − 2) = (3x − 2)(2x + 1).
So x = 2/3 or x = −1/2.

APPLICATIONA53 marks

5. Riya is revising “Quadratic Equations” (Factorisation, the quadratic formula, discriminant and nature of roots.). A classmate lost marks by doing this: Dropping the sign of b when writing −b in the formula. Apply the correct method and rewrite the scoring answer.

Show Answer

Ans:

Correct method: Splitting the middle term: you need two numbers with product a × c (not just c) and sum b. Forgetting the a is the most common error when a ≠ 1.
Why the mistake costs marks: Dropping the sign of b when writing −b in the formula.
Write the definition or formula first, then the working or example.

Show Answer

Idea: Factorisation, the quadratic formula, discriminant and nature of roots..

SHORTS73 marks

7. Solve x² + 5x + 6 = 0 by completing the square.

Show Answer

Ans:

x² + 5x = −6.
Add (5/2)² to both sides: x² + 5x + 25/4 = −6 + 25/4 = 1/4.
(x + 5/2)² = 1/4, so x + 5/2 = ±1/2.
Hence x = −2 or x = −3.

APPLICATIONA104 marks

10. A train travels 360 km at a uniform speed. If the speed were 5 km/h more, it would take 1 hour less. Find the speed of the train.

Show Answer

Ans:

Let the speed be v km/h. Time difference: 360/v − 360/(v + 5) = 1.
⇒ 360[5/(v(v + 5))] = 1 ⇒ v² + 5v − 1800 = 0.
⇒ (v + 45)(v − 40) = 0, so v = 40 (speed cannot be negative).
The train travels at 40 km/h; check: 360/40 = 9 h and 360/45 = 8 h.

LONGL115 marks

11. The sum of the areas of two squares is 468 m². If the difference of their perimeters is 24 m, find the sides of the two squares.

Show Answer

Ans:

Let the sides be x and y with x > y. Then x² + y² = 468 and 4x − 4y = 24, so x − y = 6.
Substituting y = x − 6: x² + (x − 6)² = 468 ⇒ 2x² − 12x + 36 = 468.
2x² − 12x − 432 = 0, so x² − 6x − 216 = 0.
(x − 18)(x + 12) = 0, so x = 18 (rejecting −12) and y = 12.
The sides are 18 m and 12 m.

Show Answer

Ans:

Marks lens: –7 marks. A nature-of-roots question (2–3 marks) and a word problem (4–5 marks).
Method / memory trick: Splitting the middle term: you need two numbers with product a × c (not just c) and sum b. Forgetting the a is the most common error when a ≠ 1.
Do not: Dropping the sign of b when writing −b in the formula.
