Written questions · Write

SHORTS13 marks

1. A solid consists of a cylinder with a hemisphere on one end and a cone on the other, all of radius 7 cm. The cylinder is 20 cm high, the cone 12 cm high. Find the total surface area (take π = 22/7).

Show Answer

Ans:

Cylinder CSA = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 20 = 880 cm².
Hemisphere CSA = 2 × (22/7) × 49 = 308 cm².
Cone slant height l = √(7² + 12²) = √193 ≈ 13.89 cm, so cone CSA = (22/7) × 7 × 13.89 ≈ 305.6 cm².
Total ≈ 880 + 308 + 305.6 = 1493.6 cm². The two circular joining faces are not counted.

SHORTS23 marks

2. A solid metallic sphere of radius 4.2 cm is melted and recast into a cylinder of radius 6 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.

Show Answer

Ans:

Volume of sphere = volume of cylinder.
(4/3)π(4.2)³ = π(6)²h.
h = (4 × 74.088)/(3 × 36) = 296.352/108 ≈ 2.74 cm.

LONGL34 marks

3. 100 solid iron spheres, each of radius 1 cm, are melted to form one solid sphere. Find its radius.

Show Answer

Ans:

Total volume is conserved: 100 × (4/3)π(1)³ = (4/3)πR³.
Cancelling (4/3)π: R³ = 100, so R = ∛100 ≈ 4.64 cm.

LONGL44 marks

4. A frustum of a cone has radii 14 cm and 7 cm and height 24 cm. Find its slant height and curved surface area (take π = 22/7).

Show Answer

Ans:

l = √[24² + (14 − 7)²] = √(576 + 49) = √625 = 25 cm.
CSA = π(R + r)l = (22/7) × (14 + 7) × 25 = (22/7) × 21 × 25 = 1650 cm².

APPLICATIONA53 marks

5. Riya is revising “Surface Areas and Volumes” (Combinations of solids, conversion of solids, and the frustum.). A classmate lost marks by doing this: Including the joining face in the total surface area. Apply the correct method and rewrite the scoring answer.

Show Answer

Ans:

Correct method: Hemisphere on top of a cuboid or cylinder: the flat circular base of the hemisphere is not part of the outer surface. Use only its curved area, 2πr².
Why the mistake costs marks: Including the joining face in the total surface area.
Write the definition or formula first, then the working or example.
