MCQs

The volume of a cone of radius r and height h is: (a) πr²h (b) πr²h/3 (c) 2πr²h (d) πrh

(b) πr²h/3.

When a solid is melted and recast into another shape, which quantity is conserved? (a) surface area (b) volume (c) both (d) neither

(b) Volume. Surface area almost always changes.

The slant height of a frustum with radii R and r and height h is: (a) √[h² + (R + r)²] (b) √[h² + (R − r)²] (c) h + R + r (d) √(R² − r²)

(b) √[h² + (R − r)²].

Written questions

3 marks A solid consists of a cylinder with a hemisphere on one end and a cone on the other, all of radius 7 cm. The cylinder is 20 cm high, the cone 12 cm high. Find the total surface area (take π = 22/7).

Cylinder CSA = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 20 = 880 cm².
Hemisphere CSA = 2 × (22/7) × 49 = 308 cm².
Cone slant height l = √(7² + 12²) = √193 ≈ 13.89 cm, so cone CSA = (22/7) × 7 × 13.89 ≈ 305.6 cm².
Total ≈ 880 + 308 + 305.6 = 1493.6 cm². The two circular joining faces are not counted.

3 marks A solid metallic sphere of radius 4.2 cm is melted and recast into a cylinder of radius 6 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.

Volume of sphere = volume of cylinder.
(4/3)π(4.2)³ = π(6)²h.
h = (4 × 74.088)/(3 × 36) = 296.352/108 ≈ 2.74 cm.

4 marks 100 solid iron spheres, each of radius 1 cm, are melted to form one solid sphere. Find its radius.

Total volume is conserved: 100 × (4/3)π(1)³ = (4/3)πR³.
Cancelling (4/3)π: R³ = 100, so R = ∛100 ≈ 4.64 cm.

4 marks A frustum of a cone has radii 14 cm and 7 cm and height 24 cm. Find its slant height and curved surface area (take π = 22/7).

l = √[24² + (14 − 7)²] = √(576 + 49) = √625 = 25 cm.
CSA = π(R + r)l = (22/7) × (14 + 7) × 25 = (22/7) × 21 × 25 = 1650 cm².
