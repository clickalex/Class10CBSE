Mathematics — PYQ trends

Where past papers keep returning, chapter by chapter. Use it to decide revision order — not to guess the paper.

Trend notes below are an original reading of publicly available past papers and sample papers. They are not official CBSE predictions.

ChapterTitleWhat past papers ask
Ch 1Real NumbersOne irrationality proof almost every year (√2, √3, √5 or 3√2). One HCF/LCM word problem. One terminating-decimal 1-marker.
Ch 2PolynomialsOne 2-mark question on the zeroes–coefficient relationship, usually asking for k or for an expression like 1/α + 1/β. Graph-counting appears in the objective section.
Ch 3Pair of Linear Equations in Two VariablesOne 4–5 mark word problem nearly every year. Upstream/downstream and age problems are the most repeated.
Ch 4Quadratic Equations“Find k for equal roots” is asked almost every year, plus one 4–5 mark word problem on areas or consecutive integers.
Ch 5Arithmetic ProgressionsOne 4–5 mark word problem (prize distribution, seating, savings) appears in most papers, plus a 2–3 mark term question.
Ch 6TrianglesOne BPT or Pythagoras proof almost every year, plus one similarity numerical. Objective questions on the criteria are constant.
Ch 7Coordinate GeometryOne 3-mark question on the section formula or an equidistant relation, plus objective questions on distance and midpoint.
Ch 8Introduction to TrigonometryOne identity proof (3 marks) most years, plus objective questions on standard values and complementary angles.
Ch 9Some Applications of TrigonometryOne 3–4 mark heights-and-distances problem each year. Two-position problems (walking away) are the common harder variant.
Ch 10CirclesOne tangent-length proof or numerical (3 marks) and objective questions on the number of tangents.
Ch 11Areas Related to CirclesOne sector perimeter/area question and one combined-figure question. Quadrant corners cut from a square is the classic combined figure.
Ch 12Surface Areas and VolumesOne combination-of-solids surface area question and one conversion or frustum question appear most years. The frustum has been asked repeatedly since it was retained in the syllabus.
Ch 13StatisticsOne 4–5 mark grouped-data question every year — mean, median, mode or a missing frequency. Ogive drawing is asked less often now but still appears in sample papers.
Ch 14ProbabilityOne 2–3 mark question every year, nearly always a deck of cards or two dice. Card questions are the most common.
