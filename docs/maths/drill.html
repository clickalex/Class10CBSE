Ch 1 · Real Numbers (5)

MCQM11 mark

1. The HCF of 8 and 12 is: (a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 8 (d) 24

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 4. 8 = 2³, 12 = 2² × 3; the lowest power of the common prime 2 is 2² = 4.

MCQM21 mark

2. Which has a terminating decimal expansion? (a) 7/24 (b) 5/13 (c) 9/40 (d) 11/21

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) 9/40. 40 = 2³ × 5, of the form 2ᵐ × 5ⁿ. The others have denominators with other primes.

MCQM31 mark

3. If the HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 60, one number being 12, the other is: (a) 20 (b) 30 (c) 40 (d) 60

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 30. Other number = (6 × 60)/12 = 30.

Ch 3 · Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables (5)

MCQM11 mark

1. The pair x + y = 5 and 2x + 2y = 10 represents: (a) intersecting lines (b) parallel lines (c) coincident lines (d) none

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) Coincident lines. All three ratios equal 1/2.

MCQM21 mark

2. For what value of k does kx + 3y = 7 and 2x + 6y = 14 have infinitely many solutions? (a) 1 (b) −1 (c) 2 (d) 1/2

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) 1. We need k/2 = 3/6 = 7/14, and 3/6 = 1/2, so k = 1.

MCQM31 mark

3. If a boat’s downstream speed is 12 km/h and upstream speed is 8 km/h, its still-water speed is: (a) 10 (b) 4 (c) 2 (d) 20 km/h

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) 10 km/h. Still-water speed = (downstream + upstream)/2.
