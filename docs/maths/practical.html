Mathematics — Practical & internal assessment

The 20 internal marks include a pen-paper test, multiple assessment, a portfolio and a lab activity from the NCERT Laboratory Manual.

  • Lab activity (5 marks): usually a hands-on verification — for example verifying the basic proportionality theorem with a cut-out triangle, or the relationship between the surface area of a sphere and a circumscribing cylinder.
  • Portfolio (5 marks): your assignment sheets, the two chapter tests and one model/working file.
  • What to keep ready: a clean geometry box, squared paper for graphs and ogives, and every assignment submitted on time — multiple assessment is largely built from those.
  • Graphs: use a sharp pencil, label both axes, mark the scale, and give the graph a title. Unlabelled axes lose a step mark.
