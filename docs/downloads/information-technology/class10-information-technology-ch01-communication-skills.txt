A. WRITTEN QUESTIONS (12)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. [Short · 2 marks] Enlist the different methods of communication.
    Ans:  (1) Verbal — oral (speech, phone, meeting) + written (letter, email, report). (2) Non-verbal — expressions, gestures, posture, eye contact, tone. (3) Visual — charts, maps, signals, posters, videos.

Q2. [Short · 2 marks] Which is the easiest method of communication and why?
    Ans: Non-verbal is the easiest — it needs no language or literacy. Gestures, expressions and tone are understood naturally and work across languages.

Q3. [Short · 2 marks] What is feedback? Why is it called the lifeline of communication?
    Ans: Feedback is the receiver's response flowing back to the sender. It is the lifeline because it (1) confirms the message was understood and (2) corrects errors early — completing the two-way loop.

Q4. [Short · 2 marks] Explain any two Cs of effective communication with examples.
    Ans: (1) Clear — simple, exact message (“Submit the file by Friday 5 pm”). (2) Courteous — polite words (“please”, “thank you”, “sorry for the delay”).

Q5. [Short · 2 marks] Name any four barriers to effective communication.
    Ans: Physical barriers such as noise or distance; language barriers such as jargon or an unfamiliar accent; psychological barriers such as anger, fear or prejudice; and cultural barriers, where the same word or gesture means different things. Poor listening and the absence of feedback make all of them worse.

Q6. [Application · 3 marks] Riya messages her team “do it soon”. Identify the violated C and rewrite the message correctly.
    Ans: Violates Clear (vague, no deadline) and Complete (no what/when). Rewrite: “Please submit the sales sheet by Friday 5 pm on email.” — now clear, complete and courteous.

Q7. [Application · 3 marks] A team leader’s juniors look confused by his technical jargon. What two steps should he take?
    Ans: (1) Replace jargon with simple words and show a chart (linguistic barrier). (2) Pause, ask “any doubts?”, and paraphrase replies (feedback + active listening).

Q8. [Short · 3 marks] What are the 7 Cs of effective communication? Explain any three.
    Ans:
      The seven Cs are clear, concise, concrete, correct, coherent, complete and courteous.
      Clear: say what you mean in words the listener understands, and avoid jargon.
      Concise: use only as many words as the message needs, so the point is not lost.
      Complete: give all the facts the receiver needs, including who, what, when and where, so that no further questions are needed.

Q9. [Long · 4 marks] Discuss in detail the verbal method of communication.
    Ans:
      Verbal = communication using words, in two forms.
      Oral (speech, phone, meeting): fast, instant feedback; no permanent record.
      Written (letter, email, report): permanent proof, re-readable, formal; slower, needs literacy.
      Use oral for urgent/personal matters, written when a record is needed.

Q10. [Long · 4 marks] Explain any four barriers to communication with one example and one measure each.
    Ans:
      (1) Physical — class noise → quiet room, working mic.
      (2) Linguistic — French manual → simple words + visuals.
      (3) Cultural — a thumbs-up is rude in some places → learn customs.
      (4) Emotional — anger blocks listening → stay calm, choose the right time.

Q11. [Competency · 4 marks] Read and answer:

    Amit presents with zero eye contact, 40 text-packed slides in 10 minutes, and says “no questions, I am getting late”. The manager understands nothing.

    (a) Identify four communication mistakes.
    (b) Suggest four fixes.
    Ans:
      (a) No eye contact (poor non-verbal) • overloaded slides (not concise) • too fast (not clear) • no questions (blocks feedback).
      (b) Eye contact + open posture • max 10 slides, one idea each • slow, clear speech • invite questions and summarise.

Q12. [Competency · 4 marks] Read and answer:

    For the school fest, Class X puts up posters at shops, announces on a loudspeaker, and the principal sends a written notice to parents.

    (a) Name the three methods used.
    (b) Why is combining them better than one?
    Ans:
      (a) Posters = visual; loudspeaker = oral/verbal; notice = written.
      (b) Visual attracts, oral spreads fast, written is proof — together they cover more audiences with both speed and a record.

B. MCQs (10)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

M1. Which part of communication converts an idea into words or signs? (a) Encoding (b) Receiving (c) Decoding (d) Feedback
    Ans: (a) Encoding. Encoding prepares the sender's idea as a message; decoding interprets it.

M2. A loud generator prevents a worker from hearing instructions. The barrier is: (a) physical (b) linguistic (c) cultural (d) emotional
    Ans: (a) Physical — environmental noise blocks the message.

M3. Which of the 7 Cs means “to the point, no extra words”? (a) Clear (b) Concise (c) Concrete (d) Courteous
    Ans: (b) Concise.

M4. “An honest boy” takes which article? (a) a (b) the (c) no article (d) an
    Ans: (d) an — use an before a vowel sound; h is silent in honest.

M5. A student's puzzled expression after an explanation is: (a) non-verbal feedback (b) written encoding (c) a channel (d) a language rule
    Ans: (a) Non-verbal feedback.

M6. Which sentence is complete? (a) Do that work. (b) Submit the signed form to Room 4 by Friday noon. (c) The form is important. (d) Submit it soon.
    Ans: (b) — task, place and deadline are all present.

M7. Paralanguage refers to: (a) body posture only (b) charts and maps (c) tone, pitch and speed of voice (d) grammar rules
    Ans: (c) Tone, pitch and speed of voice.

M8. The easiest method of communication is: (a) written (b) oral (c) non-verbal (d) email
    Ans: (c) Non-verbal — needs no literacy.

M9. The communication process is complete only when: (a) the message is written  (b) the receiver gives feedback  (c) the sender speaks  (d) the channel is chosen
    Ans: (b) the receiver gives feedback. Feedback tells the sender whether the message was understood.

M10. Which method of communication carries no body language or tone of voice? (a) face-to-face talk  (b) video call  (c) group discussion  (d) written communication
    Ans: (d) written communication. That is why written messages must be especially clear.
