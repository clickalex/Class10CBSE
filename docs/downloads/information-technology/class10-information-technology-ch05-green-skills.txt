A. WRITTEN QUESTIONS (12)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. [Short · 1 mark] Define sustainable development.
    Ans: Development that meets present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

Q2. [Short · 2 marks] What are green skills? Give two examples.
    Ans: Skills that help a person live and work in an environmentally friendly way. Examples: waste segregation, saving electricity, water conservation.

Q3. [Short · 2 marks] What is sustainable development?
    Ans: It is development that meets the needs of the present without reducing the ability of future generations to meet their own needs — using resources carefully, keeping pollution low and protecting ecosystems while the economy grows.

Q4. [Short · 2 marks] What is a green economy? State its three features.
    Ans: A green economy is one that improves human well-being while reducing environmental risks. Its three features are: low carbon emissions, efficient use of resources, and inclusive growth that does not leave the poor behind.

Q5. [Short · 2 marks] Arrange Reduce, Reuse, Recycle in order of preference and justify the order with one example each.
    Ans:
      First Reduce — preventing waste is best: carry a cloth bag instead of taking a plastic one each time.
      Then Reuse — using again costs nothing extra: refill the same water bottle for months.
      Last Recycle — reprocessing still needs energy: send worn-out notebooks for paper recycling only when reuse is over.

Q6. [Application · 3 marks] A computer lab leaves 20 PCs on overnight. Suggest three green practices and one green skill the lab assistant needs.
    Ans: Shut down PCs, use sleep mode, switch off AC/lights, enable duplex printing. Skill: energy-conservation habit / e-waste handling.

Q7. [Application · 3 marks] A student defines sustainable development as “using no natural resources so that nothing is wasted”. Correct the definition.
    Ans:
      Sustainable development means meeting the needs of the present without harming the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.
      It is about wise use of resources, not zero use: we still use water, energy and materials, but carefully.
      Examples are using renewable energy, saving water and recycling.

Q8. [Short · 3 marks] Give three examples of green jobs and explain what makes a job green.
    Ans:
      Installing and maintaining solar panels, managing recycling and composting, organic farming, water harvesting and energy auditing are all green jobs.
      A job is green when it reduces the use of energy or raw materials, cuts greenhouse-gas emissions and waste, and protects or restores ecosystems.
      It should also provide decent work and income, because a green economy is meant to improve human well-being as well as the environment.

Q9. [Short · 3 marks] What makes a job a ‘green job’? Name two green jobs and the green skill each needs.
    Ans:
      A job is green when it directly protects or restores the environment — through clean energy, waste handling, conservation or green construction.
      Solar-panel technician — needs the skill of installing and maintaining photovoltaic systems safely.
      Waste-segregation supervisor — needs the skill of sorting waste at source into wet, dry and hazardous streams.

Q10. [Competency · 4 marks] Read and answer:
    
    A factory dumps dye in a river but pays workers well.
    
    (a) Is this sustainable development? Why?
    (b) Name two green-economy changes.
    (c) One green job this town could create.
    Ans:
      (a) No — present wages at the cost of future water and health.
      (b) Treat effluent, switch to safer dye, reuse water.
      (c) Water-treatment operator or waste-segregation worker.

Q11. [Competency · 4 marks] Read and answer:
    
    A repair shop collects old computers, refurbishes some for sale at low cost and sends the rest to an authorised recycler.
    
    (a) Which of the 3Rs does each activity show?
    (b) Why is it dangerous to throw such equipment into the dustbin?
    (c) Name a green job linked to this work.
    Ans:
      (a) Refurbishing and reselling is reuse; sending the rest to a recycler is recycle.
      (b) E-waste contains metals such as lead and mercury, which pollute soil and water and harm health.
      (c) An e-waste recycling technician (or a refurbishing specialist).

Q12. [Long · 5 marks] Describe five green practices that a school computer lab can follow.
    Ans:
      Switch off computers, monitors and lights when not in use, and use energy-saving settings.
      Print only when necessary and print on both sides, and share files digitally.
      Repair and reuse equipment, and give old devices to an authorised e-waste recycler.
      Use rechargeable batteries and energy-efficient (LED) lighting.
      Make students aware of these habits, for example by appointing an “eco-monitor”.

B. MCQs (8)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

M1. Sustainable development looks after: (a) present without harming the future (b) only the present (c) only the future (d) only factories
    Ans: (a).

M2. The best of the 3 Rs is: (a) Recycle (b) Reuse (c) Reduce (d) Refuse nothing
    Ans: (c) Reduce — don't create the waste.

M3. A solar-panel technician is: (a) a linguistic barrier (b) an OS (c) a myth (d) a green job
    Ans: (d) a green job.

M4. Leaving a PC on overnight mainly wastes: (a) paper (b) toner only (c) nothing (d) electricity
    Ans: (d) electricity.

M5. E-waste mainly refers to: (a) kitchen waste (b) discarded electronic and electrical equipment (c) waste paper (d) plastic bottles only
    Ans: (b) discarded electronic and electrical equipment. E-waste includes old computers, phones, batteries and cables, and needs special disposal.

M6. Which of the following is a renewable source of energy? (a) solar energy (b) coal (c) petroleum (d) natural gas
    Ans: (a) solar energy. Sunlight is not used up when it is used; coal, petroleum and natural gas are fossil fuels.

M7. A green economy is one that: (a) produces the most goods  (b) bans all industry  (c) improves human well-being while reducing environmental risks  (d) depends only on coal
    Ans: (c) improves human well-being while reducing environmental risks. It is low-carbon and resource-efficient.

M8. Which of these is a green skill? (a) leaving taps running  (b) segregating waste at source  (c) burning dry leaves  (d) using a plastic bag every day
    Ans: (b) segregating waste at source. It makes recycling possible and cuts the load on landfills.
