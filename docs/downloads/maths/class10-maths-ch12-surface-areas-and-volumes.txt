A. WRITTEN QUESTIONS (12)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. [Short · 2 marks] Find the total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius 7 cm (π = 22/7).
    Ans: Total surface area of a solid hemisphere = 3πr² = 3 × (22/7) × 49 = 462 cm² (curved 308 + flat circular face 154).

Q2. [Short · 3 marks] A solid consists of a cylinder with a hemisphere on one end and a cone on the other, all of radius 7 cm. The cylinder is 20 cm high, the cone 12 cm high. Find the total surface area (take π = 22/7).
    Ans:
      Cylinder CSA = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 20 = 880 cm².
      Hemisphere CSA = 2 × (22/7) × 49 = 308 cm².
      Cone slant height l = √(7² + 12²) = √193 ≈ 13.89 cm, so cone CSA = (22/7) × 7 × 13.89 ≈ 305.6 cm².
      Total ≈ 880 + 308 + 305.6 = 1493.6 cm². The two circular joining faces are not counted.

Q3. [Short · 3 marks] A solid metallic sphere of radius 4.2 cm is melted and recast into a cylinder of radius 6 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.
    Ans:
      Volume of sphere = volume of cylinder.
      (4/3)π(4.2)³ = π(6)²h.
      h = (4 × 74.088)/(3 × 36) = 296.352/108 ≈ 2.74 cm.

Q4. [Application · 3 marks] A student finds the volume of a cone with r = 7 cm and h = 12 cm as (22/7) × 7² × 12 = 1848 cm³. Identify the error and give the correct volume.
    Ans:
      Error: that is the volume of the cylinder of the same base and height. A cone holds one-third of it.
      Correct formula: V = ⅓πr²h.
      V = ⅓ × 1848 = 616 cm³.

Q5. [Application · 3 marks] A toy is a cone mounted on a hemisphere of the same base radius 3.5 cm. The total height of the toy is 15.5 cm. Find its total surface area. (Take π = 22/7.)
    Ans:
      Height of the cone = 15.5 − 3.5 = 12 cm, so slant height l = √(3.5² + 12²) = √156.25 = 12.5 cm.
      Curved surface of the cone = πrl = (22/7) × 3.5 × 12.5 = 137.5 cm²; curved surface of the hemisphere = 2πr² = 77 cm².
      Total surface area = 137.5 + 77 = 214.5 cm². (The joining circle is inside, so it is not counted.)

Q6. [Short · 3 marks] A cylindrical bucket 32 cm high with base radius 18 cm is emptied to form a conical heap of sand 24 cm high. Find the radius of the heap.
    Ans:
      Volumes are equal: π(18)²(32) = (1/3)πr²(24).
      ⇒ 324 × 32 = 8r² ⇒ r² = 10368/8 = 1296.
      So r = 36 cm. (The height of the heap is ¼ of the cylinder’s, but the radius works out larger.)

Q7. [Short · 3 marks] A cylindrical vessel of radius 6 cm and height 12 cm is full of water. How many conical cups of radius 2 cm and height 6 cm can be filled from it?
    Ans:
      Volume of the vessel = π × 6² × 12 = 432π cm³.
      Volume of one cup = (1/3)π × 2² × 6 = 8π cm³.
      Number of cups = 432π ÷ 8π = 54.
      So 54 conical cups can be filled.

Q8. [Long · 4 marks] 100 solid iron spheres, each of radius 1 cm, are melted to form one solid sphere. Find its radius.
    Ans:
      Total volume is conserved: 100 × (4/3)π(1)³ = (4/3)πR³.
      Cancelling (4/3)π: R³ = 100, so R = ∛100 ≈ 4.64 cm.

Q9. [Long · 4 marks] A frustum of a cone has radii 14 cm and 7 cm and height 24 cm. Find its slant height and curved surface area (take π = 22/7).
    Ans:
      l = √[24² + (14 − 7)²] = √(576 + 49) = √625 = 25 cm.
      CSA = π(R + r)l = (22/7) × (14 + 7) × 25 = (22/7) × 21 × 25 = 1650 cm².

Q10. [Competency · 4 marks] Read and answer:
    
    A cylindrical water tank has a diameter of 4 m and a height of 7 m. (Take π = 22/7.)
    
    (a) Find its volume in m³.
    (b) How many litres can it hold? (1 m³ = 1000 litres)
    (c) A family uses 400 litres a day. For how many days will a full tank last?
    Ans:
      (a) r = 2 m, so V = πr²h = (22/7) × 4 × 7 = 88 m³.
      (b) 88 × 1000 = 88,000 litres.
      (c) 88,000 ÷ 400 = 220 days.

Q11. [Competency · 4 marks] Read and answer:
    
    A metal cuboid of dimensions 44 cm × 21 cm × 12 cm is melted and recast into a solid cylinder of radius 7 cm. (Take π = 22/7.)
    
    (a) Find the volume of the cuboid.
    (b) Find the height of the cylinder.
    (c) Why is the volume, and not the surface area, equated?
    Ans:
      (a) V = 44 × 21 × 12 = 11,088 cm³.
      (b) πr²h = 11,088, so (22/7) × 49 × h = 11,088, i.e. 154h = 11,088 and h = 72 cm.
      (c) Melting and recasting changes the shape but not the amount of metal, so the volume is conserved; the surface area changes.

Q12. [Application · 4 marks] A drinking glass is shaped like a frustum of a cone with height 14 cm and end diameters 4 cm and 2 cm. Find its capacity.
    Ans:
      R = 2 cm, r = 1 cm, h = 14 cm.
      Volume = (πh/3)(R² + r² + Rr) = (π × 14/3)(4 + 1 + 2) = (14π/3)(7).
      = 98π/3 = (98/3)(22/7) = 308/3 ≈ 102.7 cm³.

B. MCQs (8)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

M1. The volume of a cone of radius r and height h is: (a) πr²h  (b) πr²h/3  (c) 2πr²h  (d) πrh
    Ans: (b) πr²h/3.

M2. When a solid is melted and recast into another shape, which quantity is conserved? (a) surface area  (b) volume  (c) both  (d) neither
    Ans: (b) Volume. Surface area almost always changes.

M3. The slant height of a frustum with radii R and r and height h is: (a) √[h² + (R − r)²]  (b) √[h² + (R + r)²]  (c) h + R + r  (d) √(R² − r²)
    Ans: (a) √[h² + (R − r)²].

M4. The total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius r is: (a) 2πr²  (b) 4πr²  (c) 3πr²  (d) πr²
    Ans: (c) 3πr². Curved surface 2πr² plus the flat circular face πr² gives 3πr².

M5. If the radius of a sphere is doubled, its volume becomes: (a) 2 times  (b) 4 times  (c) 6 times  (d) 8 times
    Ans: (d) 8 times. V = (4/3)πr³, so doubling r multiplies the volume by 2³ = 8.

M6. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius r and height h is: (a) πr²h  (b) 2πr(r + h)  (c) 2πrh  (d) πrl
    Ans: (c) 2πrh. 2πr(r + h) is the total surface area and πr²h is the volume.

M7. The volume of a sphere of radius r is: (a) (4/3)πr³  (b) 4πr²  (c) (2/3)πr³  (d) πr³
    Ans: (a) (4/3)πr³. 4πr² is the surface area of the sphere.

M8. A cone, a hemisphere and a cylinder have equal bases and equal heights. The ratio of their volumes is: (a) 2 : 3 : 1  (b) 3 : 1 : 2  (c) 1 : 3 : 2  (d) 1 : 2 : 3
    Ans: (d) 1 : 2 : 3. With radius = height = r: πr³/3 : 2πr³/3 : πr³.
