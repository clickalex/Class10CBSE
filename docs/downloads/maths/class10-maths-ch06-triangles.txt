A. WRITTEN QUESTIONS (12)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. [Short · 1 mark] State the Basic Proportionality Theorem.
    Ans: If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, then the other two sides are divided in the same ratio.

Q2. [Short · 2 marks] In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC with AD = 1.5 cm, DB = 3 cm, AE = 1 cm. Find EC.
    Ans: By the Basic Proportionality Theorem, a line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides in the same ratio: AD/DB = AE/EC. So 1.5/3 = 1/EC, giving EC = (3 × 1)/1.5 = 2 cm. The theorem only applies because DE ∥ BC is given — without that, the ratio cannot be used.

Q3. [Short · 2 marks] State the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem.
    Ans: If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is parallel to the third side. In ΔABC, if a line through D on AB and E on AC satisfies AD/DB = AE/EC, then DE ∥ BC.

Q4. [Short · 3 marks] The areas of two similar triangles are in the ratio 9:16. If one side of the smaller triangle is 4.5 cm, find the corresponding side of the larger.
    Ans:
      Area ratio = square of side ratio, so 9/16 = (4.5/x)².
      Taking square roots: 3/4 = 4.5/x, so x = 6 cm.

Q5. [Short · 3 marks] Prove that in a right triangle, the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares on the other two sides.
    Ans:
      In right ΔABC, right-angled at B, draw BD ⊥ AC.
      ΔADB ∼ ΔABC (AA), so AD/AB = AB/AC, giving AB² = AD × AC.
      ΔBDC ∼ ΔABC (AA), so DC/BC = BC/AC, giving BC² = DC × AC.
      Adding: AB² + BC² = (AD + DC) × AC = AC².

Q6. [Application · 3 marks] Two similar triangles have corresponding sides 6 cm and 9 cm. A student says the ratio of their areas is 6 : 9 = 2 : 3. Correct the error, and find the area of the larger triangle if the smaller has area 40 cm².
    Ans:
      Error: the areas of similar triangles are in the ratio of the squares of corresponding sides.
      Area ratio = (6/9)² = 4/9.
      Area of the larger triangle = 40 × 9/4 = 90 cm².

Q7. [Application · 3 marks] A 6 m tall pole casts a shadow 4 m long. At the same moment a tower casts a shadow 30 m long. Name the similarity criterion used and find the height of the tower.
    Ans:
      The sun’s rays make the same angle with the ground, and both pole and tower are vertical: two angles match, so the two triangles are similar by the AA criterion.
      Corresponding sides are proportional: height of tower/6 = 30/4.
      Height of tower = 6 × 30/4 = 45 m.

Q8. [Short · 3 marks] In ΔABC, D and E lie on AB and AC with AD = 5.7 cm, DB = 9.5 cm, AE = 4.8 cm and EC = 8 cm. Is DE parallel to BC?
    Ans:
      AD/DB = 5.7/9.5 = 0.6 and AE/EC = 4.8/8 = 0.6.
      The two ratios are equal, so by the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem DE ∥ BC.

Q9. [Competency · 4 marks] Read and answer:

    A 10 m long ladder leans against a vertical wall and reaches a window 8 m above the ground.

    (a) How far is the foot of the ladder from the wall?
    (b) The foot is moved until the ladder reaches only 6 m up the wall. How far does the foot move?
    (c) Name the theorem used.
    Ans:
      (a) Distance = √(10² − 8²) = √36 = 6 m.
      (b) New distance = √(10² − 6²) = √64 = 8 m, so the foot moves 8 − 6 = 2 m away from the wall.
      (c) The Pythagoras theorem in the right-angled triangle formed by ladder, wall and ground.

Q10. [Competency · 4 marks] Read and answer:

    In a triangular park ABC, a path DE is laid parallel to the side BC, with D on AB and E on AC. AD = 4 m, DB = 6 m, AC = 15 m and BC = 20 m.

    (a) Find AE.
    (b) Find DE.
    (c) Find the ratio of the areas of ΔADE and ΔABC.
    Ans:
      (a) By BPT, AD/AB = AE/AC, so 4/10 = AE/15 and AE = 6 m.
      (b) ΔADE ~ ΔABC, so DE/BC = AD/AB = 2/5 and DE = 8 m.
      (c) Area ratio = (2/5)² = 4 : 25.

Q11. [Long · 4 marks] Prove that the ratio of the areas of two similar triangles equals the square of the ratio of their corresponding medians.
    Ans:
      Let ΔABC ~ ΔPQR with medians AD and PS to the sides BC and QR.
      Since the triangles are similar, AB/PQ = BC/QR and ∠B = ∠Q.
      D and S are midpoints, so BD = BC/2 and QS = QR/2, giving BD/QS = BC/QR = AB/PQ.
      With the included angles equal, ΔABD ~ ΔPQS (SAS), so AD/PS = AB/PQ.
      Therefore ar(ABC)/ar(PQR) = (AB/PQ)² = (AD/PS)².

Q12. [Long · 5 marks] Prove that if a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides at distinct points, it divides the two sides in the same ratio.
    Ans:
      Given: ΔABC with DE ∥ BC, D on AB and E on AC. To prove: AD/DB = AE/EC.
      Construction: join BE and CD; draw EM ⊥ AB and DN ⊥ AC.
      ar(ADE) = ½ × AD × EM and ar(BDE) = ½ × DB × EM, so ar(ADE)/ar(BDE) = AD/DB. Similarly ar(ADE)/ar(CDE) = AE/EC.
      ΔBDE and ΔCDE stand on the same base DE and lie between the same parallels DE and BC, so ar(BDE) = ar(CDE).
      Therefore AD/DB = AE/EC. ∎

B. MCQs (8)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

M1. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding sides are: (a) proportional  (b) equal  (c) perpendicular  (d) parallel
    Ans: (a) Proportional. That is the SSS similarity criterion.

M2. The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is 25:49. The ratio of their corresponding sides is: (a) 25:49  (b) 5:7  (c) 7:5  (d) 49:25
    Ans: (b) 5:7. Square root of the area ratio.

M3. In a right triangle with legs 6 cm and 8 cm, the hypotenuse is: (a) 12 cm  (b) 14 cm  (c) 10 cm  (d) 9 cm
    Ans: (c) 10 cm. √(36 + 64) = 10.

M4. If ΔABC ~ ΔPQR, ∠A = 50° and ∠Q = 60°, then ∠C equals: (a) 60°  (b) 70°  (c) 50°  (d) 80°
    Ans: (b) 70°. ∠B = ∠Q = 60° because corresponding angles of similar triangles are equal, so ∠C = 180° − 50° − 60° = 70°.

M5. A triangle has sides 7 cm, 24 cm and 25 cm. It is: (a) acute-angled  (b) obtuse-angled  (c) equilateral  (d) right-angled
    Ans: (d) right-angled. 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25², so by the converse of the Pythagoras theorem the triangle is right-angled.

M6. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are: (a) supplementary  (b) complementary  (c) proportional  (d) equal
    Ans: (d) equal. Equiangular triangles are similar — the AAA similarity criterion.

M7. In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC with AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 5 cm. Then EC = : (a) 9 cm  (b) 8 cm  (c) 7.5 cm  (d) 6.5 cm
    Ans: (c) 7.5 cm. AD/DB = AE/EC ⇒ 4/6 = 5/EC ⇒ EC = 7.5 cm.

M8. If the corresponding sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 4 : 9, their areas are in the ratio: (a) 16 : 81  (b) 4 : 9  (c) 2 : 3  (d) 8 : 27
    Ans: (a) 16 : 81. The area ratio of similar triangles is the square of the side ratio.
