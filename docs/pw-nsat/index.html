PW NSAT

Physics Wallah National Scholarship cum Admission Test

A scholarship test for PW coaching, including Vidyapeeth offline batches. It is separate from Class XI school or intermediate-college admission tests such as JMI, AMU and BHU SET.

Open official PW NSAT website / registration

Official-page snapshot

Checked on 20 September 2026. These details are a dated summary, not a live registration tracker. Dates, available slots and terms may change. Recheck the official page before applying or making travel plans.

Class 10 eligibility
Class 10 is included in the official Classes 5–12 and dropper eligibility list, subject to the applicable course criteria.
Online test window
1–15 October 2026; the page lists availability from 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM. Confirm your chosen slot during registration.
Offline test dates
3, 4, 10 and 11 October 2026.
Offline slots
10:00–11:00 AM or 4:00–5:00 PM, as listed by PW. Confirm your centre and slot on the official portal.
Duration
60 minutes.
Question format
Single-correct-answer questions; +4 for a correct answer and no negative marking in Level-1, according to the official FAQ.
Level-1 result
25 October 2026, as announced on the official page.
Registration
The page displayed 'Register for free' when checked. A closing date was not verified; confirm availability on PW's portal.
Scholarship
PW advertises up to 100% scholarships. This is not guaranteed for every student; check award conditions, eligible batches, fees and exclusions before enrolling.

Source: PW NSAT official page and FAQ.

For a Class 10 student

  1. Open the official page and select your current class: Class 10. Confirm the course and next-session eligibility.
  2. Use View Syllabus on PW's page and select Class 10. Do not assume the NSAT syllabus is identical to the CBSE board syllabus.
  3. Choose online or offline mode and confirm the date, centre and slot. Submit personal details only on the official portal.
  4. Save your confirmation and instructions privately. Do not commit application details, passwords or identity documents to this repository.
  5. Check scholarship conditions and the remaining course fees before accepting an offer. A coaching scholarship is not a school admission.
