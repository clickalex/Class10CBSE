Written questions · Write

SHORTS13 marks

1. How did Richard Ebright's mother help him in his work?

Show Answer

Ans:

She was his only companion after his father's death, and she encouraged his learning in every way. She took him on trips, bought him telescopes, microscopes, cameras and other scientific equipment, and always found work for him to do. Most importantly she gave him the children's book The Travels of Monarch X, which opened the world of science to him and started him on his work with butterflies.

SHORTS23 marks

2. What did Ebright discover about the twelve gold spots on a monarch pupa?

Show Answer

Ans:

He found that the twelve tiny gold spots on a monarch pupa were not merely ornamental, as was thought, but produced a hormone necessary for the full development of the butterfly. He demonstrated this by removing the spots and showing the effect, and the discovery won him the county science fair and a place at the National Insect Collection.

SHORTS33 marks

3. What qualities go into the making of a scientist?

Show Answer

Ans:

According to the chapter, three things are needed: a first-rate mind, curiosity, and the will to win for the right reasons — not merely to win, but to do the best job one can. Richard Ebright had all three, and he also had the advantage of a mother who encouraged him and a teacher who opened his mind to ideas and taught him to compete in a healthy way.
