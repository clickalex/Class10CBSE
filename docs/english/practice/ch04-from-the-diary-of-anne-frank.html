Written questions · Write

SHORTS13 marks

1. Why did Anne Frank feel the need for a diary?

Show Answer

Ans:

Because although she had a loving family, friends and admirers, she had no true friend — nobody to whom she could confide her innermost thoughts. She wrote that paper has more patience than people, so she began the diary as a substitute for a real friend, and named it Kitty.

SHORTS23 marks

2. What argument did Anne make in her first essay, 'A Chatterbox'?

Show Answer

Ans:

She argued that talking is a student's trait and that she would try to keep it under control, but that she would never be able to cure herself completely of the habit, because her mother talks as much as she does. She added that there is little one can do about inherited traits — an argument which Mr Keesing took in the right spirit.

LONGL35 marks

3. Describe Mr Keesing and how his attitude towards Anne changed.

Show Answer

Ans:

Mr Keesing was Anne's mathematics teacher, an old-fashioned teacher who was annoyed by her constant talking in class. He punished her by assigning an essay titled 'A Chatterbox'.
Anne's response: she wrote three pages, admitting the habit but arguing that talking is a student's trait and that her mother talks as much as she does, so it is an inherited trait and there is little one can do about it. Mr Keesing took the joke in the right spirit.
The second and third essays: when she talked again he assigned 'An Incorrigible Chatterbox', and the third time he set 'Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterbox' — this time in verse. With the help of her friend Sanne, Anne wrote a comic poem about a mother duck and a father swan whose three ducklings are bitten to death by the swan for quacking too much.
The change: Mr Keesing read the poem to his own class and then to another class, adding his own comments, and laughed at it. From then on he allowed Anne to talk in class and never gave her extra homework again. Anne calls him the nicest teacher she ever met.

SHORTS43 marks

4. What does Anne write about her grandmother?

Show Answer

Ans:

Anne writes that nobody knows how often she thinks of her grandmother and still loves her. Her grandmother was born in 1888 and died in January 1942, and Anne says that she wrote the diary partly so that this love would not be lost. She adds that this whole diary was written in the thought of her grandmother.

LONGL55 marks

5. Discuss “From the Diary of Anne Frank” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

Summary. Thirteen-year-old Anne Frank begins her diary, which she names Kitty, on her thirteenth birthday. She explains that 'paper has more patience than people': she has a loving family, an adoring father and mother, a sixteen-year-old sister Margot, about thirty friends and many admirers, but no true friend to whom she can confide her innermost thoughts. Her grandmother, whom she loved very much, died in January 1.
Marks lens: Reference-to-context extracts, usually from the opening passage about why she keeps a diary, or from the essay 'Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterbox'.
Method / memory trick: 'Paper has more patience than people' — the single most quoted line from this chapter; learn it exactly.
Do not: Saying Anne had no friends at all. She had about thirty friends and admirers, but no true friend to confide in.
