Ch 31 · Formal Letter Writing — Write & Drill

MCQs · Drill

MCQM11 mark

1. In a formal letter, the sender's name is written: (a) at the top with the address (b) at the bottom, under the signature (c) in the subject line (d) after the salutation

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) at the bottom, under the signature — the address at the top does not include the name.

MCQM21 mark

2. The word limit for a formal letter in the board examination is: (a) 50–60 words (b) 100–120 words (c) 150–200 words (d) no limit

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) 100–120 words.

MCQM31 mark

3. 'Yours faithfully' is used when the letter begins with: (a) Dear Mr Sharma (b) Sir or Madam (c) Respected Principal (d) My dear friend

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) Sir or Madam — 'Yours sincerely' is used when the recipient is addressed by name.

MCQM41 mark

4. The most exam-ready way to revise “Formal Letter Writing” is: (a) read the heading only (b) learn the key idea plus one example (c) skip written practice (d) memorise a random extra topic

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) learn the key idea plus one example. The chapter is Letters to the editor and letters of order, enquiry, complaint..

MCQM51 mark

5. A 3-mark answer on “Formal Letter Writing” should: (a) be one sentence (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method (c) copy the whole chapter (d) leave the example out

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) start with the idea, then a reason or method. Step marks follow that frame.

MCQM61 mark

6. A letter to the editor begins with: (a) the sender's address, date, and then ‘The Editor' (b) the subject line (c) ‘Dear Sir' alone (d) a question

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) the sender's address, date, and then ‘The Editor'. The format marks depend on this order.

MCQM71 mark

7. In a letter of complaint or order, the subject line should be: (a) short and specific (b) long and detailed (c) left out (d) written as a question

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) short and specific. It tells the reader at once what the letter is about.

MCQM81 mark

8. A letter of enquiry should always ask for: (a) specific details such as price, terms and delivery (b) personal favours (c) a job (d) an autograph

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) specific details such as price, terms and delivery. Vague enquiries get vague replies — and fewer marks.

Written questions · Write

SHORTS13 marks

1. Give the format of a formal letter.

Show Answer

Ans:

Sender's address at the top left, without the name; the date in full; the receiver's designation and address; a one-line subject; the salutation Sir or Madam; the body in three short paragraphs; the complimentary close Yours faithfully or Yours sincerely; and the signature, name and designation at the bottom left.

SHORTS23 marks

2. What should a letter of complaint contain?

Show Answer

Ans:

It should give the invoice or bill number and the date of purchase, describe precisely what is wrong with the goods or the service, state the remedy required — replacement, repair or refund — and give a reasonable deadline. The tone must be firm but polite, and the letter must end with a request for an early reply.

APPLICATIONA33 marks

3. Riya is revising “Formal Letter Writing” (Letters to the editor and letters of order, enquiry, complaint.). A classmate lost marks by doing this: Putting your name in the sender's address at the top. It goes at the bottom, under the signature. Apply the correct method and rewrite the scoring answer.

Show Answer

Ans:

Correct method: The subject line is one line only. If it runs to two, shorten it.
Why the mistake costs marks: Putting your name in the sender's address at the top. It goes at the bottom, under the signature.
Write the definition or formula first, then the working or example.

APPLICATIONA43 marks

4. Amit is given this exam-style task from “Formal Letter Writing”: “Give the format of a formal letter.” Treat it as an application question. Identify the idea, then write the working in points.

Show Answer

Ans:

Idea: Letters to the editor and letters of order, enquiry, complaint..
Sender's address at the top left, without the name; the date in full; the receiver's designation and address; a one-line subject; the salutation Sir or Madam; the body in three short paragraphs; the complimentary close Yours faithfully or Yours sincerely; and the signature, name and designation at the bottom left.

SHORTS53 marks

5. What is the format of a letter to the editor?

Show Answer

Ans:

Sender's address on the left, then the date, then ‘The Editor' with the name of the newspaper and its city.
Then the subject line in brief, then the salutation ‘Sir' or ‘Madam'.
The body: introduce the issue in one sentence, explain the problem and its effect, suggest two or three solutions and end with a hope that the authorities will act.
End with ‘Thanking you' or ‘Yours truly', and then the sender's name and designation or address.

SHORTS63 marks

6. What are the three paragraphs of the body of a formal letter expected to do? Illustrate with a complaint about damaged goods.

Show Answer

Ans:

Paragraph 1 states the purpose — the order number, date and the fact that the goods arrived damaged.
Paragraph 2 gives the details — which items are broken, how the packing failed, and what the invoice says.
Paragraph 3 states what you want done — replacement, refund or repair — with a clear, polite deadline.

COMPETENCYC74 marks

7. Read and answer: A Class 10 student is preparing “Formal Letter Writing”. Their note says: The format. (1) Sender's address at the top left, in two or three lines, without the name. (2) Date, written in full — 15 March 2027, not 15/3/27. (3) Receiver's designation and address — The Editor / The Manager, the name of the newspaper or firm, and the city. (4) Subject, in one line, short and s The chapter is described as: Letters to the editor and letters of order, enquiry, complaint.. (a) Name the key idea. (b) How is this usually asked in the paper? (c) State one mistake that would cost a mark.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Letters to the editor and letters of order, enquiry, complaint.
(b) marks, 100–120 words. Two kinds are set: a letter to the editor and a letter of order, enquiry or complaint to a firm or an official.
(c) Putting your name in the sender's address at the top. It goes at the bottom, under the signature.

COMPETENCYC84 marks

8. Read and answer: Two students attempt: “Give the format of a formal letter.” Student A writes one line. Student B writes points, an example and a check. (a) Which answer frame should you use for full marks? (b) Write the model points. (c) Add one memory trick you would use on exam morning.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Statement → working or reasons in points → example or check. One-line answers lose step marks.
(b) Sender's address at the top left, without the name; the date in full; the receiver's designation and address; a one-line subject; the salutation Sir or Madam; the body in three short paragraphs; the complimentary close Yours faithfully or Yours sincerely; and the signature, name and designation at the bottom left.
(c) The subject line is one line only. If it runs to two, shorten it.

APPLICATIONA94 marks

9. Write a letter to the editor of a national daily on the nuisance of loudspeakers in your locality.

Show Answer

Ans:

Start with the address, date and ‘The Editor, The Times of India, New Delhi', and give the subject: ‘Nuisance of loudspeakers in residential areas'.
Body: say that loudspeakers are played at high volume from early morning till late at night, disturbing students, the elderly and night-shift workers.
Point out that this flouts the Noise Pollution Rules and the Supreme Court's directions, and that repeated complaints to the local police have had no effect.
Suggest strict enforcement of the 10 pm limit, seizure of equipment on repetition, and a helpline for complaints; end with ‘Yours truly' and your name and address.

APPLICATIONA104 marks

10. A student writes the sender's name at the top of a formal letter and signs off ‘Yours lovingly’. Correct both errors and explain why each loses marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

First error — the sender's name at the top: only the address goes at the top (no name); the name comes at the bottom, under the signature. It breaks the format the examiner checks first.
Second error — ‘Yours lovingly’: a formal letter opening with Sir/Madam must close with ‘Yours faithfully’ (or ‘Yours sincerely’ for a named officer). An informal close contradicts the letter's tone and loses the format mark.

LONGL115 marks

11. Write a letter to the editor about the irregular water supply in your locality.

Show Answer

Ans:

Examination Hall (or the sender's address) / 12, Civil Lines / Jaipur
15 March 2027
The Editor / The Rajasthan Patrika / Jaipur
Subject: Irregular water supply in Civil Lines
Sir,
Para 1: Through the columns of your esteemed daily, I wish to draw the attention of the concerned authorities to the irregular water supply in our locality, which has caused great hardship to the residents for the past three weeks.
Para 2: Water is supplied for barely half an hour, and that too at odd hours. On many days there is no supply at all. The overhead tanks run dry, and the residents, including schoolchildren and the elderly, are forced to depend on private tankers at high rates. Two written complaints to the Jal Board have received no reply.
Para 3: I hope this letter will prompt the authorities to restore a regular supply and to inform residents of the timings in advance.
Yours faithfully, / Amit Sharma / Resident, Civil Lines

LONGL125 marks

12. Discuss “Formal Letter Writing” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

The format. (1) Sender's address at the top left, in two or three lines, without the name. (2) Date, written in full — 15 March 2027, not 15/3/27. (3) Receiver's designation and address — The Editor / The Manager, the name of the newspaper or firm, and the city. (4) Subject, in one line, short and specific. (5) Salutation — Sir / Madam. (6) Body in three short paragraphs. (7) Complimentary close — Yours faithfully /.
Marks lens: marks, 100–120 words. Two kinds are set: a letter to the editor and a letter of order, enquiry or complaint to a firm or an official.
Method / memory trick: The subject line is one line only. If it runs to two, shorten it.
Do not: Putting your name in the sender's address at the top. It goes at the bottom, under the signature.
