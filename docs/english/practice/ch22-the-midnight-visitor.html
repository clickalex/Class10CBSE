MCQs · Drill

MCQM11 mark

1. Ausable is described as: (a) tall and handsome (b) very fat and sloppy (c) thin and sinister (d) old and blind

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) very fat and sloppy — deliberately unlike the usual picture of a secret agent.

MCQM21 mark

2. The knock at the door was: (a) the police (b) another agent (c) a waiter with a drink (d) the hotel manager

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) a waiter with a drink that Ausable had ordered.

MCQM31 mark

3. Max died because: (a) Ausable shot him (b) he jumped onto a balcony that did not exist (c) the police caught him (d) he fell in the corridor

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) he jumped backwards through the window onto a balcony that did not exist, and fell six storeys.

MCQM41 mark

4. The most exam-ready way to revise “The Midnight Visitor” is: (a) read the heading only (b) learn the key idea plus one example (c) skip written practice (d) memorise a random extra topic

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) learn the key idea plus one example. The chapter is Robert Arthur: Ausable the unlikely secret agent and a invented balcony..

MCQM51 mark

5. A 3-mark answer on “The Midnight Visitor” should: (a) be one sentence (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method (c) copy the whole chapter (d) leave the example out

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) start with the idea, then a reason or method. Step marks follow that frame.

MCQM61 mark

6. Ausable told Max that the balcony he saw belonged to: (a) the next apartment (b) Ausable's own room (c) the hotel manager (d) nobody

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) the next apartment. The lie made Max step out of the window and fall.

MCQM71 mark

7. Fowler, the writer, expected a secret agent to be: (a) romantic and mysterious (b) fat and foolish (c) elderly and rich (d) an Indian

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) romantic and mysterious. Ausable, fat and unglamorous, is a deliberate contrast.

MCQM81 mark

8. Max entered Ausable's room to steal: (a) a report about new missiles (b) money (c) a passport (d) a gun

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) a report about new missiles. The secret papers were the reason for the midnight visit.

Written questions · Write

SHORTS13 marks

1. How did Ausable get rid of Max?

Show Answer

Ans:

Ausable invented a balcony outside his window and told Max that he had complained twice to the hotel management about people entering his room through it. When the knock came at the door, he said it was the police, whom he had called. Max, panicking, said he would wait on the balcony and jumped backwards through the window — onto a balcony that did not exist — and fell six storeys to his death.

SHORTS23 marks

2. How is Ausable different from the usual picture of a secret agent?

Show Answer

Ans:

He is very fat and sloppy, and lives in a small, musty room on the top floor of a gloomy French hotel, which does not fit the image of a dashing spy. He speaks French and German only passably, and has never fired a shot or been involved in dramatic action. Yet he is a highly effective agent, because he uses his wits rather than weapons.

SHORTS33 marks

3. What was the knock at the door?

Show Answer

Ans:

It was a waiter bringing the drink that Ausable had ordered before Max arrived. Ausable had already mentioned expecting a drink, so when the knock came he was able to pretend it was the police he had called. The reveal that it was only a waiter is the story's comic ending.

APPLICATIONA43 marks

4. Riya is revising “The Midnight Visitor” (Robert Arthur: Ausable the unlikely secret agent and a invented balcony.). A classmate lost marks by doing this: Saying Ausable called the police. The knock was a waiter with the drink he had ordered. Apply the correct method and rewrite the scoring answer.

Show Answer

Ans:

Correct method: Ausable never had a balcony and never called the police — both are inventions. Say so explicitly.
Why the mistake costs marks: Saying Ausable called the police. The knock was a waiter with the drink he had ordered.
Write the definition or formula first, then the working or example.

SHORTS63 marks

6. How does Ausable outwit Max?

Show Answer

Ans:

Ausable tells Max and Fowler that his room has a balcony, and that someone has already got into it; this makes Max anxious.
When the waiter knocks, Ausable says it must be the police, whom he has asked to check on him.
Max backs towards the window, steps out onto what he believes is the balcony, and falls to his death — there is no balcony at all.
Ausable had invented the whole story, and his quick thinking removes a dangerous rival without a shot being fired.

SHORTS73 marks

7. Why did Fowler feel disappointed when he first met Ausable? How had his opinion changed by the end?

Show Answer

Ans:

Fowler, a young writer, expected a romantic, mysterious secret agent and found instead a very fat, sloppy man in a cheap French hotel — nothing like the films. By the end his disappointment turns to admiration: Ausable defeats an armed rival with nothing but two invented stories, proving that real spycraft is presence of mind, not glamour.

LONGL104 marks

10. What is ironic about Ausable as a secret agent?

Show Answer

Ans:

Fowler, a writer, expects a secret agent to be slim, romantic and mysterious — a figure out of a thriller.
['Instead he meets a fat, sloppy man with a thick accent, in a shabby room on the sixth floor of a French hotel.', 'This plain, unimpressive man is the one who outwits an armed spy by using only his wits and a fabricated story.', 'The irony is that the real professional is dull and clever, while the romantic picture exists only in books — which is the point of the story.']

APPLICATIONA114 marks

11. Ausable invents the balcony and the police visit within seconds. What does this presence of mind tell us about real spycraft versus film spycraft?

Show Answer

Ans:

Film spycraft is weapons, chases and gadgets; Ausable has none of these — only his wits.
In seconds he reads Max's fear (exposure) and builds a trap from it: a balcony that does not exist and policemen who are really a waiter.
The story's point is that intelligence beats intimidation — Max holds the gun, but Ausable holds the situation. That is why appearances mislead.

LONGL125 marks

12. Discuss “The Midnight Visitor” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

Summary. Ausable is a secret agent, but he is nothing like the popular image of one: he is very fat, lives in a small, musty room on the top floor of a gloomy French hotel, and speaks French and German only passably. He has just been given an important report about some missiles. Max, a rival agent, is waiting in his room with a pistol, having entered by a passkey, and demands the report. At that moment there is a kn.
Marks lens: Short answers and a character question on Ausable.
Method / memory trick: Ausable never had a balcony and never called the police — both are inventions. Say so explicitly.
Do not: Saying Ausable called the police. The knock was a waiter with the drink he had ordered.
