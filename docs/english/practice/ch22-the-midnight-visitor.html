Written questions · Write

SHORTS13 marks

1. How did Ausable get rid of Max?

Show Answer

Ans:

Ausable invented a balcony outside his window and told Max that he had complained twice to the hotel management about people entering his room through it. When the knock came at the door, he said it was the police, whom he had called. Max, panicking, said he would wait on the balcony and jumped backwards through the window — onto a balcony that did not exist — and fell six storeys to his death.

SHORTS23 marks

2. How is Ausable different from the usual picture of a secret agent?

Show Answer

Ans:

He is very fat and sloppy, and lives in a small, musty room on the top floor of a gloomy French hotel, which does not fit the image of a dashing spy. He speaks French and German only passably, and has never fired a shot or been involved in dramatic action. Yet he is a highly effective agent, because he uses his wits rather than weapons.

SHORTS33 marks

3. What was the knock at the door?

Show Answer

Ans:

It was a waiter bringing the drink that Ausable had ordered before Max arrived. Ausable had already mentioned expecting a drink, so when the knock came he was able to pretend it was the police he had called. The reveal that it was only a waiter is the story's comic ending.

LONGL45 marks

4. Discuss “The Midnight Visitor” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

Summary. Ausable is a secret agent, but he is nothing like the popular image of one: he is very fat, lives in a small, musty room on the top floor of a gloomy French hotel, and speaks French and German only passably. He has just been given an important report about some missiles. Max, a rival agent, is waiting in his room with a pistol, having entered by a passkey, and demands the report. At that moment there is a kn.
Marks lens: Short answers and a character question on Ausable.
Method / memory trick: Ausable never had a balcony and never called the police — both are inventions. Say so explicitly.
Do not: Saying Ausable called the police. The knock was a waiter with the drink he had ordered.
