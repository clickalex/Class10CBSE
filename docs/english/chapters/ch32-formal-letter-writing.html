Chapter 32 · Formal Letter Writing

🎯 Marks lens

10 marks, 100–120 words. Two kinds are set: a letter to the editor and a letter of order, enquiry or complaint to a firm or an official.
Format marks are awarded separately. Stick to the word limit.

📖 Deep concepts

1. The format. (1) Sender's address at the top left, in two or three lines, without the name. (2) Date, written in full — 15 March 2027, not 15/3/27. (3) Receiver's designation and address — The Editor / The Manager, the name of the newspaper or firm, and the city. (4) Subject, in one line, short and specific. (5) Salutation — Sir / Madam. (6) Body in three short paragraphs. (7) Complimentary close — Yours faithfully / Yours sincerely. (8) Signature, name and designation, if given.
2. The three paragraphs. First: state the purpose in one sentence — 'Through the columns of your esteemed daily, I wish to draw the attention of the authorities to…' for a letter to the editor, or 'I wish to place an order for…' for a letter of order. Second: give the details — the problem, the specification, the number and model, the complaint, or the data and examples. Third: state what you want done, politely and specifically, and end with the expectation of a reply or of action.
3. Letter to the editor. The tone is formal and public-spirited; the letter raises a social or civic issue, supports it with two or three concrete details, and ends with a request that the authorities be urged to act. It never names individuals or makes accusations.
4. Letter of order, enquiry or complaint. Order: give the item, model, quantity, the price quoted, the mode of payment, the delivery date and the address. Enquiry: ask specific questions and request a quotation and the terms. Complaint: give the invoice number, the date, exactly what is wrong, and the remedy you expect — replacement, refund or repair — with a reasonable deadline.

🧠 Memory tricks

  • The subject line is one line only. If it runs to two, shorten it.
  • 'Yours faithfully' with Sir/Madam; 'Yours sincerely' only when you use the person's name.
  • Keep the body to three paragraphs of three to four sentences. The word limit is checked.

⚠️ Mistakes that cost marks

  • Putting your name in the sender's address at the top. It goes at the bottom, under the signature.
  • Writing the date as 15/3/27. Write it in full.
  • Exceeding 120 words — this costs marks even if the content is good.
  • Using informal language or contractions such as 'don't' or 'I'm'.

LONGL25 marks

2. Write a letter to the editor about the irregular water supply in your locality.

Show Answer

Ans:

Examination Hall (or the sender's address) / 12, Civil Lines / Jaipur
15 March 2027
The Editor / The Rajasthan Patrika / Jaipur
Subject: Irregular water supply in Civil Lines
Sir,
Para 1: Through the columns of your esteemed daily, I wish to draw the attention of the concerned authorities to the irregular water supply in our locality, which has caused great hardship to the residents for the past three weeks.
Para 2: Water is supplied for barely half an hour, and that too at odd hours. On many days there is no supply at all. The overhead tanks run dry, and the residents, including schoolchildren and the elderly, are forced to depend on private tankers at high rates. Two written complaints to the Jal Board have received no reply.
Para 3: I hope this letter will prompt the authorities to restore a regular supply and to inform residents of the timings in advance.
Yours faithfully, / Amit Sharma / Resident, Civil Lines

SHORTS33 marks

3. What should a letter of complaint contain?

Show Answer

Ans:

It should give the invoice or bill number and the date of purchase, describe precisely what is wrong with the goods or the service, state the remedy required — replacement, repair or refund — and give a reasonable deadline. The tone must be firm but polite, and the letter must end with a request for an early reply.
