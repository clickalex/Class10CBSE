Chapter 22 · The Midnight Visitor

Chapter 22 of 33. A spy story with no guns — the hero wins with words and quick thinking.

🎯 Marks lens

Short answers and a character question on Ausable.
Ausable, Max, and the invented balcony are the three names to know.

📖 Deep concepts

1. Summary. Ausable is a secret agent, but he is nothing like the popular image of one: he is very fat, lives in a small, musty room on the top floor of a gloomy French hotel, and speaks French and German only passably. He has just been given an important report about some missiles. Max, a rival agent, is waiting in his room with a pistol, having entered by a passkey, and demands the report. At that moment there is a knock at the door. Ausable says it is the police, whom he has called because someone has been trying to enter his room through the balcony — a balcony that Max believes exists because Ausable describes it in such detail, and because Max himself has climbed in through a window that he thought led to a balcony. Panicking, Max steps backwards through the window onto a balcony that is not there, and falls six storeys to his death. The knock turns out to be a waiter bringing a drink that Ausable had ordered.
2. Characters. Ausable — fat, unglamorous, quick-witted; he invents a balcony and a police call in seconds and saves himself without firing a shot. Max — a slender, sinister rival agent, confident and armed, but out-thought.
3. Theme. Intelligence and presence of mind are more valuable than weapons; and appearances are misleading.

🧠 Memory tricks

  • Ausable never had a balcony and never called the police — both are inventions. Say so explicitly.
  • The waiter with the drink is the comic reveal; do not forget it.
  • Max dies because he believes the story, not because Ausable attacks him.

⚠️ Mistakes that cost marks

  • Saying Ausable called the police. The knock was a waiter with the drink he had ordered.
  • Saying there was a real balcony. There was none — Ausable invented it.
  • Describing Ausable as a typical dashing spy. He is fat and unglamorous — that is the point.

❓ Exam Q&A

3 marks How did Ausable get rid of Max?

Ausable invented a balcony outside his window and told Max that he had complained twice to the hotel management about people entering his room through it. When the knock came at the door, he said it was the police, whom he had called. Max, panicking, said he would wait on the balcony and jumped backwards through the window — onto a balcony that did not exist — and fell six storeys to his death.

3 marks How is Ausable different from the usual picture of a secret agent?

He is very fat and sloppy, and lives in a small, musty room on the top floor of a gloomy French hotel, which does not fit the image of a dashing spy. He speaks French and German only passably, and has never fired a shot or been involved in dramatic action. Yet he is a highly effective agent, because he uses his wits rather than weapons.

3 marks What was the knock at the door?

It was a waiter bringing the drink that Ausable had ordered before Max arrived. Ausable had already mentioned expecting a drink, so when the knock came he was able to pretend it was the police he had called. The reveal that it was only a waiter is the story's comic ending.

✍️ Hands-on task

Retell the trick in five sentences: the invented balcony, the knock, the claim about the police, Max's jump, and the waiter. Then one sentence on what the story says about appearances.
