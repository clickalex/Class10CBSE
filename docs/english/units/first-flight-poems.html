First Flight · Poems

10 poems. Ten short poems. Theme, central idea and poetic devices.

  • Ch 10 · Dust of SnowRobert Frost: a crow, a hemlock tree and a change of mood.
  • Ch 11 · Fire and IceRobert Frost on desire and hatred, and how the world could end.
  • Ch 12 · A Tiger in the ZooLeslie Norris: captivity in the zoo, freedom in the forest.
  • Ch 13 · How to Tell Wild AnimalsCarolyn Wells: a humorous way to identify six animals.
  • Ch 14 · The Ball PoemJohn Berryman: a lost ball and the first lesson in loss.
  • Ch 15 · Amanda!Robin Klein: a girl's daydreams against her mother's nagging.
  • Ch 16 · The TreesAdrienne Rich: trees leaving a house to return to the forest.
  • Ch 17 · FogCarl Sandburg: six lines comparing fog to a cat.
  • Ch 18 · The Tale of Custard the DragonOgden Nash's ballad: a cowardly dragon and a real pirate.
  • Ch 19 · For Anne GregoryW. B. Yeats: is a man loved for himself or for his yellow hair?
  • Learn the central idea of each in one sentence; the extract question is worth 3–4 marks.
