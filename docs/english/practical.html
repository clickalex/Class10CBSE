English Language & Literature — Practical & internal assessment

English internal assessment is listening and speaking, not a lab exam.

  • Speaking (10 marks): usually a short picture description or topic-based speech, followed by a brief interaction. Marks go to fluency, accuracy and pronunciation — not to how fancy your vocabulary is.
  • Listening (10 marks): a recorded passage with comprehension questions. Practise note-taking while listening; you cannot rewind.
  • Reading project: an assessment of reading and reading aloud, set by your school.
  • Preparation that works: record yourself speaking for two minutes on a random topic, then listen back. Two weeks of this moves the speaking score more than any textbook.
