Assessment at a glance

ComponentMarksPreparation
Section A — Reading: two unseen passages (discursive + case-based factual)**20**Objective + short-answer questions test inference and vocabulary in context.
Section B — Writing and grammar: formal letter, analytical paragraph, grammar in context**20**Stick to the word limit; exceed it and you run out of time.
Section C — Literature: First Flight and Footprints Without Feet**40**Answers in your own words. Copying lines from the book scores badly.
Total theory**80**Three hours.
Internal assessment — listening and speaking, plus a reading/reading-aloud project**20**The ASL speaking test is scheduled by your school.

Topper’s study order

  1. Writing formats first — formal letter, analytical paragraph. 10 marks that depend only on format.
  2. Poems — ten short texts, quick to finish, reliably asked.
  3. Footprints Without Feet — nine short chapters.
  4. First Flight prose — the longest reading; do the two-part chapters early.
  5. Grammar in context — tense, modals, subject-verb agreement, reported speech, determiners.
  6. Then one unseen passage a day for a fortnight; this is a speed skill, not a knowledge skill.

The listening and speaking assessment is usually held in December–January.
