Science — Practical & internal assessment

The 20 internal marks include a practical examination on the NCERT activities plus a practical file.

  • Typical practical list: properties of acids/bases with indicators; the reaction of metals with acid and with salt solutions; the reactivity series; properties of acetic acid; binary fission in amoeba and budding in yeast; CO₂ release in respiration; dependence of current on potential difference; series and parallel resistance; properties of a convex lens; and the parts of a dicot seed embryo.
  • Practical file format per activity: aim, apparatus/materials, theory (one or two lines), procedure, observation table with units, result, precautions.
  • Diagram rules: pencil only, no shading, labels on one side with horizontal guide lines, magnification not required.
  • Viva: expect “why this precaution?” and “what does this observation prove?” — the reason matters more than the reading.
