MCQM11 mark

1. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is: (a) more than 100 Ω (b) less than 1 Ω (c) 107 Ω (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) less than 1 Ω. Connecting all four in parallel gives an equivalent resistance smaller than the smallest individual resistance, 1 Ω.

Written questions · Write

SHORTS11 mark

1. Define one ohm and name the device used to measure potential difference.

Show Answer

Ans:

One ohm is the resistance of a conductor when a potential difference of 1 volt across its ends produces a current of 1 ampere through it. Potential difference is measured with a voltmeter, connected in parallel.

SHORTS22 marks

2. Why are the coils of electric toasters and electric irons made of an alloy rather than a pure metal?

Show Answer

Ans:

Because alloys have higher resistivity than the pure metals, so they produce more heat for the same current, and they do not oxidise (burn) readily at high temperatures.

SHORTS33 marks

3. State Ohm's law and draw the V–I graph for an ohmic conductor.

Show Answer

Ans:

At constant temperature, the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across its ends: V = IR, where R is the resistance.
The graph of V against I is a straight line passing through the origin; its slope (V/I) gives the resistance of the conductor.

SHORTS43 marks

4. Three resistors of 2 Ω, 3 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in parallel. Find the equivalent resistance.

Show Answer

Ans:

1/Rp = 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/6 = (3 + 2 + 1)/6 = 6/6 = 1.
So Rp = 1 Ω — less than the smallest resistor (2 Ω), as expected for a parallel combination.

LONGL55 marks

5. A 4 Ω resistor and a 6 Ω resistor are connected in series with a 20 V battery. Find the total resistance, the current, the potential difference across each resistor, and the heat produced in the 4 Ω resistor in 1 minute.

Show Answer

Ans:

Total resistance Rs = 4 + 6 = 10 Ω.
Current I = V/R = 20/10 = 2 A — the same through both resistors.
Potential differences: across 4 Ω, V = 2 × 4 = 8 V; across 6 Ω, V = 2 × 6 = 12 V. (8 + 12 = 20 V ✓)
Heat in the 4 Ω resistor H = I²Rt = (2)² × 4 × 60 = 960 J.

SHORTS62 marks

6. An electric iron of rating 750 W, 220 V is used for 2 hours daily. Calculate the cost of using it for 30 days at ₹5 per unit.

Show Answer

Ans:

Energy per day = P × t = 750 W × 2 h = 1500 Wh = 1.5 kWh.
Energy for 30 days = 1.5 × 30 = 45 kWh = 45 units.
Cost = 45 × ₹5 = ₹225.

LONGL75 marks

7. Discuss “Electricity” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

Electric current and potential difference. Current I = Q/t, measured in amperes with an ammeter connected in series. Potential difference V = W/Q, measured in volts with a voltmeter connected in parallel; 1 volt = 1 joule per coulomb. By convention current flows from the positive to the negative terminal, opposite to the flow of electrons. A cell maintains the potential difference that drives the charge.
Marks lens: –4 marks. A series or parallel circuit numerical is almost certain, plus one question on the heating effect or on power rating.
Method / memory trick: Series = same current, voltage divides. Parallel = same voltage, current divides. Read that sentence before every circuit question.
Do not: Connecting the ammeter in parallel and the voltmeter in series in a circuit diagram — this is a marked error.
