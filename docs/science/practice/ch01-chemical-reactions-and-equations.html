MCQs · Drill

MCQM11 mark

1. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of: (a) combination (b) decomposition (c) displacement (d) double displacement

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) displacement. Aluminium, being more reactive, displaces iron from its oxide. It is also a redox and a highly exothermic (thermite) reaction.

MCQM21 mark

2. Which of the following is an endothermic process? (a) respiration (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate (d) quicklime + water

Show Answer

Ans:

(c). Decomposition always needs energy, so heating ferrous sulphate is endothermic. The other three release heat.

MCQM31 mark

3. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively: (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S (b) CuO and Ag2O (c) CuCO3 and AgS2 (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

Show Answer

Ans:

(a). Copper forms basic copper carbonate CuCO3·Cu(OH)2 (green); silver forms silver sulphide Ag2S (black).

Written questions · Write

SHORTS11 mark

1. Why must a chemical equation be balanced?

Show Answer

Ans:

Because of the law of conservation of mass — the number of atoms of each element must be the same on both sides, since atoms are neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.

SHORTS22 marks

2. Balance: HNO3 + Ca(OH)2 → Ca(NO3)2 + H2O

Show Answer

Ans:

2HNO3 + Ca(OH)2 → Ca(NO3)2 + 2H2O. This is also a neutralisation, hence a double displacement reaction.

SHORTS33 marks

3. Why is nitrogen flushed into bags of potato chips?

Show Answer

Ans:

Nitrogen is an unreactive (inert) gas. It displaces oxygen from the packet, so the fats and oils cannot be oxidised, which prevents rancidity and keeps the chips from changing smell and taste.

LONGL44 marks

4. In the reaction ZnO + C → Zn + CO, identify the substance oxidised and the substance reduced.

Show Answer

Ans:

Carbon is oxidised (it gains oxygen to form CO) and zinc oxide is reduced (it loses oxygen to form Zn). Carbon is the reducing agent; ZnO is the oxidising agent.

LONGL55 marks

5. A white salt on heating decomposes to give brown fumes and a residue. Name the salt and write the equation.

Show Answer

Ans:

Lead nitrate. The brown fumes are nitrogen dioxide. 2Pb(NO3)2 → 2PbO + 4NO2↑ + O2↑ — a thermal decomposition reaction.
