Science — Quick revision

One screen per chapter: formulas and the points that actually get asked. Expand what you need, skip what you know.

Ch 1 Chemical Reactions and Equations

Formulas

  • Fe2O3 + 3H2 → 2Fe + 3H2O (displacement / reduction)
  • 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br2 (photolytic decomposition)
  • CaO + H2O → Ca(OH)2 + heat (exothermic combination)
  • Pb(NO3)2 + 2KI → PbI2↓ + 2KNO3 (double displacement, yellow precipitate)
  • Zn + H2SO4 → ZnSO4 + H2↑
  • Conservation of mass ⇒ balance atoms, never subscripts.
  • Decomposition needs energy: heat, electricity or light.
  • Oxidation = gain of O / loss of H; reduction is the opposite; both happen together.
  • Rust = hydrated Fe2O3; rancidity is stopped by nitrogen, airtight packing, antioxidants.
Ch 2 Acids, Bases and Salts

Formulas

  • pH = −log[H+] (concept: lower pH ⇒ more H+)
  • Ca(OH)2 + Cl2 → Ca(OCl)Cl + H2O (bleaching powder)
  • NaCl + H2O + CO2 + NH3 → NaHCO3 + NH4Cl (Solvay)
  • 2NaHCO3 → Na2CO3 + H2O + CO2 (on heating)
  • CaSO4·2H2O →(373 K) CaSO4·½H2O + 1½H2O
  • CaSO4·½H2O + 1½H2O → CaSO4·2H2O (setting of POP)
  • Acid → H+(aq); base → OH−(aq); water is needed for ionisation.
  • pH 7 neutral; below 7 acidic; above 7 basic; each unit is a tenfold change.
  • Acid + metal → salt + H2 (pop); acid + carbonate → salt + CO2 (lime water milky).
  • Bleaching powder Ca(OCl)Cl; baking soda NaHCO3; washing soda Na2CO3·10H2O; POP CaSO4·½H2O from gypsum at 373 K.
Ch 3 Metals and Non-metals

Formulas

  • 2Cu + O2 → 2CuO (black); 4Al + 3O2 → 2Al2O3
  • 2Na + 2H2O → 2NaOH + H2↑
  • Fe2O3 + 2Al → 2Fe + Al2O3 + heat (thermite)
  • ZnCO3 →(calcination) ZnO + CO2
  • 2ZnS + 3O2 →(roasting) 2ZnO + 2SO2
  • ZnO + C → Zn + CO
  • Reactivity series: K Na Ca Mg Al Zn Fe Pb (H) Cu Hg Ag Au.
  • Calcination = no air (carbonate ores); roasting = air (sulphide ores).
  • Metals above carbon: electrolytic reduction. Below carbon: reduction with coke.
  • Ionic solids: hard, high melting point, conduct only when molten/dissolved.
  • Amphoteric oxides: Al2O3, ZnO. Aqua regia = 3 HCl : 1 HNO3.
Ch 4 Carbon and its Compounds

Formulas

  • Alkanes CnH2n+2 · Alkenes CnH2n · Alkynes CnH2n−2
  • CH3COOH + C2H5OH →(conc. H2SO4) CH3COOC2H5 + H2O (ester)
  • C2H5OH →(conc. H2SO4, 443 K) CH2=CH2 + H2O
  • 2C2H5OH + 2Na → 2C2H5ONa + H2↑
  • CH3COOH + NaHCO3 → CH3COONa + H2O + CO2↑
  • CH3COOH + NaOH → CH3COONa + H2O
  • Carbon: tetravalent + catenation ⇒ covalent compounds with low melting points, no conductivity.
  • CnH2n+2 alkanes, CnH2n alkenes, CnH2n−2 alkynes; each step in a series = CH2 (14 u).
  • Ethanol + Na → H2; ethanol + conc. H2SO4 (443 K) → ethene; ethanol + ethanoic acid → ester.
  • Ethanoic acid + NaHCO3 → CO2 (the acid test). Soap forms micelles; detergents work in hard water.
Ch 5 Life Processes

Formulas

  • 6CO2 + 12H2O →(chlorophyll, sunlight) C6H12O6 + 6O2 + 6H2O
  • C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + energy (aerobic)
  • C6H12O6 → 2C2H5OH + 2CO2 + energy (yeast)
  • C6H12O6 → 2 lactic acid + energy (muscle)
  • Photosynthesis: 12H2O on the left, chlorophyll and sunlight above the arrow.
  • Bile has no enzyme — it emulsifies fat only.
  • Aerobic = mitochondria, more energy; anaerobic = ethanol (yeast) or lactic acid (muscle).
  • Arteries away/thick, veins in/valves; xylem up, phloem both ways.
  • Nephron: ultrafiltration in Bowman's capsule, selective reabsorption in the tubule.
Ch 6 Control and Coordination
  • Reflex arc: receptor → sensory → spinal cord → motor → effector.
  • Forebrain = cerebrum (largest, thinking); hindbrain = pons, medulla, cerebellum.
  • Auxin, gibberellin, cytokinin promote growth; abscisic acid inhibits it.
  • Thyroxin–metabolism, growth hormone–growth, insulin–blood sugar, adrenaline–emergency.
Ch 7 How do Organisms Reproduce?
  • DNA copying creates variation — essential for the species, not the individual.
  • Amoeba binary fission; Hydra/yeast budding; Spirogyra fragmentation; Planaria regeneration; Rhizopus spores; Bryophyllum leaf buds.
  • Pollination = transfer of pollen; fertilisation = fusion in the ovule; ovule→seed, ovary→fruit.
  • Fertilisation in the fallopian tube; nutrition via the placenta; scrotum keeps testes cool.
Ch 8 Heredity

Formulas

  • Monohybrid F2 phenotypic ratio = 3 : 1
  • Monohybrid F2 genotypic ratio = 1 : 2 : 1
  • Dihybrid F2 phenotypic ratio = 9 : 3 : 3 : 1
  • Sex ratio = 50% XY (boy) : 50% XX (girl)
  • Gene = segment of DNA; alleles come in pairs; dominant masks recessive in F1.
  • Monohybrid F2: 3:1 phenotype, 1:2:1 genotype. Dihybrid F2: 9:3:3:1.
  • XX = girl, XY = boy; the father's sperm decides, 50:50.
  • Acquired traits affect somatic cells and are not inherited; only germ-cell DNA changes are.
Ch 9 Light — Reflection and Refraction

Formulas

  • Mirror formula: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f
  • Lens formula: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f
  • R = 2f
  • Magnification m = h'/h = −v/u (mirror) and m = h'/h = v/u (lens)
  • n = c/v ; n21 = n2/n1 = v1/v2
  • Power P = 1/f (f in metres), unit dioptre (D)
  • Mirror: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f, m = −v/u. Lens: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f, m = v/u.
  • u is always negative; concave mirror f negative; convex lens f positive.
  • Convex mirror → always virtual, erect, diminished (rear-view mirror).
  • P = 1/f in metres, in dioptres; powers add in contact.
  • n = c/v; denser medium bends light toward the normal.
Ch 10 The Human Eye and the Colourful World
  • Myopia → image in front → concave lens. Hypermetropia → image behind → convex lens. Presbyopia → bifocal.
  • Least distance of distinct vision 25 cm; far point infinity.
  • Dispersion: red bends least, violet most; rainbow = refraction + dispersion + TIR in droplets.
  • Twinkling, advance sunrise/delay sunset = atmospheric refraction. Blue sky, red sunrise = scattering.
Ch 11 Electricity

Formulas

  • I = Q/t ; 1 A = 1 C/s
  • V = W/Q ; 1 V = 1 J/C
  • V = IR ; R = ρl/A
  • Series: Rs = R1 + R2 + R3
  • Parallel: 1/Rp = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + 1/R3
  • H = I²Rt = VIt = V²t/R
  • P = VI = I²R = V²/R ; 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
  • V = IR; R = ρl/A; series Rs = R1+R2+R3; parallel 1/Rp = 1/R1+1/R2+1/R3.
  • Series: same current. Parallel: same voltage.
  • H = I²Rt = VIt = V²t/R; P = VI = I²R = V²/R.
  • 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J. Ammeter in series, voltmeter in parallel.
  • Fuse: low melting point, in series, protects from overcurrent; earthing prevents shock.
Ch 12 Magnetic Effects of Electric Current
  • Field lines: closed loops, N→S outside a magnet, never intersect.
  • Left-hand rule = force on a conductor = motor. Right-hand rule = induced current = generator.
  • Split ring reverses current each half rotation; AC 50 Hz reverses 100 times a second.
  • Domestic: live 220 V, neutral 0 V, earth green; fuse in series, low melting point.
Ch 13 Our Environment

Formulas

  • Energy at level n+1 = 10% of energy at level n
  • O3 formation: O2 + O →(UV) O3 ; O3 + CFC → destruction of ozone
  • Food chain arrows point in the direction of energy flow; producers are always the first trophic level.
  • 10% law: only about 10% of energy transfers upward, so chains rarely exceed four levels.
  • Biological magnification is maximum in the top carnivore (e.g. hawks, human beings).
  • O3 shields UV; CFCs deplete it; Montreal Protocol 1987 froze CFC production.
  • 5 R's: Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, Repurpose, Recycle.
