CBSE • CLASS 10 • SCIENCE

Science — PYQ trends

Where past papers keep returning, chapter by chapter. Use it to decide revision order — not to guess the paper.

Trend notes below are an original reading of publicly available past papers and sample papers. They are not official CBSE predictions.

ChapterTitleWhat past papers ask
Ch 1Chemical Reactions and EquationsOne balancing question and one 'identify the reaction type' question appear almost every year; corrosion or rancidity is the recurring 2–3 mark reasoning question.
Ch 2Acids, Bases and SaltsThe four salts — bleaching powder, baking soda, washing soda, plaster of Paris — rotate through the 3-mark slot every year, and the pH scale almost always appears as a case-based or diagram question.
Ch 3Metals and Non-metalsOne displacement question from the reactivity series and one reasoning question (kerosene, galvanising, aqua regia or the thermite reaction) appear almost every year; ionic bond formation is the recurring 3-mark answer.
Ch 4Carbon and its CompoundsOne IUPAC naming or structure question every year, plus either the soap/detergent comparison or an ethanol–ethanoic acid reaction question. Homologous series tables are a favourite case-based format.
Ch 5Life ProcessesA labelled diagram question from this chapter is close to certain (nephron, respiratory system, or the heart); double circulation and aerobic vs anaerobic respiration are the most repeated 3-mark answers.
Ch 6Control and CoordinationThe reflex arc and the hormone tables (plant hormones and human endocrine glands) alternate as the 3-mark question; iodine–goitre and insulin–diabetes are the most repeated short answers.
Ch 7How do Organisms Reproduce?One asexual reproduction question (usually a comparison or a diagram of amoeba/yeast) plus one question on the human reproductive system, contraception or the placenta. Flower parts and pollination vs fertilisation are the recurring short answers.
Ch 8HeredityEither the monohybrid or the dihybrid cross with a Punnett square appears almost every year, along with a short question on sex determination or acquired vs inherited traits.
Ch 9Light — Reflection and RefractionOne ray diagram (concave mirror or convex lens) and one numerical using the mirror/lens formula with the magnification appear nearly every year; power and the sign of the focal length are common 1-mark items.
Ch 10The Human Eye and the Colourful WorldOne defect of vision with a labelled diagram is asked almost every year; the sky-blue / red-sunrise reasoning and the rainbow are the next most common.
Ch 11ElectricityA series/parallel circuit numerical appears every year, and the heating-effect formula with a household energy-bill calculation is the most repeated 3–5 mark question in Physics.
Ch 12Magnetic Effects of Electric CurrentFleming's two rules, the motor or generator working, and the domestic circuit with the earth wire and fuse rotate through the 3-mark slot; 'why field lines never intersect' is a standard 1-marker.
Ch 13Our EnvironmentOne food chain with a 10% energy calculation is asked almost every year, alternating with ozone depletion or biodegradable vs non-biodegradable waste.
