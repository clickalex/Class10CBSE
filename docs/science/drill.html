Ch 1 · Chemical Reactions and Equations (5)

MCQM11 mark

1. The reaction Fe2O3 + 2Al → Al2O3 + 2Fe is an example of: (a) combination (b) decomposition (c) displacement (d) double displacement

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) displacement. Aluminium, being more reactive, displaces iron from its oxide. It is also a redox and a highly exothermic (thermite) reaction.

MCQM21 mark

2. Which of the following is an endothermic process? (a) respiration (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate (d) quicklime + water

Show Answer

Ans:

(c). Decomposition always needs energy, so heating ferrous sulphate is endothermic. The other three release heat.

MCQM31 mark

3. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively: (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S (b) CuO and Ag2O (c) CuCO3 and AgS2 (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

Show Answer

Ans:

(a). Copper forms basic copper carbonate CuCO3·Cu(OH)2 (green); silver forms silver sulphide Ag2S (black).

Ch 2 · Acids, Bases and Salts (5)

MCQM11 mark

1. A solution reacts with crushed egg-shell to give a gas that turns lime water milky. The solution contains: (a) NaCl (b) HCl (c) LiCl (d) KCl

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) HCl. Egg-shell is CaCO3; CaCO3 + 2HCl → CaCl2 + H2O + CO2. The CO2 turns lime water milky.

MCQM21 mark

2. Which one is a strong base? (a) NH4OH (b) Mg(OH)2 (c) NaOH (d) Cu(OH)2

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) NaOH. Alkali metal hydroxides are strong bases and ionise completely; the others are weak.

MCQM31 mark

3. The pH of gastric juice released during digestion is: (a) less than 7 (b) more than 7 (c) equal to 7 (d) equal to 0

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) less than 7. The stomach secretes hydrochloric acid, which keeps the gastric juice acidic (roughly pH 1.2–3) so that pepsin can act.

Ch 3 · Metals and Non-metals (5)

MCQM11 mark

1. A copper coin placed in silver nitrate solution turns silvery and the solution turns blue. This shows copper is: (a) less reactive than silver (b) more reactive than silver (c) equally reactive (d) an inert metal

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) more reactive than silver. Cu + 2AgNO3 → Cu(NO3)2 + 2Ag; the blue colour is due to Cu2+ ions.

MCQM21 mark

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) Al2O3 and ZnO — they react with both acids and bases to form salt and water.

MCQM31 mark

3. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is: (a) reduction with carbon (b) self reduction (c) electrolytic reduction (d) roasting

Show Answer

(c) electrolytic reduction. Sodium is above carbon in the reactivity series, so carbon cannot reduce it; the molten chloride is electrolysed.

Ch 5 · Life Processes (5)

MCQM11 mark

1. The kidneys in human beings are a part of the system for: (a) nutrition (b) respiration (c) excretion (d) transportation

Ch 8 · Heredity (5)

Ch 11 · Electricity (5)

MCQM11 mark

1. The least resistance that can be obtained by combining 2 Ω, 4 Ω, 1 Ω and 100 Ω is: (a) more than 100 Ω (b) less than 1 Ω (c) 107 Ω (d) between 2 Ω and 100 Ω

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) less than 1 Ω. Connecting all four in parallel gives an equivalent resistance smaller than the smallest individual resistance, 1 Ω.

MCQM21 mark

Ch 12 · Magnetic Effects of Electric Current (5)

MCQM11 mark

1. The direction of the magnetic field produced by a straight current-carrying wire is given by: (a) Fleming's left-hand rule (b) Fleming's right-hand rule (c) the right-hand thumb rule (d) Lenz's law

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) the right-hand thumb rule — hold the wire in the right hand with the thumb along the current; the curled fingers give the direction of the field lines.

MCQM21 mark

2. The essential difference between an AC generator and a DC generator is: (a) AC generator has an electromagnet (b) DC generator has a commutator (c) AC generator has slip rings while DC generator has a commutator (d) AC generator produces more power

(c). The slip rings of an AC generator allow the output to reverse, while the split-ring commutator of a DC generator keeps the external current in one direction.
