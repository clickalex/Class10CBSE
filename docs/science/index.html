Assessment at a glance

ComponentMarksPreparation
Theory paper — objective, short answer, long answer and case-based/source-based**80**Diagrams labelled; equations balanced; units written.
Practical / internal assessment — lab exam, practical file, two periodic tests, subject enrichment**20**The practical file must match the activity list exactly.
Total100Three hours for the theory paper.

Topper’s study order

  1. Chemistry (Chapters 1–4) first — 25 marks and largely self-contained.
  2. Biology (Chapters 5–8) next — another 25 marks, diagram-heavy.
  3. Electricity and Magnetism (11–12) — 13 marks of mostly numericals.
  4. Optics (9–10) — 12 marks; do ray diagrams before the numericals.
  5. Our Environment (13) — quick 5 marks.
  6. Then one timed paper, with the marking scheme open while you check it.

The practical exam is usually held in January–February, before the theory paper. Confirm with your school.
