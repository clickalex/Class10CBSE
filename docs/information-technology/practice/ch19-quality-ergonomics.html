MCQM71 mark

MCQM81 mark

Show Answer

Written questions · Write

SHORTS12 marks

1. What is ergonomics? Give two workstation rules.

Show Answer

Ans:

Designing work to fit the person. Rules: screen at eye level, feet flat, back supported, wrists straight, mouse beside the keyboard.

SHORTS22 marks

2. Expand 5S.

Show Answer

Ans:

5S = Sort, Set in order, Shine, Standardise, Sustain. Sort removes what is not needed; Set in order gives everything a fixed place; Shine means cleaning as you go; Standardise writes the method down so anyone can follow it; Sustain makes it a daily habit. The first three clean the workplace, the last two keep it clean.

SHORTS32 marks

3. What is ergonomics and why does it matter when working at a computer?

Show Answer

Ans:

Ergonomics is the science of designing the workplace to fit the person. At a computer it keeps the body comfortable and prevents backache, neck and shoulder pain, eye strain and repetitive strain injury caused by hours of sitting in a wrong posture with badly placed equipment.

SHORTS42 marks

4. What are the four features of quality work in IT? Illustrate with a data-entry task.

Show Answer

Ans:

Accurate — every figure matches the source; for example, marks typed without a single digit error.
Complete — nothing is missing; every student's row is filled.
On time — the file is ready before the deadline, not after.
Checked — a second pass (spell-check, totals re-added) proves the first three.

APPLICATIONA53 marks

5. Amit’s laptop is on a high table, he looks up, and his wrists bend on the edge. Two injuries this invites and two fixes.

Show Answer

Ans:

Neck strain and wrist RSI. Fixes: raise the chair or lower the desk, laptop stand + external keyboard, wrist straight, breaks.

APPLICATIONA63 marks

6. Riya is revising “Workplace Quality and Ergonomics” (Quality of work, 5S, housekeeping, ergonomic posture and workstation setup.). A classmate lost marks by doing this: Drawing the user hunched over a laptop as “correct posture”. Apply the correct method and rewrite the scoring answer.

Show Answer

Ans:

Correct method: Screen top at eye level, not looking down at a laptop on the lap.
Why the mistake costs marks: Drawing the user hunched over a laptop as “correct posture”.
Write the definition or formula first, then the working or example.

SHORTS73 marks

7. Describe a correct workstation arrangement and the working habit that prevents strain.

Show Answer

Ans:

Sit back in a chair that supports the lower back, with both feet flat on the floor and knees at about a right angle.
Keep the top of the screen at or just below eye level, about an arm's length away, with the keyboard and mouse close enough to keep the forearms horizontal and the wrists straight.
Place the document you are copying beside the screen rather than flat on the desk, so the neck does not keep turning.
Follow the 20-20-20 habit: every 20 minutes look 20 feet away for 20 seconds, and take a short break and stretch every hour.

SHORTS83 marks

8. Explain the 5S method with one computer-lab example for each S.

Show Answer

Ans:

Sort — remove what is not needed: discard dried pens and broken CDs from the lab drawers.
Set in order — give everything a place: label each shelf, cable and software CD.
Shine — clean regularly: wipe keyboards and screens on a fixed weekly roster.
Standardise — make it a rule: the same layout and cleaning checklist in every lab.
Sustain — keep it going: a monthly audit so the lab never slides back.

LONGL94 marks

9. Describe a correct computer workstation and two quality checks before submitting a practical file.

Show Answer

Ans:

Chair and desk so elbows and knees are about 90°, screen arm’s length, top at eye level, no glare. Quality: proof-read, spell-check, file named as asked, print preview, backup copy.

COMPETENCYC104 marks

10. Read and answer: A lab has tangled wires, unmarked CDs, glare, and students skip spell-check. (a) Map each problem to 5S or ergonomics or quality. (b) One action each. (c) Why quality matters in a data-entry job role.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Wires/CDs → 5S Set in order; glare → ergonomics; skip spell-check → quality.
(b) Cable ties + labels; blinds or move screens; compulsory preview + peer check.
(c) Wrong data costs money and trust — the job role is Domestic Data Entry Operator.

LONGL125 marks

12. Discuss “Workplace Quality and Ergonomics” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.
