Written questions · Write

SHORTS11 mark

1. Define sustainable development.

Show Answer

Ans:

Development that meets present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

SHORTS22 marks

2. What are green skills? Give two examples.

Show Answer

Ans:

Skills that help a person live and work in an environmentally friendly way. Examples: waste segregation, saving electricity, water conservation.

APPLICATIONA33 marks

3. A computer lab leaves 20 PCs on overnight. Suggest three green practices and one green skill the lab assistant needs.

Show Answer

Ans:

Shut down PCs, use sleep mode, switch off AC/lights, enable duplex printing. Skill: energy-conservation habit / e-waste handling.

COMPETENCYC44 marks

4. Read and answer: A factory dumps dye in a river but pays workers well. (a) Is this sustainable development? Why? (b) Name two green-economy changes. (c) One green job this town could create.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) No — present wages at the cost of future water and health.
(b) Treat effluent, switch to safer dye, reuse water.
(c) Water-treatment operator or waste-segregation worker.

LONGL55 marks

5. Discuss “Green Skills–II” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

Environment = the surroundings in which we live — air, water, land, plants, animals and humans.
Marks lens: mark. Sustainable development definition is the banker.
Method / memory trick: Sustainable development = present + future, not only “planting trees”.
Do not: Defining sustainable development as “using no resources”. It is wise use, not zero use.

APPLICATIONA63 marks

6. Riya is revising “Green Skills–II” (Sustainable development, green economy and green jobs.). A classmate lost marks by doing this: Defining sustainable development as “using no resources”. It is wise use, not zero use. Apply the correct method and rewrite the scoring answer.

Show Answer

Ans:

Correct method: Sustainable development = present + future, not only “planting trees”.
Why the mistake costs marks: Defining sustainable development as “using no resources”. It is wise use, not zero use.
Write the definition or formula first, then the working or example.
