Information Technology — PYQ trends

Where past papers keep returning, chapter by chapter. Use it to decide revision order — not to guess the paper.

Trend notes below are an original reading of publicly available past papers and sample papers. They are not official CBSE predictions.

ChapterTitleWhat past papers ask
Ch 1Communication Skills–IIOne 2-mark on methods, 7 Cs or feedback, plus 1-mark MCQs on barriers and articles.
Ch 2Self-Management Skills–IISMART expansion and two stress-management methods appear almost every sample paper.
Ch 3ICT Skills–IIOne MCQ on OS vs application, or on file/folder/care of computer.
Ch 4Entrepreneurial Skills–IIQualities + one myth, or role of entrepreneurship in society.
Ch 5Green Skills–IIDefinition of sustainable development, or one green practice / green job.
Ch 6Introduction to Styles (Writer)Types of styles + advantage of styles; practical is Heading styles + Fill Format.
Ch 7Working with Images (Writer)Wrap + anchor, or steps to insert and caption an image.
Ch 8ToC, Templates and Track ChangesToC steps, template definition, or mail-merge advantages.
Ch 9Scenarios and Goal Seek (Calc)Goal Seek steps, or difference between scenario and Goal Seek.
Ch 10Macros in SpreadsheetRecord-macro steps and one advantage; security as a 1-mark.
Ch 11Linking Spreadsheet DataWrite a cross-sheet formula, or advantage of linking vs paste.
Ch 12Share and Review a SpreadsheetShare vs record changes, or protect sheet steps.
Ch 13Introduction to DBMSData vs information, advantages of DBMS, definition of primary key.
Ch 14Tables in LibreOffice BaseCreate-table steps, choice of data type, how to set PK.
Ch 15Relationships and Multiple TablesDefine one-to-many + referential integrity; practical is drag-PK-to-FK.
Ch 16Queries in BaseWrite a criterion, or steps of Design View query.
Ch 17Forms and ReportsForm vs report, or wizard steps; practical is one of each.
Ch 18Health, Safety and Security at the WorkplaceHazard types, electrical/fire rules, or password/backup security.
Ch 19Workplace Quality and Ergonomics5S list or workstation posture; sometimes 20-20-20.
Ch 20Prevent Accidents and EmergenciesEvacuation steps or electric-shock first aid; near-miss as a value item.
