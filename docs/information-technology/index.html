Topper’s study order

  1. Unit 3 DBMS first (12 marks) — tables, relationships, queries.
  2. Unit 2 Calc on a computer (10) — Goal Seek and scenarios.
  3. Unit 4 Safety (10) — lists plus examples, fastest theory.
  4. Unit 1 Writer (8) — styles, ToC, mail merge.
  5. Part A (10) — communication 7 Cs, SMART goals, green skills.
  6. Then a timed sample paper, the practical file and a viva drill.

Practical is usually before theory, January–February. Confirm with your school. Target February–March 2027 boards.
