Ch 1 · Communication Skills–II (10)

MCQM11 mark

1. Which part of communication converts an idea into words or signs? (a) Receiving (b) Encoding (c) Decoding (d) Feedback

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) Encoding. Encoding prepares the sender's idea as a message; decoding interprets it.

MCQM21 mark

2. A loud generator prevents a worker from hearing instructions. The barrier is: (a) linguistic (b) cultural (c) physical (d) emotional

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) Physical — environmental noise blocks the message.

MCQM31 mark

3. Which of the 7 Cs means “to the point, no extra words”? (a) Clear (b) Concise (c) Concrete (d) Courteous

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) Concise.

MCQM41 mark

4. “An honest boy” takes which article? (a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) an — use an before a vowel sound; h is silent in honest.

MCQM51 mark

5. A student's puzzled expression after an explanation is: (a) written encoding (b) non-verbal feedback (c) a channel (d) a language rule

Show Answer

Ans:

(b) Non-verbal feedback.

MCQM61 mark

6. Which sentence is complete? (a) Do that work. (b) The form is important. (c) Submit the signed form to Room 4 by Friday noon. (d) Submit it soon.

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) — task, place and deadline are all present.

MCQM71 mark

7. Paralanguage refers to: (a) body posture only (b) charts and maps (c) tone, pitch and speed of voice (d) grammar rules

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) Tone, pitch and speed of voice.

MCQM81 mark

8. The easiest method of communication is: (a) written (b) oral (c) non-verbal (d) email

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) Non-verbal — needs no literacy.

MCQM91 mark

9. The communication process is complete only when: (a) the receiver gives feedback (b) the message is written (c) the sender speaks (d) the channel is chosen

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) the receiver gives feedback. Feedback tells the sender whether the message was understood.

MCQM101 mark

10. Which method of communication carries no body language or tone of voice? (a) written communication (b) face-to-face talk (c) video call (d) group discussion

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) written communication. That is why written messages must be especially clear.

Ch 2 · Self-Management Skills–II (8)

MCQM11 mark

1. SMART: the M stands for: (a) Magical (b) Measurable (c) Maximum (d) Monthly

Show Answer

(b) Measurable.

MCQM21 mark

2. A hobby after study is mainly: (a) a waste (b) a stress-management method (c) self-employment (d) a barrier

Ans:

(b) a stress-management method.

MCQM31 mark

3. Knowing your own strengths is: (a) self-awareness (b) encoding (c) a physical barrier (d) green skill

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) self-awareness.

MCQM41 mark

4. Which goal is SMART? (a) I will study more (b) I will type 40 correct rows in Calc by 3 pm (c) I will become famous (d) I will try

Show Answer

Ans:

(b).

MCQM51 mark

5. Which of the following is an example of self-regulation? (a) Feeling angry at a remark but waiting and replying politely later (b) Setting a goal to score 90% in the exam (c) Listing one’s strengths and weaknesses (d) Being able to see the cause of someone else’s stress

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Feeling angry at a remark but waiting and replying politely later. Self-regulation means controlling one’s emotions and actions; the other options show self-motivation and self-awareness.

MCQM61 mark

6. A task that is important but not urgent should be: (a) scheduled for a fixed time (b) done at once, before anything else (c) given to somebody else (d) dropped from the list

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) scheduled for a fixed time. In the urgent/important method, important but not urgent tasks are planned for later so that they do not become urgent.

MCQM71 mark

7. Self-awareness means: (a) knowing your own strengths, weaknesses and feelings (b) only knowing other people (c) working without help (d) following orders

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) knowing your own strengths, weaknesses and feelings. It is the first step of self-management.

MCQM81 mark

8. The useful stress that pushes you to perform better is called: (a) eustress (b) distress (c) burnout (d) depression

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) eustress. Distress is the harmful kind that lowers performance and health.

(b).

(b) perseverance.

MCQM31 mark

Ans:

(b).

MCQM41 mark

4. “Entrepreneurs are born, not made” is: (a) a fact (b) a myth (c) a 7 C (d) an OS function

(b) a myth.

MCQM51 mark

5. The first step in starting an enterprise is usually to: (a) register the business (b) hire employees (c) find and evaluate a business idea (d) print the visiting cards

Show Answer

Ans:

(c) find and evaluate a business idea. Everything else depends on having an idea that meets a need and can make a profit.

MCQM61 mark

Show Answer

(d) capital. Capital is the funds an entrepreneur invests in the enterprise; profit is what remains after costs.

MCQM71 mark

7. A person who starts a business, organises it and bears its risks is a/an: (a) entrepreneur (b) manager (c) employee (d) customer

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) entrepreneur. A manager runs someone else's business for a salary.

MCQM81 mark

8. Which of these is a myth about entrepreneurs? (a) they are born, not made (b) they take risks (c) they work hard (d) they need capital

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) they are born, not made. Entrepreneurial skills such as planning, selling and record-keeping can be learned.

(c) Reduce — don't create the waste.

(b) a green job.

MCQM41 mark

(b) discarded electronic and electrical equipment. E-waste includes old computers, phones, batteries and cables, and needs special disposal.

Show Answer

Ans:

(d) solar energy. Sunlight is not used up when it is used; coal, petroleum and natural gas are fossil fuels.

Show Answer

(a) improves human well-being while reducing environmental risks. It is low-carbon and resource-efficient.

MCQM81 mark

Show Answer

Ans:

MCQM51 mark

Show Answer

(d) Shift key. Holding Shift with a corner handle keeps the width-to-height ratio unchanged.

MCQM61 mark

6. Anchoring an image “To Page” means that it: (a) moves with the paragraph it is next to (b) behaves like a character in a line (c) is hidden when the document is printed (d) stays at a fixed position on the page even if the text above changes

Show Answer

Ans:

(d) stays at a fixed position on the page even if the text above changes. A “To Page” anchor ties the picture to a spot on the page, whereas “To Paragraph” makes it move with the text.
