Part A · Employability Skills

10 marks. Communication, self-management, ICT, entrepreneurial and green skills — short, definition-heavy, high marks per hour.

  • Ch 1 · Communication Skills–IIMethods, feedback, barriers, 7 Cs and basic writing skills.
  • Ch 2 · Self-Management Skills–IIStress, self-awareness, self-motivation, self-regulation, time and SMART goals.
  • Ch 3 · ICT Skills–IIOperating system, files and folders, and basic care of a computer.
  • Ch 4 · Entrepreneurial Skills–IIWho an entrepreneur is, qualities, functions, myths and role in society.
  • Ch 5 · Green Skills–IISustainable development, green economy and green jobs.
  • Finish Part A early. 10 marks of lists and examples.
