🎯 Marks lens

Evacuation order and first aid don’ts are the 2–3 markers.
Know: assembly point, don’t use lifts, report incidents even if small.

📖 Deep concepts

1. Causes of accidents: unsafe conditions (wet floor, bad wiring) and unsafe acts (running, bypassing rules). Most lab accidents are preventable.
2. Prevention: training, PPE where needed, maintenance, 5S, no horseplay, report near-misses.
3. Emergency types: fire, electric shock, medical, earthquake, data breach.
4. Evacuation: alarm → save work if safe and seconds allow → leave bags → nearest stairs, not lift → assembly point → headcount → don’t re-enter.
5. First aid principles: protect yourself, call for help, don’t move a seriously injured person, CPR only if trained, cool a burn with water (not ice/toothpaste), for shock switch off power before touching.
6. Electric shock: switch off mains, use a non-conductor to separate, call help, recovery position if breathing.
7. Reporting: date, time, place, names, what happened, injuries, witnesses, action taken. Near-misses count — they prevent the next accident.
8. Numbers: school office, local fire, ambulance 112 (India emergency). Keep them posted in the lab.

🧠 Memory tricks

  • Power off before touch is the electric-shock line examiners want.
  • Near-miss reporting is a quality/safety behaviour, not “telling on someone”.

⚠️ Mistakes that cost marks

  • Applying toothpaste or ice directly on a burn.
  • Using the lift during a fire drill.

SHORTS12 marks

1. List four steps of evacuation in the correct order.

Show Answer

Ans:

Hear the alarm → leave via stairs (not lift) → go to the assembly point → answer the headcount and wait for the all-clear.

SHORTS22 marks

2. Why report a near-miss?

Show Answer

Ans:

A near-miss shows a hazard that almost caused harm. Reporting it lets the school fix the condition before someone is actually injured.

LONGL34 marks

3. Explain first aid for (i) a small cut (ii) a minor burn (iii) suspected electric shock.

Show Answer

Ans:

Cut: wash, pressure, clean dressing. Burn: cool with running water 10 minutes, loose sterile cover, no toothpaste/ice. Shock: do not touch until power is off; then separate with a dry non-conductor, call help, monitor breathing.

APPLICATIONA43 marks

4. The fire alarm rings. A student runs back for a USB and takes the lift. Two wrong actions and the correct ones.

Show Answer

Ans:

Going back for belongings and using the lift. Correct: leave immediately by stairs, USB can be replaced, assemble for headcount.

COMPETENCYC54 marks

5. Read and answer: During a practical a student gets a shock from a damaged mouse cable, laughs it off, and no one writes it down. (a) Immediate first actions. (b) Why a report still matters. (c) Two prevention steps so it does not recur.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Switch off power, check breathing, first aid, inform the teacher.
(b) Near-miss/incident record → cable replaced, others warned, pattern spotted.
(c) Inspect peripherals, remove damaged devices, remind: report all shocks.
