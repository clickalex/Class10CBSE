🎯 Marks lens

3 marks. Stress symptoms, SMART goals and time-management steps are the usual asks.
Always add a workplace example — a data-entry operator under a deadline is enough.

📖 Deep concepts

1. Self-management is the ability to control one's emotions, thoughts and behaviour and to work towards goals without constant supervision.
2. Stress is the body's response to pressure. Eustress is positive (a deadline that focuses you); distress is harmful. Symptoms: headache, fast heartbeat, irritability, poor sleep, missed errors.
3. Stress management: deep breathing, short breaks, exercise, talking to someone, breaking work into chunks, saying no to extra load, sleep and a hobby.
4. Self-awareness is knowing your strengths, weaknesses, values and emotions. Tools: SWOT, feedback from others, a journal.
5. Self-motivation is working without being pushed. Intrinsic (interest, pride) lasts longer than extrinsic (marks, money).
6. Self-regulation is managing impulses — staying calm, delaying gratification, following rules.
7. Time management: list tasks, set priorities (urgent/important), use a timetable, avoid multitasking, finish one chunk, review at the end of the day.
8. SMART goals: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic/Relevant, Time-bound. “Type 30 invoices without error by 5 pm Friday” is SMART; “do better” is not.

🧠 Memory tricks

  • SMART → Specific Measurable Achievable Realistic Time-bound.
  • Urgent vs important: exam tomorrow is both; scrolling is neither.

⚠️ Mistakes that cost marks

  • Writing SMART expansions in the wrong order.
  • Treating all stress as bad — eustress is useful.
  • Giving no example in a 3-marker.

SHORTS12 marks

1. What is self-management? Give two of its benefits.

Show Answer

Ans:

Controlling one's emotions, thoughts and behaviour to reach goals. Benefits: better work quality, less stress, meeting deadlines, less need for supervision.

SHORTS22 marks

2. Differentiate eustress and distress with one example each.

Show Answer

Ans:

Eustress is helpful pressure — a timed practical that makes you focus. Distress is harmful — so much overtime that errors increase and sleep is lost.

LONGL34 marks

3. Explain SMART goals with a data-entry example.

Show Answer

Ans:

Specific (which file), Measurable (how many records), Achievable (within skill), Realistic (hours available), Time-bound (by 5 pm). Example: “Enter 80 customer rows in Calc with zero blanks by 4 pm today.”

APPLICATIONA43 marks

4. Meera has three tasks: a 5 pm file, a messy desk, and a social-media reel. Using urgent/important, what should she do first and why?

Show Answer

Ans:

The 5 pm file is urgent and important — do it first. The desk is important but not urgent — schedule it. The reel is neither — drop it until the file is in.

COMPETENCYC54 marks

5. Read and answer: Ravi’s hands shake before every practical, he skips breakfast and then makes typing errors. (a) Name the type of stress. (b) List four management steps. (c) Write one SMART goal for his next practical.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Distress — it is harming performance.
(b) Breakfast, 4–7 breathing, a 5-minute practice file, a checklist, sleep 7 hours.
(c) “Complete the Base table of 10 records with primary key set, in 25 minutes, with zero errors, on Friday.”
