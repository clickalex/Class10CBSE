🎯 Marks lens

Ergonomics of a computer workstation is the set diagram/answer.
5S (Sort, Set in order, Shine, Standardise, Sustain) appears as a 2-mark list.

📖 Deep concepts

1. Quality of work in IT: accurate data, readable documents, meeting time, following the house style, checking before submit.
2. Housekeeping: clear aisles, labelled cables, no food, bags not in walkways, shut down PCs.
3. 5S: Sort (remove extra), Set in order (a place for everything), Shine (clean), Standardise (same method), Sustain (habit).
4. Ergonomics designs work to fit the person. Goal: reduce strain, errors and fatigue.
5. Workstation: feet flat, knees ~90°, back supported, screen top at eye level, screen an arm’s length away, elbows ~90°, mouse next to keyboard, wrists straight.
6. 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds — reduces eye strain.
7. Lighting and noise: no glare on screen, enough light to read paper, headphones or quiet for data-entry accuracy.

🧠 Memory tricks

  • Screen top at eye level, not looking down at a laptop on the lap.
  • 5S order is asked as a list — don’t shuffle Shine and Sort.

⚠️ Mistakes that cost marks

  • Drawing the user hunched over a laptop as “correct posture”.
  • Defining quality as “working fast” with no accuracy.

SHORTS12 marks

1. What is ergonomics? Give two workstation rules.

Show Answer

Ans:

Designing work to fit the person. Rules: screen at eye level, feet flat, back supported, wrists straight, mouse beside the keyboard.

SHORTS22 marks

2. Expand 5S.

Show Answer

Ans:

Sort, Set in order, Shine, Standardise, Sustain.

LONGL34 marks

3. Describe a correct computer workstation and two quality checks before submitting a practical file.

Show Answer

Ans:

Chair and desk so elbows and knees are about 90°, screen arm’s length, top at eye level, no glare. Quality: proof-read, spell-check, file named as asked, print preview, backup copy.

APPLICATIONA43 marks

4. Amit’s laptop is on a high table, he looks up, and his wrists bend on the edge. Two injuries this invites and two fixes.

Show Answer

Ans:

Neck strain and wrist RSI. Fixes: raise the chair or lower the desk, laptop stand + external keyboard, wrist straight, breaks.

COMPETENCYC54 marks

5. Read and answer: A lab has tangled wires, unmarked CDs, glare, and students skip spell-check. (a) Map each problem to 5S or ergonomics or quality. (b) One action each. (c) Why quality matters in a data-entry job role.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Wires/CDs → 5S Set in order; glare → ergonomics; skip spell-check → quality.
(b) Cable ties + labels; blinds or move screens; compulsory preview + peer check.
(c) Wrong data costs money and trust — the job role is Domestic Data Entry Operator.
