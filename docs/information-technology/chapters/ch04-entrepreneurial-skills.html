Chapter 4 · Entrepreneurial Skills–II

Chapter 4 of 20. Definition plus four qualities plus one myth — that is the whole 3-mark answer.

Open this chapter’s questions + MCQs →Mock-test this chapter (timed, scored) →

🎯 Marks lens 📖 Deep concepts 🧮 Formulas 🛠️ Steps 🧠 Tricks ⚠️ Mistakes ❓ Exam Q&A ✍️ Task

🎯 Marks lens

3 marks. Qualities of an entrepreneur and “role in society” rotate.
Always add a small Indian example (a local bakery, a tutoring app).

📖 Deep concepts

1. Entrepreneur — a person who spots a need, takes a risk, organises resources and starts a business to earn profit and create value.
2. Entrepreneurship is the process of creating and running that venture.
3. Qualities: initiative, risk-taking, creativity, perseverance, self-confidence, leadership, problem-solving, honesty, hard work.
4. Functions: identify opportunity, arrange land/labour/capital, hire and lead, take decisions, bear risk, innovate, market the product.
5. Myths: “entrepreneurs are born, not made”; “you need a huge amount of money”; “it is only for the highly educated”; “failure means the person is useless”. All false — skills can be learned, many start small, failure is data.
6. Role in society: jobs, new products, use of local resources, wealth, solving local problems (waste, transport, tuition).
7. Types (brief): small business, scalable start-up, social entrepreneur (profit is not the only aim).

🧮 Formulas to memorise

  • Marketing mix — the 4 Ps: Product, Price, Place, Promotion
  • SWOT: Strengths, Weaknesses (internal) · Opportunities, Threats (external)
  • Profit = Total revenue − Total cost; Break-even = Fixed cost ÷ (Price − Variable cost per unit)
  • A business plan names: the idea, the customer, the money needed, and how it will be earned back

🛠️ Method, step by step

  1. Start from a problem people have, not from a product you like.
  2. Describe the customer exactly — age, place, what they pay for today.
  3. Work out the cost of making one unit and the price you can charge; the difference is your margin.
  4. Write the plan in one page: idea, customer, cost, price, and how you will reach them.

🧠 Memory tricks

  • Quality vs function: quality is a trait (perseverance); function is a job (arrange capital).
  • Myth answers always start with False, then the correction.

⚠️ Mistakes that cost marks

  • Calling every shopkeeper an entrepreneur without the risk/innovation idea.
  • Listing qualities with no example.

SHORTS12 marks

1. Who is an entrepreneur? Give two qualities.

Show Answer

Ans:

A person who spots a need, takes a risk and organises resources to start a venture. Qualities: initiative and perseverance (or creativity, self-confidence).

SHORTS22 marks

2. State two myths about entrepreneurship and correct them.

Show Answer

Ans:

(1) “Born, not made” — skills can be learned. (2) “Needs huge capital” — many start with a small idea and grow.

SHORTS32 marks

3. List any four qualities an entrepreneur needs.

Show Answer

Ans:

Self-confidence, the willingness to take calculated risks, initiative and perseverance, the ability to plan and organise, and the readiness to learn from failure and try again.

SHORTS42 marks

4. What is a social entrepreneur? How does the aim differ from that of a business entrepreneur?

Show Answer

Ans:

A social entrepreneur starts an enterprise mainly to solve a social problem — for example, cheap solar lamps for villages — rather than to maximise profit. A business entrepreneur also creates value but measures success primarily in profit and growth. Both take risks and organise resources; they differ in what counts as the return.

APPLICATIONA53 marks

5. Neha notices students waiting 40 minutes for a shared printer. She starts a paid print-and-bind desk. Which entrepreneurial qualities is she using?

Show Answer

Ans:

Opportunity-spotting, initiative, risk-taking (she invests in a printer), problem-solving and customer focus.

APPLICATIONA63 marks

6. Riya is revising “Entrepreneurial Skills–II” (Who an entrepreneur is, qualities, functions, myths and role in society.). A classmate lost marks by doing this: Calling every shopkeeper an entrepreneur without the risk/innovation idea. Apply the correct method and rewrite the scoring answer.

Show Answer

Ans:

Correct method: Quality vs function: quality is a trait (perseverance); function is a job (arrange capital).
Why the mistake costs marks: Calling every shopkeeper an entrepreneur without the risk/innovation idea.
Write the definition or formula first, then the working or example.

SHORTS73 marks

7. What functions does an entrepreneur perform in running an enterprise?

Show Answer

Ans:

Spotting an opportunity and turning it into a product or service people will pay for.
Arranging the money, buying raw materials, machines and premises, and hiring and training workers.
Organising production, keeping accounts and marketing the product.
Bearing the risk of loss, and taking the decisions that keep the enterprise alive.

LONGL83 marks

8. Explain the role of entrepreneurs in society with reference to jobs, products and local resources.

Show Answer

Ans:

Jobs — every new enterprise hires people, from a bakery's helpers to a startup's designers.
Products — entrepreneurs turn needs into goods and services, such as affordable school stationery or repair shops.
Local resources — they use nearby material and talent, for example fruit from local orchards in a jam unit, keeping wealth in the area.

LONGL94 marks

9. Explain four functions of an entrepreneur with a bakery example.

Show Answer

Ans:

Identify demand for fresh bread; arrange shop, oven and staff; take the risk of unsold stock; market via samples and a board; keep accounts. Each function maps to one quality: opportunity-spotting, organising, risk-taking, selling.

COMPETENCYC104 marks

10. Read and answer: A poster says “Only rich, highly educated people can start a business.” (a) Name the myth. (b) Give two counter-examples from daily life. (c) State two roles of entrepreneurship in society.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Myth of huge capital / only the highly educated.
(b) A tiffin service from a home kitchen; a tuition class in a garage.
(c) Jobs, new products, local resources used, local problems solved.

COMPETENCYC114 marks

11. Read and answer: A Class 10 student is preparing “Entrepreneurial Skills–II”. Their note says: Entrepreneur — a person who spots a need, takes a risk, organises resources and starts a business to earn profit and create value. The chapter is described as: Who an entrepreneur is, qualities, functions, myths and role in society.. (a) Name the key idea. (b) How is this usually asked in the paper? (c) State one mistake that would cost a mark.

Show Answer

Ans:

(a) Who an entrepreneur is, qualities, functions, myths and role in society.
(b) marks. Qualities of an entrepreneur and “role in society” rotate.
(c) Calling every shopkeeper an entrepreneur without the risk/innovation idea.

LONGL125 marks

12. Discuss “Entrepreneurial Skills–II” in detail. Include the idea that carries marks, one worked example or answer frame, and why students lose marks.

Show Answer

Ans:

Entrepreneur — a person who spots a need, takes a risk, organises resources and starts a business to earn profit and create value.
Marks lens: marks. Qualities of an entrepreneur and “role in society” rotate.
Method / memory trick: Quality vs function: quality is a trait (perseverance); function is a job (arrange capital).
Do not: Calling every shopkeeper an entrepreneur without the risk/innovation idea.

Pick one problem in your school. Write a 6-line business idea: customer, product, cost, risk, one quality you would need.
