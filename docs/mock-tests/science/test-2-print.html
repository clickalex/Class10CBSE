Questions

1. The method used to extract sodium from molten NaCl is [Metals and Non-metals]

  • (a) reduction with carbon
  • (b) self reduction
  • (c) electrolytic reduction
  • (d) roasting

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) electrolytic reduction

2. The most exam-ready way to revise “Metals and Non-metals” is [Metals and Non-metals]

  • (a) read the heading only
  • (b) learn the key idea plus one example
  • (c) skip written practice
  • (d) memorise a random extra topic

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) learn the key idea plus one example

3. Which metal reacts vigorously with cold water? [Metals and Non-metals]

  • (a) iron
  • (b) zinc
  • (c) sodium
  • (d) copper

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) sodium

4. Which pair is amphoteric? [Metals and Non-metals]

  • (a) Na2O and CaO
  • (b) Al2O3 and ZnO
  • (c) CO2 and SO2
  • (d) CuO and FeO

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) Al2O3 and ZnO

5. A 3-mark answer on “Carbon and its Compounds” should [Carbon and its Compounds]

  • (a) be one sentence
  • (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method
  • (c) copy the whole chapter
  • (d) leave the example out

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method

6. The functional group present in an alcohol is [Carbon and its Compounds]

  • (a) –CHO
  • (b) –COOH
  • (c) –OH
  • (d) –CO–

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) –OH

7. The gas evolved when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium carbonate is [Carbon and its Compounds]

  • (a) H₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) CO₂
  • (d) CO

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) CO₂

8. The most exam-ready way to revise “Carbon and its Compounds” is [Carbon and its Compounds]

  • (a) read the heading only
  • (b) learn the key idea plus one example
  • (c) skip written practice
  • (d) memorise a random extra topic

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) learn the key idea plus one example

9. The number of covalent bonds in a molecule of ethane (C2H6) is [Carbon and its Compounds]

  • (a) 5
  • (b) 6
  • (c) 7
  • (d) 8

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) 7

10. Vinegar is a solution of [Carbon and its Compounds]

  • (a) 5–8% ethanoic acid in water
  • (b) 50–80% ethanoic acid
  • (c) pure ethanoic acid
  • (d) ethanoic acid in alcohol

Answer: __________   Correct: (a) 5–8% ethanoic acid in water

11. Which compound decolorises bromine water? [Carbon and its Compounds]

  • (a) CH4
  • (b) C2H6
  • (c) C2H4
  • (d) C3H8

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) C2H4

12. Which compound decolourises alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)? [Carbon and its Compounds]

  • (a) C₂H₆
  • (b) C₂H₄
  • (c) CH₄
  • (d) C₃H₈

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) C₂H₄

13. A 3-mark answer on “Life Processes” should [Life Processes]

  • (a) be one sentence
  • (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method
  • (c) copy the whole chapter
  • (d) leave the example out

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method

14. Photosynthesis in a leaf takes place inside the [Life Processes]

  • (a) mitochondria
  • (b) chloroplast
  • (c) ribosome
  • (d) vacuole

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) chloroplast

15. The breakdown of pyruvate to give CO2, water and energy takes place in the [Life Processes]

  • (a) cytoplasm
  • (b) mitochondria
  • (c) chloroplast
  • (d) nucleus

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) mitochondria

16. The kidneys in human beings are a part of the system for [Life Processes]

  • (a) nutrition
  • (b) respiration
  • (c) excretion
  • (d) transportation

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) excretion

17. The main nitrogenous waste filtered out by the kidneys is [Life Processes]

  • (a) carbon dioxide
  • (b) urea
  • (c) glucose
  • (d) glycogen

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) urea

18. The most exam-ready way to revise “Life Processes” is [Life Processes]

  • (a) read the heading only
  • (b) learn the key idea plus one example
  • (c) skip written practice
  • (d) memorise a random extra topic

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) learn the key idea plus one example

19. Which of the following is not a function of the stomach? [Life Processes]

  • (a) killing bacteria
  • (b) digestion of protein
  • (c) absorption of digested food
  • (d) protection of the stomach wall by mucus

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) absorption of digested food

20. Which of these is an autotroph? [Life Processes]

  • (a) mushroom
  • (b) Cuscuta
  • (c) green plant
  • (d) human being

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) green plant
