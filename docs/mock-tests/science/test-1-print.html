2. Rancidity of fat-containing food is prevented by [Chemical Reactions and Equations]

  • (a) storing it in the open
  • (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen
  • (c) adding oxidising agents
  • (d) heating it repeatedly

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) adding antioxidants and flushing with nitrogen

3. The brown fumes evolved when lead nitrate is heated are of [Chemical Reactions and Equations]

  • (a) NO₂
  • (b) O₂
  • (c) NO
  • (d) PbO

Answer: __________   Correct: (a) NO₂

4. The green coating on copper and the black coating on silver are respectively [Chemical Reactions and Equations]

  • (a) basic copper carbonate and Ag2S
  • (b) CuO and Ag2O
  • (c) CuCO3 and AgS2
  • (d) Cu(OH)2 and AgO

  • (c) displacement
  • (d) double displacement

7. Which of the following is an endothermic process? [Chemical Reactions and Equations]

  • (b) decomposition of vegetable matter into compost
  • (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate
  • (d) quicklime + water

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) process of decomposition of ferrous sulphate

8. Which of these is a combination reaction? [Chemical Reactions and Equations]

  • (a) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
  • (b) 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
  • (c) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
  • (d) AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

  • (b) HCl

  • (b) 5

  • (a) less reactive than silver
  • (b) more reactive than silver
  • (c) equally reactive
  • (d) an inert metal

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) more reactive than silver

  • (b) copper and zinc
