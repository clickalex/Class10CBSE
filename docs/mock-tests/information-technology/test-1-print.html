Instructions: 1 mark each, no negative. Time: 60 minutes. Circle the correct option, then check with answer key below.

Questions

1. A loud generator prevents a worker from hearing instructions. The barrier is [Communication Skills–II]

  • (a) linguistic
  • (b) cultural
  • (c) physical
  • (d) emotional

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) physical

2. A student's puzzled expression after an explanation is [Communication Skills–II]

  • (a) written encoding
  • (b) non-verbal feedback
  • (c) a channel
  • (d) a language rule

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) non-verbal feedback

3. Paralanguage refers to [Communication Skills–II]

  • (a) body posture only
  • (b) charts and maps
  • (c) tone, pitch and speed of voice
  • (d) grammar rules

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) tone, pitch and speed of voice

4. The communication process is complete only when [Communication Skills–II]

  • (a) the receiver gives feedback
  • (b) the message is written
  • (c) the sender speaks
  • (d) the channel is chosen

Answer: __________   Correct: (a) the receiver gives feedback

5. The easiest method of communication is [Communication Skills–II]

  • (a) written
  • (b) oral
  • (c) non-verbal
  • (d) email

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) non-verbal

6. Which method of communication carries no body language or tone of voice? [Communication Skills–II]

  • (a) written communication
  • (b) face-to-face talk
  • (c) video call
  • (d) group discussion

Answer: __________   Correct: (a) written communication

7. Which of the 7 Cs means “to the point, no extra words”? [Communication Skills–II]

  • (a) Clear
  • (b) Concise
  • (c) Concrete
  • (d) Courteous

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) Concise

8. Which part of communication converts an idea into words or signs? [Communication Skills–II]

  • (a) Receiving
  • (b) Encoding
  • (c) Decoding
  • (d) Feedback

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) Encoding

9. Which sentence is complete? [Communication Skills–II]

  • (a) Do that work.
  • (b) The form is important.
  • (c) Submit the signed form to Room 4 by Friday noon.
  • (d) Submit it soon.

Answer: __________   Correct: (c) Submit the signed form to Room 4 by Friday noon.

10. “An honest boy” takes which article? [Communication Skills–II]

  • (a) a
  • (b) an
  • (c) the
  • (d) no article

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) an

11. A 3-mark answer on “Self-Management Skills–II” should [Self-Management Skills–II]

  • (a) be one sentence
  • (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method
  • (c) copy the whole chapter
  • (d) leave the example out

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method

12. A hobby after study is mainly [Self-Management Skills–II]

  • (a) a waste
  • (b) a stress-management method
  • (c) self-employment
  • (d) a barrier

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) a stress-management method

13. Knowing your own strengths is [Self-Management Skills–II]

  • (a) self-awareness
  • (b) encoding
  • (c) a physical barrier
  • (d) green skill

Answer: __________   Correct: (a) self-awareness

14. SMART: the M stands for [Self-Management Skills–II]

  • (a) Magical
  • (b) Measurable
  • (c) Maximum
  • (d) Monthly

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) Measurable

15. Self-awareness means [Self-Management Skills–II]

  • (a) knowing your own strengths, weaknesses and feelings
  • (b) only knowing other people
  • (c) working without help
  • (d) following orders

Answer: __________   Correct: (a) knowing your own strengths, weaknesses and feelings

16. The most exam-ready way to revise “Self-Management Skills–II” is [Self-Management Skills–II]

  • (a) read the heading only
  • (b) learn the key idea plus one example
  • (c) skip written practice
  • (d) memorise a random extra topic

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) learn the key idea plus one example

17. The useful stress that pushes you to perform better is called [Self-Management Skills–II]

  • (a) eustress
  • (b) distress
  • (c) burnout
  • (d) depression

Answer: __________   Correct: (a) eustress

18. Which goal is SMART? [Self-Management Skills–II]

  • (a) I will study more
  • (b) I will type 40 correct rows in Calc by 3 pm
  • (c) I will become famous
  • (d) I will try

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) I will type 40 correct rows in Calc by 3 pm

19. A 3-mark answer on “ICT Skills–II” should [ICT Skills–II]

  • (a) be one sentence
  • (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method
  • (c) copy the whole chapter
  • (d) leave the example out

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) start with the idea, then a reason or method

20. A computer should be shut down [ICT Skills–II]

  • (a) by pulling the plug
  • (b) using the OS shutdown command
  • (c) by holding the monitor button
  • (d) never

Answer: __________   Correct: (b) using the OS shutdown command
