# 05 · Social Science (Code 087)

One paper, four disciplines, **20 marks each**.

- **Session:** 2026–27 · folder tree and chapter list checked 12 Sep 2026
- **Theory:** 80 marks · **Internal assessment:** 20 marks · **Time:** 3 hours
- **Textbooks:** four separate NCERT books, one per discipline

| Discipline | NCERT book | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| History | *India and the Contemporary World — II* | 20 (incl. map pointing) |
| Geography | *Contemporary India — II* | 20 (incl. map pointing) |
| Political Science | *Democratic Politics — II* | 20 |
| Economics | *Understanding Economic Development* | 20 |

Notes and solutions are split by discipline first, then by chapter:
`03-Notes/History/ch02-Nationalism-in-India/` and so on.

## Map work

`10-Map-Work/History/` and `10-Map-Work/Geography/` hold the official CBSE list
of map items for the session plus your practice outlines. Map questions are the
cheapest marks in the paper — they are pure recall and come with a printed
outline. Download the current session's list of map items from CBSE Academic and
keep it in `01-Syllabus/`.

`11-Project-Work/` is for the Economics Consumer Rights project and any other
internal-assessment project.
