**Official website: <https://www.pw.live/scholarship/vidyapeeth/nsat>**

[NSAT-pattern mock test](https://clickalex.github.io/Class10CBSE/mock-test/pw-nsat/)

banks, not PW papers).

PW NSAT is Physics Wallah's National Scholarship cum Admission Test for its
coaching offerings. **It is not a Class XI school/college admission test** and
is separate from JMI, AMU and BHU/CHS entrance exams.

| Detail | Official-page information |

| Offline test | 3, 4, 10 and 11 October 2026 |
| Offline slots | 10–11 AM or 4–5 PM; confirm centre and slot on the portal |

| Level-1 format | Single-correct-answer questions; +4 correct, no negative marking |

| Registration | Page displayed “Register for free”; closing date not verified |

Source: [official PW NSAT page and FAQ](https://www.pw.live/scholarship/vidyapeeth/nsat).
This is a **dated snapshot**, not live availability. Recheck dates, slots and
terms before applying. “Up to 100% scholarship” is not a guaranteed award;
confirm eligible batches, exclusions and remaining fees directly with PW.

The website snapshot is maintained in `site/content/pw-nsat.json`; update that
file and rebuild `docs/` when details change. The 9 PM admission and scholarship checker now includes PW NSAT, while
keeping its coaching scholarship route separate from school admissions.

Keep downloaded PDFs and all personal registration records locally. Never add
